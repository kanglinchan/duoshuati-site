<WpmeText text="3. and what's below?The field is the comparatively little and insignificant whirling ball on whichwelive—the earth.Howdid our planet come into being, what is it made of, how were its mountains, oceans, rivers,plains, and valleys formed, and what's down deep if you start digging?A geologist" />

<WpmeText text="4. what is life?The field is all living organisms--from the simplest one-celled amoeba to the amazingly complex and mystifying structure we call a human being.
