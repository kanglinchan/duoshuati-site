# 第 45 课：The Power of the Press

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

The power of the press<br><span class="zh">媒体的力量</span>

Does the writer think the parents were lucky or unlucky to gain prosperity in this way? Why?<br><span class="zh">作者认为，这些父母通过这种方式获得财富是幸运的，还是不幸的？为什么？</span>

In democratic countries any efforts to restrict the freedom of the press are rightly condemned.<br><span class="zh">在民主国家中，任何限制新闻自由的行为都应受到谴责。</span>

However, this freedom can easily be abused.<br><span class="zh">然而，这种自由很容易被滥用。</span>

Stories about people often attract far more public attention than political events.<br><span class="zh">关于普通人的故事往往比政治事件更能吸引公众的注意力。</span>

Though we may enjoy reading about the lives of others,<br><span class="zh">虽然我们可能会喜欢阅读关于他人生活的故事，</span>

it is extremely doubtful whether we would equally enjoy reading about ourselves.<br><span class="zh">我们是否也会同样喜欢阅读关于自己的内容，这一点实在非常值得怀疑。</span>

Acting on the contention that facts are sacred,<br><span class="zh">基于“事实是神圣的”这一观点，</span>

reporters can cause untold suffering to individuals by publishing details about their private lives.<br><span class="zh">记者通过公开个人的私生活细节，可能会给这些人带来难以估量的痛苦。</span>

Newspapers exert such tremendous influence that they can not only bring about major changes to the lives of ordinary people but can even overthrow a government.<br><span class="zh">报纸的影响力极其巨大；它们不仅能够对普通人的生活产生重大影响，甚至有可能推翻一个政府。</span>

The story of a poor family that acquired fame and fortune overnight, dramatically illustrates the power of the press.<br><span class="zh">这个关于一个贫困家庭如何一夜之间获得名望和财富的故事，生动地展示了媒体的影响力。</span>

The family lived in Aberdeen, a small town of 23, 000 inhabitants in South Dakota.<br><span class="zh">这家人住在阿伯丁（Aberdeen），一个位于南达科他州（South Dakota）的小镇上，该镇共有23,000名居民。</span>

As the parents had five children, life was a perpetual struggle against poverty.<br><span class="zh">由于这对父母有五个孩子，他们的生活始终在与贫困作斗争。</span>

They were expecting their sixth child and were faced with even more pressing economic problems.<br><span class="zh">他们即将迎来第六个孩子，同时也面临着更加紧迫的经济问题。</span>

If they had only had one more child, the fact would have passed unnoticed.<br><span class="zh">如果他们再多要一个孩子的话，这件事可能就会被忽视、无人注意了。</span>

They would have continued to struggle against economic odds and would have lived in obscurity.<br><span class="zh">他们本会继续在艰难的经济环境中挣扎，过着默默无闻的生活。</span>

But they suddenly became the parents of quintuplets, four girls and a boy, an event which radically changed their lives.<br><span class="zh">但他们突然成为了五胞胎的父母——四个女孩和一个男孩，这一事件彻底改变了他们的生活。</span>

The day after the birth of the five children, an aeroplane arrived in Aberdeen bringing sixty reporters and photographers.<br><span class="zh">在五个孩子出生的第二天，一架飞机抵达阿伯丁，带来了六十名记者和摄影师。</span>

The rise to fame was swift.<br><span class="zh">他们一举成名。</span>

Television cameras and newspapers carried the news to everyone in the country.<br><span class="zh">电视摄像机与报纸将这一消息传遍了全国的每一个人。</span>

Newspapers and magazines offered the family huge sums for the exclusive rights to publish stories and photographs.<br><span class="zh">报纸和杂志向这家人提供了巨额报酬，以获得独家报道这些事件和发布相关照片的权利。</span>

Gifts poured in not only from unknown people,<br><span class="zh">礼物不仅来自陌生人，</span>

but from baby food and soap manufacturers who wished to advertise their products.<br><span class="zh">也来自希望借此宣传产品的婴儿食品和肥皂制造商。</span>

The old farmhouse the family lived in was to be replaced by a new $500, 000 home.<br><span class="zh">他们曾经居住的旧农舍将被一座价值50万美元的新房子所取代。</span>

Reporters kept pressing for interviews so lawyers had to be employed to act as spokesmen for the family at press conferences.<br><span class="zh">记者们不断要求采访，因此这家人不得不聘请律师在新闻发布会上担任发言人。</span>

While the five babies were still quietly sleeping in oxygen tents in a hospital nursery,<br><span class="zh">当这五个婴儿还在医院婴儿房的氧气帐篷里安静地睡着时……</span>

their parents were paying the price for fame.<br><span class="zh">他们的父母正在为他们的名气付出代价。</span>

It would never again be possible for them to lead normal lives.<br><span class="zh">他们再也无法过上正常的生活了。</span>

They had become the victims of commercialization, for their names had acquired a market value.<br><span class="zh">他们成为了商业化的受害者，因为他们的名字已经具有了市场价值。</span>

Instead of being five new family members, these children had immediately become a commodity.<br><span class="zh">这些孩子没有成为家庭的新成员，反而立刻被当成了商品。</span>
