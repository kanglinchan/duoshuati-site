# 第 16 课：Mary Had a Little Lamb

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Mary had a little lamb<br><span class="zh">玛丽有一只小羊羔。</span>

Was Dimitri right to apologize to his neighbour? Why not?<br><span class="zh">迪米特里向邻居道歉，这样做对吗？为什么不对？</span>

Mary and her husband Dimitri lived in the tiny village of Perachora in southern Greece.<br><span class="zh">玛丽和她的丈夫迪米特里住在希腊南部的一个小村庄——佩拉霍拉（Perachora）。</span>

One of Mary's prize possessions was a little white lamb which her husband had given her.<br><span class="zh">玛丽最珍视的物品之一是一只白色的小羊羔，这是她丈夫送给她的。</span>

She kept it tied to a tree in a field during the day and went to fetch it every evening.<br><span class="zh">她白天把它拴在田野里的一棵树上，每晚都会去把它取回来。</span>

One evening, however the lamb was missing.<br><span class="zh">然而，有一天晚上，那只小羊不见了。</span>

The rope had been cut, so it was obvious that the lamb had been stolen.<br><span class="zh">绳子已经被剪断了，因此很明显这只小羊是被偷走了。</span>

When Dimitri came in from the fields, his wife told him what had happened.<br><span class="zh">当迪米特里从田里回来时，他的妻子告诉了他发生的事情。</span>

Dimitri at once set out to find the thief.<br><span class="zh">迪米特里立刻着手寻找小偷。</span>

He knew it would not prove difficult in such a small village.<br><span class="zh">他知道在这么小的村庄里，要找到小偷应该不难。</span>

After telling several of his friends about the theft,<br><span class="zh">在向他的几个朋友讲述了这起盗窃事件之后，</span>

Dimitri found out that his neighbour, Aleko, had suddenly acquired a new lamb.<br><span class="zh">迪米特里发现他的邻居阿莱科突然养了一只小羊。</span>

Dimitri immediately went to Aleko's house and angrily accused him of stealing the lamb.<br><span class="zh">迪米特里立刻去了阿莱科的家，愤怒地指责他偷了小羊。</span>

He told him he had better return it or he would call the police.<br><span class="zh">他告诉阿莱科最好把羊还回来，否则他就报警。</span>

Aleko denied taking it and led Dimitri into his backyard.<br><span class="zh">阿莱科否认拿了羊，并带着迪米特里去了他的后院。</span>

It was true that he had just bought a lamb he explained, but his lamb was black.<br><span class="zh">他解释说，他确实刚买了一只小羊，不过那只小羊是黑色的。</span>

Ashamed of having acted so rashly, Dimitri apologized to Aleko for having accused him.<br><span class="zh">迪米特里为自己如此鲁莽的行为感到羞愧，便向阿莱科道歉，为自己冤枉了他。</span>

While they were talking it began to rain and Dimitri stayed in Aleko's house until the rain stopped.<br><span class="zh">他们正在谈话的时候，天开始下雨了。迪米特里便待在阿莱科的家里，直到雨停。</span>

When he went outside half an hour later, he was astonished to find that the little black lamb was almost white.<br><span class="zh">半小时后，当他走到外面时，惊讶地发现那只小黑羊几乎变成了白色。</span>

Its wool, which had been dyed black, had been washed clean by the rain!<br><span class="zh">那块原本被染成黑色的羊毛，被雨水冲洗得干干净净了！</span>
