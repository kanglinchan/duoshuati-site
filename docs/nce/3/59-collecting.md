# 第 59 课：Collecting

<NcePair en="What in particular does a person gain when he or she becomes a serious collector?" zh="一个人成为真正的收藏家后，具体能获得什么呢？" />

<NcePair en="People tend to amass possessions, sometimes without being aware of doing so." zh="人们往往会积累财物，有时甚至自己都没有意识到这一点。" />

<NcePair en="Indeed they can have a delightful surprise when they find something useful which they did not know they owned." zh="确实，当他们发现某些自己原本不知道拥有的、但却非常实用的东西时，会感到非常惊喜。" />

<NcePair en="Those who never have to move house become indiscriminate collectors of what can only be described as clutter." zh="那些从未搬过家的人，会无差别地收集各种杂物——这些杂物其实只能被归类为“杂乱无章的堆积物”。" />

<NcePair en="They leave unwanted objects in drawers, cupboards and attics for years, in the belief that they may one day need just those very things." zh="他们把不需要的物品放在抽屉、橱柜或阁楼里多年，认为有朝一日自己可能会用到那些东西。" />

<NcePair en="As they grow old, people also accumulate belongings for two other reasons," zh="随着年龄的增长，人们积累物品还有另外两个原因：" />

<NcePair en="lack of physical and mental energy, both of which are essential in turning out and throwing away, and sentiment." zh="缺乏体力和精力——这两者都是清理和丢弃物品所必需的；此外还有情感因素。" />

<NcePair en="Things owned for a long time are full of associations with the past," zh="那些已经拥有很长时间的东西，都承载着与过去相关的种种回忆和情感联系。" />

<NcePair en="perhaps with relatives who are dead, and so they gradually acquire a value beyond their true worth." zh="也许这些物品与已经去世的亲人有关联，因此它们逐渐被赋予了超出其实际价值的意义（或：这些物品因为与逝去亲人的联系而变得更有价值）。" />

<NcePair en="Some things are collected deliberately in the home in an attempt to avoid waste." zh="有些物品是人们特意在家里收集起来的，目的是为了减少浪费。" />

<NcePair en="Among these I would list string and brown paper," zh="在这些物品中，我会列举细绳和牛皮纸。" />

<NcePair en="kept by thrifty people when a parcel has been opened, to save buying these two requisites." zh="节俭的人在拆开包裹后，会保留这些物品，以避免再次购买这两样必需品。" />

<NcePair en="Collecting small items can easily become a mania." zh="收集小物件很容易变成一种狂热。" />

<NcePair en="I know someone who always cuts sketches out from newspapers of model clothes that she would like to buy if she had the money." zh="我认识一个人，她总是从报纸上剪下那些她想要购买的模特服装的图片；如果她有钱的话，一定会买下这些衣服。" />

<NcePair en="As she is not rich, the chances that she will ever be able to afford such purchases are remote;" zh="由于她并不富裕，她能够负担得起这类购买的费用的可能性微乎其微。" />

<NcePair en="but she is never sufficiently strongminded to be able to stop the practice." zh="但她始终缺乏足够的意志力来改掉这个习惯。" />

<NcePair en="It is a harmless habit, but it litters up her desk to such an extent that every time she opens it, loose bits of paper fall out in every direction." zh="这其实是个无伤大雅的习惯，但这个习惯让她的办公桌变得非常杂乱——每次她打开桌子时，都会有一堆散落的纸屑从各个方向掉出来。" />

<NcePair en="Collecting as a serious hobby is quite different and has many advantages." zh="将收藏作为一种严肃的爱好来从事，其实有着许多独特的优势。" />

<NcePair en="It provides relaxation for leisure hours, as just looking at one's treasures is always a joy." zh="它为休闲时光带来了放松与愉悦；仅仅欣赏自己的收藏品，本身就是一种享受。" />

<NcePair en="One does not have to go outside for amusement, since the collection is housed at home." zh="人们不必外出寻找娱乐方式，因为这些藏品都收藏在家里。" />

<NcePair en="Whatever it consists of, stamps, records, first editions of books, china, glass," zh="无论是邮票、唱片、初版书籍、瓷器、玻璃制品，" />

<NcePair en="antique furniture, pictures, model cars, stuffed birds, toy animals, there is always something to do in connection with it," zh="古董家具、画作、模型汽车、标本鸟、玩具动物……总有相关的事情可做，" />

<NcePair en="from finding the right place for the latest addition, to verifying facts in reference books." zh="从为最新添加的内容找到合适的位置，到在参考书中核实事实……" />

<NcePair en="This hobby educates one not only in the chosen subject, but also in general matters which have some bearing on it." zh="这个爱好不仅能让人在所选择的领域内学到知识，还能帮助人们了解与该领域相关的一些通用性知识。" />

<NcePair en="There are also other benefits." zh="还有其他一些好处。" />

<NcePair en="One wants to meet like-minded collectors, to get advice, to compare notes, to exchange articles, to show off the latest find." zh="人们希望结识志同道合的收藏家，获取建议，交流心得，交换藏品，展示最新发现。" />

<NcePair en="So one's circle of friends grows." zh="因此，一个人的朋友圈子也会逐渐扩大。" />

<NcePair en="Soon the hobby leads to travel, perhaps to a meeting in another town," zh="很快，这个爱好还会促使人们去旅行，也许到另一个城镇参加聚会，" />

<NcePair en="possibly a trip abroad in search of a rare specimen, for collectors are not confined to any one country." zh="也许他们会去国外寻找稀有的标本，因为收藏家们并不局限于某个特定的国家。" />

<NcePair en="Over the years, one may well become an authority on one's hobby and will very probably be asked to give informal talks to little gatherings" zh="随着时间的推移，一个人很可能会在自己的爱好领域成为专家，并且很可能会被邀请在一些小型聚会上进行非正式的演讲。" />

<NcePair en="and then, if successful, to larger audiences." zh="然后，如果讲得成功，就会面向更多听众演讲。" />

<NcePair en="In this way self-confidence grows, first from mastering a subject, then from being able to talk about it." zh="通过这种方式，自信心得以增长：先是源于对一门学科的掌握，然后是源于能够就此侃侃而谈。" />

<NcePair en="Collecting, by occupying spare time so constructively, makes a person contented, with no time for boredom." zh="通过利用空闲时间来从事有意义的活动，一个人会感到满足，从而不会有无聊的感觉。" />
