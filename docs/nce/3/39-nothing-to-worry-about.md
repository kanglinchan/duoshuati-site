# 第 39 课：Nothing to Worry About

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Nothing to worry about<br><span class="zh">没什么好担心的。</span>

What was the differnce between Bruce's behaviour and that of other people?<br><span class="zh">布鲁斯的行为与其他人的行为有什么不同？</span>

The rough road across the plain soon became so bad that we tried to get Bruce to drive back to the village we had come from.<br><span class="zh">穿过平原的崎岖道路状况越来越糟糕，我们便决定让布鲁斯开车把我们送回我们出发的村庄。</span>

Even though the road was littered with boulders and pitted with holes, Bruce was not in the least perturbed.<br><span class="zh">尽管路上布满了巨石，还有许多坑洞，布鲁斯却丝毫没有感到不安。</span>

Glancing at his map, he informed us that the next village was a mere twenty miles away.<br><span class="zh">他看了一眼地图，然后告诉我们下一个村庄只有二十英里远。</span>

It was not that Bruce always underestimated difficulties.<br><span class="zh">并不是说布鲁斯总是低估困难。</span>

He simply had no sense of danger at all.<br><span class="zh">他完全没有任何危险意识。</span>

No matter what the conditions were, he believed that a car should be driven as fast as it could possibly go.<br><span class="zh">无论条件如何，他都认为汽车应该能开多快就开多快。</span>

As we bumped over the dusty track, we swerved to avoid large boulders.<br><span class="zh">我们在颠簸的土路上行驶时，不断转向以避开那些巨大的岩石。</span>

The wheels scooped up stones which hammered ominously under the car.<br><span class="zh">车轮卷起了石子，这些石子在汽车下方发出令人不安的撞击声。</span>

We felt sure that sooner or later a stone would rip a hole in our petrol tank or damage the engine.<br><span class="zh">我们确信，迟早会有石头划破我们的油箱，或者损坏发动机。</span>

Because of this, we kept looking back, wondering if we were leaving a trail of oil and petrol behind us.<br><span class="zh">正因如此，我们不断回头查看，担心自己是否留下了一道油迹。</span>

What a relief it was when the boulders suddenly disappeared,<br><span class="zh">当那些巨石突然消失的时候，真是让人松了一口气啊。</span>

giving way to a stretch of plain where the only obstacles were clumps bushes.<br><span class="zh">取而代之的是一片开阔的平原，唯一的障碍只是一些灌木丛。</span>

But there was worse to come. Just ahead of us there was a huge fissure.<br><span class="zh">但更糟糕的事情还在后面。就在我们前方，有一条巨大的裂缝。</span>

In response to renewed pleadings, Bruce stopped.<br><span class="zh">在我们再次恳求下，布鲁斯停了下来。</span>

Though we all got out to examine the fissure, he remained in the car.<br><span class="zh">虽然我们都下车去查看那条裂缝，但他却留在了车里。</span>

We informed him that the fissure extended for fifty yards and was two feet wide and four feet deep.<br><span class="zh">我们告诉他，那条裂缝长达五十码（约45.7米），宽度为两英尺（约0.6米），深度为四英尺（约1.2米）。</span>

Even this had no effect.<br><span class="zh">即使这样，也没有任何效果。</span>

Bruce went into a low gear and drove at a terrifying speed,<br><span class="zh">布鲁斯挂上低速挡，以惊人的速度向前冲去，</span>

keeping the front wheels astride the crack as he followed its zigzag course.<br><span class="zh">他让前轮分别跨在裂缝两侧，沿着蜿蜒的路线前进。</span>

Before we had time to worry about what might happen, we were back on the plain again.<br><span class="zh">我们还来不及担心会发生什么，就已经回到了平原上。</span>

Bruce consulted the map once more and told us that the village was now only fifteen miles away.<br><span class="zh">布鲁斯再次查看了地图，然后告诉我们那个村庄现在距离我们只有十五英里了。</span>

Our next obstacle was a shallow pool of water about half a mile across.<br><span class="zh">我们的下一个障碍是一个大约半英里宽的浅水池。</span>

Bruce charged at it, but in the middle, the car came to a grinding halt.<br><span class="zh">布鲁斯驾车冲了过去，但车子在半路上突然停了下来。</span>

A yellow light on the dashboard flashed angrily and Bruce cheerfully announced that there was no oil in the engine!<br><span class="zh">仪表盘上的黄灯愤怒地闪烁着，布鲁斯却高兴地宣布：发动机里没油了！</span>
