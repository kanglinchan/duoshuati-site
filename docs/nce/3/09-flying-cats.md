# 第 9 课：Flying Cats

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Flying cats<br><span class="zh">会飞的猫</span>

How do cats try to protect themselves when falling from great heights?<br><span class="zh">当猫从高处坠落时，它们会如何保护自己呢？</span>

Cats never fail to fascinate human beings.<br><span class="zh">猫总是能让人类着迷。</span>

They can be friendly and affectionate towards humans, but they lead mysterious lives of their own as well.<br><span class="zh">它们对人类可能表现出友好和亲昵的态度，但同时也过着充满神秘的生活。</span>

They never become submissive like dogs and horses.<br><span class="zh">与狗或马不同，猫永远不会变得温顺听话。</span>

As a result, humans have learned to respect feline independence.<br><span class="zh">因此，人类逐渐学会了尊重猫的独立性。</span>

Most cats remain suspicious of humans all their lives.<br><span class="zh">大多数猫一生都对人类保持警惕。</span>

One of the things that fascinates us most about cats is the popular belief that they have nine lives.<br><span class="zh">关于猫最让我们感到好奇的一点，就是人们普遍认为它们有九条命。</span>

Apparently, there is a good deal of truth in this idea.<br><span class="zh">事实上，这个说法确实有一定道理。</span>

A cat's ability to survive falls is based on fact.<br><span class="zh">猫之所以能够在坠落中存活下来，是有科学依据的。</span>

Recently the New York Animal Medical Centre made a study of 132 cats over a period of five months.<br><span class="zh">最近，纽约动物医疗中心对132只猫进行了为期五个月的研究。</span>

All these cats had one experience in common: they had fallen off high buildings,<br><span class="zh">所有这些猫都有一个共同的经历：它们都从高楼上掉了下来。</span>

yet only eight of them died from shock or injuries.<br><span class="zh">然而，只有八只猫因休克或受伤而死亡。</span>

Of course, New York is the ideal place for such an interesting study, because there is no shortage of tall buildings.<br><span class="zh">当然，纽约是进行这项研究的理想地点——因为那里的高楼大厦实在太多了。</span>

There are plenty of high-rise windowsills to fall from!<br><span class="zh">有足够多的高楼窗台可供坠落！</span>

One cat, Sabrina, fell 32 storeys, yet only suffered from a broken tooth.<br><span class="zh">有一只名叫萨布丽娜的猫从32层楼的高度坠落，却只摔断了一颗牙齿。</span>

'Cats behave like well-trained paratroopers,' a doctor said.<br><span class="zh">“猫的行为就像训练有素的伞兵一样，”一位医生说道。</span>

It seems that the further cats fall, the less they are likely to injure themselves.<br><span class="zh">看起来，猫掉得越远，它们受伤的可能性就越小。</span>

In a long drop, they reach speeds of 60 miles an hour and more.<br><span class="zh">在自由落体的过程中，它们的速度可以达到每小时60英里甚至更高。</span>

At high speeds, falling cats have time to relax.<br><span class="zh">高速下落时，猫有时间放松身体。</span>

They stretch out their legs like flying squirrels.<br><span class="zh">它们像飞鼠一样伸开四肢。</span>

This increases their air-resistance and reduces the shock of impact when they hit the ground.<br><span class="zh">这会增加它们的空气阻力，但同时也能减少它们落地时所受的冲击力。</span>
