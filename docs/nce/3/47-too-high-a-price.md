# 第 47 课：Too High a Price

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Too high a price?<br><span class="zh">代价是否太高？</span>

What does the writer describe as an 'amusing old-fashioned source of noise' ?<br><span class="zh">作者将什么描述为“一种有趣的老式噪音来源”？</span>

Pollution is the price we pay for an overpopulated, overindustrialized planet.<br><span class="zh">污染是我们这个人口过剩、过度工业化的星球所必须付出的代价。</span>

When you come to think about it, there are only four ways you can deal with rubbish:<br><span class="zh">仔细想想，处理垃圾其实只有四种方法：</span>

dump it, burn it, turn it into something you can use again, attempt to produce less of it.<br><span class="zh">把它扔掉、烧掉，或者把它改造成可以再次使用的东西；尽量减少它的生产量。</span>

We keep trying all four methods, but sheer volume of rubbish we produce worldwide threatens to overwhelm us.<br><span class="zh">我们一直在尝试这四种方法，但全球产生的垃圾数量实在庞大，几乎让我们应接不暇。</span>

Rubbish, however, is only part of the problem of polluting our planet.<br><span class="zh">然而，垃圾仅仅是污染我们地球问题的一个方面罢了。</span>

The need to produce ever increasing quantities of cheap food leads to a different kind of pollution.<br><span class="zh">对廉价食品日益增长的需求导致了另一种形式的污染。</span>

Industriallized farming metheods produce cheap meat products: beef, pork and chicken.<br><span class="zh">工业化农业生产方式能够制造出价格低廉的肉类产品，包括牛肉、猪肉和鸡肉。</span>

The use of pesticides and fertilizers produces cheap grain and vegetables.<br><span class="zh">使用农药和化肥可以生产出价格低廉的谷物和蔬菜。</span>

The price we pay for cheap food may be already too high:<br><span class="zh">我们为廉价食品所支付的价格可能已经太高了。</span>

Mad Cow Disease (BSE) in cattle, salmonella in chicken and eggs, and listeria in dairy products.<br><span class="zh">牛的疯牛病（BSE）、鸡肉和鸡蛋中的沙门氏菌、乳制品中的李斯特菌……</span>

And if you think you'll abandon meat and become a vegetarian,<br><span class="zh">如果你打算放弃吃肉、成为素食者，</span>

you have the choice of very expensive organically-grown vegetables<br><span class="zh">你也有选择：要么购买价格昂贵的有机蔬菜，</span>

or a steady diet of pesticides every time you think you're eating fresh salads and vegetables,<br><span class="zh">要么每次以为自己在吃新鲜沙拉或蔬菜时，其实都在摄入农药；</span>

or just having an innocent glass of water!<br><span class="zh">或者，你也可以选择简单地喝一杯水……</span>

However, there is an even more insidious kind of pollution that particularly affects urban area and invades our daily lives, and that is noise.<br><span class="zh">然而，还有一种更为隐蔽的污染形式，它尤其影响城市地区，并渗透到我们的日常生活中，那就是噪音。</span>

Burglar alarms going off at any time of the day or night serve only to annoy passers-by and actually assist burglars to burgle.<br><span class="zh">防盗警报在日夜任何时候突然响起，不仅会惹恼路人，反而会帮助窃贼作案；</span>

Car alarms constantly scream at us in the street and are a source of profound irritation.<br><span class="zh">汽车警报声在街上不断刺耳地鸣叫，令人极度烦躁。</span>

A recent survey of the effects of noise revealed (surprisingly?)<br><span class="zh">最近一项关于噪音影响的调查显示（令人惊讶的是），</span>

that dogs barking incessantly in the night rated the highest form of noise pollution on a scale ranging from 1 to 7.<br><span class="zh">夜间狗不停地吠叫被列为噪音污染中最严重的形式（在1到7的评分体系中得分最高）。</span>

The survey revealed a large number of sources of noise that we really dislike.<br><span class="zh">这项调查还揭示了许多我们非常讨厌的噪音来源：</span>

Lawn mowers whining on a summer's day, late-night parties in apartment blocks,<br><span class="zh">夏日里割草机的嗡嗡声、公寓楼里的深夜聚会……</span>

noisy neighbours, vehicles of all kinds,<br><span class="zh">吵闹的邻居们，各种各样的车辆……</span>

especially large container trucks thundering through quiet villages,<br><span class="zh">尤其是那些巨大的集装箱卡车，在宁静的村庄里轰鸣着驶过……</span>

planes and helicopters flying overhead, large radios carried round in public places and played at maximum volume.<br><span class="zh">飞机和直升机在头顶上飞过；在公共场所，人们会携带大型收音机，并将音量调至最大来播放音乐。</span>

New technology has also made its own contribution to noise.<br><span class="zh">新技术也为噪音问题做出了自己的'贡献'。</span>

A lot of people object to mobile phones, especially when they are used in public places like restaurant or on public transport.<br><span class="zh">很多人反对在公共场所（如餐厅或公共交通工具上）使用手机。</span>

Loud conversations on mobile phones invade our thoughts or interrupt the pleasure of meeting friends for a quiet chat.<br><span class="zh">手机上大声的通话声会干扰我们的思绪，或者打断我们与朋友安静交谈的乐趣。</span>

The noise pollution survey revealed a rather surprising and possibly amusing old-fashioned source of noise.<br><span class="zh">噪音污染调查显示了一个相当令人惊讶、甚至有点滑稽的老式噪音来源。</span>

It turned out to be snoring! Men were found to be the worst offenders.<br><span class="zh">原来问题出在打鼾上！研究发现，男性是打鼾问题最严重的群体。</span>

It was revealed that 20% of men in their mid-thirties snore.<br><span class="zh">据调查显示，20%的三十多岁男性有打鼾的习惯。</span>

This figure rises to a staggering 60% of men in their sixties.<br><span class="zh">而在六十多岁的男性中，这个数字惊人地高达60%。</span>

Against these figures, it was found that only 5% of women snore regularly,<br><span class="zh">与这些数据相比，研究发现只有5%的女性经常打鼾。</span>

while the rest are constantly woken or kept awake by their trumpeting partners.<br><span class="zh">而其余的女性则不断被她们如吹号般的伴侣吵醒或无法入睡。</span>

Whatever the source of noise, one thing is certain: silence, it seems, has become a golden memory.<br><span class="zh">无论噪音的来源是什么，有一点是肯定的：沉默似乎已经成为了一段珍贵的回忆。</span>
