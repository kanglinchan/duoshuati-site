# 第 44 课：Speed and Comfort

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Speed and comfort<br><span class="zh">速度与舒适性</span>

Which type of transport does the writer prefer, do you think?<br><span class="zh">你认为作者更喜欢哪种交通工具呢？</span>

People travelling long distances frequently have to decide whether they would prefer to go by land, sea, or air.<br><span class="zh">长途旅行的人经常需要决定是选择陆路、海路还是航空方式来出行。</span>

Hardly anyone can positively enjoy sitting in a train for more than a few hours.<br><span class="zh">几乎没有人能够真正享受在火车上坐上几个小时以上的时光。</span>

Train compartments soon get cramped and stuffy.<br><span class="zh">火车车厢很快就会变得拥挤不堪、空气也不流通（即非常闷热）。</span>

It is almost impossible to take your mind off the journey.<br><span class="zh">几乎不可能让你把注意力从这段旅程上移开。</span>

Reading is only a partial solution, for the monotonous rhythm of the wheels clicking on the rails soon lull you to sleep.<br><span class="zh">阅读只能提供部分缓解；车轮在铁轨上单调地咔嗒作响，这种声音很快就会让你入睡。</span>

During the day, sleep comes in snatches.<br><span class="zh">白天里，睡眠总是断断续续的。</span>

At night, when you really wish to go to sleep, you rarely manage to do so.<br><span class="zh">到了晚上，当你真的想入睡的时候，却往往难以入睡。</span>

If you are lucky enough to get a sleeper,<br><span class="zh">如果你有幸买到一张卧铺票，</span>

you spend half the night staring at the small blue light in the ceiling, or fumbling to find your ticket for inspection.<br><span class="zh">你会花上半个晚上盯着天花板上的小蓝灯，或者摸索着找票接受检查。</span>

Inevitably you arrive at your destination almost exhausted.<br><span class="zh">最终，你几乎精疲力尽地到达了目的地。</span>

Long car journeys are even less pleasant, for it is quite impossible even to read.<br><span class="zh">长途驾车旅行更加令人不适，因为在这种情况下甚至连看书都几乎不可能做到。</span>

On motorways you can, at least, travel fairly safely at high speeds,<br><span class="zh">在高速公路上，至少你可以以较高的速度安全地行驶。</span>

but more often than not, the greater part of the journey is spent on roads with few service stations and too much traffic.<br><span class="zh">但大多数情况下，旅途中的大部分时间都是在那些服务站稀少、交通拥堵严重的道路上度过的。</span>

By comparison, ferry trips or cruises offer a great variety of civilized comforts.<br><span class="zh">相比之下，渡轮旅行或游轮旅行提供了多种舒适的设施和服务。</span>

You can stretch your legs on the spacious decks, play games,<br><span class="zh">你可以在宽敞的甲板上伸展双腿、玩游戏，</span>

meet interesting people and enjoy good food--always assuming, of course, that the sea is calm.<br><span class="zh">结识有趣的人，享受美味的美食——当然，这一切的前提都是海面必须风平浪静。</span>

If it is not, and you are likely to get seasick, no form of transport could be worse.<br><span class="zh">如果海面不平静，而你又容易晕船，那就没有比这更糟糕的交通方式了。</span>

Even if you travel in ideal weather, sea journeys take a long time.<br><span class="zh">即使你在理想的天气条件下旅行，海上航行仍然需要很长时间。</span>

Relatively few people are prepared to sacrifice holiday time for the pleasure of travelling by sea.<br><span class="zh">只有少数人愿意牺牲假期时间，去享受海上旅行的乐趣。</span>

Aeroplanes have the reputation of being dangerous and even hardened travellers are intimidated by them.<br><span class="zh">飞机一直被认为是一种危险的交通工具，就连那些经验丰富的旅客也会对它们感到恐惧。</span>

They also have the disadvantage of being an expensive form of transport.<br><span class="zh">它们的另一个缺点是费用昂贵。</span>

But nothing can match them for speed and comfort.<br><span class="zh">但是，在速度和舒适度方面，没有任何东西能比得上它们。</span>

Travelling at a height of 30, 000 feet, far above the clouds, and at over 500 miles an hour is an exhilarating experience.<br><span class="zh">在30,000英尺的高空飞行——远远高于云层之上——并以每小时500英里的速度前进，确实是一种令人兴奋的体验。</span>

You do not have to devise ways of taking your mind off the journey, for an aeroplane gets you to your destination rapidly.<br><span class="zh">你不必费心去寻找方法来转移自己的注意力，因为飞机能迅速将你送到目的地。</span>

For a few hours, you settle back in a deep armchair to enjoy the flight.<br><span class="zh">你可以安坐在宽大的扶手椅里，享受飞行的乐趣。</span>

The real escapist can watch a film and sip champagne on some services.<br><span class="zh">在有些航班上，真正想放松的人可以看看电影，喝喝香槟。</span>

But even when such refinements are not available, there is plenty to keep you occupied.<br><span class="zh">但即使没有这些精致的服务，也有足够多的事情让你打发时间。</span>

An aeroplane offers you an unusual and breathtaking view of the world.<br><span class="zh">从飞机上，你可以看到一个独特而令人惊叹的世界景象。</span>

You soar effortlessly over high mountains and deep valleys.<br><span class="zh">你轻松地翱翔在高山之上，穿过幽深的山谷。</span>

You really see the shape of the land.<br><span class="zh">你真的能够清楚地看到这片土地的轮廓和地形特征。</span>

If the landscape is hidden from view,<br><span class="zh">如果地面景色被云层遮挡，</span>

you can enjoy the extraordinary sight of unbroken cloud plains that stretch out for miles before you, while the sun shines brilliantly in a clear sky.<br><span class="zh">你可以欣赏到那令人惊叹的景象：连绵不断的云层平铺在眼前，一望无际；而太阳则在清澈的天空下闪耀着耀眼的光芒。</span>

The journey is so smooth that there is nothing to prevent you from reading or sleeping.<br><span class="zh">旅途非常平稳，没有任何因素会妨碍你阅读或睡觉。</span>

However you decide to spend your time, one thing is certain: you will arrive at your destination fresh and uncrumpled.<br><span class="zh">无论你如何度过时光，有一点是确定的：到达目的地时，你会精神焕发，毫无倦意。</span>

You will not have to spend the next few days recovering from a long and arduous journey.<br><span class="zh">在接下来的几天里，你不必花费时间来恢复体力——毕竟你刚刚完成了一段漫长而艰辛的旅程。</span>
