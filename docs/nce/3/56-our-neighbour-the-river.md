<NcePair en="As the floods had put the telephone out of order, we could not find out how they were managing." zh="由于洪水使电话中断，我们无法知道他们的情况如何。" />

<NcePair en="and at the most critical juncture we took turns in watching that point." zh="在最关键的时刻，我们轮流监视那个地点。" />
