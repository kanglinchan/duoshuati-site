# 第 56 课：Our Neighbour, the River

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Our neighbour, the river<br><span class="zh">我们的邻居——河流</span>

Why had the neighbours left their farm?<br><span class="zh">构成我们农场东边界的这条河流，一直在我们的生活中扮演着重要的角色。</span>

The river which forms the eastern boundary of our farm has always played an important part in our lives.<br><span class="zh">没有它，我们就无法谋生。</span>

Without it we could not make a living.<br><span class="zh">这里的泉水仅够满足房屋的需求，因此我们必须从河里抽水来用于农场灌溉。</span>

There is only enough spring water to supply the needs of the house so we have to pump from the river for farm use.<br><span class="zh">我们会把所有的秘密都告诉这条河流。</span>

We tell the river all our secrets.<br><span class="zh">我们凭直觉明白：就像养蜂人对待他们的蜜蜂一样，如果我们生活中的重要事件没有告诉这条河流，不幸就可能降临到我们头上。</span>

We know instinctively, just as beekeepers with their bees,<br><span class="zh">我们凭直觉明白，就像养蜂人对待他们的蜜蜂一样，</span>

that misfortune might overtake us if the important events of our lives were not related to it.<br><span class="zh">如果我们生活中的重要事件没有告诉这条河流，不幸就可能降临到我们头上。</span>

We have special river birthday parties in the summer.<br><span class="zh">夏天，我们会为这条河流举办特别的“生日派对”。</span>

Sometimes we go upstream to a favourite backwater,<br><span class="zh">有时我们会去上游一个我们喜欢的小水湾，</span>

sometimes we have our party at the boathouse,<br><span class="zh">有时则会在船屋里举行派对。</span>

which a predecessor of ours at the farm built in the meadow hard by the deepest pool for swimming and diving.<br><span class="zh">这是我们农场的一位前辈在草地上建造的，就在最深的池塘旁边，供游泳和跳水使用。</span>

In a heat wave we choose a midnight birthday party and that is the most exciting of all.<br><span class="zh">在热浪期间，我们选择在午夜举办生日派对——这真的是所有庆祝方式中最令人兴奋的。</span>

We welcome the seasons by the riverside, crowning the youngest girl with flowers in the spring,<br><span class="zh">我们在河边迎接四季的更替；春天来临时，我们会用鲜花为最年幼的女孩戴上花环。</span>

holding a summer festival on Midsummer Eve, giving thanks for the harvest in the autumn, and throwing a holly wreath into the current in the winter.<br><span class="zh">在仲夏夜举办夏季庆典，感谢秋天的丰收；而在冬天，则将冬青花环抛入河流中。</span>

After a long period of rain the river may overflow its banks.<br><span class="zh">经过长时间的降雨，河流可能会泛滥出河岸。</span>

This is a rare occurrence as our climate seldom goes to extremes.<br><span class="zh">这种情况很少见，因为我们的气候很少会走极端。</span>

We are lucky in that only the lower fields,<br><span class="zh">我们很幸运的是，只有下方的田地，</span>

which make up a very small proportion of our farm, are affected by flooding,<br><span class="zh">这些田地只占我们农场的很小一部分，会受到洪水影响，</span>

but other farms are less favourably sited, and flooding can sometimes spell disaster for their owners.<br><span class="zh">但其他农场的地理位置较为不利，洪水有时会对农场主造成严重的灾难性后果。</span>

One bad winter we watched the river creep up the lower meadows.<br><span class="zh">有一个严冬，我们眼睁睁看着河水逐渐淹没了下游的草地。</span>

All the cattle had been moved into stalls and we stood to lose little.<br><span class="zh">所有的牛都被赶进了牛棚里，所以我们几乎不会有什么损失。</span>

We were, however, worried about our nearest neighbours, whose farm was low lying and who were newcomers to the district.<br><span class="zh">然而，我们很担心我们的近邻——他们的农场位于地势较低的地方，而且他们是刚搬到这个地区来的新居民。</span>

As the floods had put the telephone out of order, we could not find out how they were managing.<br><span class="zh">由于洪水使电话中断，我们无法知道他们的情况如何。</span>

From an attic window we could get a sweeping view of the river where their land joined ours,<br><span class="zh">从阁楼的窗户里，我们可以看到河流的全景；他们的土地与我们的土地在那里交汇。</span>

and at the most critical juncture we took turns in watching that point.<br><span class="zh">在最关键的时刻，我们轮流监视那个地点。</span>

The first sign of disaster was a dead sheep floating down.<br><span class="zh">灾难的第一个征兆是一只死羊漂浮在水面上。</span>

Next came a horse, swimming bravely,<br><span class="zh">接下来是一匹马，它勇敢地游着。</span>

but we were afraid that the strength of the current would prevent its landing anywhere before it became exhausted.<br><span class="zh">但我们担心，水流的力量会使它在精疲力竭之前无法靠岸。</span>

Suddenly a raft appeared, looking rather like Noah's ark, carrying the whole family, a few hens, the dogs, a cat, and a bird in a cage.<br><span class="zh">突然，一只木筏出现了，它看起来很像诺亚方舟；木筏上载着整个家庭、几只母鸡、几只狗、一只猫，以及一只关在笼子里的鸟。</span>

We realized that they must have become unduly frightened by the rising flood,<br><span class="zh">我们意识到，他们一定是被不断上涨的洪水吓坏了。</span>

for their house, which had sound foundations, would have stood stoutly even if it had been almost submerged.<br><span class="zh">他们的房子地基非常坚固，即使几乎被水淹没，也能依然屹立不倒。</span>

The men of our family waded down through our flooded meadows with boat hooks,<br><span class="zh">我们家的男人们拿着船钩，蹚过被洪水淹没的草地。</span>

in the hope of being able to grapple a corner of the raft and pull it out of the current towrds our bank.<br><span class="zh">希望能钩住木筏的一角，把它从急流中拉到我们岸边。</span>

We still think it a miracle that they were able to do so.<br><span class="zh">我们至今仍认为他们能这样做是个奇迹。</span>
