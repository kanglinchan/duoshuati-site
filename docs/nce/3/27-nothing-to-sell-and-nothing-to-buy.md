<NcePair en="Though it may be possible to measure the value of material goods in terms of money," zh="虽然可以用金钱来衡量物质财富的价值，" />

<NcePair en="it is extremely difficult to estimate the true value of the services which people perform for us." zh="很难准确估计人们为我们提供的服务的真正价值。" />

<NcePair en="There are times when we would willingly give everything we possess to save our lives," zh="有时候，为了挽救自己的生命，我们甚至愿意放弃自己拥有的一切。" />

<NcePair en="yet we might grudge paying a surgeon a high fee for offering us precisely this service." zh="然而，我们可能会不愿意为外科医生支付高额的费用，仅仅因为他们为我们提供了这项服务。" />

<NcePair en="The conditions of society are such that skills have to be paid for in the same way that goods are paid for at a shop." zh="当前的社会状况决定了：技能的价值必须像商品在商店里被出售时那样，通过金钱来体现（即技能也需要通过金钱来获得）。" />

<NcePair en="Everyone has something to sell." zh="每个人都有东西可以出售。" />

<NcePair en="Tramps seem to be the only exception to this general rule." zh="流浪汉似乎是这条普遍规则的唯一例外。" />

<NcePair en="Beggars almost sell themselves as human beings to arouse the pity of passers-by." zh="乞丐们几乎是在“出卖自己的人格”，以激起路人的同情心。" />

<NcePair en="But real tramps are not beggars." zh="但是，真正的流浪者并不是乞丐。" />

<NcePair en="They have nothing to sell and require nothing from others." zh="他们没有什么可以出售的，也不需要从别人那里得到任何东西。" />

<NcePair en="In seeking independence, they do not sacrifice their human dignity." zh="在追求独立的过程中，他们并没有牺牲自己的人格尊严。" />

<NcePair en="A tramp may ask you for money, but he will never ask you to feel sorry for him." zh="一个流浪汉可能会向你乞讨钱财，但他永远不会要求你同情他。" />

<NcePair en="He has deliberately chosen to lead the life he leads and is fully aware of the consequences." zh="他自己是主动选择了这样的生活方式，并且完全清楚这样做会带来什么样的后果。" />

<NcePair en="He may never be sure where the next meal is coming from," zh="他可能永远都无法确定下一顿饭从哪里来（也就是说，他可能永远不知道自己下一顿饭的食物来源或来源地）。" />

<NcePair en="but he is free from the thousands of anxieties which afflict other people." zh="但他摆脱了那些困扰其他人的成千上万种焦虑情绪。" />

<NcePair en="His few material possessions make it possible for him to move from place to place with ease." zh="他仅有的一些物质财富使他能够轻松地从一个地方搬到另一个地方。" />

<NcePair en="By having to sleep in the open, he gets far closer to the world of nature than most of us ever do." zh="由于不得不在户外露宿，他比我们大多数人更接近大自然的世界。" />

<NcePair en="He may hunt, beg, or steal occasionally to keep himself alive;" zh="他可能会偶尔通过打猎、乞讨或偷窃来维持生计。" />

<NcePair en="he may even, in times of real need, do a little work; but he will never sacrifice his freedom." zh="在真正需要的时候，他或许也会稍微工作一下；但他永远不会牺牲自己的自由。" />

<NcePair en="We often speak of tramps with contempt and put them in the same class as beggars," zh="我们常常轻蔑地看待流浪者，把他们和乞丐归为同一类，" />
