<NcePair en="It is not uncommon to hear that a shipping company has made a claim for the cost of salvaging a sunken ship." zh="显然，保险公司承担的风险越大，你需要支付的保费也就越高。" />

<NcePair en="But the claim made by a local authority to recover the cost of salvaging a sunken pie dish must surely be unique." zh="但是，一个地方政府为打捞一个沉没的派盘而提出的索赔，肯定是非常独特的。" />

<NcePair en="Admittedly it was an unusual pie dish, for it was eighteen feet long and six feet wide." zh="这个派盘确实很不寻常——它的长度为18英尺，宽度为6英尺。" />

<NcePair en="It had been purchased by a local authority so that an enormous pie could be baked for an annual fair." zh="它是被当地政府购买的，用于为年度集市制作一个巨大的派。" />

<NcePair en="The pie committee decided that the best way to transport the dish would be by canal so they insured it for the trip." zh="派委员会认为通过运河运输派盘的最佳方式，因此他们为这次行程购买了保险。" />

<NcePair en="Shortly after it was launched, the pie committee went to a local inn to celebrate." zh="派盘下水后不久，派委员会成员们便前往一家当地酒馆庆祝。" />

<NcePair en="At the same time, a number of teenagers climbed on to the dish and held a little party of their own." zh="与此同时，一些青少年爬上了那个盘子，并在那里举办了一场属于自己的小型聚会。" />

<NcePair en="Dancing proved to be more than the dish could bear," zh="跳舞的震动超出了派盘所能承受的范围，" />

<NcePair en="for during the party it capsized and sank in seven feet of water." zh="因为在派对期间，派盘翻了，沉入了七英尺深的水中。" />

<NcePair en="The pie committee telephoned a local garage owner who arrived in a recovery truck to salvage the pie dish." zh="派委员会给当地一家修车厂老板打了电话，他开着一辆救援卡车前来打捞派盘。" />

<NcePair en="Shivering in their wet clothes, the teenagers looked on while three men dived repeatedly into the water to locate the dish." zh="那些青少年穿着湿衣服瑟瑟发抖地看着，三个男人一次又一次地潜入水中寻找派盘。" />

<NcePair en="They had little difficulty in finding it, but hauling it out of the water proved to be a serious problem." zh="他们找到它并不费什么劲，但将其从水中拖出来却成了一个严重的问题。" />

<NcePair en="The sides of the dish were so smooth that it was almost impossible to attach hawsers and chains to the rim without damaging it." zh="这个盘子的边缘非常光滑，几乎不可能在不损坏它的情况下将缆绳或链条固定在盘子的边缘上。" />

<NcePair en="Eventually chains were fixed to one end of the dish and a powerful winch was put into operation." zh="最终，那些链条被固定在盘子的一端；随后，一台强大的绞车也被启动了起来。" />

<NcePair en="The dish rose to the surface and was gently drawn towards the canal bank." zh="派盘浮到水面，被缓缓地拉向运河岸边。" />

<NcePair en="For one agonizing moment, the dish was perched precariously on the bank of the canal," zh="在令人揪心的一瞬间，派盘摇摇晃晃地停在运河岸边，" />

<NcePair en="but it suddenly overbalanced and slid back into the water." zh="但它突然失去了平衡，又滑回了水中。" />

<NcePair en="The men were now obliged to try once more." zh="这些人现在不得不再次尝试。" />

<NcePair en="This time they fixed heavy metal clamps to both sides of the dish so that they could fasten the chains." zh="这次，他们在盘子的两侧安装了沉重的金属夹具，以便固定链条。" />

<NcePair en="The dish now had to be lifted vertically because one edge was resting against the side of the canal." zh="由于盘子的一边紧贴着运河的侧壁，因此必须将其垂直抬起。" />

<NcePair en="The winch was again put into operation and one of the men started up the truck." zh="绞车再次被启动，其中一名男子启动了卡车。" />

<NcePair en="Several minutes later, the dish was successfully hauled above the surface of the water." zh="几分钟后，盘子终于被成功吊到了水面上方。" />

<NcePair en="Water streamed in torrents over its sides with such force that it set up a huge wave in the canal." zh="水从派盘边缘倾泻而下，水流如此之猛，在运河里掀起了一阵巨浪。" />

<NcePair en="There was a danger that the wave would rebound off the other side of the bank and send the dish plunging into the water again." zh="有危险的是，这股浪可能会从对岸反弹回来，把派盘重新冲回水里。" />

<NcePair en="By working at tremendous speed, the men managed to get the dish on to dry land before the wave returned." zh="以极快的速度工作，这些人总算在浪头返回前把派盘弄上了岸。" />
