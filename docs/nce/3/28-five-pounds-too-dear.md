# 第 28 课：Five Pounds Too Dear

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Five pounds too dear<br><span class="zh">贵了五英镑</span>

Why was even five pounds 'too dear' ?<br><span class="zh">为什么连五英镑都嫌贵呢？</span>

Small boats loaded with wares sped to the great liner as she was entering the harbour.<br><span class="zh">当那艘大型客轮驶入港口时，载满货物的小船迅速朝它驶去。</span>

Before she had anchored, the men from the boats had climbed on board<br><span class="zh">在它抛锚之前，小船上的人就已经爬上了大船。</span>

and the decks were soon covered with colourful rugs from Persia,<br><span class="zh">很快，这些甲板上就铺上了来自波斯的彩色地毯。</span>

silks from India, copper coffee pots, and beautiful handmade silverware.<br><span class="zh">来自印度的丝绸、铜制的咖啡壶，以及精美的手工银器。</span>

It was difficult not to be tempted.<br><span class="zh">很难抗拒这种诱惑。</span>

Many of the tourists on board had begun bargaining with the tradesmen,<br><span class="zh">船上的许多游客已经开始与商贩们讨价还价了。</span>

but I decided not to buy anything until I had disembarked.<br><span class="zh">但我决定在离船之前不买任何东西。</span>

I had no sooner got off the ship than I was assailed by a man who wanted to sell me a diamond ring.<br><span class="zh">我刚下船，就立刻被一个男人缠住了——他想要向我推销一枚钻石戒指。</span>

I had no intention of buying one,<br><span class="zh">我本来就没有打算买一个的。</span>

but I could not conceal the fact that I was impressed by the size of the diamonds.<br><span class="zh">但我无法隐瞒这样一个事实：这些钻石的尺寸确实让我印象深刻。</span>

Some of them were as big as marbles.<br><span class="zh">其中一些钻石有弹珠那么大。</span>

The man went to great lengths to prove that the diamonds were real.<br><span class="zh">这个人付出了巨大的努力来证明这些钻石是真品。</span>

As we were walking past a shop, he held a diamond firmly against the window and made a deep impression in the glass.<br><span class="zh">当我们经过一家商店时，他用力将那颗钻石压在窗户上，从而在玻璃上留下了一个明显的印记。</span>

It took me over half an hour to get rid of him.<br><span class="zh">我花了半个多小时才把他打发走。</span>

The next man to approach me was selling expensive pens and watches.<br><span class="zh">下一个向我走来的人在推销昂贵的钢笔和手表。</span>

I examined one of the pens closely. It certainly looked genuine.<br><span class="zh">我仔细检查了其中一支笔。它看起来确实是真的。</span>

At the base of the gold cap, the words 'made in the U.S.A.' had been neatly inscribed.<br><span class="zh">在金笔帽的底部，刻着‘美国制造’的字样，非常工整。</span>

The man said that the pen was worth ￡50, but as a special favour, he would let me have it for ￡30.<br><span class="zh">那个人说这支笔的价值是 50 英镑，但作为一项特别的优惠，他愿意以 30 英镑的价格把笔卖给我。</span>

I shook my head and held up five fingers indicating that I was willing to pay ￡5.<br><span class="zh">我摇了摇头，然后竖起了五根手指，表示我愿意支付 5 英镑。</span>

Gesticulating wildly, the man acted as if he found my offer outrageous,<br><span class="zh">那男人夸张地比划着，好像我的报价让他很生气，</span>

but he eventually reduced the price to 10 pounds.<br><span class="zh">但他最终将价格降到了10英镑。</span>

Shrugging my shoulders I began to walk away when,<br><span class="zh">我耸了耸肩，正准备走开时，突然……</span>

a moment later, he ran after me and thrust the pen into my hands.<br><span class="zh">过了一会儿，他追上来，把那支笔塞到了我的手里。</span>

Though he kept throwing up his arms in despair, he readily accepted the ￡5 I gave him.<br><span class="zh">尽管他绝望地举起双臂，但还是爽快地收下了我给的5英镑。</span>

I felt especially pleased with my wonderful bargain--until I got back to the ship.<br><span class="zh">我对这笔“超值”的交易感到非常满意——直到我回到船上。</span>

No matter how hard I tried,<br><span class="zh">不管我怎么试，</span>

it was impossible to fill this beautiful pen with ink and to this day it has never written a single word!<br><span class="zh">这枝漂亮的钢笔根本无法装上墨水；直到今天，它仍然一个字也没写过！</span>
