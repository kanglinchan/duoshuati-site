# 第 33 课：A Day to Remember

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

A day to remember<br><span class="zh">一个值得铭记的日子</span>

What incident began the series of traffic accidents?<br><span class="zh">我们都经历过那些一切都出错的日子。</span>

We have all experienced days when everything goes wrong.<br><span class="zh">是什么事件引发了这一连串的交通事故？</span>

A day may begin well enough, but suddenly everything seems to get out of control.<br><span class="zh">一天可能开始得很顺利，但突然间，一切似乎都失控了。</span>

What invariably happens is that a great number of things choose to go wrong at precisely the same moment.<br><span class="zh">一天可能开始得很顺利，但突然间，一切似乎都失控了。</span>

It is as if a single unimportant event set up a chain of reactions.<br><span class="zh">不可避免的是，许多事情会在同一时刻同时出错。</span>

Let us suppose that you are preparing a meal and keeping an eye on the baby at the same time.<br><span class="zh">就好像某个看似微不足道的事件引发了一连串的连锁反应。</span>

The telephone rings and this marks the prelude to an unforeseen series of catastrophes.<br><span class="zh">电话突然响了，而这成了随后一系列意外事件的导火线。</span>

While you are on the phone, the baby pulls the tablecloth off the table,<br><span class="zh">电话突然响了，而这成了随后一系列意外事件的导火线。</span>

smashing half your best crockery and cutting himself in the process.<br><span class="zh">你正在打电话时，婴儿把桌布从桌子上扯了下来，打碎了你一半最好的餐具，还划伤了自己。</span>

You hang up hurriedly and attend to baby, crockery, etc.<br><span class="zh">你匆忙挂断电话，然后开始处理婴儿和餐具的问题……</span>

Meanwhile, the meal gets burnt.<br><span class="zh">与此同时，饭菜被烧焦了。</span>

As if this were not enough to reduce you to tears, your husband arrives, unexpectedly bringing three guests to dinner.<br><span class="zh">仿佛这些还不够让你心烦意乱似的，你的丈夫突然回来了，还带着三位客人一起来吃晚饭。</span>

Things can go wrong on a big scale as a number of people recently discovered in Parramatta, a suburb of Sydney.<br><span class="zh">最近，在悉尼的郊区帕拉马塔，许多人发现事情也可能在大范围内出错。</span>

During the rush hour one evening two cars collided and both drivers began to argue.<br><span class="zh">一天晚上的高峰时段，两辆车发生了碰撞，两名司机随即开始争吵。</span>

The woman immediately behind the two cars happened to be a learner.<br><span class="zh">就坐在那两辆车后面的那位女士，恰好是一名正在学习驾驶的新手。</span>

She suddenly got into a panic and stopped her car.<br><span class="zh">她突然陷入了恐慌，立刻停下了车子。</span>

This made the driver following her brake hard.<br><span class="zh">这导致跟在她后面的司机也紧急踩下了刹车。</span>

His wife was sitting beside him holding a large cake.<br><span class="zh">他的妻子坐在他旁边，手里拿着一个大蛋糕。</span>

As she was thrown forward,<br><span class="zh">当她被向前抛出时……</span>

the cake went right through the windscreen and landed on the road.<br><span class="zh">蛋糕直接穿过了挡风玻璃，落在了路上。</span>

Seeing a cake flying through the air,<br><span class="zh">看到一个蛋糕在空中飞过……</span>

a lorry driver who was drawing up alongside the car, pulled up all of a sudden.<br><span class="zh">一名正在靠近那辆汽车的卡车司机突然停了下来。</span>

The lorry was loaded with empty beer bottles and hundreds of them slid off the back of the vehicle and on to the road.<br><span class="zh">卡车上装满了空啤酒瓶，结果有数百个瓶子从卡车后部滑落到了路上。</span>

This led to yet another angry argument. Meanwhile, the traffic piled up behind.<br><span class="zh">这又引发了一场激烈的争吵。与此同时，后面的交通也陷入了堵塞。</span>

It took the police nearly an hour to get the traffic on the move again.<br><span class="zh">警察花了将近一个小时才让交通恢复正常。</span>

In the meantime, the lorry driver had to sweep up hundreds of broken bottles.<br><span class="zh">在此期间，卡车司机不得不把那些破碎的瓶子全部清理干净。</span>

Only two stray dogs benefited from all this confusion, for they greedily devoured what was left of the cake.<br><span class="zh">只有两只流浪狗从这场混乱中得到了好处——它们贪婪地吃掉了剩下的蛋糕。</span>

It was just one of those days!<br><span class="zh">唉，这不过是又平常的一天罢了！</span>
