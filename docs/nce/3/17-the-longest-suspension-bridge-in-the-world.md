# 第 17 课：The Longest Suspension Bridge in the World

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

The longest suspension bridge in the world<br><span class="zh">世界上最长的悬索桥</span>

How is the bridge supported?<br><span class="zh">这座桥是如何被支撑起来的？</span>

Verrazano, an Italian about whom little is known, sailed into New York Harbour in 1524 and named it Angouleme.<br><span class="zh">维拉扎诺（Verrazano）是一位鲜为人知的意大利人。他在1524年航行至纽约港，并将其命名为“昂古莱姆”（Angouleme）。</span>

He described it as 'a very agreeable situation located within two small hills in the midst of which flowed a great river.'<br><span class="zh">他将其描述为“一个非常宜人的地方，位于两座小山之间；这两座小山中间流淌着一条大河。”</span>

Though Verrazano is by no means considered to be a great explorer, his name will probably remain immortal,<br><span class="zh">虽然维拉扎诺绝不能被视为一位伟大的探险家，但他的名字很可能会永垂不朽。</span>

for on November 21st, 1964, the longest suspension bridge in the world was named after him.<br><span class="zh">因为在1964年11月21日，世界上最长的悬索桥被以他的名字命名。</span>

The Verrazano Bridge, which was designed by Othmar Ammann, joins Brooklyn to Staten Island.<br><span class="zh">维拉扎诺大桥（Verrazano Bridge）由奥特马尔·阿曼（Othmar Ammann）设计，它连接了布鲁克林（Brooklyn）和斯塔滕岛（Staten Island）。</span>

It has a span of 4, 260 feet.<br><span class="zh">它的跨度为 4,260 英尺。</span>

The bridge is so long that the shape of the earth had to be taken into account by its designer.<br><span class="zh">这座桥非常长，以至于设计者必须考虑到地球的曲率。</span>

Two great towers support four huge cables.<br><span class="zh">两座高大的塔架支撑着四根巨大的缆绳。</span>

The towers are built on immense underwater platforms made of steel and concrete.<br><span class="zh">这些塔架建在巨大的水下平台上，这些平台由钢铁和混凝土制成。</span>

The platforms extend to a depth of over 100 feet under the sea.<br><span class="zh">这些平台延伸至海面下100英尺以上的深度。</span>

These alone took sixteen months to build.<br><span class="zh">仅这些部分的建设就花费了十六个月的时间。</span>

Above the surface of the water, the towers rise to a height of nearly 700 feet.<br><span class="zh">在水面之上，这些塔楼的高度接近700英尺。</span>

They support the cables from which the bridge has been suspended.<br><span class="zh">它们支撑着悬挂着桥梁的缆绳。</span>

Each of the four cables contains 26, 108 lengths of wire.<br><span class="zh">这四根缆绳中的每一根都包含26,108股钢丝。</span>

It has been estimated that if the bridge were packed with cars, it would still only be carrying a third of its total capacity.<br><span class="zh">据估计，即使这座桥上挤满了汽车，它所承载的重量也仅相当于其总承载能力的三分之一。</span>

However, size and strength are not the only important things about this bridge.<br><span class="zh">然而，这座桥的重要性并不仅仅体现在它的规模和承重能力上。</span>

Despite its immensity, it is both simple and elegant,<br><span class="zh">尽管它规模庞大，但它却既简洁又优雅。</span>

fulfilling its designer's dream to creat 'an enormous object drawn as faintly as possible'.<br><span class="zh">实现了设计师的梦想：创造出一个“尽可能轻描淡写地勾勒出的庞然大物”。</span>
