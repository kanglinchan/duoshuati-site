# 第 40 课：Who's Who

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Who's who<br><span class="zh">谁是谁？</span>

How did the policeman discover that the whole thing was a joke?<br><span class="zh">警察是怎么发现这一切其实只是个玩笑的呢？</span>

It has never been explained why university students seem to enjoy practical jokes more than anyone else.<br><span class="zh">学生们擅长一种特定的恶作剧：骗局。</span>

Students specialize in a particular type of practical joke: the hoax.<br><span class="zh">学生们专精于一种特定类型的恶作剧——那就是“骗局”（即通过虚假信息或欺骗手段来制造混乱或误导他人）。</span>

Inviting the fire brigade to put out a nonexistent fire is a crude form of deception which no self-respecting student would ever indulde in.<br><span class="zh">请消防队来扑灭不存在的火灾是一种低劣的骗术，任何有自尊的学生都不会染指。</span>

Students often create amusing situations which are funny to everyone except the victims.<br><span class="zh">学生们经常制造一些有趣的、滑稽的情境，但这些情境只有受害者自己觉得不好笑，其他人反而都觉得很好笑。</span>

When a student recently saw two workmen using a pneumatic drill outside his university,<br><span class="zh">最近，一名学生在自己的大学外面看到两名工人正在使用气动钻机。</span>

he immediately telephoned the police and informed them that two students dressed up as workmen<br><span class="zh">他立即拨打了报警电话，并告诉警方有两名学生装扮成工人。</span>

were tearing up the road with a pneumatic drill.<br><span class="zh">正用气动钻破坏路面。</span>

As soon as he had hung up, he went over to the workmen and told them that if a policeman ordered them to go away,<br><span class="zh">他一挂断电话，就走到工人们跟前，告诉他们如果警察命令他们离开，</span>

they were not to take him seriously.<br><span class="zh">他们不应该把他当回事。</span>

He added that a student had dressed up as a policeman and was playing all sorts of silly jokes on people.<br><span class="zh">他还补充说，有个学生打扮成警察的样子，对周围的人开了各种滑稽的玩笑。</span>

Both the police and the workmen were grateful to the student for this piece of advance information.<br><span class="zh">警察和工人们都很感谢这名学生提供的这个消息。</span>

The student hid in an archway nearby where he could watch and hear everything that went on.<br><span class="zh">那个学生躲在附近的一个拱门里，从那里他可以观察到发生的一切，也能听到所有的声音。</span>

Sure enough, a policeman arrived on the scene and politely asked the workmen to go away.<br><span class="zh">果然，一名警察来到了现场，并礼貌地要求工人们离开。</span>

When he received a very rude reply from one of the workmen, he threatened to remove them by force.<br><span class="zh">当他收到其中一名工人的粗鲁回复时，他威胁说要强行将他们赶走。</span>

The workmen told him to do as he pleased and the policeman telephoned for help.<br><span class="zh">工人们让他随意行事，而警察则拨打了求救电话。</span>

Shortly afterwards, 4 more policemen arrived and remonstrated with the workmen.<br><span class="zh">不久，又有4名警察赶到，他们劝告工人们。</span>

As the men refused to stop working, the police attempted to seize the pneumatic drill.<br><span class="zh">由于那些工人拒绝停止工作，警方试图没收那台气动钻机。</span>

The workmen struggled fiercely and one of them lost his temper.<br><span class="zh">工人们激烈反抗，其中一人发火了。</span>

He threatened to call the police.<br><span class="zh">他威胁说要报警。</span>

At this, the police pointed out ironically that this would hardly be necessary as the men were already under arrest.<br><span class="zh">这时，警察讽刺地指出，这实在没必要，因为这些人已经被逮捕了。</span>

Pretending to speak seriously, one of the workmen asked if he might make a telephone call before being taken to the station.<br><span class="zh">装出一副认真的样子，其中一名工人询问是否可以在被带到警察局之前打个电话。</span>

Permission was granted and a policeman accompanied him to a pay phone.<br><span class="zh">获得了许可后，一名警察陪同他前往了一部公用电话。</span>

Only when he saw that the man was actually telephoning the police did he realize that they had all been the victims of a hoax.<br><span class="zh">直到看到那个人真的在报警时，他才意识到他们所有人其实都成了一个骗局的受害者。</span>
