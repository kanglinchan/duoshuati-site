# 第 26 课：Wanted a Large Biscuit Tin

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Wanted: a large biscuit tin<br><span class="zh">征购：一个大号的饼干盒</span>

Who won the prize for the biggest biscuit?<br><span class="zh">谁赢得了“最大饼干”奖呢？</span>

No one can avoid being influenced by advertisements.<br><span class="zh">没有人能够完全不受广告的影响。</span>

Much as we may pride ourselves on our good taste,<br><span class="zh">尽管我们常常为自己的品味感到自豪，</span>

we are no longer free to choose the things we want,<br><span class="zh">但实际上我们已不再能够自由选择自己真正想要的东西，</span>

for advertising exerts a subtle influence on us.<br><span class="zh">因为广告对我们产生了潜移默化的影响。</span>

In their efforts to persuade us to buy this or that product,<br><span class="zh">为了说服我们购买某种产品，</span>

advertisers have made a close study of human nature and have classified all our little weaknesses.<br><span class="zh">广告商们深入研究了人类的心理，并分类整理了我们所有的小弱点。</span>

Advertisers discovered years ago that all of us love to get something for nothing.<br><span class="zh">多年前，广告商们就发现：我们都喜欢免费获得东西。</span>

An advertisement which begins with the magic word FREE can rarely go wrong.<br><span class="zh">任何以“免费”这个词开头的广告几乎都不会失败。</span>

These days, advertisers not only offer free samples,<br><span class="zh">如今，广告商们不仅提供免费样品，……</span>

but free cars, free houses, and free trips round the world as well.<br><span class="zh">还有免费的汽车、免费的房子，以及免费的环球旅行机会。</span>

They devise hundreds of competitions which will enable us to win huge sums of money.<br><span class="zh">他们设计了数百种比赛，让我们有机会赢得巨额奖金。</span>

Radio and television have made it possible for advertisers to capture the attention of millions of people in this way.<br><span class="zh">广播和电视使得广告商能够以这种方式吸引数百万人的注意力。</span>

During a radio programme, a company of biscuit manufacturers once asked listeners to bake biscuits and send them to their factory.<br><span class="zh">在一次广播节目中，一家饼干制造商邀请听众们烤制饼干并寄到他们的工厂。</span>

They offered to pay $10 a pound for the biggest biscuit baked by a listener.<br><span class="zh">他们提出愿意以每磅10美元的价格购买听众们烤制的最大尺寸的饼干。</span>

The response to this competition was tremendous.<br><span class="zh">这次比赛的反响非常热烈（或：这次比赛的响应非常强烈）。</span>

Before long, biscuits of all shapes and sizes began arriving at the factory.<br><span class="zh">不久之后，各种形状和大小的饼干开始运送到工厂里。</span>

One lady brought biscuit on a wheelbarrow. It weighed nearly 500 pounds.<br><span class="zh">一位女士用手推车运来一块饼干，它重达近500磅。</span>

A little later, a man came along with a biscuit which occupied the whole boot of his car.<br><span class="zh">过了一会儿，一个男人走了过来，他带来的饼干占据了他汽车整个后备箱的空间。</span>

All the biscuits that were sent were carefully weighed.<br><span class="zh">所有被寄出的饼干都经过了仔细的称重。</span>

The largest was 713 pounds. It seemed certain that this would win the prize.<br><span class="zh">最大的那个重达713磅；看起来它肯定会赢得这个奖项。</span>

But just before the competition closed,<br><span class="zh">但在比赛即将结束之前，</span>

a lorry arrived at the factory with a truly colossal biscuit which weighed 2, 400 pounds.<br><span class="zh">一辆卡车将一块重量高达2,400磅的巨型饼干送到了工厂。</span>

It had been baked by a college student who had used over 1, 000 pounds of flour, 800 pounds of sugar,<br><span class="zh">这块饼干是由一名大学生烤制的；他用了超过1000磅的面粉和800磅的糖。</span>

200 pounds of fat, and 400 pounds of various other ingredients.<br><span class="zh">200磅的脂肪，以及400磅的其他各种成分。</span>

It was so heavy that a crane had to be used to remove it from the lorry.<br><span class="zh">它太重了，必须使用起重机才能将其从卡车上卸下来。</span>

The manufacturers had to pay more money than they had anticipated, for they bought the biscuit from the student for $24, 000.<br><span class="zh">制造商们不得不支付比预期更多的钱，因为他们以24,000美元的价格从那位学生手中买下了这块饼干。</span>
