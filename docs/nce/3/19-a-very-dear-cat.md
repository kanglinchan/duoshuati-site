# 第 19 课：A Very Dear Cat

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

A very dear cat<br><span class="zh">一只非常珍贵的猫</span>

Why was Rastus 'very dear' in more ways than one?<br><span class="zh">绑匪通常对动物不感兴趣，但他们最近却对埃莉诺·拉姆齐夫人的猫产生了浓厚的兴趣。</span>

Kidnappers are rarely interested in animals, but they recently took considerable interest in Mrs. Eleanor Ramsay's cat.<br><span class="zh">埃莉诺·拉姆齐夫人是一位非常富有的老太太，她多年来一直和她的猫拉斯图斯合住一套公寓。</span>

Mrs. Eleanor Ramsay, a very wealthy old lady, has shared a flat with her cat, Rastus, for a great many years.<br><span class="zh">拉斯图斯的生活非常有规律。</span>

Rastus leads an orderly life.<br><span class="zh">他通常在傍晚散会儿步，并且总是在七点前回家。</span>

He usually takes a short walk in the evenings and is always home by seven o'clock.<br><span class="zh">然而，一天晚上他没有按时回家。</span>

One evening, however he failed to arrive.<br><span class="zh">拉姆齐夫人非常担心。</span>

Mrs. Ramsay got very worried.<br><span class="zh">她到处找他，但没能找到。</span>

She looked everywhere for him but could not find him.<br><span class="zh">拉斯图斯失踪三天后，拉姆齐夫人收到了一封匿名信。</span>

Three days after Rastus' disappearance, Mrs. Ramsay received an anonymous letter.<br><span class="zh">写信人声称拉斯图斯安然无恙，如果拉姆齐夫人支付1000英镑的赎金，他会立即被送回。</span>

The writer stated that Rastus was in safe hands and would be returned immediately if Mrs. Ramsay paid a ransom of ￡1, 000.<br><span class="zh">拉姆齐夫人被要求把钱放在一个纸板箱里，然后把它放在门外。</span>

Mrs.Ramsay was instructed to place the money in a cardboard box and to leave it outside her door.<br><span class="zh">起初，她决定去报警，</span>

At first, she decided to go to the police,<br><span class="zh">但害怕再也见不到拉斯图斯——信里已经说得很清楚了——她改变了主意。</span>

but fearing that she would never see Rastus again--the letter had made that quite clear--she changed her mind.<br><span class="zh">她从银行取了1000英镑，并按照绑匪的指示做了。</span>

She withdrew ￡1000 from her bank and followed the kidnapper's instructions.<br><span class="zh">第二天早上，箱子不见了，但拉姆齐夫人确信绑匪会信守承诺。</span>

The next morning, the box had disappeared but Mrs.Ramsay was sure that the kidnapper would keep his word.<br><span class="zh">果然，那天晚上七点整，拉斯图斯准时回来了。</span>

Sure enough, Rastus arrived punctually at seven o'clock that evening.<br><span class="zh">他看起来很好，只是相当渴，因为他喝了半瓶牛奶。</span>

He looked very well, though he was rather thirsty, for he drank half a bottle of milk.<br><span class="zh">当拉姆齐夫人告诉警察她做了什么时，他们都非常震惊。</span>

The police were astounded when Mrs. Ramsay told them what she had done.<br><span class="zh">她解释说拉斯图斯对她来说非常珍贵。</span>

She explained that Rastus was very dear to her.<br><span class="zh">考虑到她支付的金额，他可真是“昂贵”啊！</span>

Considering the amount she paid, he was dear in more ways than one!<br><span class="zh">考虑到她支付的金额，他可真是“昂贵”啊！</span>
