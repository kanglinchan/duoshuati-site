# 第 7 课：Mutilated Ladies

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Mutilated Ladies<br><span class="zh">残币清点小组</span>

Why did Jane cook John's wallet?<br><span class="zh">你有没有遇到过这种事？</span>

Has it ever happened to you?<br><span class="zh">你有没有遇到过这种事？</span>

Have you ever put your trousers in the washing machine and then remembered there was a large bank note in your back pocket?<br><span class="zh">你是否曾经把裤子放进洗衣机，后来才想起后口袋里还有一张大面额的钞票？</span>

When you rescued your trousers, did you find the note was whiter than white?<br><span class="zh">当你把裤子取出来时，发现那张钞票已经变得雪白一片了……</span>

People who live in Britain needn't despair when they make mistakes like this (and a lot of people do)!<br><span class="zh">生活在英国的人们遇到这种错误时不必绝望（其实很多人都遇到过这种情况！）</span>

Fortunately for them, the Bank of England has a team called Mutilated Ladies<br><span class="zh">幸运的是，英格兰银行有一个名为“残币清点小组”的专门部门</span>

which deals with claims from people who fed their money to a machine or to their dog.<br><span class="zh">这个部门负责处理那些把钱喂给机器或狗的人的索赔申请</span>

Dogs, it seems, love to chew up money!<br><span class="zh">看来，狗确实很喜欢咬钱啊！</span>

A recent case concerns Jane Butlin whose fiance, John, runs a successful furniture business.<br><span class="zh">最近有一个案例与简·巴特林有关：她的未婚夫约翰经营着一家成功的家具公司。</span>

John had a very good day and put his wallet containing ￡3, 000 into the microwave oven for safekeeping.<br><span class="zh">约翰那天生意很好，为了安全起见，他把装有3000英镑的钱包放进了微波炉里保管</span>

Then he and Jane went horse-riding.<br><span class="zh">然后他和简一起去骑马了。</span>

When they got home, Jane cooked their dinner in the microwave oven and without realizing it, cooked her fiance's wallet as well.<br><span class="zh">当他们回到家后，简用微波炉做晚饭，不知不觉中，她把未婚夫的钱包也一起加热了</span>

Imagine their dismay when they found a beautifully-cooked wallet and notes turned to ash!<br><span class="zh">可以想象他们当时有多沮丧：当他们发现钱包被烤得焦黑，钞票都变成了灰烬时</span>

John went to see his bank manager who sent the remains of wallet and the money<br><span class="zh">约翰去找他的银行经理，经理把钱包和钱的残骸</span>

to the special department of the Bank of England in Newcastle: the Mutilated Ladies!<br><span class="zh">送到了位于纽卡斯尔的英格兰银行特别部门——残币清点小组！</span>

They examined the remains and John got all his money back.<br><span class="zh">他们检查了这些残骸，约翰拿回了全部的钱</span>

'So long as there's something to identify, we will give people their money back,' said a spokeswoman for the Bank.<br><span class="zh">“只要还能辨认出是什么，我们就会把钱退还给客户，”银行的一位女发言人说</span>

'Last year, we paid ￡1.5 million on 21,000 claims.'<br><span class="zh">去年，我们为21,000起索赔支付了150万英镑。</span>
