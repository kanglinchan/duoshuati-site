<NcePair en="police officers, health inspectors or tax-collectors can make mistakes or act over-zealously in the belief that they are serving the public." zh="警察、卫生检查员或税务官员都可能犯错，或者因为自认为是在为公众服务而表现得过于热心（即行为过于激进）。" />

<NcePair en="As long ago as 1809, the Swedish Parliament introduced a scheme to safeguard the interest of the individual." zh="早在1809年，瑞典议会就出台了一项旨在保护个人权益的方案。" />

<NcePair en="A parliamentary committee representing all political parties appoints a person who is suitably qualified to investigate private grievances against the State." zh="一个代表所有政党的议会委员会，会任命一位具备适当资格的人来调查公民对政府提出的个人投诉。" />

<NcePair en="The official title of the person is 'Justiteombudsman', but the Swedes commonly refer to him as the 'J.O.' or 'Ombudsman'." zh="这个人的正式头衔是“司法监察专员”（Justice Ombudsman），但瑞典人通常称他为“J.O.”或“Ombudsman”。" />

<NcePair en="The Ombudsman is not subject to political pressure." zh="监察专员不受政治压力的影响。" />

<NcePair en="He investigates complaints large and small that come to him from all levels of society." zh="他负责调查来自社会各个阶层的各种投诉，无论这些投诉的规模大小如何。" />

<NcePair en="As complaints must be made in writing, the Ombudsman receives an average of 1, 200 letters a year." zh="由于投诉必须以书面形式提出，因此监察专员每年平均会收到 1,200 封来信。" />

<NcePair en="He has eight lawyer assistants to help him and he examines every single letter in detail." zh="他有八名律师助理协助他工作，他会仔细审查每一封信的内容。" />

<NcePair en="There is nothing secretive about the Ombudsman's work, for his correspondence is open to public inspection." zh="监察专员的工作没有任何保密性；他的所有通信记录都可供公众查阅。" />

<NcePair en="If a citizen's complaint is justified the Ombudsman will act on his behalf." zh="如果公民的投诉是合理的，监察专员将会代表该公民采取行动。" />

<NcePair en="The action he takes varies according to the nature of the complaint." zh="他采取的行动会根据投诉的性质而有所不同。" />

<NcePair en="He may gently reprimand an official or even suggest to parliament that a law be altered." zh="他可能会温和地告诫某位官员，甚至向议会提出修改法律的建议。" />

<NcePair en="The following case is a typical example of the Ombudsman's work." zh="以下案例是监察专员工作的典型例子。" />

<NcePair en="A foreigner living in a Swedish village wrote to the Ombudsman complaining that he had been ill-treated by the police," zh="一位居住在瑞典村庄的外国人写信给监察专员，投诉称自己受到了警方的虐待。" />

<NcePair en="simply because he was a foreigner." zh="仅仅因为他是外国人。" />

<NcePair en="The Ombudsman immediately wrote to the Chief of Police in the district asking him to send a record of the case." zh="监察专员立即致函该地区的警察局长，要求他提供该案件的详细记录。" />

<NcePair en="There was nothing in the record to show that the foreigner's complaint was justified" zh="记录中没有任何内容表明那位外国人的投诉是合理的" />

<NcePair en="and the Chief of Police strongly denied the accusation." zh="警方局长强烈否认了这一指控。" />

<NcePair en="It was impossible for the Ombudsman to take action," zh="监察专员根本无法采取任何行动。" />

<NcePair en="but when he received a similar complaint from another foreigner in the same village," zh="但是当他收到同一村庄里另一位外国人类似的投诉时……" />

<NcePair en="he immediately sent one of his lawyers to investigate the matter." zh="他立即派了自己的律师去调查这件事。" />

<NcePair en="The lawyer ascertained that a policeman had indeed dealt roughly with foreigners on several occasions." zh="律师证实，确实有警察在多次场合中对外国人采取了粗暴的态度。" />

<NcePair en="The fact that the policeman was prejudiced against foreigners could not be recorded in the official files." zh="这名警察对外国人存在偏见这一事实，无法被记录在官方文件中。" />

<NcePair en="It was only possible for the Ombudsman to find this out by sending one of his representatives to check the facts." zh="只有通过派遣他的代表去核实事实，监察专员才得以发现这一情况。" />

<NcePair en="The policeman in question was severely reprimanded" zh="这名涉事的警察受到了严厉的训斥。" />

<NcePair en="and was informed that if any further complaints were lodged against him, he would be prosecuted." zh="并且被告知，如果再有人对他提出投诉，他将会被起诉。" />

<NcePair en="The Ombudsman's prompt action at once put an end to an unpleasant practice which might have gone unnoticed." zh="监察专员的迅速行动立即制止了这一可能被忽视的不良行为。" />
