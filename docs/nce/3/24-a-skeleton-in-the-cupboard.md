# 第 24 课：A Skeleton in the Cupboard

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

A skeleton in the cupboard<br><span class="zh">柜中骷髅（比喻不可告人的秘密）</span>

Who was Sebastian?<br><span class="zh">塞巴斯蒂安是谁？</span>

We often read in novels how a seemingly respectable person<br><span class="zh">我们在小说中经常看到这样的情节：一个表面上看起来很体面、值得尊敬的人……</span>

or family has some terrible secret which has been concealed from strangers for years.<br><span class="zh">或者这个家庭隐藏着一个可怕的秘密，这个秘密多年来一直被对外人隐瞒着。</span>

The English language possesses a vivid saying to describe this sort of situation.<br><span class="zh">英语中有一句非常生动的表达，用来形容这种情况。</span>

The terrible secret is called 'a skeleton in the cupboard'.<br><span class="zh">这个可怕的秘密就叫“柜中骷髅”。</span>

At some dramatic moment in the story, the terrible secret becomes known and a reputation is ruined.<br><span class="zh">在故事中的某个关键时刻，那个可怕的秘密被揭露了，从而导致一个人的声誉彻底毁掉。</span>

The reader's hair stands on end when he reads in the final pages of the novel that the heroine,<br><span class="zh">当读者读到小说最后几页，发现女主角……</span>

a dear old lady who had always been so kind to everybody, had, in her youth, poisoned every one of her five husbands.<br><span class="zh">一位一直对所有人都非常友善的善良老妇人，在她年轻时，竟然毒死了她的五位丈夫。</span>

It is all very well for such things to occur in fiction.<br><span class="zh">这种事发生在小说里倒也罢了。</span>

To varying degrees, we all have secrets which we do not want even our closest friends to learn,<br><span class="zh">在某种程度上，我们每个人都有自己不想让最亲密的朋友知道的秘密。</span>

but few of us have skeletons in the cupboard.<br><span class="zh">但我们中很少有人真有“柜中骷髅”。</span>

The only person I know who has a skeleton in the cupboard is George Carlton, and he is very proud of the fact.<br><span class="zh">我认识的唯一有“柜中骷髅”的人是乔治·卡尔顿，而且他还对此引以为豪。</span>

George studied medicine in his youth.<br><span class="zh">乔治年轻时学习过医学。</span>

Instead of becoming a doctor, however, he became a successful writer of detective stories.<br><span class="zh">然而，他并没有成为医生，而是成为了一名成功的侦探小说作家。</span>

I once spent an uncomfortable weekend which I shall never forget at his house.<br><span class="zh">我曾在他的家里度过了一个极其不愉快的周末，那个经历我永远都不会忘记。</span>

George showed me to the guestroom which, he said, was rarely used.<br><span class="zh">乔治带我去了客房，他说这个房间很少有人使用。</span>

He told me to unpack my things and then come down to dinner.<br><span class="zh">他让我先把行李收拾好，然后再下来吃晚饭。</span>

After I had stacked my shirts and underclothes in two empty drawers, I decided to hang one of the two suits I had brought with me in the cupboard.<br><span class="zh">我把衬衫和内衣整理好后，放进了两个空抽屉里；接着，我决定把带来的两套西装中的一套挂在衣柜里。</span>

I opened the cupboard door and then stood in front of it petrified.<br><span class="zh">我打开了橱柜的门，然后站在那里，吓得动弹不得。</span>

A skeleton was dangling before my eyes.<br><span class="zh">一具骷髅悬挂在我的眼前。</span>

The sudden movement of the door made it sway slightly and it gave me the impression that it was about to leap out at me.<br><span class="zh">门的突然晃动让它也跟着摇摆起来，给我的感觉是它好像要朝我扑过来似的。</span>

Dropping my suit, I dashed downstairs to tell George.<br><span class="zh">我立刻放下西装，冲下楼去告诉乔治。</span>

This was worse than 'a terrible secret'; this was a real skeleton!<br><span class="zh">这比“一个可怕的秘密”还要糟糕；那简直是一具真正的骷髅！</span>

But George was unsympathetic.<br><span class="zh">但乔治却毫无同情之心。</span>

'Oh, that,' he said with a smile as if he were talking about an old friend.<br><span class="zh">“哦，那个啊，”他笑着说，仿佛在谈论一个老朋友一样，</span>

'That's Sebastian. You forget that I was a medical student once upon a time.'<br><span class="zh">“那是塞巴斯蒂安。你忘了，我曾经也是一名医学生。”</span>
