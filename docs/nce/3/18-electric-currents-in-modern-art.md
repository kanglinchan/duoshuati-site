# 第 18 课：Electric Currents in Modern Art

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Electric currents in modern art<br><span class="zh">现代艺术中的电流（注：此处“electric currents”可能为比喻，实际含义需结合上下文确定）</span>

How might some of the exhibits have been dangerous?<br><span class="zh">现代雕塑早已不再让我们感到惊讶。</span>

Modern sculpture rarely surprises us any more.<br><span class="zh">认为现代艺术只能通过博物馆来欣赏，这种观点是错误的。</span>

The idea that modern art can only be seen in museums is mistaken.<br><span class="zh">现代雕塑早已不再让我们感到惊讶。</span>

Even people who take no interest in art cannot have failed to notice examples of modern sculpture on display in public places.<br><span class="zh">认为现代艺术只能通过博物馆来欣赏，这种观点是错误的。</span>

Strange forms stand in gardens, and outside buildings and shops.<br><span class="zh">即使是对艺术毫无兴趣的人，也应该注意到公共场所中展示的现代雕塑作品——那些奇形怪状的雕塑矗立在花园里、建筑物和商店的外面。</span>

We have got quite used to them.<br><span class="zh">我们已经习惯了它们的存在。</span>

Some so-called 'modern' pieces have been on display for nearly eighty years.<br><span class="zh">有些所谓的“现代”艺术品已经展出了近八十年。</span>

In spite of this, some people--including myself--were surprised by a recent exhibition of modern sculpture.<br><span class="zh">尽管如此，包括我在内的一些人，还是被最近的一场现代雕塑展览所震撼。</span>

The first thing I saw when I entered the art gallery was a notice which said: 'Do not touch the exhibits, some of them are dangerous!'<br><span class="zh">当我走进艺术画廊时，首先看到的是一张告示，上面写着：“请勿触摸展品，其中一些具有危险性！”</span>

The objects on display were pieces of moving sculpture.<br><span class="zh">展出的作品都是动态雕塑。</span>

Oddly shaped forms that are suspended from the ceiling and move in response to a gust of wind are quite familiar to everybody.<br><span class="zh">那些形状奇特、悬挂在天花板上的物体，会随着一阵风吹动而移动——这种景象对每个人来说都十分熟悉。</span>

These objects, however, were different.<br><span class="zh">然而，这些物体有所不同。</span>

Lined up against the wall, there were long thin wires attached to metal spheres.<br><span class="zh">靠墙排列着一排排细长的金属丝，每根都连接着一个金属球。</span>

The spheres had been magnetized and attracted or repelled each other all the time.<br><span class="zh">这些球体被磁化了，因此它们会不断地相互吸引或相互排斥。</span>

In the centre of the hall,<br><span class="zh">在大厅的中央，</span>

there were a number of tall structures which contained coloured lights.<br><span class="zh">矗立着许多高大的结构，上面装有彩色的灯光。</span>

These lights flickered continuously like traffic lights which have gone mad.<br><span class="zh">这些灯光不停地闪烁，就像那些失控的交通信号灯一样。</span>

Sparks were emitted from small black boxes and red lamps flashed on and off angrily.<br><span class="zh">从小巧的黑色盒子中迸发出火花；红色的灯泡则愤怒地闪烁着。</span>

It was rather like an exhibition of prehistoric electronic equipment.<br><span class="zh">这有点像一场展示史前电子设备的展览。</span>

These peculiar forms not only seemed designed to shock people emotionally, but to give them electric shocks as well!<br><span class="zh">这些奇特的设计不仅旨在从情感上震撼人们，还似乎能够给他们带来电击般的刺激！</span>
