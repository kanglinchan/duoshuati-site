# 第 60 课：Too Early and Too Late

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Too early and too late<br><span class="zh">太早与太晚</span>

Why did the young girl miss the train?<br><span class="zh">为什么那个小女孩错过了火车？</span>

Punctuality is a necessary habit in all public affairs in civilized society.<br><span class="zh">在文明社会的所有公共事务中，守时是一种必不可少的习惯。</span>

Without it, nothing could ever be brought to a conclusion; everything would be in a state of chaos.<br><span class="zh">没有它，任何事情都无法办成；一切都将陷入混乱。</span>

Only in a sparsely-populated rural community is it possible to disregard it.<br><span class="zh">只有在人口稀少的农村社区，人们才可能忽视它。</span>

In ordinary living, there can be some tolerance of unpunctuality.<br><span class="zh">在日常生活中，对于迟到这种行为，人们通常可以有一定的宽容度。</span>

The intellectual, who is working on some abstruse problem, has everything coordinated and organized for the matter in hand.<br><span class="zh">这位正在研究某个复杂问题的学者，已经将自己手头的工作所需的一切资料和信息都整理得井井有条、有条不紊了。</span>

He is therefore forgiven if late for a dinner party.<br><span class="zh">因此，如果他赴宴迟到，也是可以被原谅的。</span>

But people are often reproached for unpunctuality when their only fault is cutting things fine.<br><span class="zh">但是，人们常常因为不守时而受到指责，而他们唯一的过错只是把时间算得太紧。</span>

It is hard for energetic, quick-minded people to waste time,<br><span class="zh">对于那些精力充沛、思维敏捷的人来说，浪费时间是一件很难做到的事情。</span>

so they are often tempted to finish a job before setting out to keep an appointment.<br><span class="zh">因此，他们常常会忍不住在赴约之前先把工作完成。</span>

If no accidents occur on the way, like punctured tyres, diversions of traffic, sudden descent of fog, they will be on time.<br><span class="zh">如果途中没有发生任何意外（比如轮胎爆裂、交通延误、雾突然变浓等情况），他们就能准时到达。</span>

They are often more industrious, useful citizens than those who are never late.<br><span class="zh">他们通常比那些从不迟到的人更勤奋、更有用。</span>

The over-punctual can be as much a trial to others as the unpunctual.<br><span class="zh">过分守时和迟到一样，都可能让人难以忍受。</span>

The guest who arrives half an hour too soon is the greatest nuisance.<br><span class="zh">那位提前半小时就到的客人真是最让人烦恼的人了。</span>

Some friends of my family had this irritating habit.<br><span class="zh">我家的一些朋友有个让人很恼火的习惯。</span>

The only thing to do was ask them to come half an hour later than the other guests.<br><span class="zh">唯一可行的办法就是让他们比其他客人晚半小时到达。</span>

Then they arrived just when we wanted them.<br><span class="zh">然后他们正好在我们需要的时候到了。</span>

If you are catching a train, it is always better to be comfortably early than even a fraction of a minute too late.<br><span class="zh">如果你要赶火车，宁可从容地早到，也绝不迟到哪怕一秒钟。</span>

Although being early may mean wasting a little time,<br><span class="zh">虽然提前行动可能会浪费一些时间，</span>

this will be less than if you miss the train and have to wait an hour or more for the next one;<br><span class="zh">这总比你错过火车、然后不得不等待下一班火车（一个小时或更长时间）要好吧。</span>

and you avoid the frustration of arriving at the very moment when the train is drawing out of the station and being unable to get on it.<br><span class="zh">这样你就避免了那种令人沮丧的情况：当你赶到车站时，火车正好已经启动离开，而你却无法上车。</span>

An even harder situation is to be on the platform in good time for a train and still to see it go off without you.<br><span class="zh">更糟糕的情况是：你虽然及时赶到了站台，却还是眼睁睁看着火车开走，把你丢下。</span>

Such an experience befell a certain young girl the first time she was travelling alone.<br><span class="zh">这样的经历发生在一个年轻女孩第一次独自旅行的时候。</span>

She entered the station twenty minutes before the train was due,<br><span class="zh">她在火车预定到站前二十分钟进了车站。</span>

since her parents had impressed upon her<br><span class="zh">因为她的父母一直向她灌输这样的观念（或：因为她的父母让她明白了这一点）。</span>

that it would be unforgivable to miss it and cause the friends with whom she was going to stay to make two journeys to meet her.<br><span class="zh">错过这班火车是不可原谅的，因为这会让要来接她的朋友们不得不跑两趟。</span>

She gave her luggage to a porter and showed him her ticket.<br><span class="zh">她把行李交给搬运工，并给他看了车票。</span>

To her horror he said that she was two hours too soon.<br><span class="zh">令她惊恐的是，他说她来得太早了——比预定的时间早了两个小时。</span>

She felt in her handbag for the piece of paper on which her father had written down all the details of the journey and gave it to the porter.<br><span class="zh">她在手提包里寻找那张纸，上面写着父亲记录的所有旅行细节，然后把纸交给了行李员。</span>

He agreed that a train did come into the station at the time on the paper and that it did stop, but only to take on mail, not passengers.<br><span class="zh">他承认，根据记录，当时确实有一列火车驶入了车站并且停了下来，但那列火车只是来取邮件的，并没有搭载乘客。</span>

The girl asked to see a timetable, feeling sure that her father could not have made such a mistake.<br><span class="zh">女孩要求查看时刻表，因为她确信她的父亲不可能犯这样的错误。</span>

The porter went to fetch one and arrived back with the station master,<br><span class="zh">搬运工去取时刻表，回来时带着站长。</span>

who produced it with a flourish and pointed out a microscopic 'o' beside the time of the arrival of the train at his station;<br><span class="zh">站长拿着时刻表，夸张地一挥，指出了他车站火车到站时间旁边一个极小的'o'；</span>

this little 'o' indicated that the train only stopped for mail.<br><span class="zh">这个小小的“O”符号表示火车只是为了取邮件而停靠的。</span>

Just as that moment the train came into the station.<br><span class="zh">就在那一刻，火车驶进了车站。</span>

The girl, tears streaming down her face, begged to be allowed to slip into the guard's van.<br><span class="zh">女孩泪流满面，恳求允许她溜进列车员车厢。</span>

But the station master was adamant: rules could not be broken.<br><span class="zh">但站长态度坚决：规定不能违反。</span>

And she had to watch that train disappear towards her destination while she was left behind.<br><span class="zh">她只能眼睁睁看着火车驶向目的地，而自己却被留在了车站。</span>
