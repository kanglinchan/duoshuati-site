# 第 36 课：A Chance in a Million

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

A chance in a million<br><span class="zh">百万分之一的机会</span>

What was the chance in a million?<br><span class="zh">那百万分之一的机会究竟是什么呢？</span>

We are less credulous than we used to be.<br><span class="zh">我们不再像过去那样容易轻信他人或事物了。</span>

In the nineteenth century, a novelist would bring his story to a conclusion<br><span class="zh">在十九世纪，小说家会通过一系列巧合来结束他的故事</span>

by presenting his readers with a series of coincidences--most of them wildly improbable.<br><span class="zh">——其中大多数都极不可能发生。</span>

Readers happily accepted the fact that an obscure maidservant was really the hero's mother.<br><span class="zh">读者们欣然接受了这样一个事实：那位默默无闻的女仆其实就是这位英雄的母亲。</span>

A long-lost brother, who was presumed dead, was really alive all the time<br><span class="zh">一个被认定已经去世的“失散多年的兄弟”，其实一直都活着。</span>

and wickedly plotting to bring about the hero's downfall. And so on.<br><span class="zh">并且一直在暗中策划要毁掉英雄，诸如此类。</span>

Modern readers would find such naive solutions totally unacceptable.<br><span class="zh">现代读者会认为这种天真的解决方案完全不可接受。</span>

Yet, in real life, circumstances do sometimes conspire to bring about coincidences which anyone but a nineteenth century novelist would find incredible.<br><span class="zh">然而，在现实生活中，情况确实有时会凑巧促成一些巧合，这些巧合除了十九世纪的小说家外，任何人都会觉得难以置信。</span>

When I was a boy, my grandfather told me how a German taxi driver, Franz Bussman,<br><span class="zh">当我还是个孩子的时候，我的祖父给我讲过一个德国出租车司机弗朗茨·布斯曼的故事。</span>

found a brother who was thought to have been killed twenty years before.<br><span class="zh">找到了一个被认为在二十年前就已经去世的兄弟。</span>

While on a walking tour with his wife, he stopped to talk to a workman.<br><span class="zh">在与妻子一起散步时，他停下来与一位工人交谈。</span>

After they had gone on, Mrs. Bussman commented on the workman's close resemblance to her husband<br><span class="zh">在他们离开后，巴斯曼夫人评论说那个工人和她丈夫长得非常像。</span>

and even suggested that he might be his brother.<br><span class="zh">甚至暗示他可能是她丈夫的兄弟。</span>

Franz poured scorn on the idea,<br><span class="zh">弗朗茨对这个想法嗤之以鼻。</span>

pointing out that his brother had been killed in action during the war.<br><span class="zh">他指出他的兄弟在战争中阵亡了。</span>

Though Mrs. Bussman was fully acquainted with this story,<br><span class="zh">尽管布斯曼夫人对这件事了如指掌，</span>

she thought that there was a chance in a million that she might be right.<br><span class="zh">她认为自己有百万分之一的可能性是对的。</span>

A few days later, she sent a boy to the workman to ask him if his name was Hans Bussman.<br><span class="zh">几天后，她派了一个男孩去问那个工人，他的名字是不是汉斯·布斯曼（Hans Bussman）。</span>

Needless to say, the man's name was Hans Bussman and he really was Franz's long-lost brother.<br><span class="zh">不用说，这个男人的名字是汉斯·布斯曼，他确实是弗朗茨失散已久的兄弟。</span>

When the brothers were reunited,<br><span class="zh">当兄弟俩重聚时，</span>

Hans explained how it was that he was still alive.<br><span class="zh">汉斯解释了自己是如何活下来的。</span>

After having been wounded towards the end of the war,<br><span class="zh">在战争接近尾声时受伤之后，</span>

he had been sent to hospital and was separated from his unit.<br><span class="zh">他被送到了医院，也因此与他的部队分开了。</span>

The hospital had been bombed and Hans had made his way back into Western Germany on foot.<br><span class="zh">这家医院遭到了轰炸，汉斯只好徒步回到了西德。</span>

Meanwhile, his unit was lost and all records of him had been destroyed.<br><span class="zh">与此同时，他的部队失踪了，关于他的所有记录也被毁掉了。</span>

Hans returned to his family home, but the house had been bombed<br><span class="zh">汉斯回到了他的家庭住宅，但房子已经被炸毁了。</span>

and no one in the neighbourhood knew what had become of the inhabitants.<br><span class="zh">而且邻居中没有人知道住户们后来怎么样了。</span>

Assuming that his family had been killed during an air raid,<br><span class="zh">汉斯以为他的家人在一次空袭中丧生了，</span>

Hans settled down in a village fifty miles away where he had remained ever since.<br><span class="zh">汉斯在五十英里外的一个村庄安顿了下来，从那以后他就一直住在那里。</span>
