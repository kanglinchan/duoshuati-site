# 第 25 课：The Cutty Sark

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

The Cutty Sark<br><span class="zh">“Cutty Sark”是一艘著名的帆船，以其独特的船型和卓越的航海性能而闻名于世。</span>

What piece of bad luck prevented the Cutty Sark from winning the race?<br><span class="zh">是什么倒霉的事情导致“Cutty Sark”号没能赢得这场比赛呢？</span>

One of the most famous sailing ships of the nineteenth century, the Cutty Sark, can still be seen at Greenwich.<br><span class="zh">作为19世纪最著名的帆船之一，“Cutty Sark”至今仍可以在格林尼治博物馆看到。</span>

She stands on dry land and is visited by thousands of people each year.<br><span class="zh">她站在陆地上，每年都有成千上万的人前来参观她。</span>

She serves as an impressive reminder of the great ships of the past.<br><span class="zh">她仿佛是一面令人印象深刻的镜子，让人们想起过去那些宏伟的船只。</span>

Before they were replaced by steamships,<br><span class="zh">在它们被蒸汽船取代之前，</span>

sailing vessels like the Cutty Sark were used to carry tea from China and wool from Australia.<br><span class="zh">像“Cutty Sark”这样的帆船曾被用来从中国运输茶叶，从澳大利亚运输羊毛。</span>

The Cutty Sark was one of the fastest sailing ships that has ever been built.<br><span class="zh">“Cutty Sark”是有史以来建造的最快的帆船之一。</span>

The only other ship to match her was the Thermopylae.<br><span class="zh">唯一能与她相媲美的船只就是“Thermopylae”号了。</span>

Both these ships set out from Shanghai on June 18th, 1872 on an exciting race to England.<br><span class="zh">这两艘船都于1872年6月18日从上海出发，参加了一场前往英国的激动人心的竞赛。</span>

This race, which went on for exactly four months, was the last of its kind.<br><span class="zh">这场比赛持续了整整四个月，也是这类比赛中的最后一场比赛。</span>

It marked the end of the great tradition of ships with sails and the beginning of a new era.<br><span class="zh">这标志着帆船这一伟大传统的终结，以及一个新时代的开始。</span>

The first of the two ships to reach Java after the race had begun was the Thermopylae,<br><span class="zh">在比赛开始后，最先抵达爪哇的两艘船只中，第一艘是“Thermopylae”号。</span>

but on the Indian Ocean, the Cutty Sark took the lead.<br><span class="zh">但在印度洋上，库蒂·萨克号（Cutty Sark）却取得了领先地位。</span>

It seemed certain that she would be the first ship home,<br><span class="zh">看起来她肯定会成为第一个返回家的“船只”（即第一个完成任务的成员）。</span>

but during the race she had a lot of bad luck.<br><span class="zh">但在比赛中，她遭遇了很多不幸（或者说：她遇到了很多麻烦或困难）。</span>

In August, she was struck by a very heavy storm during which her rudder was torn away.<br><span class="zh">八月份，她遭遇了一场猛烈的风暴，风暴中她的舵被彻底损坏（即被风暴中的力量扯掉了）。</span>

The Cutty Sark rolled from side to side and it became impossible to steer her.<br><span class="zh">“Cutty Sark”号开始左右摇晃，根本无法再控制它的航向了。</span>

A temporary rudder was made on board from spare planks and it was fitted with great difficulty.<br><span class="zh">他们在船上用多余的木板制作了一个临时舵，并且安装这个临时舵的过程非常困难。</span>

This greatly reduced the speed of the ship, for there was a danger that if she travelled too quickly,<br><span class="zh">这大大降低了船的速度，因为如果船行驶得太快，就存在危险。</span>

this rudder would be torn away as well.<br><span class="zh">这个舵也会被扯掉的。</span>

Because of this, the Cutty Sark lost her lead.<br><span class="zh">正因如此，Cutty Sark失去了领先优势。</span>

After crossing the Equator, the captain called in at a port to have a new rudder fitted,<br><span class="zh">在越过赤道后，船长停靠在一个港口，更换了船上的舵。</span>

but by now the Thermopylae was over five hundred miles ahead.<br><span class="zh">但那时，Thermopylae号已经领先了五百多英里。</span>

Though the new rudder was fitted at tremendous speed, it was impossible for the Cutty Sark to win.<br><span class="zh">尽管新的舵被迅速安装上了，Cutty Sark号还是无法赶上它。</span>

She arrived in England a week after the Thermopylae.<br><span class="zh">Cutty Sark号在Thermopylae号之后一周才抵达英格兰。</span>

Even this was remarkable, considering that she had had so many delays.<br><span class="zh">考虑到途中经历了许多延误，这一成绩已经非常了不起了。</span>

There is no doubt that if she had not lost her rudder she would have won the race easily.<br><span class="zh">毫无疑问，如果她没有失去舵，她本可以轻松赢得比赛。</span>
