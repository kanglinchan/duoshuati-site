# 第 37 课：The Westhaven Express

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

The Westhaven Express<br><span class="zh">西黑文特快列车</span>

What was the mistake the author made?<br><span class="zh">作者犯了什么错误？</span>

We have learnt to expect that trains will be punctual.<br><span class="zh">我们已经习惯了火车会准点到达这一情况。</span>

After years of conditioning, most of us have developed an unshakable faith in railway timetables.<br><span class="zh">经过多年的习惯养成，我们大多数人都对铁路时刻表产生了坚定不移的信念。</span>

Ships may be delayed by storms;<br><span class="zh">船只可能会因为风暴而延误。</span>

flights may be cancelled because of bad weather; but trains must be on time.<br><span class="zh">航班可能会因为恶劣天气而被取消；但火车必须准时运行。</span>

Only an exceptionally heavy snow fall might temporarily dislocate railway services.<br><span class="zh">只有当降雪量异常大时，铁路服务才可能暂时中断。</span>

It is all too easy to blame the railway authorities when something does go wrong.<br><span class="zh">当出现问题时，人们很容易将责任归咎于铁路管理部门。</span>

The truth is that when mistakes occur, they are more likely to be ours than theirs.<br><span class="zh">事实上，当错误发生时，这些错误更有可能是我们自己造成的，而不是他们的。</span>

After consulting my railway timetable, I noted with satisfaction that there was an express train to Westhaven.<br><span class="zh">在查看了铁路时刻表后，我满意地发现有一趟直达韦斯特黑文的快车。</span>

It went direct from my local station and the journey lasted a mere hour and seventeen minutes.<br><span class="zh">它直接从我所在的车站出发，全程仅需一小时十七分钟。</span>

When I boarded the train, I could not help noticing that a great many local people got on as well.<br><span class="zh">当我登上火车时，我不禁注意到有很多当地人也都上了车。</span>

At the time, this did not strike me as odd.<br><span class="zh">当时，我并没有觉得这有什么奇怪的。</span>

I reflected that there must be a great many people besides myself who wished to take advantage of this excellent service.<br><span class="zh">我想，除了我之外，肯定还有很多人希望能够利用这项优质的服务。</span>

Neither was I surprised when the train stopped at Widley, a tiny station a few miles along the line.<br><span class="zh">当火车在威德利（Widley）这个位于铁路线上几英里外的小车站停下来的时候，我也没有感到惊讶。</span>

Even a mighty express train can be held up by signals.<br><span class="zh">即使是强大的特快列车也可能被信号灯拦停。</span>

But when the train dawdled at station after station, I began to wonder.<br><span class="zh">但是当火车在一个又一个车站上缓慢停留、迟迟不前时，我开始感到疑惑了。</span>

It suddenly dawned on me that this express was not roaring down the line at ninety miles an hour, but barely chugging along at thirty.<br><span class="zh">我突然意识到，这趟特快列车并非以每小时90英里的速度飞驰，而是以每小时30英里的速度缓慢行驶。</span>

One hour and seventeen minutes passed and we had not even covered half the distance.<br><span class="zh">一个小时十七分钟过去了，我们甚至还没有走完一半的路程。</span>

I asked a passenger if this was the Westhaven Express, but he had not even heard of it.<br><span class="zh">我问了一位乘客这是否是“Westhaven Express”列车，但他甚至从未听说过这趟列车。</span>

I determined to lodge a complaint as soon as we arrived.<br><span class="zh">我们一到达，我就决定立即提出投诉。</span>

Two hours later, I was talking angrily to the station master at Westhaven.<br><span class="zh">两小时后，我正在西黑文（Westhaven）愤怒地与车站站长交谈。</span>

When he denied the train's existence, I borrowed his copy of the timetable.<br><span class="zh">当他否认火车的存在时，我就借用了他的时刻表。</span>

There was a note of triumph in my voice when I told him that it was there in black and white.<br><span class="zh">当我告诉他白纸黑字写着这趟列车时，我的声音带着一丝得意的语气。</span>

Glancing at it briefly, he told me to look again.<br><span class="zh">他匆匆瞥了一眼后，让我再仔细看一遍。</span>

A tiny asterisk conducted me to a footnote at the bottom of the page.<br><span class="zh">一个小小的星号指引我看到了页面底部的脚注。</span>

It said: 'This service has been suspended.'<br><span class="zh">上面写着：‘此班次已暂停运营。’</span>
