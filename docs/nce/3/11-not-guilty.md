# 第 11 课：Not Guilty

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Not guilty<br><span class="zh">“无罪”</span>

What was the Customs Officer looking for?<br><span class="zh">海关官员在寻找什么？</span>

Customs Officers are quite tolerant these days, but they can still stop you when you are going through the Green Channel and have nothing to declare.<br><span class="zh">如今的海关官员通常比较宽容，但即便你通过绿色通道且没有任何需要申报的物品，他们仍有可能拦下你。</span>

Even really honest people are often made to feel guilty.<br><span class="zh">如今的海关官员通常比较宽容，但即便你通过绿色通道且没有任何需要申报的物品，他们仍有可能拦下你。</span>

The hardened professional smuggler, on the other hand, is never troubled by such feelings,<br><span class="zh">即使是那些非常诚实的人，也常常会因此感到内疚。</span>

even if he has five hundred gold watches hidden in his suitcase.<br><span class="zh">而那些老练的走私者则完全不会受到这种情绪的困扰——</span>

When I returned from abroad recently, a particularly officious young Customs Officer<br><span class="zh">即使他们的行李箱里藏着五百块金表。</span>

clearly regarded me as a smuggler.<br><span class="zh">最近我从国外回来时，一位特别爱管闲事的年轻海关官员明显把我当成了走私者。</span>

'Have you anything to declare?' he asked, looking me in the eye.<br><span class="zh">“您有什么需要申报的吗？”他直视着我的眼睛问道。</span>

'No,' I answered confidently.<br><span class="zh">“没有，”我自信地回答。</span>

'Would you mind unlocking this suitcase please? '<br><span class="zh">“请您把这个行李箱打开好吗？”</span>

'Not at all,' I answered.<br><span class="zh">“一点也不，”我回答道。</span>

The Officer went through the case with great care.<br><span class="zh">这位官员非常仔细地检查了行李箱。</span>

All the things I had packed so carefully were soon in a dreadful mess.<br><span class="zh">我精心打包的所有东西，很快就变得一片狼藉。</span>

I felt sure I would never be able to close the case again.<br><span class="zh">我确信自己再也无法关上这个箱子了。</span>

Suddenly, I saw the Officer's face light up.<br><span class="zh">突然，我看到那位警官的脸上露出了笑容。</span>

He had spotted a tiny bottle at the bottom of my case<br><span class="zh">他在我的行李箱底部发现了一个小瓶子。</span>

and he pounced on it with delight.<br><span class="zh">他高兴地抓住了它。</span>

'Perfume, eh?' he asked sarcastically.<br><span class="zh">“香水，是吗？”他讽刺地问道。</span>

'You should have declared that.<br><span class="zh">你本应该申报这个的。</span>

Perfume is not exempt from import duty.  '<br><span class="zh">香水同样需要缴纳进口关税。</span>

'But it isn't perfume, I said. 'It's hair gel.  '<br><span class="zh">“但这不是香水，”我说，“这是发胶。”</span>

Then I added with a smile,<br><span class="zh">然后我微笑着补充道：</span>

'It's a strange mixture I make myself.<br><span class="zh">这是我自己调制的一种奇怪混合物。</span>

As I expected, he did not believe me.<br><span class="zh">正如我所料，他根本不相信我的话。</span>

'Try it!' I said encouragingly.<br><span class="zh">“试试看吧！”我鼓励地说道。</span>

The Officer unscrewed the cap and put the bottle to his nostrils.<br><span class="zh">那位警官拧开了瓶盖，将瓶子靠近自己的鼻孔。</span>

He was greeted by an unpleasant smell which convinced him that I was telling the truth.<br><span class="zh">一股难闻的气味扑面而来，这让他确信我说的是实话。</span>

A few minutes later, I was able to hurry away with precious chalk marks on my baggage.<br><span class="zh">几分钟后，我终于带着行李匆匆离开了——我的行李上留下了那些珍贵的粉笔痕迹。</span>
