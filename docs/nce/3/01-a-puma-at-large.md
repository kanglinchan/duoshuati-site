# 第 1 课：A Puma at Large

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

A puma at large<br><span class="zh">逃遁的美洲狮</span>

Where must the puma have come from?<br><span class="zh">美洲狮是产于美洲的一种体型较大、像猫一样的动物。</span>

Pumas are large, cat-like animals which are found in America.<br><span class="zh">美洲狮是一种体型较大的、类似猫的动物，它们生活在美洲地区。</span>

When reports came into London Zoo that a wild puma had been spotted forty-five miles south of London,<br><span class="zh">当伦敦动物园接到报告称在伦敦以南45英里处发现了一只野生美洲狮时……</span>

they were not taken seriously.<br><span class="zh">这些报告并没有被认真对待。</span>

However, as the evidence began to accumulate,<br><span class="zh">然而，随着证据的不断积累……</span>

experts from the Zoo felt obliged to investigate,<br><span class="zh">动物园的专家们觉得有责任对此事进行调查。</span>

for the descriptions given by people who claimed to have seen the puma were extraordinarily similar.<br><span class="zh">那些声称见过美洲狮的人所提供的描述非常相似。</span>

The hunt for the puma began in a small village where a woman picking blackberries saw 'a large cat'only five yards away from her.<br><span class="zh">对这只美洲狮的搜寻始于一个小镇。在那里，一位正在采摘黑莓的女子发现了一只“大型猫科动物”，它就站在离她仅五码远的地方。</span>

It immediately ran away when she saw it,<br><span class="zh">它一看到她，立刻就跑开了。</span>

and experts confirmed that a puma will not attack a human being unless it is cornered.<br><span class="zh">专家们证实，美洲狮只有在被逼入绝境时才会攻击人类。</span>

The search proved difficult,<br><span class="zh">这次搜索工作非常困难。</span>

for the puma was often observed at one place in the morning and at another place twenty miles away in the evening.<br><span class="zh">人们经常在早晨看到那只美洲狮出现在一个地方，而到了晚上，它却出现在二十英里外的另一个地方。</span>

Wherever it went, it left behind it a trail of dead deer and small animals like rabbits.<br><span class="zh">无论它走到哪里，都会留下一串死鹿和兔子之类的小动物的踪迹。</span>

Paw prints were seen in a number of places and puma fur was found clinging to bushes.<br><span class="zh">在多个地方都发现了爪印，同时还发现了一些美洲狮的毛发粘在灌木丛上。</span>

Several people complained of 'cat-like noises' at night and a businessman on a fishing trip saw the puma up a tree.<br><span class="zh">有几个人抱怨晚上听到了类似猫叫声的声音；一位正在钓鱼的商人还看到一只美洲狮爬在树上。</span>

The experts were now fully convinced that the animal was a puma,<br><span class="zh">专家们现在完全确信这种动物是一只美洲狮。</span>

but where had it come from?<br><span class="zh">但是，它究竟是从哪里来的呢？</span>

As no pumas had been reported missing from any zoo in the country,<br><span class="zh">由于该国没有任何动物园报告有美洲狮失踪的情况，</span>

this one must have been in the possession of a private collector and somehow managed to escape.<br><span class="zh">这只美洲狮一定是某位私人收藏家的，后来不知怎么设法逃了出来。</span>

The hunt went on for several weeks,<br><span class="zh">这次搜寻持续了好几周。</span>

but the puma was not caught.<br><span class="zh">但是那只美洲狮没有被抓住。</span>

It is disturbing to think that a dangerous wild animal is still at large in the quiet countryside.<br><span class="zh">想到有一只危险的野生动物仍然在宁静的乡村里自由活动，实在令人不安。</span>
