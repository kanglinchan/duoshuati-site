# 第 46 课：Do It Yourself

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Do it yourself<br><span class="zh">自己动手做吧。</span>

Did the writer repair his lawn mower in the end? Why/Why not?<br><span class="zh">最后，作者修好了他的割草机吗？为什么（或为什么不）？</span>

So great is our passion for doing things for ourselves,<br><span class="zh">我们对亲自动手做事的热情是如此之强烈，</span>

that we are becoming increasingly less dependent on specialized labour.<br><span class="zh">以至于我们越来越不依赖专业劳动力了。</span>

No one can plead ignorance of a subject any longer, for there are countless do-it-yourself publications.<br><span class="zh">如今，再没有人能够以“对某个主题一无所知”为借口了，因为市面上有数不胜数的 DIY（自己动手做）类出版物可供参考。</span>

Armed with the right tools and materials, newlyweds gaily embark on the task of decorating their own homes.<br><span class="zh">配备了合适的工具和材料后，新婚夫妇便兴高采烈地开始装饰自己的家。</span>

Men, particularly, spend hours of their leisure time installing their own fireplaces,<br><span class="zh">尤其是男性，他们会花费大量的闲暇时间来自己安装壁炉。</span>

laying out their own gardens; building garages and making furniture.<br><span class="zh">他们自己规划花园的布局，建造车库，并制作家具。</span>

Some really keen enthusiasts go so far as to build their own computers.<br><span class="zh">一些非常热衷的爱好者甚至会自己组装电脑。</span>

Shops cater for the do-it-yourself craze not only by running special advisory services for novices,<br><span class="zh">商店不仅通过为新手提供专门的咨询服务，</span>

but by offering consumers bits and pieces which they can assemble at home.<br><span class="zh">还通过向消费者提供各种可以自行组装的零件来迎合这股热潮。</span>

Such things provide an excellent outlet for pent up creative energy, but unfortunately not all of us are born handymen.<br><span class="zh">这类活动为人们提供了释放内心创造力的绝佳途径，可惜并不是每个人天生就擅长动手做事（或：并不是每个人天生都具备动手能力）。</span>

Some wives tend to believe that their husbands are infinitely resourceful and can fix anything.<br><span class="zh">有些妻子总是认为自己的丈夫无所不能，什么问题都能解决。</span>

Even men who can hardly drive a nail in straight are supposed to be born electricians, carpenters, plumbers and mechanics.<br><span class="zh">即使是那些连把钉子都钉不直的男人，也被认为天生就适合从事电工、木匠、水管工或机械师等工作。</span>

When lights fuse, furniture gets rickety, pipes get clogged, or vacuum cleaners fail to operate,<br><span class="zh">当保险丝烧断、家具摇晃、管道堵塞或吸尘器失灵时，</span>

some women assume that their husbands will somehow put things right.<br><span class="zh">有些女性认为，她们的丈夫会想办法把事情解决好。</span>

The worst thing about the do-it-yourself game is that sometimes even men live under the delusion that they can do anything,<br><span class="zh">自己动手这事儿最糟糕的地方在于，有时候连男人自己都会误以为无所不能，</span>

even when they have repeatedly been proved wrong.<br><span class="zh">即使他们的观点已经被多次证明是错误的，他们仍然坚持自己的立场。</span>

It is a question of pride as much as anything else.<br><span class="zh">这主要是面子问题。</span>

Last spring my wife suggested that I call in a man to look at our lawn mower.<br><span class="zh">去年春天，我妻子建议我请一位专业人士来检查一下我们的割草机。</span>

It had broken down the previous summer, and though I promised to repair it, I had never got round to it.<br><span class="zh">它在前一个夏天就坏掉了，虽然我答应过要修理它，但一直没能抽空去修。</span>

I would not hear of the suggestion and said that I would fix it myself.<br><span class="zh">我断然拒绝了她的建议，说我自己能修。</span>

One Saturday afternoon, I hauled the machine into the garden and had a close look at it.<br><span class="zh">一个星期六的下午，我把那台机器搬到了花园里，仔细地观察了它一番。</span>

As far as I could see, it needed only a minor adjustment:<br><span class="zh">据我所见，它只需要进行微小的调整即可。</span>

a turn of a screw here, a little tightening up there, a drop of oil and it would be as good as new.<br><span class="zh">这里拧个螺丝，那里紧一紧，再滴几滴油，它就能焕然一新了。</span>

Inevitably the repair job was not quite so simple.<br><span class="zh">不可避免地，这次维修工作并没有那么简单。</span>

The mower firmly refused to mow, so I decided to dismantle it.<br><span class="zh">割草机就是不肯割草，于是我决定把它拆开。</span>

The garden was soon littered with chunks of metal which had once made up a lawn mower.<br><span class="zh">花园里很快就散落着许多金属碎片——那些碎片原本是割草机的一部分。</span>

But I was extremely pleased with myself. I had traced the cause of the trouble.<br><span class="zh">但我对自己感到非常满意。我已经找到了问题的根源。</span>

One of the links in the chain that drives the wheels had snapped.<br><span class="zh">驱动轮子的链条上有一节断了。</span>

After buying a new chain I was faced with the insurmountable task of putting the confusing jigsaw puzzle together again.<br><span class="zh">买了新链条后，我面临着一个无法完成的任务：把这个令人头疼的拼图重新装回去。</span>

I was not surprised to find that the machine still refused to work after I had reassembled it,<br><span class="zh">当我重新组装好这台机器后，发现它仍然无法正常工作时，我并不感到惊讶。</span>

for the simple reason that I was left with several curiously shaped bits of metal which did not seem to fit anywhere.<br><span class="zh">原因很简单：我手里还剩几块形状古怪的金属片，怎么也装不上去。</span>

I gave up in despair.<br><span class="zh">我绝望地放弃了。</span>

The weeks passed and the grass grew.<br><span class="zh">几周过去了，草也长高了。</span>

When my wife nagged me to do something about it,<br><span class="zh">当妻子催我处理这事时，</span>

I told her that either I would have to buy a new mower or let the grass grow.<br><span class="zh">我告诉她，要么我得买一台新的割草机，要么就任由草继续长下去。</span>

Needless to say our house is now surrounded by a jungle.<br><span class="zh">不用说，我们家现在被一片丛林包围了。</span>

Buried somewhere in deep grass there is a rusting lawn mower which I have promised to repair one day.<br><span class="zh">在茂密的草丛中，埋着一把生锈的割草机；我曾承诺有朝一日一定会把它修好。</span>
