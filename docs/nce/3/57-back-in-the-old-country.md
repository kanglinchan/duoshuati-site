# 第 57 课：Back in the Old Country

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Back in the old country<br><span class="zh">回到故乡</span>

Did the narrater find his mother's grave?<br><span class="zh">我停下车，让发动机冷却一下，同时研究地图。</span>

I stopped to let the car cool off and to study the map.<br><span class="zh">我停下来，让车子冷却一下，同时仔细研究地图。</span>

I had expected to be near my objective by now, but everything still seemed alien to me.<br><span class="zh">我本以为自己应该已经接近目的地了，但周围的一切依然让我感到陌生。</span>

I was only five when my father had taken me abroad, and that was eighteen years ago.<br><span class="zh">我五岁的时候，父亲把我带到了国外，那已经是十八年前的事了。</span>

When my mother had died after a tragic accident, he did not quickly recover from the shock and loneliness.<br><span class="zh">母亲在一次悲剧性的事故中去世后，他久久无法从震惊和孤独中走出来。</span>

Everything around him was full of her presence, continually reopening the wound.<br><span class="zh">他身边的每一样东西都让他想起她，那些回忆不断重新揭开他的伤痛。</span>

So he decided to emigrate.<br><span class="zh">于是，他决定移民。</span>

In the new country he became absorbed in making a new life for the two of us, so that he gradually ceased to grieve.<br><span class="zh">在新的国家里，他全身心地投入到为我们俩创造新生活的过程中，渐渐地，他的悲伤也减轻了。</span>

He did not marry again and I was brought up without a woman's care; but I lacked for nothing, for he was both father and mother to me.<br><span class="zh">他再也没有再婚，我在没有女性照顾的环境中长大；不过我并不缺乏什么，因为父亲同时扮演着母亲的角色。</span>

He always meant to go back one day, but not to stay.<br><span class="zh">他一直说总有一天会回去，但并不是为了定居。</span>

His roots and mine had become too firmly embedded in the new land.<br><span class="zh">他的根基和我的根基都已经深深地扎根在这片新的土地上。</span>

But he wanted to see the old folk againand to visit my mother's grave.<br><span class="zh">但他想再见见那些老朋友，也想去看看我母亲的坟墓。</span>

He became mortally ill a few months before we had planned to go and, when he knew that he was dying, he made me promise to go on my own.<br><span class="zh">就在我们计划出发的几个月前，他病入膏肓。当他知道自己时日无多时，他让我答应独自完成这次旅程。</span>

I hired a car the day after landing and bought a comprehensive book of maps,<br><span class="zh">我落地后的第二天就租了一辆车，并买了一本内容详尽的地图册。</span>

which I found most helpful on the cross-country journey, but which I did not think I should need on the last stage.<br><span class="zh">这在我长途旅行的过程中确实非常有用，不过我觉得在最后一段旅程中应该并不需要它。</span>

It was not that I actually remembered anything at all.<br><span class="zh">其实我根本什么都不记得了。</span>

But my father had described over and over again what we should see at every milestone after leaving the nearest town,<br><span class="zh">但我父亲一遍又一遍地描述过，在离开最近的那个小镇之后，我们在每一个重要的里程碑处应该看到什么。</span>

so that I was positive I should recognize it as familiar territory.<br><span class="zh">这样我就能肯定自己会认出这片熟悉的地方。</span>

Well, I had been wrong, for I was now lost.<br><span class="zh">好吧，我错了——因为我现在真的迷路了。</span>

I looked at the map and then at the milometer.<br><span class="zh">我先看了看地图，然后又看了看里程表。</span>

I had come ten miles since leaving the town and at this point, according to my father,<br><span class="zh">自从离开小镇后，我已经走了十英里。据我父亲说，</span>

I should be looking at farms and cottages in a valley,<br><span class="zh">我本应看到山谷里的农场和小屋，</span>

with the spire of the church of our village showing in the far distance.<br><span class="zh">远处，我们村庄教堂的尖顶清晰可见。</span>

I could see no valley, no farms, no cottages and no church spire-only a lake.<br><span class="zh">我看不到任何山谷、农场、小屋，也没有教堂的尖塔——只有一片湖泊。</span>

I decided that I must have taken a wrong turning somewhere.<br><span class="zh">我意识到自己肯定在某个地方走错了路（即选择了错误的方向或路径）。</span>

So I drove back to the town and began to retrace the route, taking frequent glances at the map.<br><span class="zh">于是我开车回到了镇上，开始沿着原来的路线返回，同时不时地查看地图。</span>

I landed up at the same corner.<br><span class="zh">结果我又回到了同一个路口。</span>

The curious thing was that the lake was not marked on the map.<br><span class="zh">奇怪的是，这个湖泊在地图上根本没有标注。</span>

I felt as if I had stumbled into a nightmare country, as you sometimes do in dreams.<br><span class="zh">我感觉自己仿佛闯入了一个噩梦般的国度——就像在梦中有时会遇到的那样。</span>

And, as in a nightmare, there was nobody in sight to help me.<br><span class="zh">就像在噩梦中一样，周围根本没有人能够帮助我。</span>

Fortunately for me, as I was wondering what to do next,<br><span class="zh">幸运的是，就在我思考接下来该做什么的时候……</span>

there appeared on the horizon a man on horseback, riding in my direction.<br><span class="zh">地平线上出现了一个骑马的人，他正朝我的方向走来。</span>

I waited till he came near, then I asked him the way to our old village.<br><span class="zh">我等他走近后，便向他打听去我们老村庄的路。</span>

He said that there was now no village.<br><span class="zh">他说，那个村庄现在已经不存在了。</span>

I thought he must have misunderstood me, so I repeated its name.<br><span class="zh">我以为他一定误解了我的意思，于是我又重复了一遍那个名字。</span>

This time he pointed to the lake.<br><span class="zh">这次他指向了那个湖泊。</span>

The village no longer existed because it had been submerged, and all the valley too.<br><span class="zh">那个村庄已经不存在了——因为它被水淹没了，整个山谷也都消失了。</span>

The lake was not a natural one, but a man-made reservoir.<br><span class="zh">那个湖泊并不是天然的，而是一个人工建造的水库。</span>
