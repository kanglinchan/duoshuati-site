# 第 35 课：Justice was Done

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Justice was done<br><span class="zh">正义得到了伸张。</span>

The word 'justice' is given two different meanings in the text.<br><span class="zh">在这段文字中，“justice”这个词被赋予了两种不同的含义。</span>

What is the distinction between them?<br><span class="zh">它们之间的区别是什么？</span>

The word justice is usually associated with courts of law.<br><span class="zh">“正义”这个词通常与法庭联系在一起。</span>

We might say that justice has been done when a man's innocence or guilt has been proved beyond doubt.<br><span class="zh">我们可以说，当一个人的清白或罪行被确凿无疑地证明时，正义就得到了伸张。</span>

Justice is part of the complex machinery of the law.<br><span class="zh">正义是法律这一复杂体系的重要组成部分。</span>

Those who seek it, undertake an arduous journey and can never be sure that they will find it.<br><span class="zh">那些追寻它的人，必须踏上一段艰辛的旅程；而且他们永远无法确定自己是否真的能够找到它。</span>

Judges, however wise or eminent, are human and can make mistakes.<br><span class="zh">然而，无论法官多么明智或德高望重，他们终究也是人，因此也可能会犯错。</span>

There are rare instances when justice almost ceases to be an abstract concept.<br><span class="zh">在极少数情况下，正义几乎不再是一个抽象的概念。</span>

Reward or punishment are meted out quite independent of human interference.<br><span class="zh">奖惩的施行完全不受人为干预。</span>

At such times, justice acts like a living force.<br><span class="zh">在这样的时刻，正义就像一股活生生的力量。</span>

When we use a phrase like 'it serves him right',<br><span class="zh">当我们使用“这是他应得的”这样的表达时，</span>

we are, in part, admitting that a certain set of circumstances has enabled justice to act of its own accord.<br><span class="zh">在某种程度上，我们承认：某些特定的情况让正义自行发挥了作用。</span>

When a thief was caught on the premises of a large jewellery store one morning,<br><span class="zh">一天早晨，一个小偷在一家大型珠宝店的店内被抓获。</span>

the shop assistants must have found it impossible to resist the temptation to say 'it serves him right'.<br><span class="zh">店员们肯定忍不住想说：“他活该。”</span>

The shop was an old converted house with many large, disused fireplaces and tall, narrow chimneys.<br><span class="zh">这家商店是一栋经过改造的旧房子，里面有许多大型、已经废弃的壁炉，以及高而狭窄的烟囱。</span>

Towards midday, a girl heard a muffled cry coming from behind one of the walls.<br><span class="zh">临近中午时分，一个女孩听到从某面墙后面传来的微弱哭声。</span>

As the cry was repeated several times,<br><span class="zh">当那声呼喊被重复了几次之后……</span>

she ran to tell the manager who promptly rang up the fire brigade.<br><span class="zh">她跑去告诉了经理，经理立刻拨打了消防队的电话。</span>

The cry had certainly come from one of the chimneys,<br><span class="zh">那声哭喊肯定是从其中一根烟囱里传出来的。</span>

but as there were so many of them, the firemen could not be certain which one it was.<br><span class="zh">但由于烟囱太多，消防员们无法确定是哪一根。</span>

They located the right chimney by tapping at the walls and listening for the man's cries.<br><span class="zh">他们通过敲击墙壁并聆听那个人的呼喊声，最终找到了正确的烟囱。</span>

After chipping through a wall which was eighteen inches thick,<br><span class="zh">在凿穿了一面厚度为十八英寸的墙壁之后……</span>

they found that a man had been trapped in the chimney.<br><span class="zh">他们发现有一名男子被困在了烟囱里。</span>

As it was extremely narrow, the man was unable to move,<br><span class="zh">由于通道非常狭窄，那个人根本无法移动。</span>

but the fire fighters were eventually able to free him by cutting a huge hole in the wall.<br><span class="zh">但是消防员最终通过在墙上切出一个大洞，成功将他救了出来。</span>

The sorry-looking, blackened figure that emerged,<br><span class="zh">那个狼狈不堪、满身漆黑的身影一出现，</span>

admitted at once that he had tried to break into the shop during the night but had got stuck in the chimney.<br><span class="zh">他立刻承认，自己确实在夜里试图闯入那家商店，但结果被困在了烟囱里。</span>

He had been there for nearly ten hours.<br><span class="zh">他已经在那里待了将近十个小时了。</span>

Justice had been done even before the man was handed over to the police.<br><span class="zh">正义已经得到了伸张，甚至在那个男人被交给警察之前就已经如此了。</span>
