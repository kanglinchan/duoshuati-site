# 第 32 课：A Lost Ship

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

A lost ship<br><span class="zh">一艘失踪的船只</span>

Did the crew of the Elkor find what they were looking for? Why?<br><span class="zh">埃尔科号（Elkor）的船员们找到他们想要的东西了吗？为什么？</span>

The salvage operation had been a complete failure.<br><span class="zh">这次打捞作业彻底失败了。</span>

The small ship, Elkor, which had been searching the Barents Sea for weeks, was on its way home.<br><span class="zh">那艘名为“Elkor”的小船已经在巴伦支海搜寻了数周，现在正返回家中。</span>

A radio message from the mainland had been received by the ship's captain instructing him to give up the search.<br><span class="zh">船长接收到了一则来自大陆的无线电消息，命令他停止搜索。</span>

The captain knew that another attempt would be made later,<br><span class="zh">船长知道，稍后还会有人再次尝试。</span>

for the sunken ship he was trying to find had been carrying a precious cargo of gold bullion.<br><span class="zh">因为他试图寻找的那艘沉船，当时正载着一批珍贵的黄金货物。</span>

Despite the message, the captain of the Elkor decided to try once more.<br><span class="zh">尽管收到了那条消息，埃尔科号（Elkor）的船长还是决定再试一次。</span>

The sea bed was scoured with powerful nets and there was tremendous excitement on board when a chest was raised from the bottom.<br><span class="zh">海床被强大的渔网彻底清理过；当一个箱子从海底被打捞上来时，船上的所有人都兴奋不已。</span>

Though the crew were at first under the impression that the lost ship had been found,<br><span class="zh">虽然船员们最初以为那艘失踪的船只已经被找到了，</span>

the contents of the chest proved them wrong.<br><span class="zh">箱子里的物品证明他们是错的。</span>

What they had in fact found was a ship which had been sunk many years before.<br><span class="zh">他们实际上发现的是一艘多年前就已经沉没的船只。</span>

The chest contained the personal belongings of a seaman, Alan Fielding.<br><span class="zh">这个箱子里装着水手艾伦·菲尔丁的个人物品。</span>

There were books, clothing and photographs, together with letters which the seaman had once received from his wife.<br><span class="zh">那里有书籍、衣物和照片，还有一些信件——这些都是那位水手曾经从他的妻子那里收到的。</span>

The captain of the Elkor ordered his men to salvage as much as possible from the wreck.<br><span class="zh">埃尔科号（Elkor）的船长命令他的船员们从残骸中尽可能多地抢救出有用的物品。</span>

Nothing of value was found,<br><span class="zh">没有发现任何有价值的东西。</span>

but the numerous items which were brought to the surface proved to be of great interest.<br><span class="zh">但是，那些被发掘出来的众多物品都引起了极大的兴趣。</span>

From a heavy gun that was raised, the captain realized that the ship must have been a cruiser.<br><span class="zh">当那门大炮被抬起来时，船长意识到这艘船应该是一艘巡洋舰。</span>

In another chest which contained the belongings of a ship's officer,<br><span class="zh">在另一个箱子里，装着一名船员的个人物品。</span>

there was an unfinished letter which had been written on March 14th, 1943.<br><span class="zh">有一封未完成的信，写于1943年3月14日。</span>

The captain learnt from the letter that the name of the lost ship was the Karen.<br><span class="zh">从这封信中，船长得知那艘失踪的船只的名字叫“Karen”。</span>

The most valuable find of all was the ship's log book, parts of which it was still possible to read.<br><span class="zh">最珍贵的发现是那艘船的航海日志；其中的一部分内容仍然可以辨认出来。</span>

From this the captain was able to piece together all the information that had come to light.<br><span class="zh">通过这些信息，船长得以整理出所有已经公开的事实和细节。</span>

The Karen had been sailing in a convoy to Russia when she was torpedoed by an enemy submarine.<br><span class="zh">那艘名为“Karen”的船当时正与一支船队一起前往俄罗斯，途中被敌方潜艇发射的鱼雷击中。</span>

This was later confirmed by a naval official at the Ministry of Defence after the Elkor had returned home.<br><span class="zh">这一事件在“Elkor”号返回国内后，得到了国防部海军官员的证实。</span>

All the items that were found were sent to the War Museum.<br><span class="zh">船上发现的所有物品都被送到了战争博物馆。</span>
