# 第 49 课：The Ideal Servant

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

The ideal servant<br><span class="zh">理想的仆人</span>

What was Bessie's 'little weakness' ?<br><span class="zh">贝西的“小缺点”是什么？</span>

It is a good thing my aunt Harriet died years ago.<br><span class="zh">幸好我的阿姨哈丽特几年前就去世了。</span>

If she were alive today she would not be able to air her views on her favourite topic of conversation: domestic servants.<br><span class="zh">如果她今天还活着，她将无法再公开表达自己最热衷讨论的话题——也就是关于家庭佣人的看法了。</span>

Aunt Harriet lived in that leisurely age when servants were employed to do housework.<br><span class="zh">哈丽特阿姨生活在那个时代：那时人们还会雇佣仆人来帮忙做家务。</span>

She had a huge, rambling country house called 'The Gables'.<br><span class="zh">她拥有一座规模庞大、布局错综复杂的乡村住宅，名叫“The Gables”。</span>

She was sentimentally attached to this house,<br><span class="zh">她对这栋房子怀有深厚的情感依恋。</span>

for even though it was far too big for her needs, she persisted in living there long after her husband's death.<br><span class="zh">尽管那套房子远远超出了她的实际需求（空间太大，不符合她的居住要求），但在丈夫去世后，她仍然坚持住在那里很长时间。</span>

Before she grew old, aunt Harriet used to entertain lavishly.<br><span class="zh">在哈丽特阿姨年老之前，她总是非常热情地招待客人，总是准备丰盛的美食和精彩的娱乐活动来款待他们。</span>

I often visited The Gables when I was a boy.<br><span class="zh">我小时候经常去“The Gables”那个地方。</span>

No matter how many guests were present, the great house was always immaculate.<br><span class="zh">无论有多少客人来访，那座宏伟的房子总是保持得一尘不染、整洁有序。</span>

The parquet floors shone like mirrors;<br><span class="zh">镶木地板闪闪发光，就像镜子一样。</span>

highly polished silver was displayed in gleaming glass cabinets;<br><span class="zh">那些经过精心打磨的银器被陈列在闪闪发光的玻璃柜中。</span>

even my uncle's huge collection of books was kept miraculously free from dust.<br><span class="zh">就连我叔叔那庞大的藏书量，也奇迹般地没有一点灰尘。</span>

Aunt Harriet presided over an invisible army of servants that continuously scrubbed, cleaned, and polished.<br><span class="zh">哈丽特阿姨指挥着一支“看不见的仆人队伍”，这支队伍不断地擦拭、清洁和抛光各种物品。</span>

She always referred to them as 'the shifting population',<br><span class="zh">她总是把他们称为“流动的人口”（即那些不断迁移、居无定所的人群）。</span>

for they came and went with such frequency that I never even got a chance to learn their names.<br><span class="zh">因为他们来得频繁，去得也快，以至于我根本没机会记住他们的名字。</span>

Though my aunt pursued what was, in those days an enlightened policy,<br><span class="zh">尽管我的姑姑在当时推行了一项被认为具有前瞻性的政策，</span>

in that she never allowed her domestic staff to work more than eight hours a day, she was extremely difficult to please.<br><span class="zh">因为她从不允许家中的佣人每天工作超过八小时，所以她非常难以取悦。</span>

While she always criticized the fickleness of human nature,<br><span class="zh">虽然她总是批评人性的善变，</span>

she carried on an unrelenting search for the ideal servant to the end of her days,<br><span class="zh">她一生都在不懈地寻找那个理想的仆人，直到生命的最后一刻。</span>

even after she had been sadly disillusioned by Bessie.<br><span class="zh">即使在她被贝西（Bessie）彻底打消了所有希望之后……</span>

Bessie worked for aunt Harriet for three years.<br><span class="zh">贝西在哈丽特阿姨家工作了三年。</span>

During that time she so gained my aunt's confidence, that she was put in charge of the domestic staff.<br><span class="zh">在那段时间里，她赢得了我阿姨的充分信任，以至于被委以管理所有家务人员的重任。</span>

Aunt Harriet could not find words to praise Bessie's industriousness and efficiency.<br><span class="zh">哈丽特阿姨实在找不到合适的词语来赞扬贝西的勤奋和高效。</span>

In addition to all her other qualifications, Bessie was an expert cook.<br><span class="zh">除了拥有其他各种技能外，贝西还是一位烹饪高手。</span>

She acted the role of the perfect servant for three years before Aunt Harriet discovered her 'little weakness'.<br><span class="zh">她扮演了“完美仆人”的角色长达三年，直到哈丽特阿姨发现了她的“小缺点”。</span>

After being absent from The Gables for a week,<br><span class="zh">在缺席了《The Gables》节目一周之后……</span>

my aunt unexpectedly returned one afternoon with a party of guests and instructed Bessie to prepare dinner.<br><span class="zh">一天下午，我的阿姨突然带着一群客人回来了，她吩咐贝西准备晚餐。</span>

Not only was the meal well below the usual standard, but Bessie seemed unable to walk steadily.<br><span class="zh">这顿饭的质量远低于平时的标准；而且贝西似乎也走不稳了。</span>

She bumped into the furniture and kept mumbling about the guests.<br><span class="zh">她撞到了家具，然后不停地嘟囔着关于那些客人的事情。</span>

When she came in with the last course--a huge pudding--she tripped on the carpet<br><span class="zh">当她端着最后一道菜（一道巨大的布丁）进来时，她在地毯上绊倒了。</span>

and the pudding went flying through the air, narrowly missed my aunt, and crashed on the dining table with considerable force.<br><span class="zh">布丁突然飞到了空中，差点撞到我的阿姨，然后猛烈地摔在了餐桌上。</span>

Though this caused great mirth among the guests, Aunt Harriet was horrified.<br><span class="zh">虽然这引起了在场宾客们的阵阵笑声，但哈丽特阿姨却感到非常震惊。</span>

She reluctantly came to the conclusion that Bessie was drunk.<br><span class="zh">她勉强得出结论：贝西肯定是喝醉了。</span>

The guests had, of course, realized this from the moment Bessie opened the door for them and, long before the final catastrophe,<br><span class="zh">当然，客人们从贝西为他们开门的那一刻起就意识到了这一点；早在最终灾难发生之前，他们就已经察觉到了不对劲。</span>

had had a difficult time trying to conceal their amusement.<br><span class="zh">他们很难掩饰自己的笑意。</span>

The poor girl was dismissed instantly.<br><span class="zh">那个可怜的女孩立刻就被解雇了。</span>

After her departure, Aunt Harriet discovered that there were piles of empty wine bottles of all shapes and sizes neatly stacked in what had once been Bessie's wardrobe.<br><span class="zh">在她离开后，哈丽特阿姨发现：曾经是贝西衣柜的地方，现在整齐地堆放着一堆堆形状各异、大小不一的空酒瓶。</span>

They had mysteriously found their way there from the wine cellar!<br><span class="zh">它们竟然神秘地从酒窖里找到了通往那里的路！</span>
