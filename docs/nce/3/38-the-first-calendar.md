# 第 38 课：The First Calendar

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

The first calendar<br><span class="zh">第一个日历</span>

What is the importance of the dots, lines, and symbols engraved on stone, bones and ivory?<br><span class="zh">刻在石头、骨头和象牙上的点、线以及符号究竟有什么重要性呢？</span>

Future historians will be in a unique position when they come to record the history of our own times.<br><span class="zh">未来的历史学家在记录我们这个时代的历史时，将会处于一个非常独特的地位。</span>

They will hardly know which facts to select from the great mass of evidence that steadily accumulates.<br><span class="zh">他们几乎无法从不断积累的大量证据中判断出应该选择哪些事实。</span>

What is more, they will not have to rely solely on the written word.<br><span class="zh">此外，他们将不必仅仅依赖书面文字。</span>

Films, videos, CDs and CD-ROMs are just some of the bewildering amount of information they will have.<br><span class="zh">电影、视频、CD以及CD-ROM只是他们所拥有的海量信息中的一部分而已。</span>

They will be able, as it were, to see and hear us in action.<br><span class="zh">他们将能够，可以说是，亲眼看到我们的活动，亲耳听到我们的声音。</span>

But the historian attempting to reconstruct the distant past is always faced with a difficult task.<br><span class="zh">但是，试图重建遥远过去的历史学家总是面临着一项艰巨的任务。</span>

He has to deduce what he can from the few scanty clues available.<br><span class="zh">他必须从现有的一些零星线索中尽可能地进行推断。</span>

Even seemingly insignificant remains can shed interesting light on the history of early man.<br><span class="zh">即使是一些看似微不足道的遗物，也能为了解早期人类的历史提供有趣的线索。</span>

Up to now, historians have assumed that calendars came into being with the advent of agriculture,<br><span class="zh">到目前为止，历史学家们一直认为日历的出现是与农业的诞生同时发生的。</span>

for then man was faced with a real need to understand something about the seasons.<br><span class="zh">因为那时，人类确实迫切需要了解关于季节变化的规律。</span>

Recent scientific evidence seems to indicate that this assumption is incorrect.<br><span class="zh">最近的科学证据似乎表明，这一假设是不正确的。</span>

Historians have long been puzzled by dots, lines and symbols which have been engraved on walls, bones, and the ivory tusks of mammoths.<br><span class="zh">历史学家们长期以来一直对这些刻在墙壁上、骨头以及猛犸象象牙上的点、线条和符号感到困惑。</span>

The nomads who made these markings lived by hunting and fishing during the last Ice Age which began about 35, 000 B.C. and ended about 10, 000 B.C.<br><span class="zh">制造这些标记的游牧民族在最后一次冰河时代（大约始于公元前35000年，结束于公元前10000年）以狩猎和捕鱼为生。</span>

By correlating markings made in various parts of the world,<br><span class="zh">通过将世界各地留下的标记进行关联分析，</span>

historians have been able to read this difficult code.<br><span class="zh">历史学家们已经成功解读了这段难以理解的代码。</span>

They have found that it is connected with the passage of days and the phases of the moon.<br><span class="zh">他们发现这与日期的流逝和月亮的相位有关。</span>

It is, in fact, a primitive type of calendar.<br><span class="zh">实际上，这是一种非常原始的日历形式。</span>

It has long been known that the hunting scenes depicted on walls were not simply a form of artistic expression.<br><span class="zh">人们早就知道，墙上描绘的狩猎场景并非仅仅是一种艺术表达形式。</span>

They had a definite meaning, for they were as near as early man could get to writing.<br><span class="zh">这些符号具有明确的含义，因为它们是早期人类所能达到的最接近文字的表达方式。</span>

It is possible that there is a definite relation between these paintings and the markings that sometimes accompany them.<br><span class="zh">这些壁画与有时伴随出现的标记之间可能存在某种明确的联系。</span>

It seems that man was making a real effort to understand the seasons 20, 000 years earlier than has been supposed.<br><span class="zh">看来，早在人们原先认为的时间之前2万年，人类就已经在努力理解四季的变化规律了。</span>
