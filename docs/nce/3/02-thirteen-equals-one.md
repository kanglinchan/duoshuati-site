# 第 2 课：Thirteen Equals One

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Thirteen equals one<br><span class="zh">十三等于一</span>

Was the vicar pleased that the clock was striking? Why?<br><span class="zh">我们的牧师总是在为这样或那样的事情筹款，</span>

Our vicar is always raising money for one cause or another,<br><span class="zh">我们的牧师总是为各种慈善事业筹款，</span>

but he has never managed to get enough money to have the church clock repaired.<br><span class="zh">但他始终没能筹集到足够的钱来修理教堂的时钟。</span>

The big clock which used to strike the hours day and night was damaged many years ago and has been silent ever since.<br><span class="zh">那座曾经日夜报时的大钟在多年前就损坏了，从那以后就一直没再响过。</span>

One night, however, our vicar woke up with a start:the clock was striking the hours!<br><span class="zh">然而一天晚上，我们的牧师突然惊醒：时钟竟然在报时了！</span>

Looking at his watch, he saw that it was one o'clock,<br><span class="zh">他看了看手表，发现时间是凌晨一点，</span>

but the bell struck thirteen times before it stopped.<br><span class="zh">但钟声却响了十三下才停止。</span>

Armed with a torch, the vicar went up into the clock tower to see what was going on.<br><span class="zh">牧师拿着手电筒走上钟楼，想看看发生了什么。</span>

In the torchlight, he caught sight of a figure whom he immediately recognized as Bill Wilkins, our local grocer.<br><span class="zh">在手电筒的光亮中，他看到一个人影，立刻认出那是我们当地的杂货商比尔·威尔金斯。</span>

'Whatever are you doing up here Bill?' asked the vicar in surprise.<br><span class="zh">“比尔，你到底在这里干什么？”牧师惊讶地问。</span>

'I'm trying to repair the bell, ' answered Bill.<br><span class="zh">“我正在尝试修理这个铃铛，”比尔回答道。</span>

'I've been coming up here night after night for weeks now.<br><span class="zh">“这几周来，我夜夜都来这里。</span>

You see, I was hoping to give you a surprise.'<br><span class="zh">你看，我本来想给你一个惊喜的。</span>

'You certainly did give me a surprise!' said the vicar.<br><span class="zh">“你确实给了我一个惊喜！”牧师说道。</span>

'You've probably woken up everyone in the village as well.<br><span class="zh">“你可能把全村人都给吵醒了。</span>

Still, I'm glad the bell is working again.'<br><span class="zh">不过，我很高兴这个铃铛又能正常使用了。</span>

'That's the trouble, vicar, 'answered Bill. 'It's working all right,<br><span class="zh">“问题就在这儿，牧师，”比尔回答说，“钟是能走，</span>

but I'm afraid that at one o'clock it will strike thirteen times and there's nothing I can do about it.'<br><span class="zh">但我担心，到了凌晨一点的时候，钟声会敲响十三下，而我对此无能为力。</span>

'We'll get used to that, Bill, 'said the vicar.<br><span class="zh">“我们会慢慢习惯的，比尔，”牧师说道。</span>

'Thirteen is not as good as one, but it's better than nothing.<br><span class="zh">“十三下虽然不如一下，但总比不响好。</span>

Now let's go downstairs and have a cup of tea.'<br><span class="zh">现在，我们下楼去喝杯茶吧。</span>
