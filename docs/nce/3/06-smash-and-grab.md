# 第 6 课：Smash-and-Grab

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Smash-and-grab<br><span class="zh">砸窗抢劫</span>

How did Mr. Taylor try to stop the thieves?<br><span class="zh">泰勒先生是如何试图阻止那些小偷的？</span>

The expensive shops in a famous arcade near Piccadilly were just opening.<br><span class="zh">在早晨的这个时间，拱廊街里几乎空无一人。</span>

At this time of the morning, the arcade was almost empty.<br><span class="zh">在这个清晨的时刻，游戏厅里几乎空无一人。</span>

Mr. Taylor, the owner of a jewellery shop was admiring a new window display.<br><span class="zh">泰勒先生是一家珠宝店的老板，他正在欣赏店内的新橱窗陈列。</span>

Two of his assistants had been working busily since 8 o'clock and had only just finished.<br><span class="zh">他的两名助手从早上8点开始就一直在忙碌地工作，直到现在才刚刚完成工作。</span>

Diamond necklaces and rings had been beautifully arranged on a background of black velvet.<br><span class="zh">钻石项链和戒指被精心地摆放在黑色天鹅绒的背景上。</span>

After gazing at the display for several minutes, Mr. Taylor went back into his shop.<br><span class="zh">在凝视了橱窗陈列几分钟后，泰勒先生回到了他的店里。</span>

The silence was suddenly broken when a large car, with its headlights on and its horn blaring, roared down the arcade.<br><span class="zh">突然，寂静被打破了——一辆大型汽车亮着车灯、响着喇叭，呼啸着冲过拱廊街。</span>

It came to a stop outside the jeweller's.<br><span class="zh">它停在了珠宝店的外面。</span>

One man stayed at the wheel while two others with black stockings over their faces jumped out and smashed the window of the shop with iron bars.<br><span class="zh">一个人留在方向盘前，另外两个脸上蒙着黑色长筒袜的人跳下车，用铁棍砸碎了商店的橱窗。</span>

While this was going on, Mr. Taylor was upstairs.<br><span class="zh">就在这件事发生的时候，泰勒先生正在楼上。</span>

He and his staff began throwing furniture out of the window.<br><span class="zh">他和他的员工开始把家具从窗户扔出去。</span>

Chairs and tables went flying into the arcade.<br><span class="zh">椅子和桌子都被抛飞到了拱廊里。</span>

One of the thieves was struck by a heavy statue,<br><span class="zh">其中一个小偷被一座沉重的雕像砸中了。</span>

but he was too busy helping himself to diamonds to notice any pain.<br><span class="zh">但他正忙着抢钻石，根本没感觉到疼痛。</span>

The raid was all over in three minutes, for the men scrambled back into the car and it moved off at a fantastic speed.<br><span class="zh">这次突袭在短短三分钟内就结束了；那些人纷纷跳回车上，车子随后以极快的速度离开了现场。</span>

Just as it was leaving,<br><span class="zh">就在它即将离开的时候……</span>

Mr.Taylor rushed out and ran after it throwing ashtrays and vases,<br><span class="zh">泰勒先生冲了出去，追着车扔烟灰缸和花瓶，</span>

but it was impossible to stop the thieves.<br><span class="zh">但是，根本无法阻止那些小偷。</span>

They had got away with thousands of pounds worth of diamonds.<br><span class="zh">他们成功偷走了价值数千英镑的钻石。</span>
