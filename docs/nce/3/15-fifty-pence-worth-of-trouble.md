# 第 15 课：Fifty Pence Worth of Trouble

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

Fifty pence worth of trouble<br><span class="zh">五十便士的“代价”</span>

Did George get anything for his fifty pence? What?<br><span class="zh">孩子们总是很欣赏小额的金钱礼物。</span>

Children always appreciate small gifts of money.<br><span class="zh">孩子们总是很乐意接受小额的金钱礼物。</span>

Mum or dad, of course, provides a regular supply of pocket money, but uncles and aunts are always a source of extra income.<br><span class="zh">当然，爸爸妈妈会定期给他们零花钱，但叔叔阿姨们也是他们额外的收入来源。</span>

With some children, small sums go a long way.<br><span class="zh">对一些孩子来说，这些小额的钱可以派上大用场。</span>

If fifty pence pieces are not exchanged for sweets, they rattle for months inside money boxes.<br><span class="zh">如果五十便士硬币没有被用来买糖果，它们就会在存钱罐里待上好几个月。</span>

Only very thrifty children manage to fill up a money box.<br><span class="zh">只有非常节俭的孩子才能真正把存钱罐填满。</span>

For most of them, fifty pence is a small price to pay for a nice big bar of chocolate.<br><span class="zh">对大多数孩子来说，五十便士不过是买一块美味巧克力的小小代价罢了。</span>

My nephew, George, has a money box but it is always empty.<br><span class="zh">我的侄子乔治有一个存钱罐，但里面总是空空如也。</span>

Very few of the fifty pence pieces and pound coins I have given him have found their way there.<br><span class="zh">我给他的那些五十便士硬币和一英镑硬币，几乎没有一个真正进了那个存钱罐。</span>

I gave him fifty pence yesterday and advised him to save it.<br><span class="zh">昨天我又给了他五十便士，并建议他要把钱存起来。</span>

Instead, he bought himself fifty pence worth of trouble.<br><span class="zh">相反，他给自己惹来了价值五十便士的麻烦。</span>

On his way to the sweet shop, he dropped his fifty pence and it bounced along the pavement and then disappeared down a drain.<br><span class="zh">在去糖果店的路上，他掉了那50便士硬币；硬币沿着人行道弹跳着滚去，最后掉进了下水道里不见了。</span>

George took off his jacket,<br><span class="zh">乔治脱下了他的夹克。</span>

rolled up his sleeves and pushed his right arm through the drain cover.<br><span class="zh">他卷起了袖子，然后将右臂伸进了排水口的盖子里。</span>

He could not find his 50 pence piece anywhere, and what is more, he could not get his arm out.<br><span class="zh">他到处都找不到那枚50便士的硬币，而且更糟糕的是，他的手臂还拔不出来了。</span>

A crowd of people gathered round him and a lady rubbed his arm with soap and butter, but George was firmly stuck.<br><span class="zh">一群人围了上来，一位女士用肥皂和黄油涂抹他的手臂，但乔治还是被卡得牢牢的。</span>

The fire brigade was called and two fire fighters freed George using a special type of grease.<br><span class="zh">消防队被叫来了，两名消防员用一种特殊的润滑剂把乔治解救了出来。</span>

George was not too upset by his experience because the lady who owns the sweet shop heard about his troubles and rewarded him with a large box of chocolates.<br><span class="zh">乔治对他的遭遇并不太在意，因为那家糖果店的老板听说了他的麻烦后，送了他一盒大巧克力作为奖励。</span>
