# 第 50 课：New Year Resolutions

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

New Year resolutions<br><span class="zh">新年决心</span>

What marked the end of the writer's New Year resolutions?<br><span class="zh">是什么标志着这位作家新年决心的结束呢？</span>

The New Year is a time for resolutions.<br><span class="zh">新年是一个制定新目标的时刻。</span>

Mentally, at least, most of us could compile formidable lists of 'dos' and 'don'ts'.<br><span class="zh">至少在精神层面上，我们大多数人都能列出一份详尽的“该做”与“不该做”的清单。</span>

The same old favourites recur year in year out with monotonous regularity.<br><span class="zh">那些老掉牙的决心年复一年地重复出现，单调得令人厌烦。</span>

We resolve to get up earlier each morning, eat less,<br><span class="zh">我们决定每天早上早起一些，少吃一些食物。</span>

find more time to play with the children, do a thousand and one jobs about the house,<br><span class="zh">多花些时间陪孩子们玩耍，同时还要处理家里的各种琐事（比如打扫卫生、做饭、洗衣服等等）。</span>

be nice to people we don't like, drive carefully, and take the dog for a walk every day.<br><span class="zh">对待那些我们不喜欢的人也要友善；开车时要小心谨慎；每天还要带狗狗去散步。</span>

Past experience has taught us that certain accomplishments are beyond attainment.<br><span class="zh">过去的经验告诉我们，有些成就其实是无法实现的。</span>

If we remain inveterate smokers, it is only because we have so often experienced the frustration that results from failure.<br><span class="zh">如果我们还是顽固的烟民，那只是因为我们太多次地经历了因失败而产生的挫败感。</span>

Most of us fail in our efforts at sel-improvement because our schemes are too ambitious and we never have time to carry them out.<br><span class="zh">我们大多数人在自我提升的努力中失败，是因为我们的计划过于宏大，而我们根本没有时间去执行。</span>

We also make the fundamental error of announcing our resolutions to everybody<br><span class="zh">我们还有一个根本性的错误：那就是把我们的决心或计划公之于众。</span>

so that we look even more foolish when we slip back into our bad old ways.<br><span class="zh">这样，当我们重新回到那些糟糕的老习惯时，看起来就会更加愚蠢了。</span>

Aware of these pitfalls, this year I attempted to keep my resolutions to myself.<br><span class="zh">意识到这些陷阱后，今年我试着把决心只留给自己。</span>

I limited myself to two modest ambitions: to do physical exercises every morning and to read more of an evening.<br><span class="zh">我给自己设定了两个简单的目标：每天早上坚持做体育锻炼，晚上多读书。</span>

An all-night party on New Year's Eve provided me with a good excuse for not carrying out either of these new resolutions on the first day of the year,<br><span class="zh">新年前夜的通宵派对为我提供了一个很好的借口，让我能够在新年的第一天不执行任何这些新年决心。</span>

but on the second, I applied myself assiduously to the task.<br><span class="zh">但到了第二天，我勤勉地执行起这项任务。</span>

The daily exercises lasted only eleven minutes and I proposed to do them early in the morning before anyone had got up.<br><span class="zh">每天的锻炼只有十一分钟，我打算在清晨大家还没起床前做。</span>

The self-discipline required to drag myself out of bed 11 minutes earlier than usual was considerable.<br><span class="zh">要比平时早11分钟起床，所需要的自制力是相当大的。</span>

Nevertheless, I managed to creep down into the living room for two days before anyone found me out.<br><span class="zh">尽管如此，我还是设法在两天里悄悄溜进客厅，直到被家人发现。</span>

After jumping about on the carpet and twisting the human frame into uncomfortable positions,<br><span class="zh">在毯子上跳来跳去、把人的身体扭成各种不舒服的姿势之后……</span>

I sat down at the breakfast table in an exhausted condition.<br><span class="zh">我疲惫不堪地坐到了早餐桌前。</span>

It was this that betrayed me.<br><span class="zh">正是这个状态出卖了我。</span>

The next morning the whole family trooped in to watch the performance.<br><span class="zh">第二天早上，全家人都聚在一起观看这场表演。</span>

That was really unsettling but I fended off the taunts and jibes of the family good-humouredly and soon everybody got used to the idea.<br><span class="zh">那确实让人不自在，但我用幽默的态度抵挡了家人的嘲笑和讥讽，大家很快就习惯了。</span>

However, my enthusiasm waned.<br><span class="zh">然而，我的热情逐渐消退了。</span>

The time I spent at exercises gradually diminished.<br><span class="zh">我花在锻炼上的时间逐渐减少了。</span>

Little by little the eleven minutes fell to zero.<br><span class="zh">渐渐地，这十一分钟减少到了零。</span>

By January 10th, I was back to where I had started from.<br><span class="zh">到了1月10日，我又回到了起点。</span>

I argued that if I spent less time exhausting myself at exercises in the morning,<br><span class="zh">我心想，如果早上少花些时间把自己累得筋疲力尽，</span>

I would keep my mind fresh for reading when I got home from work.<br><span class="zh">下班回家后我就能保持头脑清醒来读书了。</span>

Resisting the hypnotizing effect of television,<br><span class="zh">抗拒电视的催眠效果……</span>

I sat in my room for a few evenings with my eyes glued to a book.<br><span class="zh">有好几个晚上，我待在房间里，眼睛一直粘在书上。</span>

One night, however, feeling cold and lonely, I went downstairs and sat in front of the television pretending to read.<br><span class="zh">然而，有一天晚上，我感到寒冷又孤独，于是下楼坐在电视机前，假装在看书。</span>

That proved to be my undoing, for I soon got back to my old bad habit of dozing off in front of the screen.<br><span class="zh">这证明是我的败因，因为我很快又恢复了在屏幕前打瞌睡的老毛病。</span>

I still haven't given up my resolution to do more reading.<br><span class="zh">我仍然没有放弃多读书的决心。</span>

In fact, I have just bought a book entitled How to Read a Thousand Words a Minute.<br><span class="zh">事实上，我刚刚买了一本书，书名是《如何一分钟读完一千个单词》。</span>

Perhaps it will solve my problem, but I just haven't had time to read it!<br><span class="zh">也许这本书能解决我的问题，只是我还没时间去读它罢了！</span>
