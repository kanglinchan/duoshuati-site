# 第 30 课：The Death of a Ghost

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

The death of a ghost<br><span class="zh">一个“幽灵”的死亡</span>

Why did the two brothers keep the secret?<br><span class="zh">为什么这两兄弟要保守这个秘密呢？</span>

For years, villagers believed that Endley farm was haunted.<br><span class="zh">多年来，村民们一直相信恩德利农场有鬼魂出没。</span>

The farm was owned by two brothers, Joe and Bob Cox.<br><span class="zh">这个农场归两兄弟乔·考克斯和鲍勃·考克斯所有。</span>

They employed a few farmhands, but no one was willing to work there long.<br><span class="zh">他们雇了一些农场工人，但没有人愿意在那里长期工作。</span>

Every time a worker gave up his job, he told the same story.<br><span class="zh">每次有工人辞职时，他都会讲述同一个故事。</span>

Farm labourers said that they always woke up to find that work had been done overnight.<br><span class="zh">农场工人们说，他们早上醒来时总会发现，头天晚上的活儿都干完了。</span>

Hay had been cut and cowsheds had been cleaned.<br><span class="zh">干草已经被割下，牛棚也已经被清理干净了。</span>

A farm worker, who stayed up all night, claimed to have seen a figure cutting corn in the moonlight.<br><span class="zh">一位熬夜的农场工人声称，他曾在月光下看见一个人影在收割庄稼。</span>

In time, it became an accepted fact that the Cox brothers employed a conscientious ghost that did most of their work for them.<br><span class="zh">久而久之，大家就都相信考克斯兄弟雇了个勤快的鬼，帮他们干了大部分的活儿。</span>

No one suspected that there might be someone else on the farm who had never been seen.<br><span class="zh">谁也没想到农场里还藏着个谁也没见过的人。</span>

This was indeed the case.<br><span class="zh">事实确实如此。</span>

A short time ago, villagers were astonished to learn that the ghost of Endley had died.<br><span class="zh">不久前，村民们听说恩德利的鬼魂死了，都大吃一惊。</span>

Everyone went to the funeral, for the 'ghost' was none other than Eric Cox,<br><span class="zh">大家都参加了葬礼，因为那个“鬼魂”其实就是埃里克·考克斯。</span>

a third brother who was supposed to have died as a young man.<br><span class="zh">是他们的三弟，人们原以为他年轻时就死了。</span>

After the funeral, Joe and Bob revealed a secret which they had kept for over fifty years.<br><span class="zh">葬礼结束后，乔和鲍勃透露了一个他们保守了五十多年的秘密。</span>

Eric had been the eldest son of the family, very much older than his two brothers.<br><span class="zh">埃里克是家中的老大，比两个弟弟年长很多。</span>

He had been obliged to join the army during the Second World War.<br><span class="zh">在第二次世界大战期间，他被迫加入了军队。</span>

As he hated army life, he decided to desert his regiment.<br><span class="zh">因为他特别讨厌军队生活，就决定当逃兵。</span>

When he learnt that he would be sent abroad,<br><span class="zh">当他得知自己将被派往国外时，</span>

he returned to the farm and his father hid him until the end of the war.<br><span class="zh">他回到了农场，他的父亲把他藏了起来，直到战争结束。</span>

Fearing the authorities, Eric remained in hiding after the war as well.<br><span class="zh">因为怕被政府发现，埃里克战后也一直躲着。</span>

His father told everybody that Eric had been killed in action.<br><span class="zh">他的父亲告诉所有人，埃里克在战斗中牺牲了。</span>

The only other people who knew the secret were Joe and Bob.<br><span class="zh">除了乔和鲍勃，再没有别人知道这个秘密了。</span>

They did not even tell their wives.<br><span class="zh">他们甚至都没有告诉自己的妻子。</span>

When their father died they thought it their duty to keep Eric in hiding.<br><span class="zh">父亲去世后，他们觉得继续把埃里克藏起来是自己的责任。</span>

All these years, Eric had lived as a recluse.<br><span class="zh">这些年来，埃里克一直过着隐居的生活。</span>

He used to sleep during the day and work at night,<br><span class="zh">他过去常常在白天睡觉，晚上工作。</span>

quite unaware of the fact that he had become the ghost of Endley.<br><span class="zh">他压根儿不知道自己已经成了恩德利的鬼。</span>

When he died, however, his brothers found it impossible to keep the secret any longer.<br><span class="zh">然而，当他去世后，他的兄弟们发现再也无法保守这个秘密了。</span>
