# 第 10 课：The Loss of the Titanic

The loss of the Titanic<br><span class="zh">泰坦尼克号的沉没</span>

What'd have happened if only two of the 16 water-tight compartments had been flooded?<br><span class="zh">这艘巨轮泰坦尼克号于1912年4月10日从南安普顿出发，驶向纽约。</span>

The great ship, Titanic sailed for New York from Southampton on April 10th, 1912.<br><span class="zh">船上载有1,316名乘客和891名船员。</span>

She was carrying 1, 316 passengers and a crew of 891.<br><span class="zh">即便以现代标准来看，这艘46,000吨的泰坦尼克号也是一艘巨轮。</span>

Even by modern standards, the 46, 000 ton Titanic was a colossal ship.<br><span class="zh">然而在当时，它不仅是建造过的最大船只，而且被认为永不沉没，因为它有16个防水隔舱。</span>

At that time, however, she was not only the largest ship that had ever been built, but was regarded as unsinkable, for she had sixteen water-tight compartments.<br><span class="zh">即使其中两个隔舱进水，它仍然能够漂浮。</span>

Even if two of these were flooded, she would still be able to float.<br><span class="zh">这艘巨轮的悲剧性沉没将永远被人们铭记，因为它在首航中沉没，造成了重大生命损失。</span>

The tragic sinking of this great liner will always be remembered, for she went down on her first voyage with heavy loss of life.<br><span class="zh">出发四天后，当泰坦尼克号正航行在北大西洋的冰冷水域时，</span>

Four days after setting out, while the Titanic was sailing across the icy waters of the North Atlantic,<br><span class="zh">瞭望员突然发现了一座巨大的冰山。</span>

a huge iceberg was suddenly spotted by a lookout.<br><span class="zh">警报发出后，这艘巨轮急转弯以避免直接相撞。</span>

After the alarm had been given, the great ship turned sharply to avoid a direct collision.<br><span class="zh">泰坦尼克号及时转向，勉强避开了那座高出水面100英尺的巨大冰墙。</span>

The Titanic turned just in time, narrowly missing the immense wall of ice which rose over 100 feet out of the water beside her.<br><span class="zh">突然，从船底传来一阵轻微的颤动声，</span>

Suddenly, there was a slight trembling sound from below,<br><span class="zh">船长走下船舱去看发生了什么事。</span>

and the captain went down to see what had happened.<br><span class="zh">声音非常微弱，以至于没人认为船体受损了。</span>

The noise had been so faint that no one thought that the ship had been damaged.<br><span class="zh">在船舱里，船长惊恐地发现泰坦尼克号正在迅速下沉，</span>

Below, the captain realized to his horror that the Titanic was sinking rapidly,<br><span class="zh">因为它的16个防水隔舱中已经有5个进水了！</span>

for five of her sixteen watertight compartments had already been flooded!<br><span class="zh">弃船命令下达后，数百人跳入冰冷的海水中。</span>

The order to abandon ship was given and hundreds of people plunged into the icy water.<br><span class="zh">由于救生艇不够，1,500人丧生。</span>

As there were not enough life-boats for everybody, 1, 500 lives were lost.<br><span class="zh">由于救生艇的数量不足以容纳所有人，因此有 1,500 人失去了生命。</span>
