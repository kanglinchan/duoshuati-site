# 第 31 课：A Lovable Eccentric

> [新概念英语 · 第三册 培养技能](/nce/ "返回目录")

A lovable eccentric<br><span class="zh">一个可爱的怪人</span>

Why did the shop assistant refuse to serve Dickie?<br><span class="zh">为什么店员拒绝为迪基提供服务呢？</span>

True eccentrics never deliberately set out to draw attention to themselves.<br><span class="zh">真正的怪人从来都不会刻意去吸引别人的注意。</span>

They disregard social conventions without being conscious that they are doing anything extraordinary.<br><span class="zh">他们无视社会习俗，却完全没有意识到自己的行为有什么特别之处。</span>

This invariably wins them the love and respect of others, for they add colour to the dull routine of everyday life.<br><span class="zh">这无疑为他们赢得了他人的喜爱与尊重，因为他们为平淡的日常生活增添了色彩。</span>

Up to the time of his death, Richard Colson was one of the most notable figures in our town.<br><span class="zh">直到去世，理查德·科尔森一直是我们镇上最杰出的人物之一。</span>

He was a shrewd and wealthy businessman,<br><span class="zh">他是一位精明且富有的商人。</span>

but most people in the town hardly knew anything about this side of his life.<br><span class="zh">但镇上的大多数人对他这一面的生活几乎一无所知。</span>

He was known to us all as Dickie and his eccentricity had become legendary long before he died.<br><span class="zh">我们都叫他迪基，他的古怪行为早在他去世前就已成了传奇。</span>

Dickie disliked snobs intensely.<br><span class="zh">迪基极其讨厌势利小人。</span>

Though he owned a large car, he hardly ever used it, preferring always to go on foot.<br><span class="zh">尽管他拥有一辆豪车，但他几乎从不使用它，总是更喜欢步行出行。</span>

Even when it was raining heavily, he refused to carry an umbrella.<br><span class="zh">即使下着大雨，他也拒绝打伞。</span>

One day, he walked into an expensive shop after having been caught in a particularly heavy shower.<br><span class="zh">一天，他在一场倾盆大雨中淋得湿透后，走进了一家高档商店。</span>

He wanted to buy a ￡300 watch for his wife,<br><span class="zh">他想给妻子买一块价值300英镑的手表。</span>

but he was in such a bedraggled condition that an assistant refused to serve him.<br><span class="zh">但他当时的样子实在太狼狈了，以至于店员拒绝为他服务。</span>

Dickie left the shop without a word and returned carrying a large cloth bag.<br><span class="zh">迪基一言不发地离开了商店，然后回来时手里拿着一个大布袋。</span>

As it was extremely heavy, he dumped it on the counter.<br><span class="zh">由于它非常重，他就把它扔在了柜台上。</span>

The assistant asked him to leave, but Dickie paid no attention to him and requested to see the manager.<br><span class="zh">助理让他离开，但迪基根本没理会，反而要求见经理。</span>

Recognizing who the customer was,<br><span class="zh">认出了这位顾客是谁，</span>

the manager was most apologetic and reprimanded the assistant severely.<br><span class="zh">经理非常抱歉，并严厉地训斥了那位助理。</span>

When Dickie was given the watch, he presented the assistant with the cloth bag.<br><span class="zh">当迪基收到那块手表时，他把那个布袋送给了助手。</span>

It contained ￡300 in pennies.<br><span class="zh">里面装着300英镑的便士。</span>

He insisted on the assistant's counting the money before he left--30, 000 pennies in all!<br><span class="zh">他坚持要求店员当面把钱点清——总共三万枚便士！</span>

On another occasion, he invited a number of important critics to see his private collection of modern paintings.<br><span class="zh">还有一次，他邀请了许多知名评论家来参观他的私人现代绘画收藏。</span>

This exhibition received a great deal of attention in the press,<br><span class="zh">这个展览在媒体上受到了极大的关注。</span>

for though the pictures were supposed to be the work of famous artists,<br><span class="zh">因为尽管这些画被认为是著名艺术家的作品，</span>

they had in fact been painted by Dickie.<br><span class="zh">实际上，这些画都是迪基画的。</span>

It took him four years to stage this elaborate joke simply to prove that critics do not always know what they are talking about.<br><span class="zh">他花了四年时间精心策划这个恶作剧，只是为了证明评论家们并非总是言之有物。</span>
