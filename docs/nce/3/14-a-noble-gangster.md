<NcePair en="Six hundred years ago, Sir John Hawkwood arrived in Italy with a band of soldiers and settled near Florence." zh="六百年前，约翰·霍克伍德带着一队士兵来到意大利，并在佛罗伦萨附近安顿下来。" />

<NcePair en="He soon made a name for himself and came to be known to the Italians as Giovanni Acuto." zh="他很快就声名鹊起，在意大利人中被称为乔瓦尼·阿库托（Giovanni Acuto）。" />

<NcePair en="Whenever the Italian city-states were at war with each other, Hawkwood used to hire his soldiers to princes who were willing to pay the high price he demanded." zh="每当意大利各城邦之间发生战争时，霍克伍德就会把他的士兵雇佣给那些愿意出高价的王子们。" />

<NcePair en="In times of peace, when business was bad, Hawkwood and his men would march into a city-state, and after burning down a few farms, would offer to go away if protection money was paid to them." zh="在和平时期，当没生意可做时，霍克伍德和他的手下就会开进某个城邦，烧毁几个农场，然后提出只要付给他们保护费，他们就会离开。" />

<NcePair en="Hawkwood made large sums of money in this way." zh="霍克伍德就是通过这种方式赚到了大量的钱。" />
