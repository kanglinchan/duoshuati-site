# 第 57-58 课：An Unusual Day

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

An unusual day<br><span class="zh">一个不同寻常的日子</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。</span>

What is Mr. Sawyer doing tonight?<br><span class="zh">索耶先生今晚在做什么？</span>

It is eight o'clock.<br><span class="zh">现在是八点钟。</span>

The children go to school by car every day,<br><span class="zh">孩子们每天都乘车去上学。</span>

but today, they are going to school on foot.<br><span class="zh">但是今天，他们要步行去上学。</span>

It is ten o'clock.<br><span class="zh">现在是十点钟。</span>

Mrs. Sawyer usually stays at home in the morning,<br><span class="zh">索耶太太早上通常待在家里。</span>

but this morning, she is going to the shops.<br><span class="zh">但是今天早上，她要去商店。</span>

It is four o'clock.<br><span class="zh">现在是四点钟。</span>

In the afternoon, Mrs. Sawyer usually drinks tea in the living room.<br><span class="zh">下午，索耶夫人通常会在客厅里喝茶。</span>

But this afternoon, she is drinking tea in the garden.<br><span class="zh">但今天下午，她正在花园里喝茶。</span>

It is six o'clock.<br><span class="zh">现在是六点钟。</span>

In the evening, the children usually do their homework,<br><span class="zh">晚上，孩子们通常会做作业。</span>

but this evening, they are not doing their homework.<br><span class="zh">但是今晚，他们没有做作业。</span>

At the moment, they are playing in the garden.<br><span class="zh">他们现在正在花园里玩耍。</span>

It is nine o'clock.<br><span class="zh">现在是九点钟。</span>

Mr. Sawyer usually reads his newspaper at night.<br><span class="zh">索耶先生通常在晚上读报纸。</span>

But he's not reading his newspaper tonight.<br><span class="zh">但他今晚没在看报纸。</span>

At the moment, he's reading an interesting book.<br><span class="zh">此刻，他正在读一本有趣的书。</span>
