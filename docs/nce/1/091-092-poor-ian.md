# 第 91-92 课：Poor Ian

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

Poor Ian!<br><span class="zh">可怜的伊恩！</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。</span>

Who wanted to sell the house?<br><span class="zh">谁想卖掉这栋房子呢？</span>

Has Ian sold his house yet?<br><span class="zh">伊恩已经卖掉他的房子了吗？</span>

Yes, he has.<br><span class="zh">是的，他已经卖了。</span>

He sold it last week.<br><span class="zh">他上周把它卖掉了。</span>

Has he moved to his new house yet?<br><span class="zh">他已经搬进新房子了吗？</span>

No, not yet.<br><span class="zh">不，还没有。</span>

He's still here.<br><span class="zh">他还在这里。</span>

He's going to move tomorrow.<br><span class="zh">他明天就要搬家了。</span>

When? Tomorrow morning?<br><span class="zh">什么时候？明天早上吗？</span>

No. Tomorrow afternoon.<br><span class="zh">不，明天下午。</span>

I'll miss him.<br><span class="zh">我会想念他的。</span>

He has always been a good neighbour.<br><span class="zh">他一直是个很好的邻居。</span>

He's a very nice person.<br><span class="zh">他是个非常友善的人。</span>

We'll all miss him.<br><span class="zh">我们都会想念他的。</span>

When will the new people move into this house?<br><span class="zh">新搬来的人什么时候会入住这栋房子呢？</span>

I think that they'll move in the day after tomorrow.<br><span class="zh">我认为他们会在后天搬进来。</span>

Will you see Ian today, Jenny?<br><span class="zh">珍妮，你今天会见到伊恩吗？</span>

Yes, I will.<br><span class="zh">是的，我会的。</span>

Please give him my regards.<br><span class="zh">请代我向他问好。</span>

Poor Ian! He didn't want to leave this house.<br><span class="zh">可怜的伊恩！他根本不想离开这栋房子。</span>

No, he didn't want to leave, but his wife did!<br><span class="zh">不，他不想离开，但他妻子想！</span>
