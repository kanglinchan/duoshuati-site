# 第 69-70 课：The Car Race

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

The car race<br><span class="zh">汽车比赛</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。</span>

Which car was the winner in 1995?<br><span class="zh">哪辆车在1995年赢得了比赛？</span>

There is a car race near our town every year.<br><span class="zh">每年我们镇附近都会举行一场汽车比赛。</span>

In 1995, there was a very big race.<br><span class="zh">1995年，有一场规模非常大的比赛。</span>

There were hundreds of people there.<br><span class="zh">那里有数百人。</span>

My wife and I were at the race.<br><span class="zh">我和我的妻子一起去了那场比赛。</span>

Our friends Julie and Jack were there, too.<br><span class="zh">我们的朋友朱莉和杰克也都在那里。</span>

You can see us in the crowd.<br><span class="zh">你们可以在人群中看到我们。</span>

We are standing on the left.<br><span class="zh">我们站在左边。</span>

There were twenty cars in the race.<br><span class="zh">比赛中有二十辆汽车。</span>

There were English cars, French cars, German cars, Italian cars, American cars and Japanese cars.<br><span class="zh">有英国汽车、法国汽车、德国汽车、意大利汽车、美国汽车和日本汽车。</span>

It was an exciting finish.<br><span class="zh">比赛的结尾非常激动人心。</span>

The winner was Billy Stewart.<br><span class="zh">获胜者是比利·斯图尔特。</span>

He was in car number fifteen.<br><span class="zh">他驾驶的是十五号车。</span>

Five other cars were just behind him.<br><span class="zh">还有另外五辆车紧跟在他后面。</span>

On the way home, my wife said to me, 'Don't drive so quickly!<br><span class="zh">在回家的路上，我的妻子对我说：‘别开得太快！’</span>

You're not Billy Stewart!'<br><span class="zh">“你又不是比利·斯图尔特！”</span>
