# 第 143-144 课：A Walk Through the Woods

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

A walk through the woods<br><span class="zh">在树林中散步</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。</span>

What was so funny about the words on the sign?<br><span class="zh">那个招牌上的文字有什么好笑的呢？</span>

I live in a very old town which is surrounded by beautiful woods.<br><span class="zh">我住在一个非常古老的城镇里，这个城镇被美丽的树林环绕着。</span>

It is a famous beauty spot.<br><span class="zh">这是一个著名的风景区。</span>

On Sundays, hundreds of people come from the city to see our town and to walk through the woods.<br><span class="zh">每到周日，都会有数百人从城里来到我们这个小镇，然后漫步在树林里。</span>

Visitors have been asked to keep the woods clean and tidy.<br><span class="zh">游客们被要求保持树林的整洁。</span>

Litter baskets have been placed under the trees, but people still throw their rubbish everywhere.<br><span class="zh">虽然树底下已经放置了垃圾篓，人们仍然把垃圾扔得到处都是。</span>

Last Wednesday, I went for a walk in the woods.<br><span class="zh">上周三，我去树林里散步了。</span>

What I saw made me very sad.<br><span class="zh">我所看到的一切让我感到非常难过。</span>

I counted seven old cars and three old refrigerators.<br><span class="zh">我数了七辆旧汽车和三台旧冰箱。</span>

The little baskets were empty and the ground was covered with pieces of paper,<br><span class="zh">那些小篮子都是空的，地上到处是纸片、</span>

cigarette ends, old tyres, empty bottles and rusty tins.<br><span class="zh">烟头、旧轮胎、空瓶子以及生锈的铁罐。</span>

Among the rubbish, I found a sign which said, 'Anyone who leaves litter in these woods will be prosecuted!'<br><span class="zh">在那些垃圾中，我发现了一张告示，上面写着：“任何在这些树林里乱扔垃圾的人都将受到法律追究！”</span>
