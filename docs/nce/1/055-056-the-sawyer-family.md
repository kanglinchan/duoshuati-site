# 第 55-56 课：The Sawyer Family

<NcePair en="Listen to the tape then answer this question." zh="先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。" ex="听力指令句：先听录音，再回答标题下的问题，答案就藏在课文里。" />

<NcePair en="When do the children do their homework?" zh="孩子们什么时候做作业？" ex="句中两个 do：句首的 do 是**助动词**，构成疑问；do their homework 的 do 是**实义动词**“做”。**do one's homework** 做作业，one's 随主语变为 their；When 就时间提问。" />

<NcePair en="The Sawyers live at 87 King Street." zh="索耶一家住在国王街87号。" ex="**the Sawyers**：单数姓氏加 -s 再加 the，指“索耶一家”。**live at + 门牌地址**（at 87 King Street）；一般现在时表示长期居住。TB 操练：Do the Sawyers live at…? / Where do the Sawyers live?" />

<NcePair en="In the morning, Mr. Sawyer goes to work and the children go to school." zh="早上，索耶先生去上班，孩子们去上学。" ex="**go to work / go to school** 表示习惯性上班、上学，名词前不加冠词；三单主语 Mr. Sawyer 后动词加 -s（goes），复数 the children 用原形。**in the morning** 早上的固定介词搭配。" />

<NcePair en="Their father takes them to school every day." zh="他们的父亲每天送他们去上学。" ex="**take sb. to school** 送某人上学，take 后接宾格 them（they 的宾格）+ to 地点。**every day**（分开写）是一般现在时的典型时间标志。" />

<NcePair en="Mrs. Sawyer stays at home every day." zh="索耶夫人每天都待在家里。" ex="**stay at home** 待在家里：home 在此作副词，前面不加介词；every day 置句尾，陈述天天如此的习惯。" />

<NcePair en="She does the housework." zh="她做家务。" ex="**do the housework** 做家务：housework /ˈhaʊswɜːk/ 不可数，习惯搭配必须加 the；does 是 do 的第三人称单数形式。" />

<NcePair en="She always eats her lunch at noon." zh="她总是在中午吃午饭。" ex="**always** 等**频度副词放在实义动词之前**（always eats）；**at noon** 在中午，介词用 at；her lunch 与主语 she 呼应，英语习惯说 eat one's lunch。" />

<NcePair en="In the afternoon, she usually sees her friends." zh="下午，她通常会和朋友见面。" ex="**usually** 位置同 always，置于实义动词前；**see her friends** 的 see 是“会面、看望”之意，不是“看见”；in the afternoon 与 in the morning 同构。" />

<NcePair en="They often drink tea together." zh="他们经常一起喝茶。" ex="**drink tea** 喝茶（英式下午茶习惯）；**often** 频度副词位置与 always、usually 一致；**together** 一起，置于句尾。" />

<NcePair en="In the evening, the children come home from school." zh="傍晚，孩子们放学回家。" ex="**come home from school** 放学回家：from 表示来源；home 作副词，前面不加 to。in the evening 指傍晚时段。" />

<NcePair en="They arrive home early." zh="他们很早就到家了。" ex="**arrive** 不及物，接名词地点要加 in/at，但 **home 是副词**，直接说 arrive home；**early** 副词“早”，紧跟动词作状语。" />

<NcePair en="Mr. Sawyer comes home from work." zh="索耶先生下班回家。" ex="**come home from work** 下班回家，与 come home from school 平行：去时 go to work / go to school，回时 come home from…。" />

<NcePair en="He arrives home late." zh="他很晚才回到家。" ex="**late** 副词“晚、迟”，与 early 相对；arrive home late 很晚到家，与孩子们 arrive home early 形成对照。" />

<NcePair en="At night, the children always do their homework." zh="晚上，孩子们总是做作业。" ex="**at night** 在夜里，介词用 at（at noon、at + 钟点同理）；always 置于实义动词 do 之前。本句正是课首问句 When do the children do their homework? 的答案。" />

<NcePair en="Then they go to bed." zh="然后他们就上床睡觉了。" ex="**go to bed** 上床就寝，bed 前不加冠词，强调“就寝”这一习惯而非床铺本身；**then** 然后，连接先后动作。" />

<NcePair en="Mr. Sawyer usually reads his newspaper, but sometimes he and his wife watch television." zh="索耶先生通常会读报纸，但有时候他和他妻子也会一起看电视。" ex="**but** 转折；**sometimes** 可置于句首；主语 he and his wife 是复数，动词还原形 watch；**watch television** 看电视，television 前不加 the。" />
