# 第 15-16 课：Your Passports, Please.

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

Your passports please.<br><span class="zh">请出示你们的护照。</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。</span>

Is there a problem with the Customs officer?<br><span class="zh">海关官员有什么问题吗？</span>

Are you Swedish?<br><span class="zh">你是瑞典人吗？</span>

No we are not.<br><span class="zh">不，我们不是。</span>

We are Danish.<br><span class="zh">我们是丹麦人。</span>

Are your friends Danish too?<br><span class="zh">你的朋友们也是丹麦人吗？</span>

No  they aren't.<br><span class="zh">不，他们不是。</span>

They are Norwegian.<br><span class="zh">他们是挪威人。</span>

Your passports please.<br><span class="zh">请出示你们的护照。</span>

Here they are.<br><span class="zh">给您。</span>

Are these your cases?<br><span class="zh">这些是你的箱子吗？</span>

No they aren't.<br><span class="zh">不，它们不是。</span>

Our cases are brown.<br><span class="zh">我们的箱子是棕色的。</span>

Here they are.<br><span class="zh">给您。</span>

Are you tourists?<br><span class="zh">你们是游客吗？</span>

Yes we are.<br><span class="zh">是的，我们是。</span>

Are your friends tourists too?<br><span class="zh">你们的朋友也是游客吗？</span>

Yes they are.<br><span class="zh">是的，他们也是游客。</span>

That's fine.<br><span class="zh">好了。/ 没问题了。</span>

Thank you very much.<br><span class="zh">非常感谢你们。</span>
