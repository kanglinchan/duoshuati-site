Why didn't Caroline recognize the customer straight away?<br><span class="zh">为什么卡罗琳没有立刻认出那位顾客？</span>

Who served you, sir?<br><span class="zh">先生，是谁接待您的？</span>

The lady who is standing behind the counter.<br><span class="zh">是站在柜台后面的那位女士。</span>

Which books did you buy?<br><span class="zh">您买了哪些书？</span>

The books which are on the counter.<br><span class="zh">就是放在柜台上的那些书。</span>

Did you serve this gentleman half an hour ago, Caroline?<br><span class="zh">卡罗琳，半小时前你接待过这位先生吗？</span>

He says he's the man who bought these books.<br><span class="zh">他说他就是买这些书的人。</span>

I can't remember.<br><span class="zh">我记不起来了。</span>

The man who I served was wearing a hat.<br><span class="zh">我接待过的那位男士戴着帽子。</span>

Have you got a hat, sir?<br><span class="zh">先生，您有帽子吗？</span>

Yes, I have.<br><span class="zh">是的，我有。</span>

Would you put it on, please?<br><span class="zh">请您戴上它好吗？</span>

All right.<br><span class="zh">好的。</span>

Is this the man that you served, Caroline?<br><span class="zh">卡罗琳，这就是你接待过的那位男士吗？</span>

Yes.<br><span class="zh">是的。</span>

I recognize him now.<br><span class="zh">我现在认出他来了。</span>
