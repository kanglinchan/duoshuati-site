# 第 111-112 课：The Most Expensive Model

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

The most expensive model<br><span class="zh">最昂贵的型号</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。</span>

Can Mr. Frith buy the television on instalments? How does it work?<br><span class="zh">弗里斯先生可以分期付款购买这台电视机吗？具体是怎么付款的？</span>

I like this television very much.<br><span class="zh">我非常喜欢这台电视机。</span>

How much does it cost?<br><span class="zh">这需要多少钱？</span>

It's the most expensive model in the shop.<br><span class="zh">这是店里最贵的型号。</span>

It costs five hundred pounds.<br><span class="zh">它的价格是五百英镑。</span>

That's too expensive for us.<br><span class="zh">那对我们来说太贵了。</span>

We can't afford all that money.<br><span class="zh">我们负担不起那么多钱。</span>

This model's less expensive than that one.<br><span class="zh">这个型号比那个便宜。</span>

It's only three hundred pounds.<br><span class="zh">它只要三百英镑。</span>

But, of course, it's not as good as the expensive one.<br><span class="zh">但是，当然了，它没有那个昂贵的模型好。</span>

I don't like this model.<br><span class="zh">我不喜欢这个型号。</span>

The other model's more expensive, but it's worth the money.<br><span class="zh">另一个型号虽然更贵，但它的价格确实物有所值。</span>

Can we buy it on instalments?<br><span class="zh">我们可以分期付款购买吗？</span>

Of course.<br><span class="zh">当然可以。</span>

You can pay a deposit of thirty pounds,<br><span class="zh">你可以先支付30英镑的定金，</span>

and then fourteen pounds a month for three years.<br><span class="zh">然后每月支付14英镑，共分三年。</span>

Do you like it, dear?<br><span class="zh">你喜欢吗，亲爱的？</span>

I certainly do, but I don't like the price.<br><span class="zh">我当然喜欢，但我不喜欢这个价格。</span>

You always want the best, but we can't afford it.<br><span class="zh">你总是想要最好的，但我们负担不起。</span>

Sometimes you think you're a millionaire!<br><span class="zh">有时候你真觉得自己是个百万富翁啊！</span>

Millionaires don't buy things on instalments!<br><span class="zh">百万富翁才不会分期付款买东西呢！</span>
