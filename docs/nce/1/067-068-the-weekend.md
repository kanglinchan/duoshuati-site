# 第 67-68 课：The Weekend

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

The weekend<br><span class="zh">周末</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听录音，然后回答这个问题：</span>

What are the Johnsons going to do at the weekend?<br><span class="zh">约翰逊一家周末打算做什么？</span>

Hello. Were you at the butcher's?<br><span class="zh">你好。你去过肉店吗？</span>

Yes, I was.<br><span class="zh">是的，我去了。</span>

Were you at the butcher's, too?<br><span class="zh">你也去肉店了吗？</span>

No, I wasn't.<br><span class="zh">没有，我没去。</span>

I was at the greengrocer's.<br><span class="zh">没有，我去的是蔬菜店。</span>

How's Jimmy today?<br><span class="zh">吉米今天怎么样？</span>

He's very well, thank you.<br><span class="zh">他很好，谢谢。</span>

Was he absent from school last week?<br><span class="zh">他上周缺课了吗？</span>

Yes, he was.<br><span class="zh">是的，他缺课了。</span>

He was absent on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.<br><span class="zh">他周一、周二、周三和周四都缺席了。</span>

How are you all keeping?<br><span class="zh">大家最近怎么样？过得还好吗？</span>

Very well, thank you.<br><span class="zh">好的，谢谢您。</span>

We're going to spend three days in the country!<br><span class="zh">我们打算在乡下待三天！</span>

We're going to stay at my mother's for the weekend.<br><span class="zh">我们这个周末将住在妈妈家。</span>

Friday, Saturday and Sunday in the country!<br><span class="zh">周五、周六和周日都在乡下！</span>

Aren't you lucky!<br><span class="zh">你真幸运啊！</span>
