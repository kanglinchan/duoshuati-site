# 第 119-120 课：A True Story

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

A true story<br><span class="zh">一个真实的故事</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。</span>

Who called out to the thieves in the dark?<br><span class="zh">是谁在黑暗中向那些小偷喊叫的？</span>

Do you like stories?<br><span class="zh">你喜欢故事吗？</span>

I want to tell you a true story.<br><span class="zh">我想告诉你一个真实的故事。</span>

It happened to a friend of mine a year ago.<br><span class="zh">这件事发生在我一个朋友身上，是在一年前。</span>

While my friend, George, was reading in bed,<br><span class="zh">当我的朋友乔治在床上读书的时候，</span>

two thieves climbed into his kitchen.<br><span class="zh">两个小偷爬进了他的厨房。</span>

After they had entered the house,<br><span class="zh">他们进入房子后，</span>

they went into the dining room.<br><span class="zh">他们走进了餐厅。</span>

It was very dark, so they turned on a torch.<br><span class="zh">天非常黑，于是他们打开了一个手电筒。</span>

Suddenly, they heard a voice behind them.<br><span class="zh">突然，他们听到身后传来一个声音。</span>

'What's up? What's up?' someone called.<br><span class="zh">“怎么了？出什么事了？”有人喊道。</span>

The thieves dropped the torch and ran away as quickly as they could.<br><span class="zh">小偷们扔掉了手电筒，然后飞快地逃跑了。</span>

George heard the noise and came downstairs quickly.<br><span class="zh">乔治听到了那个声音，立刻快步下楼去了。</span>

He turned on the light, but he couldn't see anyone.<br><span class="zh">他打开了灯，却看不到任何人。</span>

The thieves had already gone.<br><span class="zh">小偷们已经逃走了。</span>

But George's parrot, Henry, was still there.<br><span class="zh">但是乔治的鹦鹉亨利还在那里。</span>

What's up, George? he called.<br><span class="zh">“怎么了，乔治？”他问道。</span>

'Nothing, Henry,' George said and smiled.<br><span class="zh">“没什么，亨利。”乔治微笑着说。</span>

'Go back to sleep.'<br><span class="zh">回去睡觉吧。</span>
