# 第 75-76 课：Uncomfortable Shoes

> [新概念英语 · 第一册 英语初阶](/nce/ "返回目录")

Uncomfortable shoes<br><span class="zh">不舒服的鞋子</span>

Listen to the tape then answer this question.<br><span class="zh">先听这段录音，然后再回答这个问题。</span>

What's wrong with the fashionable shoes?<br><span class="zh">这些时髦的鞋子有什么问题？</span>

Do you have any shoes like these?<br><span class="zh">你们有类似的鞋子吗？</span>

What size?<br><span class="zh">什么尺寸？</span>

Size five.<br><span class="zh">尺码五。</span>

What colour?<br><span class="zh">什么颜色？</span>

Black.<br><span class="zh">黑色。</span>

I'm sorry.<br><span class="zh">对不起。</span>

We don't have any.<br><span class="zh">我们没有（这样的鞋子）。</span>

But my sister bought this pair last month.<br><span class="zh">但我姐姐上个月买了这双鞋。</span>

Did she buy them here?<br><span class="zh">她是在这里买的吗？</span>

No, she bought them in the U. S.<br><span class="zh">不，她是在美国买的。</span>

We had some shoes like those a month ago, but we don't have any now.<br><span class="zh">我们一个月前还有一些那样的鞋子，但现在一只都没有了。</span>

Can you get a pair for me, please?<br><span class="zh">能帮我弄一双吗？</span>

I'm afraid that I can't.<br><span class="zh">恐怕不行。</span>

They were in fashion last year and the year before last.<br><span class="zh">它们在去年和前年都很流行。</span>

But they're not in fashion this year.<br><span class="zh">但它们今年已经过时了（不再流行了）。</span>

These shoes are in fashion now.<br><span class="zh">这双鞋现在非常流行。</span>

They look very uncomfortable.<br><span class="zh">这双鞋看起来很不舒服。</span>

They are very uncomfortable.<br><span class="zh">这双鞋确实很不舒服。</span>

But women always wear uncomfortable shoes!<br><span class="zh">但是女性总是穿着不舒服的鞋子啊！</span>
