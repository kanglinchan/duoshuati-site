# 第 46 课：Expensive and Uncomfortable

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What did the man in this story do?<br><span class="zh">这个故事里的男人做了什么？</span>

When a plane from London arrived at Sydney airport,<br><span class="zh">当一架从伦敦起飞的飞机抵达悉尼机场时，</span>

workers began to unload a number of wooden boxes which contained clothing.<br><span class="zh">工人们开始卸下许多装有衣物的木箱。</span>

No one could account for the fact that one of the boxes was extremely heavy.<br><span class="zh">没有人能够解释为什么其中一个箱子会异常地重。</span>

It suddenly occurred to one of the workers to open up the box.<br><span class="zh">突然，其中一个工人想到应该打开那个箱子。</span>

He was astonished at what he found.<br><span class="zh">他对自己的发现感到非常惊讶。</span>

A man was lying in the box on top of a pile of woollen goods.<br><span class="zh">一个男人躺在箱子里，躺在一堆羊毛制品的上面。</span>

He was so surprised at being discovered that he did not even try to run away.<br><span class="zh">他发现自己被发现了，惊讶得连逃跑的念头都没有。</span>

After he was arrested,<br><span class="zh">在他被逮捕之后，</span>

the man admitted hiding in the box before the plane left London.<br><span class="zh">那名男子承认在飞机离开伦敦之前躲在了那个箱子里。</span>

He had had a long and uncomfortable trip,<br><span class="zh">他经历了一段漫长而令人不适的旅程。</span>

for he had been confined to the wooden box for over eighteen hours.<br><span class="zh">因为他已经被关在那个木箱里超过十八个小时了。</span>

The man was ordered to pay ￡3, 500 for the cost of the trip.<br><span class="zh">这名男子被责令支付3,500英镑的旅费。</span>

The normal price of a ticket is ￡2, 000!<br><span class="zh">而一张普通机票的价格才2,000英镑！</span>
