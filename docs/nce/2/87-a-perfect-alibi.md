# 第 87 课：A Perfect Alibi

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What was wrong with the man's story?<br><span class="zh">这个男人的故事有什么问题吗？</span>

'At the time the murder was committed, I was travelling on the 8 o'clock train to London,' said the man.<br><span class="zh">“谋杀案发生的时候，我正在乘坐早上8点的火车前往伦敦，”那名男子说道。</span>

'Do you always catch such an early train?' asked the inspector.<br><span class="zh">“你总是这么早赶火车吗？”检查员问道。</span>

'Of course I do,' answered the man.<br><span class="zh">“当然了，”那个男人回答道。</span>

'I must be at work at 10 o'clock.<br><span class="zh">我必须在10点钟去上班。</span>

My employer will confirm that I was there on time.'<br><span class="zh">我的雇主会证实我确实准时到达了。</span>

'Would a later train get you to work on time?' asked the inspector.<br><span class="zh">“晚一点的火车能让你准时到达工作地点吗？”检查员问道。</span>

'I suppose it would, but I never catch a later train.'<br><span class="zh">“我想应该是这样的，但我从来都赶不上晚点的火车。”</span>

'At what time did you arrive at the station?'<br><span class="zh">你什么时候到达车站的？</span>

'At ten to eight. I bought a paper and waited for the train.'<br><span class="zh">八点十分的时候，我买了一张报纸，然后等待火车的到来。</span>

'And you didn't notice anything unusual?'<br><span class="zh">“那你没有注意到任何异常的情况吗？”</span>

'Of course not.'<br><span class="zh">“当然不会。”</span>

'I suggest,' said the inspector,' that you are not telling the truth.<br><span class="zh">“我认为，”检查员说，“你并没有说实话。”</span>

I suggest that you did not catch the 8 o'clock train,<br><span class="zh">我认为你可能没有赶上8点的火车。</span>

but that you caught the 8.25 which would still get you to work on time.<br><span class="zh">不过你赶上了8点25分的班车，这样仍然可以准时到达工作地点。</span>

You see, on the morning of the murder, the 8 o'clock train did not run at all.<br><span class="zh">你知道吗，在谋杀案发生的那个早晨，8点的火车根本就没有运行。</span>

It broke down at Ferngreen station and was taken off the line.'<br><span class="zh">它在费恩格林车站发生了故障，随后被从铁路上撤下（即不再允许列车在该站停靠或通过）。</span>
