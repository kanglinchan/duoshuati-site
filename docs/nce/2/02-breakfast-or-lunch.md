# 第 2 课：Breakfast or Lunch

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why was the writer's aunt surprised?<br><span class="zh">为什么作者的阿姨会感到惊讶呢？</span>

It was Sunday.<br><span class="zh">那是星期天。</span>

I never get up early on Sundays.<br><span class="zh">我从来不在周日早起。</span>

I sometimes stay in bed until lunchtime.<br><span class="zh">有时候我会一直躺在床上，直到午餐时间才起床。</span>

Last Sunday I got up very late.<br><span class="zh">上周日，我起得很晚。</span>

I looked out of the window.<br><span class="zh">我望向了窗外。</span>

It was dark outside.<br><span class="zh">外面一片漆黑。</span>

'What a day!' I thought.<br><span class="zh">“真是美好的一天啊！”我想。</span>

'It's raining again.'<br><span class="zh">“又下雨了。”</span>

Just then, the telephone rang.<br><span class="zh">就在这时，电话响了。</span>

It was my aunt Lucy.<br><span class="zh">那是我的阿姨露西。</span>

'I've just arrived by train,' she said.<br><span class="zh">“我刚乘火车到达的，”她说。</span>

'I'm coming to see you.'<br><span class="zh">“我来看你了。”</span>

'But I'm still having breakfast,' I said.<br><span class="zh">“但我还在吃早餐呢，”我说。</span>

'What are you doing?' she asked.<br><span class="zh">“你在做什么？”她问。</span>

'I'm having breakfast,' I repeated.<br><span class="zh">“我在吃早餐，”我重复道。</span>

'Dear me,' she said.<br><span class="zh">“天哪，”她说。</span>

'Do you always get up so late?<br><span class="zh">“你总是起这么晚吗？</span>

It's one o'clock!'<br><span class="zh">现在都一点钟了！”</span>
