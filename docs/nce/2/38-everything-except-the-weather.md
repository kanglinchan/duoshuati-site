<NcePair en="about the weather," zh="抱怨天气" ex="**complain about** 抱怨某事,后接抱怨的对象;若要说出向谁抱怨,用 complain to sb,名词形式是 complaint。" />

<NcePair en="He acted as if he had never lived in England before." zh="他的举止就好像他以前从未在英国生活过一样。" ex="**as if**(=as though)“好像、仿佛”,从句用过去完成时 had never lived,表示与过去事实相反的虚拟;同类如 He talks as if he were not tired。" />

<NcePair en="In the end, it was more than he could bear." zh="最终，这超出了他的承受能力。" ex="**more than one can** “超出某人所能……”,全句即 he could not bear it,忍无可忍;同类如 more than I can say 难以言表。" />
