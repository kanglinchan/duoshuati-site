# 第 41 课：Do You Call That a Hat

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What kind of shopping does the writer enjoy, do you think?<br><span class="zh">你觉得这位作家喜欢哪种类型的购物呢？</span>

'Do you call that a hat?' I said to my wife.<br><span class="zh">“你那叫帽子吗？”我对我的妻子说道。</span>

'You needn't be so rude about it,'<br><span class="zh">“你没必要这么无礼啊。”</span>

my wife answered as she looked at herself in the mirror.<br><span class="zh">我妻子一边看着镜子中的自己，一边回答了我。</span>

I sat down on one of those modern chairs with holes in it and waited.<br><span class="zh">我坐在一把带洞的现代椅子上等着。</span>

We had been in the hat shop for half an hour and my wife was still in front of the mirror.<br><span class="zh">我们在那家帽子店已经待了半个小时了，而我妻子仍然站在镜子前。</span>

'We mustn't buy things we don't need,' I remarked suddenly.<br><span class="zh">“我们不应该购买那些我们不需要的东西，”我突然说道。</span>

I regretted saying it almost at once.<br><span class="zh">我几乎立刻就后悔说了那句话。</span>

'You needn't have said that,' my wife answered.<br><span class="zh">“你其实没必要那么说，”我的妻子回答道。</span>

'I needn't remind you of that terrible tie you bought yesterday.'<br><span class="zh">“我其实没必要再提醒你昨天你买的那条糟糕的领带了。”</span>

'I find it beautiful,' I said. 'A man can never have too many ties.'<br><span class="zh">“我觉得它很漂亮，”我说，“一个男人有多少领带也不嫌多。”</span>

'And a woman can't have too many hats,' she answered.<br><span class="zh">“而女人的帽子也是有多少都不嫌多，”她回答道。</span>

Ten minutes later we walked out of the shop together.<br><span class="zh">十分钟后，我们一起走出了那家商店。</span>

My wife was wearing a hat that looked like a lighthouse!<br><span class="zh">我妻子戴着一顶看起来像灯塔的帽子！</span>
