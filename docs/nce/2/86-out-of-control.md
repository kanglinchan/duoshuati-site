# 第 86 课：Out of Control

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What was the danger?<br><span class="zh">危险是什么？</span>

As the man tried to swing the speedboat round, the steering wheel came away in his hands.<br><span class="zh">当那个男人试图调转快艇时，方向盘突然从他手中脱落了。</span>

He waved desperately to his companion, who had been water skiing for the last fifteen minutes.<br><span class="zh">他拼命地向他的同伴挥手，而他的同伴已经滑水滑了十五分钟了。</span>

Both men had hardly had time to realize what was happening when they were thrown violently into the sea.<br><span class="zh">两人几乎还没来得及反应发生了什么，就被猛地抛进了海里。</span>

The speedboat had struck a buoy, but it continued to move very quickly across the water.<br><span class="zh">快艇撞到了一个浮标上，但仍然以极快的速度继续在水面上行驶。</span>

Both men had just begun to swim towards the shore,<br><span class="zh">两人刚刚开始朝岸边游去，</span>

when they noticed with dismay that the speedboat was moving in a circle.<br><span class="zh">就惊恐地发现快艇正在打转。</span>

It now came straight towards them at tremendous speed.<br><span class="zh">现在，快艇正以惊人的速度朝他们直冲过来。</span>

In less than a minute, it roared past them only a few feet away.<br><span class="zh">不到一分钟的时间，它就从他们身边呼啸而过，距离他们只有几英尺远。</span>

After it had passed, they swam on as quickly as they could because they knew that the boat would soon return.<br><span class="zh">快艇驶过后，他们拼命地继续游动，因为他们知道快艇很快就会再次回来。</span>

They had just had enough time to swim out of danger when the boat again completed a circle.<br><span class="zh">他们刚好来得及游出危险区域，这时快艇又转了一圈。</span>

On this occasion, however, it had slowed down considerably.<br><span class="zh">然而这一次，它的速度明显慢了很多。</span>

The petrol had nearly all been used up.<br><span class="zh">汽油几乎已经用完了。</span>

Before long, the noise dropped completely and the boat began to drift gently across the water.<br><span class="zh">没过多久，引擎声完全消失了，小船开始在水面上缓缓漂动。</span>
