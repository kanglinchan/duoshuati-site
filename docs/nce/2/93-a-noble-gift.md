# 第 93 课：A Noble Gift

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Where was the Statue of Liberty made?<br><span class="zh">自由女神像是在哪里制造的？</span>

One of the most famous monuments in the world, the Statue of Liberty,<br><span class="zh">自由女神像是世界最著名的纪念碑之一。</span>

was presented to the USA in the nineteenth century by the people of France.<br><span class="zh">这座雕像是在十九世纪由法国人民赠送给美国的。</span>

The great statue, which was designed by the sculptor Auguste Bartholdi, took ten years to complete.<br><span class="zh">这座宏伟的雕像由雕塑家奥古斯特·巴托尔迪设计，耗时十年才完成。</span>

The actual figure was made of copper supported by a metal framework which had been especially constructed by Eiffel.<br><span class="zh">雕像主体由铜制成，由埃菲尔特别设计的金属框架支撑。</span>

Before it could be transported to the United States,<br><span class="zh">在它被运往美国之前，</span>

a site had to be found for it and a pedestal had to be built.<br><span class="zh">必须为它找到一个安放地点，并建造一个基座。</span>

The site chosen was an island at the entrance of New York Harbour.<br><span class="zh">所选的位置是一座位于纽约港入口处的岛屿。</span>

By 1884, a statue which was 151 feet tall had been erected in Paris.<br><span class="zh">到1884年，一座151英尺高的雕像已在巴黎建成。</span>

The following year, it was taken to pieces and sent to America.<br><span class="zh">第二年，它被拆解后运往美国。</span>

By the end of October 1886,<br><span class="zh">到1886年10月底，</span>

the statue had been put together again and it was officially presented to the American people by Bartholdi.<br><span class="zh">到1886年10月底，雕像已重新组装完毕，由巴托尔迪正式赠予美国人民。</span>

Ever since then, the great monument has been a symbol of liberty<br><span class="zh">从那时起，这座宏伟的纪念碑就成为了自由的象征。</span>

for the millions of people who have passed through New York Harbour to make their homes in America.<br><span class="zh">对于数百万通过纽约港来到美国安家落户的人们来说……</span>
