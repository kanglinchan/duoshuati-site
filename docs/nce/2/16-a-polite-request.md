# 第 16 课：A Polite Request

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What was the polite request?<br><span class="zh">那个礼貌的请求是什么？</span>

If you park your car in the wrong place,<br><span class="zh">如果你把车停在了错误的地方，</span>

a traffic policeman will soon find it.<br><span class="zh">交通警察很快就会发现它。</span>

You will be very lucky if he lets you go without a ticket.<br><span class="zh">如果你能不被开罚单就放你走，那你就非常幸运了。</span>

However, this does not always happen.<br><span class="zh">不过，这种情况并不总是发生。</span>

Traffic police are sometimes very polite.<br><span class="zh">有时候，交通警察会非常礼貌。</span>

During a holiday in Sweden,<br><span class="zh">在瑞典的一个假期里，</span>

I found this note on my car:<br><span class="zh">我在我的车上发现了一张纸条：</span>

'sir, we welcome you to our city. This is a No Parking area.<br><span class="zh">“先生，欢迎您来到我们的城市。这里是禁止停车区。</span>

You will enjoy your stay here if you pay attention to our street signs. This note is only a reminder.'<br><span class="zh">如果您注意看街道上的标志，您的停留会非常愉快。这张纸条只是为了提醒您而已。”</span>

If you receive a request like this, you cannot fail to obey it!<br><span class="zh">如果你收到这样的请求，你一定会遵守的！</span>
