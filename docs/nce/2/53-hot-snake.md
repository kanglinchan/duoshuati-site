# 第 53 课：Hot Snake

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What caused the fire?<br><span class="zh">是什么导致了这场火灾？</span>

At last firemen have put out a big forest fire in California.<br><span class="zh">消防员们终于扑灭了加利福尼亚州的那场大规模森林火灾。</span>

Since then, they have been trying to find out how the fire began.<br><span class="zh">从那以后，他们一直在试图查明火灾的起因。</span>

Forest fires are often caused by broken glass or by cigarette ends which people carelessly throw away.<br><span class="zh">森林火灾通常是由碎玻璃或人们随意丢弃的烟头引起的。</span>

Yesterday the firemen examined the ground carefully,<br><span class="zh">昨天，消防员们仔细地检查了那片地面。</span>

but were not able to find any broken glass.<br><span class="zh">但他们没有发现任何碎玻璃。</span>

They were also quite sure that a cigarette end did not start the fire.<br><span class="zh">他们也非常确定，火灾并不是由烟头引起的。</span>

This morning, however, a firemen accidentally discovered the cause.<br><span class="zh">然而，今天早上，一名消防员意外地发现了火灾的原因。</span>

He noticed the remains of a snake which was wound round the electric wires of a 16, 000-volt power line.<br><span class="zh">他发现了一条蛇的残骸，那条蛇缠绕在一条16,000伏高压电线上。</span>

In this way, he was able to solve the mystery.<br><span class="zh">通过这种方式，他终于解开了这个谜团。</span>

The explanation was simple but very unusual.<br><span class="zh">这个解释很简单，但却非常不寻常。</span>

A bird had snatched up the snake from the ground and then dropped it on to the wires.<br><span class="zh">一只鸟从地上抓起了那条蛇，然后把它扔到了电线上。</span>

The snake then wound itself round the wires.<br><span class="zh">然后，那条蛇缠住了那些电线。</span>

When it did so, it sent sparks down to the ground and these immediately started a fire.<br><span class="zh">当它这样做时，火花溅到了地上，立刻引发了火灾。</span>
