# 第 79 课：By Air

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did the plane turn back?<br><span class="zh">飞机为什么返航了？</span>

I used to travel by air a great deal when I was a boy.<br><span class="zh">我小时候经常坐飞机旅行。</span>

My parents used to live in South America and I used to fly there from Europe in the holidays.<br><span class="zh">我的父母曾住在南美洲，假期里我常从欧洲飞往他们那里。</span>

A flight attendant would take charge of me and I never had an unpleasant experience.<br><span class="zh">每次都有空乘人员来照顾我，而我从未有过任何不愉快的经历。</span>

I am used to travelling by air and only on one occasion have I ever felt frightened.<br><span class="zh">我习惯乘坐飞机出行，只有一次感到过害怕。</span>

After taking off, we were flying low over the city and slowly gaining height,<br><span class="zh">起飞后，我们低空飞过城市，然后逐渐开始上升。</span>

when the plane suddenly turned round and flew back to the airport.<br><span class="zh">突然，飞机掉头飞回了机场。</span>

While we were waiting to land, a flight attendant told us to keep calm<br><span class="zh">在我们等待降落的期间，一位空乘人员让我们保持冷静。</span>

and to get off the plane quietly as soon as it had touched down.<br><span class="zh">并且要在飞机一降落就立刻悄悄地离开飞机。</span>

Everybody on board was worried and we were curious to find out what had happened.<br><span class="zh">机上的每个人都很担心，我们都好奇想知道发生了什么事。</span>

Later we learnt that there was a very important person on board.<br><span class="zh">后来我们得知，机上有一位非常重要的人物。</span>

The police had been told that a bomb had been planted on the plane.<br><span class="zh">警方接到消息称，飞机上被安置了一枚炸弹。</span>

After we had landed, the plane was searched thoroughly.<br><span class="zh">我们降落之后，飞机被彻底搜查了一遍。</span>

Fortunately, nothing was found and five hours later we were able to take off again.<br><span class="zh">幸运的是，什么也没找到，五小时后我们又能起飞了。</span>
