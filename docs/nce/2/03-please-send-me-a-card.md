# 第 3 课：Please Send Me a Card

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How many cards did the writer send?<br><span class="zh">作者寄了多少张明信片？</span>

Postcards always spoil my holidays.<br><span class="zh">明信片总是把我的假期搅得一团糟。</span>

Last summer, I went to Italy.<br><span class="zh">去年夏天，我去了意大利。</span>

I visited museums and sat in public gardens.<br><span class="zh">我参观了博物馆，还坐在公共花园里休息。</span>

A friendly waiter taught me a few words of Italian.<br><span class="zh">一位友好的服务员教了我几句意大利语。</span>

Then he lent me a book.<br><span class="zh">之后他还借给我一本书。</span>

I read a few lines, but I did not understand a word.<br><span class="zh">我读了几行，但一个词儿也不懂。</span>

Everyday I thought about postcards.<br><span class="zh">我每天都想着明信片的事。</span>

My holidays passed quickly,<br><span class="zh">假期很快就过去了，</span>

but I did not send cards to my friends.<br><span class="zh">但我却没有给朋友们寄任何卡片。</span>

On the last day I made a big decision.<br><span class="zh">在最后一天，我下了一个大决心。</span>

I got up early and bought thirty-seven cards.<br><span class="zh">我早早起床，买了三十七张明信片。</span>

I spent the whole day in my room,<br><span class="zh">我一整天都待在房间里，</span>

but I did not write a single card!<br><span class="zh">却一张卡片都没写！</span>
