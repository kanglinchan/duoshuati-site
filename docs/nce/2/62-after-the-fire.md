# 第 62 课：After the Fire

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What was the danger to the villages after the fire?<br><span class="zh">火灾过后，村庄面临着哪些危险？</span>

Firemen had been fighting the forest fire for nearly three weeks before they could get it under control.<br><span class="zh">消防员们与这场森林火灾抗争了近三周，才终于将其控制住。</span>

A short time before, great trees had covered the countryside for miles around.<br><span class="zh">就在不久之前，这片乡村的周围几英里范围内，都长满了高大的树木。</span>

Now, smoke still rose up from the warm ground over the desolate hills.<br><span class="zh">此刻，烟雾仍从那片荒凉山丘上温暖的地面中升起。</span>

Winter was coming on and the hills threatened the surrounding villages with destruction,<br><span class="zh">冬天即将来临，那些山丘威胁着周围的村庄，随时可能将其摧毁。</span>

for heavy rain would not only wash away the soil but would cause serious floods as well.<br><span class="zh">因为大雨不仅会冲走土壤，还会引发严重的洪水。</span>

When the fire had at last been put out,<br><span class="zh">当大火终于被扑灭后，</span>

the forest authorities ordered several tons of a special type of grass-seed which would grow quickly.<br><span class="zh">森林管理部门订购了几吨一种特殊的草籽，这种草籽生长速度非常快。</span>

The seed was sprayed over the ground in huge quantities by aeroplanes.<br><span class="zh">这些种子被飞机大量喷洒到地面上。</span>

The planes had been planting seed for nearly a month when it began to rain.<br><span class="zh">这些飞机已经在进行播种作业近一个月了，就在这时，雨开始下了。</span>

By then, however, in many places the grass had already taken root.<br><span class="zh">然而，到那时为止，草已经在许多地方生根发芽了。</span>

In place of the great trees which had been growing there for centuries,<br><span class="zh">取而代之的是，那些已经生长了几个世纪的参天大树不见了。</span>

patches of green had begun to appear in the blackened soil.<br><span class="zh">在烧焦的土壤中，开始出现了一小块块绿色的斑点。</span>
