# 第 51 课：Reward for Virtue

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did Hugh's diet not work?<br><span class="zh">为什么休的节食计划没有效果呢？</span>

My friend, Hugh, has always been fat,<br><span class="zh">我的朋友休一直都很胖。</span>

but things got so bad recently that he decided to go on a diet.<br><span class="zh">但最近情况变得非常糟糕，以至于他决定开始节食。</span>

He began his diet a week ago.<br><span class="zh">他一周前开始节食了。</span>

First of all, he wrote out a long list of all the foods which were forbidden.<br><span class="zh">首先，他列出了一份长长的清单，上面列出了所有被禁止食用的食物。</span>

The list included most of the things Hugh loves:<br><span class="zh">这份清单上列出了休最喜欢的大多数东西。</span>

butter, potatoes, rice, beer, milk, chocolate, and sweets.<br><span class="zh">黄油、土豆、米饭、啤酒、牛奶、巧克力和糖果。</span>

Yesterday I paid him a visit.<br><span class="zh">昨天我去拜访了他。</span>

I rang the bell and was not surprised to see that Hugh was still as fat as ever.<br><span class="zh">我按响了门铃，看到休还是和以前一样胖，并没有感到惊讶。</span>

He led me into his room and hurriedly hid a large parcel under his desk.<br><span class="zh">他把我带进了他的房间，然后匆忙地把一个大包裹藏在了桌子下面。</span>

It was obvious that he was very embarrassed.<br><span class="zh">很明显，他感到非常尴尬。</span>

When I asked him what he was doing,<br><span class="zh">当我问他正在做什么时，</span>

he smiled guiltily and then put the parcel on the desk.<br><span class="zh">他内疚地笑了笑，然后把包裹放在了桌子上。</span>

He explained that his diet was so strict that he had to reward himself occasionally.<br><span class="zh">他解释说，他的饮食控制非常严格，因此偶尔需要奖励自己一下。</span>

Then he showed me the contents of the parcel.<br><span class="zh">然后他向我展示了包裹里的物品。</span>

It contained five large bars of chocolate and three bags of sweets!<br><span class="zh">里面是五块大巧克力和三袋糖果！</span>
