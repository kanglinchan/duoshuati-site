# 第 63 课：She Was Not Amused

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did Jenny want to leave the wedding reception?<br><span class="zh">为什么珍妮想离开婚礼现场呢？</span>

Jeremy Hampden has a large circle of friends and is very popular at parties.<br><span class="zh">杰里米·汉普登拥有很多朋友，在聚会上非常受欢迎。</span>

Everybody admires him for his great sense of humour--everybody, that is, except his six-year-old daughter, Jenny.<br><span class="zh">大家都很欣赏他的幽默感——当然，除了他六岁的女儿珍妮之外。</span>

Recently, one of Jeremy's closest friends asked him to make a speech at a wedding reception.<br><span class="zh">最近，杰里米的一位密友请求他在一场婚礼招待会上发表演讲。</span>

This is the sort of thing that Jeremy loves.<br><span class="zh">这正是杰里米喜欢做的事情。</span>

He prepared the speech carefully and went to the wedding with Jenny.<br><span class="zh">他仔细准备了演讲稿，然后和珍妮一起参加了婚礼。</span>

He had included a large number of funny stories in the speech and, of course, it was a great success.<br><span class="zh">他在演讲中穿插了许多有趣的故事，当然，这次演讲非常成功。</span>

As soon as he had finished Jenny told him she wanted to go home.<br><span class="zh">他刚说完，珍妮就告诉他她想回家了。</span>

Jeremy was a little disappointed by this but he did as his daughter asked.<br><span class="zh">杰里米对此感到有些失望，但他还是按照女儿的要求做了。</span>

On the way home, he asked Jenny if she had enjoyed the speech.<br><span class="zh">在回家的路上，他问珍妮是否喜欢那场演讲。</span>

To his surprise she said she hadn't.<br><span class="zh">令他惊讶的是，她说她并不喜欢。</span>

Jeremy asked her why this was so and she told him that she did not like to see so many people laughing at him!<br><span class="zh">杰里米问她为什么，她告诉他她不喜欢看到那么多人嘲笑他！</span>
