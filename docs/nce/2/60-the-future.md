# 第 60 课：The Future

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Does what Madam Bellinsky said come true?<br><span class="zh">贝尔林斯基夫人说的话会成真吗？</span>

At a village fair, I decided to visit a fortune-teller called Madam Bellinsky.<br><span class="zh">在一个乡村集市上，我决定去拜访一位名叫贝林斯基夫人的算命师。</span>

I went into her tent and she told me to sit down.<br><span class="zh">我走进她的帐篷，她让我坐下。</span>

After I had given her some money, she looked into a crystal ball and said:<br><span class="zh">在我给了她一些钱之后，她看了看那个水晶球，然后说道：</span>

'A relation of yours is coming to see you.<br><span class="zh">你的一个亲戚要来看你。</span>

She will be arriving this evening and intends to stay for a few days.<br><span class="zh">她将于今晚到达，并打算在这里待上几天。</span>

The moment you leave this tent, you will get a big surprise.<br><span class="zh">你一离开这个帐篷，就会大吃一惊。</span>

A woman you know well will rush towards you.<br><span class="zh">一个你非常熟悉的女人会冲向你。</span>

She will speak to you and then she will lead you away from this place. That is all.'<br><span class="zh">她会和你说话，然后带你离开这个地方。就这些。</span>

As soon as I went outside, I forgot all about Madam Bellinsky because my wife hurried towards me.<br><span class="zh">我一走到外面，就把贝林斯基夫人忘得一干二净了，因为我的妻子正朝我匆匆走来。</span>

'Where have you been hiding?' she asked impatiently.<br><span class="zh">你跑哪儿去了？她不耐烦地问道。</span>

'Your sister will be here in less than an hour and we must be at the station to meet her. We are late already.'<br><span class="zh">你妹妹将在一小时内到达，我们必须赶到车站去接她。我们已经迟到了。</span>

As she walked away, I followed her out of the fair.<br><span class="zh">她转身走开时，我便跟着她离开了集市。</span>
