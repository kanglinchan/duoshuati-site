# 第 80 课：The Crystal Palace

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How many people visited the Great Exhibition of 1851?<br><span class="zh">有多少人参观了1851年的万国博览会？</span>

Perhaps the most extraordinary building of the 19th century was the Crystal Palace<br><span class="zh">也许19世纪最非凡的建筑当属水晶宫（Crystal Palace）了。</span>

which was built in Hyde Park for the Great Exhibition of 1851.<br><span class="zh">这座建筑是为1851年的万国博览会而在海德公园建造的。</span>

The Crystal Palace was different from all other buildings in the world, for it was made of iron and glass.<br><span class="zh">水晶宫与世界上所有的其他建筑都不同，因为它是由铁和玻璃建造的。</span>

It was one of the biggest buildings of all time and a lot of people from many countries came to see it.<br><span class="zh">这是有史以来最大的建筑之一，许多来自不同国家的人都前来参观它。</span>

A great many goods were sent to the exhibition from various parts of the world.<br><span class="zh">大量商品从世界各地被运送到这次展览上。</span>

There was also a great deal of machinery on display.<br><span class="zh">展会上还展示了许多机械设备。</span>

The most wonderful piece of machinery on show was Nasmyth's steam hammer.<br><span class="zh">展会上最令人惊叹的机械设备是纳斯米思（Nasmyth）制造的蒸汽锤。</span>

Though in those days travelling was not as easy as it is today,<br><span class="zh">尽管在那个时候旅行并不像现在这样方便，</span>

steam boats carried thousands of visitors across the Channel from Europe.<br><span class="zh">蒸汽船将成千上万的游客从欧洲运送到英吉利海峡对岸。</span>

On arriving in England, they were, taken to the Crystal Palace by train.<br><span class="zh">抵达英格兰后，他们乘火车被送往水晶宫（Crystal Palace）。</span>

There were six million visitors in all,<br><span class="zh">总共有六百万名游客。</span>

and the profits from the exhibition were used to build museums and colleges.<br><span class="zh">展览的收入被用来建设博物馆和学院。</span>

Later, the Crystal Palace was moved to South London.<br><span class="zh">后来，水晶宫被搬到了伦敦南部。</span>

It remained one of the most famous buildings in the world until it was burnt down in 1936.<br><span class="zh">它一直是世界上最著名的建筑之一，直到 1936 年被烧毁。</span>
