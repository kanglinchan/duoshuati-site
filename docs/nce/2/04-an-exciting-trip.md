# 第 4 课：An Exciting Trip

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why is Tim finding this trip exciting?<br><span class="zh">为什么蒂姆觉得这次旅行如此令人兴奋呢？</span>

I have just received a letter from my brother, Tim.<br><span class="zh">我刚刚收到了我哥哥蒂姆的一封信。</span>

He is in Australia.<br><span class="zh">他在澳大利亚。</span>

He has been there for six months.<br><span class="zh">他已经在那里待了六个月了。</span>

Tim is an engineer.<br><span class="zh">蒂姆是一名工程师。</span>

He is working for a big firm and he has already visited<br><span class="zh">他在一家大公司工作，并且已经去过澳大利亚的许多不同地方。</span>

a great number of different places in Australia.<br><span class="zh"></span>

He has just bought an Australian car and has gone to Alice Springs,<br><span class="zh">他刚买了一辆澳大利亚产的汽车，然后去了爱丽丝泉（Alice Springs）。</span>

a small town in the centre of Australia.<br><span class="zh">澳大利亚中部的一个小镇。</span>

He will soon visit Darwin.<br><span class="zh">他很快将会去达尔文。</span>

From there, he will fly to Perth.<br><span class="zh">从那里，他将飞往珀斯。</span>

My brother has never been abroad before,<br><span class="zh">我哥哥以前从未出过国。</span>

so he is finding this trip very exciting.<br><span class="zh">所以他觉得这次旅行非常令人兴奋。</span>
