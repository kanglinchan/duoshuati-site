# 第 66 课：Sweet as Honey

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What was 'sweet as honey' and why?<br><span class="zh">什么东西“甜如蜜”？为什么这么说呢？</span>

In 1963 a Lancaster bomber crashed on Wallis Island,<br><span class="zh">1963年，一架兰开斯特轰炸机在沃利斯岛上坠毁了。</span>

a remote place in the South Pacific, a long way west of Samoa.<br><span class="zh">一个位于南太平洋的偏远之地，在萨摩亚以西很远的地方。</span>

The plane wasn't too badly damaged,<br><span class="zh">飞机的损坏程度并不严重。</span>

but over the years the crash was forgotten and the wreck remained undisturbed.<br><span class="zh">然而，随着时间的推移，这场坠机事故被人们遗忘了，飞机残骸也一直无人打扰。</span>

Then in 1989, twenty-six years after the crash, the plane was accidentally rediscovered in an aerial survey of the island.<br><span class="zh">然后在1989年，也就是飞机失事26年后，这架飞机在一次对该岛屿的航空勘测中被意外重新发现了。</span>

By this time, a Lancaster bomber in reasonable condition was rare and worth rescuing.<br><span class="zh">到了这个时候，一架状况良好的兰开斯特轰炸机已经非常稀少，值得抢救。</span>

The French authorities had the plane packaged and moved in parts back to France.<br><span class="zh">法国当局将飞机打包，分部件运回了法国。</span>

Now a group of enthusiasts are going to have the plane restored.<br><span class="zh">现在，一群爱好者正着手修复这架飞机。</span>

It has four Rolls-Royce Merlin engines but the group will need to have only three of them rebuilt.<br><span class="zh">这架飞机有四台罗尔斯-罗伊斯“梅林”发动机，但这群人只需要重建其中三台。</span>

Imagine their surprise and delight when they broke open the packing cases<br><span class="zh">想象一下，当他们打开包装箱时，他们的惊讶和喜悦之情吧！</span>

and found that the fourth engine was sweet as honey--still in perfect condition.<br><span class="zh">结果发现第四台发动机“甜如蜜”——状况依然完好。</span>

A colony of bees had turned the engine into a hive and it was totally preserved in beeswax!<br><span class="zh">一群蜜蜂将那台发动机改造成了一个蜂巢，并且整个发动机都被完美地保存在了蜂蜡之中！</span>
