# 第 56 课：Faster Than Sound

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How fast did the winning car go?<br><span class="zh">那辆获胜的汽车速度有多快？</span>

Once a year, a race is held for old cars.<br><span class="zh">每年都会举办一场专为老爷车设计的比赛。</span>

A lot of cars entered for this race last year and there was a great deal of excitement just before it began.<br><span class="zh">去年有很多车报名参加了这场比赛，比赛开始前气氛十分热烈。</span>

One of the most handsome cars was a Rolls-Royce Silver Ghost.<br><span class="zh">其中最引人注目的汽车之一是劳斯莱斯银魅（Rolls-Royce Silver Ghost）。</span>

The most unusual car was a Benz which had only three wheels.<br><span class="zh">最不寻常的汽车是一辆只有三个轮子的奔驰（Benz）汽车。</span>

Built in 1885, it was the oldest car taking part.<br><span class="zh">这辆汽车建于1885年，是参赛车辆中历史最悠久的一辆。</span>

After a great many loud explosions, the race began.<br><span class="zh">在一阵巨大的轰鸣声后，比赛开始了。</span>

Many of the cars broke down on the course and some drivers spent more time under their cars than in them!<br><span class="zh">许多汽车在赛道上发生了故障；有些司机花在检查汽车故障上的时间，甚至比他们实际驾驶汽车的时间还要长！</span>

A few cars, however, completed the race.<br><span class="zh">然而，还是有几辆车完成了比赛。</span>

The winning car reached a speed of forty miles an hour -- much faster than any of its rivals.<br><span class="zh">获胜的汽车达到了每小时40英里的速度——这比它的所有竞争对手都要快得多。</span>

It sped downhill at the end of the race and its driver had a lot of trouble trying to stop it.<br><span class="zh">比赛结束时，这辆车飞速冲下山坡，驾驶员费了好大劲才把它停下来。</span>

The race gave everyone a great deal of pleasure.<br><span class="zh">这场比赛给大家带来了极大的乐趣。</span>

It was very different from modern car races but no less exciting.<br><span class="zh">这与现代的汽车比赛非常不同，但同样令人兴奋。</span>
