The Greenwood Boys are a group of pop singers.<br><span class="zh">The Greenwood Boys是一支流行歌手组合。</span>

At present, they are visiting all parts of the country.<br><span class="zh">目前，他们正在全国巡回演出。</span>

They will be arriving here tomorrow.<br><span class="zh">他们明天会到达这里。</span>

They will be coming by train and most of the young people in the town will be meeting them at the station.<br><span class="zh">他们将乘火车前来，镇上的大多数年轻人都会在火车站迎接他们。</span>

Tomorrow evening they will be singing at the Workers' Club.<br><span class="zh">明晚他们将在工人俱乐部演出。</span>

The Greenwood Boys will be staying for five days.<br><span class="zh">格林伍德男孩们将在此逗留五天。</span>

During this time, they will give five performances.<br><span class="zh">在这段时间里，他们将进行五场演出。</span>

As usual, the police will have a difficult time.<br><span class="zh">像往常一样，警方会遇到很大的困难。</span>

They will be trying to keep order.<br><span class="zh">他们会努力维持秩序。</span>
