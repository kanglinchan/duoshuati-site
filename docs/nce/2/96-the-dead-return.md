# 第 96 课：The Dead Return

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What happens to the lanterns at the end of the festival?<br><span class="zh">节庆结束时，那些灯笼会怎么样呢？</span>

A Festival for the Dead is held once a year in Japan.<br><span class="zh">在日本，每年都会举行一次“亡灵节”（Festival for the Dead）。</span>

This festival is a cheerful occasion,<br><span class="zh">这个节日是一个充满欢乐的场合。</span>

for on this day, the dead are said to return to their homes and they are welcomed by the living.<br><span class="zh">因为据说在这一天，死者会回到他们的家中，而生者会迎接他们。</span>

As they are expected to be hungry after their long journey, food is laid out for them.<br><span class="zh">由于他们在长途旅行后很可能会感到饥饿，所以已经为他们准备好了食物。</span>

Specially-made lanterns are hung outside each house to help the dead to find their way.<br><span class="zh">每户人家的外面都挂有特制的灯笼，目的是帮助逝者找到回家的路。</span>

All night long, people dance and sing.<br><span class="zh">整晚，人们都在跳舞和唱歌。</span>

In the early morning the food that had been laid out for the dead<br><span class="zh">清晨时分，那些原本为死者准备的食物……</span>

is thrown into a river or into the sea as it is considered unlucky for anyone living to eat it.<br><span class="zh">这些食物会被扔进河流或海洋中，因为人们认为活人吃了会不吉利。</span>

In towns that are near the sea, the tiny lanterns which had been hung in the streets the night before,<br><span class="zh">在那些靠近海边的小镇上，前一晚被挂在街上的那些小灯笼……</span>

are placed into the water when the festival is over.<br><span class="zh">这些灯笼会在节日结束后被放入水中。</span>

Thousands of lanterns slowly drift out to sea guiding the dead on their return journey to the other world.<br><span class="zh">成千上万的灯笼缓缓被放流入海，为逝者指引返回另一个世界的道路。</span>

This is a moving spectacle, for crowds of people<br><span class="zh">这是一场令人感动的场面，成群的人们都聚集在一起观看。</span>

stand on the shore watching the lanterns drifting away until they can be seen no more.<br><span class="zh">站在岸边，看着那些灯笼渐渐远去，直到它们完全消失在视线之外。</span>
