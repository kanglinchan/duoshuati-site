<NcePair en="A Festival for the Dead is held once a year in Japan." zh="在日本，每年都会举行一次“亡灵节”（Festival for the Dead）。" />

<NcePair en="This festival is a cheerful occasion," zh="这个节日是一个充满欢乐的场合。" />

<NcePair en="for on this day, the dead are said to return to their homes and they are welcomed by the living." zh="因为据说在这一天，死者会回到他们的家中，而生者会迎接他们。" />

<NcePair en="As they are expected to be hungry after their long journey, food is laid out for them." zh="由于他们在长途旅行后很可能会感到饥饿，所以已经为他们准备好了食物。" />

<NcePair en="Specially-made lanterns are hung outside each house to help the dead to find their way." zh="每户人家的外面都挂有特制的灯笼，目的是帮助逝者找到回家的路。" />

<NcePair en="All night long, people dance and sing." zh="整晚，人们都在跳舞和唱歌。" />

<NcePair en="In the early morning the food that had been laid out for the dead" zh="清晨时分，那些原本为死者准备的食物……" />

<NcePair en="is thrown into a river or into the sea as it is considered unlucky for anyone living to eat it." zh="这些食物会被扔进河流或海洋中，因为人们认为活人吃了会不吉利。" />

<NcePair en="In towns that are near the sea, the tiny lanterns which had been hung in the streets the night before," zh="在那些靠近海边的小镇上，前一晚被挂在街上的那些小灯笼……" />

<NcePair en="are placed into the water when the festival is over." zh="这些灯笼会在节日结束后被放入水中。" />

<NcePair en="Thousands of lanterns slowly drift out to sea guiding the dead on their return journey to the other world." zh="成千上万的灯笼缓缓被放流入海，为逝者指引返回另一个世界的道路。" />

<NcePair en="This is a moving spectacle, for crowds of people" zh="这是一场令人感动的场面，成群的人们都聚集在一起观看。" />

<NcePair en="stand on the shore watching the lanterns drifting away until they can be seen no more." zh="站在岸边，看着那些灯笼渐渐远去，直到它们完全消失在视线之外。" />
