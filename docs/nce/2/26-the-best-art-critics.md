# 第 26 课：The Best Art Critics

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Who is the student's best critic?<br><span class="zh">谁是这位学生最好的评论家？</span>

I am an art student and I paint a lot of pictures.<br><span class="zh">我是一名艺术学生，经常画很多画。</span>

Many people pretend that they understand modern art.<br><span class="zh">很多人假装自己理解现代艺术。</span>

They always tell you what a picture is 'about'.<br><span class="zh">当然，很多画其实什么也不是。</span>

Of course, many pictures are not 'about' anything.<br><span class="zh">当然，很多图片其实并没有明确的主题或意义（即它们“并不表达任何特定的内容”）。</span>

They are just pretty patterns.<br><span class="zh">它们只不过是一些漂亮的图案罢了。</span>

We like them in the same way that we like pretty curtain material.<br><span class="zh">我们喜欢它们的方式，就如同我们喜欢漂亮的窗帘面料一样。</span>

I think that young children often appreciate modern pictures better than anyone else.<br><span class="zh">我认为，小孩子往往比任何人都更懂得欣赏现代画。</span>

They notice more.<br><span class="zh">他们看到的更多。</span>

My sister is only seven,<br><span class="zh">我妹妹才七岁。</span>

but she always tells me whether my pictures are good or not.<br><span class="zh">但她总会告诉我我的画好不好。</span>

She came into my room yesterday.<br><span class="zh">她昨天进了我的房间。</span>

'What are you doing?' she asked.<br><span class="zh">“你在做什么？”她问。</span>

'I'm hanging this picture on the wall,' I answered. 'It's a new one. Do you like it?'<br><span class="zh">“我在把这幅画挂在墙上。”我回答说，“这是一幅新画。你喜欢吗？”</span>

She looked at it critically for a moment.<br><span class="zh">她挑剔地看了一会儿。</span>

'It's all right,' she said, 'but isn't it upside down?'<br><span class="zh">“还行吧，”她说，“不过它不是倒挂了吗？”</span>

I looked at it again.<br><span class="zh">我又看了一遍。</span>

She was right! It was!<br><span class="zh">她说得对！确实是这样！</span>
