<NcePair en="I am an art student and I paint a lot of pictures." zh="我是一名艺术学生，经常画很多画。" />

<NcePair en="Many people pretend that they understand modern art." zh="很多人假装自己理解现代艺术。" />

<NcePair en="They always tell you what a picture is 'about'." zh="当然，很多画其实什么也不是。" />

<NcePair en="Of course, many pictures are not 'about' anything." zh="当然，很多图片其实并没有明确的主题或意义（即它们“并不表达任何特定的内容”）。" />

<NcePair en="They are just pretty patterns." zh="它们只不过是一些漂亮的图案罢了。" />

<NcePair en="We like them in the same way that we like pretty curtain material." zh="我们喜欢它们的方式，就如同我们喜欢漂亮的窗帘面料一样。" />

<NcePair en="I think that young children often appreciate modern pictures better than anyone else." zh="我认为，小孩子往往比任何人都更懂得欣赏现代画。" />

<NcePair en="They notice more." zh="他们看到的更多。" />

<NcePair en="My sister is only seven," zh="我妹妹才七岁。" />

<NcePair en="but she always tells me whether my pictures are good or not." zh="但她总会告诉我我的画好不好。" />

<NcePair en="She came into my room yesterday." zh="她昨天进了我的房间。" />

<NcePair en="'What are you doing?' she asked." zh="“你在做什么？”她问。" />

<NcePair en="'I'm hanging this picture on the wall,' I answered. 'It's a new one. Do you like it?'" zh="“我在把这幅画挂在墙上。”我回答说，“这是一幅新画。你喜欢吗？”" />

<NcePair en="She looked at it critically for a moment." zh="她挑剔地看了一会儿。" />

<NcePair en="'It's all right,' she said, 'but isn't it upside down?'" zh="“还行吧，”她说，“不过它不是倒挂了吗？”" />

<NcePair en="I looked at it again." zh="我又看了一遍。" />

<NcePair en="She was right! It was!" zh="她说得对！确实是这样！" />
