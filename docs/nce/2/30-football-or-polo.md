# 第 30 课：Football or Polo

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What happened to the man in the boat?<br><span class="zh">船上的那个男人怎么了？</span>

The Wayle is a small river that cuts across the park near my home.<br><span class="zh">韦尔河是一条小河，流经我家附近的公园。</span>

I like sitting by the Wayle on fine afternoons.<br><span class="zh">我喜欢在天气好的下午坐在韦尔河畔。</span>

It was warm last Sunday, so I went and sat on the river bank as usual.<br><span class="zh">上周日天气很暖和，所以我像往常一样去河边坐着。</span>

Some children were playing games on the bank and there were some people rowing on the river.<br><span class="zh">有些孩子在河岸上玩游戏，还有一些人在河上划船。</span>

Suddenly, one of the children kicked a ball very hard and it went towards a passing boat.<br><span class="zh">突然，其中一个孩子用力踢了一个球，球飞向了一艘正在经过的船。</span>

Some people on the bank called out to the man in the boat, but he did not hear them.<br><span class="zh">河岸上的一些人向船上的男人喊话，但他没有听到。</span>

The ball struck him so hard that he nearly fell into the water.<br><span class="zh">球重重地打在他身上，差点把他撞进水里。</span>

I turned to look at the children, but there weren't any in sight: they had all run away!<br><span class="zh">我转身看向那些孩子，但他们已经不见了——他们都跑掉了！</span>

The man laughed when he realized what had happened.<br><span class="zh">当那个男人意识到发生了什么时，他笑了起来。</span>

He called out to the children and threw the ball back to the bank.<br><span class="zh">他朝孩子们喊了一声，然后把球扔回了岸上。</span>
