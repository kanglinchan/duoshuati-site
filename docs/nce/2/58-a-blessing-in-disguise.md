# 第 58 课：A Blessing in Disguise

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why does the vicar refuse to cut down the tree?<br><span class="zh">为什么牧师拒绝砍倒那棵树呢？</span>

The tiny village of Frinley is said to possess a 'cursed tree'.<br><span class="zh">据说，弗林利这个小村庄里有一棵“被诅咒的树”。</span>

Because the tree was mentioned in a newspaper,<br><span class="zh">因为这棵树在报纸上被提及后，</span>

the number of visitors to Frinley has now increased.<br><span class="zh">来弗林利参观的游客数量现在已经增加了。</span>

The tree was planted near the church fifty years ago, but it is only in recent years that it has gained an evil reputation.<br><span class="zh">这棵树是在五十年前种在教堂附近的，但直到最近几年，它才获得了“邪恶”的名声。</span>

It is said that if anyone touches the tree, he will have bad luck; if he picks a leaf, he will die.<br><span class="zh">据说，如果有人触碰这棵树，就会遭遇厄运；如果他摘下一片叶子，就会死亡。</span>

Many villagers believe that the tree has already claimed a number of victims.<br><span class="zh">许多村民认为，这棵树已经害死了不少人。</span>

The vicar has been asked to have the tree cut down, but so far he has refused.<br><span class="zh">牧师被要求把那棵树砍掉，但他至今仍然拒绝这样做。</span>

He has pointed out that the tree is a useful source of income,<br><span class="zh">他指出，这棵树能带来可观的收入，</span>

as tourists have been coming from all parts of the country to see it.<br><span class="zh">因为游客们从全国各地纷纷前来参观它。</span>

In spite of all that has been said,<br><span class="zh">尽管有这么多传言，</span>

the tourists have been picking leaves and cutting their names on the tree-trunk.<br><span class="zh">游客们一直在摘树叶，并在树干上刻下自己的名字。</span>

So far, not one of them has been struck down by sudden death!<br><span class="zh">到目前为止，他们当中还没有一个人突然死亡！</span>
