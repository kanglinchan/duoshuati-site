# 第 9 课：A Cold Welcome

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What does 'a cold welcome' refer to?<br><span class="zh">“冷遇”（a cold welcome）指的是一种不热情、冷漠或甚至带有敌意的接待方式。</span>

On Wednesday evening, we went to the Town Hall.<br><span class="zh">周三晚上，我们去了市政厅。</span>

It was the last day of the year<br><span class="zh">那是这一年中的最后一天。</span>

and a large crowd of people had gathered under the Town Hall clock.<br><span class="zh">一大群人聚集在市政厅的钟楼下方。</span>

It would strike twelve in twenty minutes' time.<br><span class="zh">二十分钟后，钟声就会敲响十二下。</span>

Fifteen minutes passed and then,<br><span class="zh">十五分钟过去了，然后……</span>

at five to twelve, the clock stopped.<br><span class="zh">在十二点差五分的时候，钟停了。</span>

The big minute hand did not move.<br><span class="zh">那个大型的分针一动也没有动。</span>

We waited and waited,<br><span class="zh">我们等啊等啊，</span>

but nothing happened.<br><span class="zh">但是什么事情都没有发生。</span>

Suddenly someone shouted.<br><span class="zh">突然，有人喊了一声。</span>

'It's two minutes past twelve!<br><span class="zh">已经十二点过两分了！</span>

The clock has stopped!'<br><span class="zh">“时钟停了！”</span>

I looked at my watch.<br><span class="zh">我看了看我的手表。</span>

It was true.<br><span class="zh">确实如此。</span>

The big clock refused to welcome the New Year.<br><span class="zh">那座大钟拒绝迎接新年。</span>

At that moment, everybody began to laugh and sing.<br><span class="zh">就在那一刻，所有人都开始大笑起来，还唱起了歌。</span>
