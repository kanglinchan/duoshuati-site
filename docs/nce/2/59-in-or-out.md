# 第 59 课：In or Out

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did Rex run away?<br><span class="zh">为什么雷克斯会逃跑呢？</span>

Our dog, Rex, used to sit outside our front gate and bark.<br><span class="zh">我们的狗雷克斯过去常常坐在前门外吠叫。</span>

Every time he wanted to come into the garden he would bark until someone opened the gate.<br><span class="zh">每次他想进入花园时，都会不停地吠叫，直到有人打开大门。</span>

As the neighbours complained of the noise, my husband spent weeks training him to press his paw on the latch to let himself in.<br><span class="zh">由于邻居们抱怨噪音太大，我丈夫花了好几个星期训练它用爪子按门闩，好让它自己进来。</span>

Rex soon became an expert at opening the gate.<br><span class="zh">雷克斯很快就掌握了开门的技巧。</span>

However, when I was going out shopping last week, I noticed him in the garden near the gate.<br><span class="zh">然而，上周我去购物的时候，我在大门附近的花园里看到了他。</span>

This time he was barking so that someone would let him out!<br><span class="zh">这次他大声吠叫，就是为了有人能放他出去！</span>

Since then, he has developed another bad habit.<br><span class="zh">从那以后，他又养成了一个坏习惯。</span>

As soon as he opens the gate from the outside, he comes into the garden and waits until the gate shuts.<br><span class="zh">他一从外面打开大门，就走进花园，然后等待大门关上。</span>

Then he sits and barks until someone lets him out.<br><span class="zh">然后他就坐着，不停地叫，直到有人放他出去。</span>

After this he immediately lets himself in and begins barking again.<br><span class="zh">之后，他立刻自己进了门，又开始吠叫起来。</span>

Yesterday my husband removed the gate and Rex got so annoyed we have not seen him since.<br><span class="zh">昨天我丈夫把门拆了，雷克斯气坏了，从那以后我们就再也没见过它了。</span>
