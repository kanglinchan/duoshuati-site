# 第 24 课：It Could be Worse

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Had the writer's money been stolen?<br><span class="zh">那位作家的钱被偷了吗？</span>

I entered the hotel manager's office and sat down.<br><span class="zh">我走进酒店经理的办公室，坐了下来。</span>

I had just lost ￡50 and I felt very upset.<br><span class="zh">我刚刚损失了50英镑，感到非常沮丧。</span>

'I left the money in my room,' I said, 'and it's not there now.  '<br><span class="zh">“我把钱放在房间里了，但现在钱不见了。”我说。</span>

The manager was sympathetic, but he could do nothing.<br><span class="zh">经理很同情我，但他无能为力。</span>

'Everyone's losing money these days,' he said.<br><span class="zh">“如今大家都在丢钱，”他说。</span>

He started to complain about this wicked world but was interrupted by a knock at the door.<br><span class="zh">他开始抱怨这个邪恶的世界，但被一阵敲门声打断了。</span>

A girl came in and put an envelope on his desk.<br><span class="zh">一个女孩走了进来，把一个信封放在他的桌子上。</span>

It contained ￡50.<br><span class="zh">信封里装着50英镑。</span>

'I found this outside this gentleman's room,' she said.<br><span class="zh">“我在这位先生的房间外面发现了这个。”她说。</span>

'Well,' I said to the manager,'there is still some honesty in this world!'<br><span class="zh">“嗯，”我对经理说，“这个世界上还是有一些诚实的人的！”</span>
