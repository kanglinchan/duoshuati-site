# 第 40 课：Food and Talk

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Was Mrs. Rumbold a good companion at dinner?<br><span class="zh">兰博尔德夫人在晚餐时是个不错的交谈伙伴吗？</span>

Last week at a dinner party,<br><span class="zh">上周在一个晚宴上，</span>

the hostess asked me to sit next to Mrs. Rumbold.<br><span class="zh">女主人让我坐在兰博尔德夫人旁边。</span>

Mrs. Rumbold was a large,<br><span class="zh">兰博尔德太太身材高大，</span>

unsmiling lady in a tight black dress.<br><span class="zh">那位面无笑容的女士穿着一件紧身的黑色连衣裙。</span>

She did not even look up when I took my seat beside her.<br><span class="zh">当我坐在她旁边时，她甚至都没有抬头看我一眼。</span>

Her eyes were fixed on her plate and in a short time,<br><span class="zh">她的目光紧紧盯着自己的盘子，不一会儿……</span>

she was busy eating.<br><span class="zh">她当时正忙着吃饭。</span>

I tried to make conversation.<br><span class="zh">我试着和她交谈。</span>

'A new play is coming to "The Globe" soon,'<br><span class="zh">一部新剧很快将在“The Globe”剧院上演。</span>

I said. 'Will you be seeing it?'<br><span class="zh">我说：“您会去看吗？”</span>

'No,' she answered.<br><span class="zh">“不，”她回答道。</span>

'Will you be spending your holidays abroad this year?' I asked.<br><span class="zh">“今年你打算在国外度过假期吗？”我问道。</span>

'No,' she answered.<br><span class="zh">“不，”她回答。</span>

'Will you be staying in England?' I asked.<br><span class="zh">“那你会留在英国吗？”我又问。</span>

'No,' she answered.<br><span class="zh">“不，”她回答。</span>

In despair, I asked her whether she was enjoying her dinner.<br><span class="zh">我有些绝望，便问她晚餐吃得怎么样。</span>

'Young man,' she answered,<br><span class="zh">“年轻人，”她说道，</span>

'if you ate more and talked less, we would both enjoy our dinner!'<br><span class="zh">“如果你多吃点、少说点话，我们俩的晚餐都会更愉快！”</span>
