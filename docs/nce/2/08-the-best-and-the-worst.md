# 第 8 课：The Best and the Worst

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why is Joe's garden<br><span class="zh">为什么乔的花园是</span>

the most beautiful one in the town?<br><span class="zh">镇上最漂亮的那一个？</span>

Joe Sanders has the most beautiful garden<br><span class="zh">乔·桑德斯拥有最美丽的花园。</span>

in our town.<br><span class="zh">在我们镇上。</span>

Nearly everybody enters for<br><span class="zh">几乎每个人都参加</span>

'The Nicest Garden Competition' each year,<br><span class="zh">“最美丽花园竞赛”</span>

but Joe wins every time.<br><span class="zh">但是乔每次都能赢。</span>

Bill Frith's garden is larger than Joe's.<br><span class="zh">比尔·弗里斯的花园比乔的大。</span>

Bill works harder than Joe<br><span class="zh">比尔比乔工作更努力。</span>

and grows more flowers and vegetables,<br><span class="zh">并且能种植出更多的鲜花和蔬菜。</span>

but Joe's garden is more interesting.<br><span class="zh">不过乔的花园更有趣。</span>

He has made neat paths<br><span class="zh">他修了整洁的小路</span>

and has built a wooden bridge over a pool.<br><span class="zh">还在池塘上建了一座木桥。</span>

I like gardens too,<br><span class="zh">我也喜欢花园。</span>

but I do not like hard work.<br><span class="zh">但我不喜欢辛苦的工作。</span>

Every year<br><span class="zh">每年</span>

I enter for the garden competition too,<br><span class="zh">我也会参加花园比赛。</span>

and I always win a little prize<br><span class="zh">而且我总能赢得一个小奖品。</span>

for the worst garden in the town!<br><span class="zh">因为镇上最差的花园！</span>
