# 第 42 课：Not Very Musical

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What happened when the snake charmer began to play jazz?<br><span class="zh">当那个耍蛇的人开始演奏爵士乐时，发生了什么？</span>

As we had had a long walk through one of the markets of old Delhi,<br><span class="zh">我们沿着旧德里的一个市场走了很长一段路后，</span>

we stopped at a square to have a rest.<br><span class="zh">我们在一个广场上停下来休息。</span>

After a time, we noticed a snake charmer with<br><span class="zh">过了一段时间，我们注意到有一个耍蛇的人……</span>

two large baskets at the other side of the square,<br><span class="zh">在广场的另一边，带着两个大篮子。</span>

so we went to have a look at him.<br><span class="zh">于是我们便走过去看看他。</span>

As soon as he saw us,<br><span class="zh">他一看到我们，</span>

he picked up a long pipe which was covered with coins and opened one of the baskets.<br><span class="zh">他拿起一根缀满硬币的长笛，然后打开了其中一个篮子。</span>

When he began to play a tune,<br><span class="zh">当他开始演奏一首曲子时，</span>

we had our first glimpse of the snake.<br><span class="zh">我们第一次看到了那条蛇。</span>

It rose out of the basket and began to follow the movements of the pipe.<br><span class="zh">它从篮子里探出身来，开始随着笛子的摆动而舞动。</span>

We were very much surprised when the snake charmer<br><span class="zh">当那个耍蛇的人突然开始演奏爵士乐和现代流行歌曲时，我们非常惊讶。</span>

suddenly began to play jazz and modern pop songs.<br><span class="zh">他突然开始演奏爵士乐和现代流行歌曲。</span>

The snake, however, continued to 'dance' slowly.<br><span class="zh">然而，那条蛇仍然继续缓慢地“跳舞”。</span>

It obviously could not tell the difference between Indian music and jazz!<br><span class="zh">显然，它根本分不清印度音乐和爵士乐的区别！</span>
