# 第 29 课：Taxi

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Does Captain Fawcett think any trip is too dangerous?<br><span class="zh">福塞特船长认为有哪次旅行是过于危险的吗？</span>

Captain Ben Fawcett has bought an unusual taxi and has begun a new service.<br><span class="zh">本·福塞特船长买了一辆与众不同的出租车，并开始提供一项新的服务。</span>

The 'taxi' is a small Swiss aeroplane called a 'Pilatus Porter'.<br><span class="zh">这架“出租车”其实是一架小型瑞士飞机，被称为“Pilatus Porter”。</span>

This wonderful plane can carry seven passengers.<br><span class="zh">这架出色的飞机可以搭载七名乘客。</span>

The most surprising thing about it, however, is that it can land anywhere: on snow, water, or even on a ploughed field.<br><span class="zh">然而，最令人惊讶的是：它可以在任何地方降落——无论是雪地上、水面上，还是已经翻耕过的田地里。</span>

Captain Fawcett's first passenger was a doctor who flew from Birmingham to a lonely village in the Welsh mountains.<br><span class="zh">福塞特船长的第一位乘客是一位医生，他乘坐飞机从伯明翰飞往威尔士山区的一个偏远村庄。</span>

Since then, Captain Fawcett has flown passengers to many unusual places.<br><span class="zh">从那以后，福塞特船长已经把乘客送到了许多不寻常的地方。</span>

Once he landed on the roof of a block of flats and on another occasion, he landed in a deserted car park.<br><span class="zh">有一次，他把飞机降落在一栋公寓楼的屋顶上；还有一次，他降落在一个废弃的停车场里。</span>

Captain Fawcett has just refused a strange request from a businessman.<br><span class="zh">福塞特船长刚刚拒绝了一位商人的奇怪请求。</span>

The man wanted to fly to Rockall, a lonely island in the Atlantic Ocean,<br><span class="zh">这个人想要飞往罗卡尔岛（Rockall），那是大西洋上一座孤寂的岛屿。</span>

but Captain Fawcett did not take him because the trip was too dangerous.<br><span class="zh">但是福塞特船长没有带他去，因为这次旅行太危险了。</span>
