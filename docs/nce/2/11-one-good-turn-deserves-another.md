# 第 11 课：One Good Turn Deserves Another

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Who paid for Tony's dinner?<br><span class="zh">谁付了托尼晚餐的钱？</span>

I was having dinner at a restaurant when Tony Steele came in.<br><span class="zh">我正在餐厅吃晚饭，这时托尼·斯蒂尔走了进来。</span>

Tony worked in a lawyer's office years ago,<br><span class="zh">托尼几年前在一家律师事务所工作过。</span>

but he is now working at a bank.<br><span class="zh">但他现在在一家银行工作。</span>

He gets a good salary,<br><span class="zh">他的薪水很高。</span>

but he always borrows money from his friends and never pays it back.<br><span class="zh">但他总是向朋友借钱，却从不还钱。</span>

Tony saw me and came and sat at the same table.<br><span class="zh">托尼看到我后，走过来坐在了我的旁边。</span>

He has never borrowed money from me.<br><span class="zh">他从来没有向我借过钱。</span>

While he was eating, I asked him to lend me twenty pounds.<br><span class="zh">他正在吃饭的时候，我请他借给我二十英镑。</span>

To my surprise, he gave me the money immediately.<br><span class="zh">令我惊讶的是，他立刻就把钱给了我。</span>

'I have never borrowed any money from you,'<br><span class="zh">“我从来没有向你借过任何钱。”</span>

Tony said, 'so now you can pay for my dinner!'<br><span class="zh">托尼说：“现在你可以付我晚餐的钱了！”</span>
