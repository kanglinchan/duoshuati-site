# 第 10 课：Not for Jazz

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What happened to the clavichord?<br><span class="zh">那架击弦古钢琴后来怎么样了？</span>

We have an old musical instrument.<br><span class="zh">我们有一件古老的乐器。</span>

It is called a clavichord.<br><span class="zh">它叫击弦古钢琴。</span>

It was made in Germany in 1681.<br><span class="zh">它于1681年在德国制造。</span>

Our clavichord is kept in the living room.<br><span class="zh">我们的击弦古钢琴放在客厅里。</span>

It has belonged to our family for a long time.<br><span class="zh">它已经属于我们家族很长时间了。</span>

The instrument was bought by my grandfather many years ago.<br><span class="zh">这件乐器是我祖父多年前买的。</span>

Recently it was damaged by a visitor.<br><span class="zh">最近，它被一位访客损坏了。</span>

She tried to play jazz on it!<br><span class="zh">她竟然用它弹起了爵士乐！</span>

She struck the keys too hard and two of the strings were broken.<br><span class="zh">她用力过猛地敲击琴键，结果有两根琴弦断裂了。</span>

My father was shocked.<br><span class="zh">我父亲非常震惊。</span>

Now we are not allowed to touch it.<br><span class="zh">现在我们不准碰它了。</span>

It is being repaired by a friend of my father's.<br><span class="zh">我父亲的一个朋友正在修理它。</span>
