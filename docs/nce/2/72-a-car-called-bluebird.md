# 第 72 课：A Car Called Bluebird

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What mistake was made?<br><span class="zh">出了什么差错？</span>

The great racing driver, Sir Malcolm Campbell, was the first man to drive at over 300 miles per hour.<br><span class="zh">伟大的赛车手马尔科姆·坎贝尔爵士（Sir Malcolm Campbell）是第一个驾驶汽车时速超过300英里的人。</span>

He set up a new world record in September 1935 at Bonneville Salt Flats, Utah.<br><span class="zh">他在1935年9月于犹他州的博纳维尔盐滩（Bonneville Salt Flats）创造了新的世界纪录。</span>

Bluebird, the car he was driving, had been specially built for him.<br><span class="zh">他驾驶的那辆名为“Bluebird”的汽车，是专门为他定制制造的。</span>

It was over 30 feet in length and had a 2, 500-horsepower engine.<br><span class="zh">它的长度超过了30英尺（约9米），配备了一台功率为2500马力的发动机。</span>

Although Campbell reached a speed of over 304 miles per hour,<br><span class="zh">尽管坎贝尔的速度超过了每小时304英里，</span>

he had great difficulty in controlling the car because a tyre burst during the first run.<br><span class="zh">他在控制汽车时遇到了很大的困难，因为在第一次试跑中一个轮胎爆了。</span>

After his attempt, Campbell was disappointed to learn that his average speed had been 299 miles per hour.<br><span class="zh">尝试之后，坎贝尔得知自己的平均速度仅为每小时299英里，这让他感到非常失望。</span>

However, a few days later, he was told that a mistake had been made.<br><span class="zh">然而，几天后他被告知，原来出现了一个错误。</span>

His average speed had been 301 miles per hour.<br><span class="zh">他的平均速度是每小时301英里。</span>

Since that time, racing drivers have reached speeds over 600 miles an hour.<br><span class="zh">从那时起，赛车手的行驶速度已经超过了每小时600英里。</span>

Following in his father's footsteps many years later, Sir Malcolm's son, Donald, also set up a world record.<br><span class="zh">多年以后，马尔科姆爵士的儿子唐纳德追随父亲的足迹，也创造了一项世界纪录。</span>

Like his father, he was driving a car called Bluebird.<br><span class="zh">和他父亲一样，他驾驶着一辆名叫“Bluebird”的汽车。</span>
