# 第 45 课：A Clear Conscience

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How did Sam get his money back?<br><span class="zh">萨姆是怎么把钱拿回来的？</span>

The whole village soon learnt that a large sum of money had been lost.<br><span class="zh">整个村庄很快都知道有一大笔钱丢失了。</span>

Sam Benton, the local butcher,<br><span class="zh">山姆·本顿，当地的肉铺老板……</span>

had lost his wallet while taking his savings to the post office.<br><span class="zh">他在去邮局存钱的时候丢失了钱包。</span>

Sam was sure that the wallet must have been found by one of the villagers,<br><span class="zh">萨姆确信那个钱包一定是被某个村民找到的。</span>

but it was not returned to him.<br><span class="zh">但是，那个东西并没有被归还给他。</span>

Three months passed, and then one morning, Sam found his wallet outside his front door.<br><span class="zh">三个月过去了，一天早晨，山姆发现他的钱包放在自家前门外面。</span>

It had been wrapped up in newspaper and it contained half the money he had lost,<br><span class="zh">它被用报纸包了起来，里面装着他丢失的钱的一半。</span>

together with a note which said:<br><span class="zh">还附有一张纸条，上面写着：</span>

'A thief, yes, but only 50 per cent a thief!'<br><span class="zh">“他确实是个小偷，但只能算是个‘50%的小偷’罢了！”</span>

Two months later,<br><span class="zh">两个月后，</span>

some more money was sent to Sam with another note:<br><span class="zh">又有一些钱被寄给了萨姆，并附上了一张便条。</span>

'Only 25 per cent a thief now!'<br><span class="zh">“现在只有25%的人是小偷了！”</span>

In time, all Sam's money was paid back in this way.<br><span class="zh">随着时间的推移，山姆的所有钱都通过这种方式被还清了。</span>

The last note said: 'I am 100 per cent honest now!'<br><span class="zh">最后一张纸条上写着：“现在我100%是诚实的了！”</span>
