# 第 22 课：A Glass Envelope

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How did Jane receive a letter from a stranger?<br><span class="zh">简是怎么收到一封来自陌生人的信的？</span>

My daughter, Jane, never dreamed of receiving a letter from a girl of her own age in Holland.<br><span class="zh">我的女儿简，从未想过会收到来自荷兰的一个同龄女孩的信。</span>

Last year, we were travelling across the Channel and Jane put a piece of paper with her name and address on it into a bottle.<br><span class="zh">去年，我们在渡英吉利海峡时，简把一张写有她名字和地址的纸条放进了瓶子里。</span>

She threw the bottle into the sea.<br><span class="zh">她把瓶子扔进了海里。</span>

She never thought of it again, but ten months later, she received a letter from a girl in Holland.<br><span class="zh">她再也没有想起过这件事，但十个月后，她收到了一封来自荷兰女孩的信。</span>

Both girls write to each other regularly now.<br><span class="zh">现在，这两个女孩经常互相写信。</span>

However, they have decided to use the post office.<br><span class="zh">然而，她们决定用邮局寄信。</span>

Letters will cost a little more, but they will certainly travel faster.<br><span class="zh">信件的费用会稍高一些，但它们的传递速度肯定更快。</span>
