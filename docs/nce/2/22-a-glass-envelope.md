<NcePair en="My daughter, Jane, never dreamed of receiving a letter from a girl of her own age in Holland." zh="我的女儿简，从未想过会收到来自荷兰的一个同龄女孩的信。" ex="**dream of/about** 本指'梦到',与 never、little 等否定词连用即'做梦也想不到',如 I never dreamed that he would come;后接动名词;**of her own age** '与她同龄的'。" />

<NcePair en="Last year, we were travelling across the Channel and Jane put a piece of paper with her name and address on it into a bottle." zh="去年，我们在渡英吉利海峡时，简把一张写有她名字和地址的纸条放进了瓶子里。" ex="**were travelling** 过去进行时提供背景,put 是期间发生的短暂动作;paper 后接 with 复合结构(with her name and address on it)作后置修饰,'上面写有姓名和地址'。" />

<NcePair en="She threw the bottle into the sea." zh="她把瓶子扔进了海里。" ex="**throw...into...** '把……扔进……';into 强调进入水面的动态,与表静态的 in 不同。" />

<NcePair en="She never thought of it again, but ten months later, she received a letter from a girl in Holland." zh="她再也没有想起过这件事，但十个月后，她收到了一封来自荷兰女孩的信。" ex="**think of** 与 think about 都表'想',一般可互换,但表'想起、记起'(如 can't think of his name)时不能用 think about;ten months later 是叙述文常用的时点过渡。" />

<NcePair en="Both girls write to each other regularly now." zh="现在，这两个女孩经常互相写信。" ex="**both** 修饰名词可说 both girls 或 both of the girls;**each other** '互相';regularly '定期地',配一般现在时 write 表经常性行为。" />

<NcePair en="However, they have decided to use the post office." zh="然而，她们决定用邮局寄信。" ex="**decide to do** 决定做某事;现在完成时 have decided 强调决定已作出并对现在生效;however 承接上文表转折。" />

<NcePair en="Letters will cost a little more, but they will certainly travel faster." zh="信件的费用会稍高一些，但它们的传递速度肯定更快。" ex="**cost** 的主语只能是物或事,无被动语态;人作主语用 **spend**(spend...on / spend...doing);**a little** 可修饰比较级'稍微';cost a little more 与 travel faster 构成贵与快的取舍。" />
