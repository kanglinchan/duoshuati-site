# 第 81 课：Escape

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did the prisoner attack the driver?<br><span class="zh">为什么囚犯会攻击司机？</span>

When he had killed the guard, the prisoner of war quickly dragged him into the bushes.<br><span class="zh">当他杀死了那个卫兵后，这名战俘立刻把他拖进了灌木丛中。</span>

Working rapidly in the darkness, he soon changed into the dead man's clothes.<br><span class="zh">他在黑暗中迅速行动，很快就换上了那具死者的衣服。</span>

Now, dressed in a blue uniform and with a rifle over his shoulder,<br><span class="zh">现在，他穿着蓝色制服，肩上扛着一把步枪。</span>

the prisoner marched boldly up and down in front of the camp.<br><span class="zh">这名囚犯在营地前大胆地来回踱步。</span>

He could hear shouting in the camp itself.<br><span class="zh">他能听到营地里传来的喊叫声。</span>

Lights were blazing and men were running here and there: they had just discovered that a prisoner had escaped.<br><span class="zh">营地里灯火通明，人们四处奔忙——他们刚刚发现有一名囚犯逃走了。</span>

At that moment, a large black car with four officers inside it, stopped at the camp gates.<br><span class="zh">就在那一刻，一辆载有四名军官的黑色轿车停在了营地的门口。</span>

The officers got out and the prisoner stood to attention and saluted as they passed.<br><span class="zh">军官们下了车，囚犯立正敬礼，目送他们经过。</span>

When they had gone the driver of the car came towards him.<br><span class="zh">当他们离开后，那辆车的司机朝他走了过来。</span>

The man obviously wanted to talk.<br><span class="zh">显然，这个人想说话。</span>

He was rather elderly with grey hair and clear blue eyes.<br><span class="zh">他年纪较大，头发已经花白，眼睛则是清澈的蓝色。</span>

The prisoner felt sorry for him, but there was nothing else he could do.<br><span class="zh">囚犯对他感到同情，但他无能为力。</span>

As the man came near the prisoner knocked him to the ground with a sharp blow.<br><span class="zh">当那个人靠近时，囚犯用一记猛烈的打击将他打倒在地。</span>

Then, jumping into the car, he drove off as quickly as he could.<br><span class="zh">随后，他跳进车里，以最快的速度驾车逃离。</span>
