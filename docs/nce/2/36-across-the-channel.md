# 第 36 课：Across the Channel

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What is Debbie going to try to do?<br><span class="zh">黛比打算做什么呢？</span>

Debbie Hart is going to swim<br><span class="zh">黛比·哈特将要游泳</span>

across the English Channel tomorrow.<br><span class="zh">横渡英吉利海峡。</span>

She is going to set out from the French coast<br><span class="zh">她将从法国海岸出发。</span>

at five o'clock in the morning.<br><span class="zh">早上五点。</span>

Debbie is only eleven years old<br><span class="zh">黛比只有十一岁。</span>

and she hopes to set up a new world record.<br><span class="zh">她希望能够创造一项新的世界纪录。</span>

She is a strong swimmer and many people feel<br><span class="zh">她是个游泳健将，很多人都认为</span>

that she is sure to succeed.<br><span class="zh">她一定会成功。</span>

Debbie's father will set out with her<br><span class="zh">黛比的父亲会和她一起出发。</span>

in a small boat.<br><span class="zh">乘坐一艘小船。</span>

Mr. Hart has trained his daughter for years.<br><span class="zh">哈特先生多年来一直在训练他的女儿。</span>

Tomorrow he will be watching her anxiously<br><span class="zh">明天，他会焦急地注视着她。</span>

as she swims the long distance to England.<br><span class="zh">当她游向遥远的英格兰时。</span>

Debbie intends to take short rests every two hours.<br><span class="zh">黛比打算每两小时休息一会儿。</span>

She will have something to drink<br><span class="zh">她会喝点东西</span>

but she will not eat any solid food.<br><span class="zh">但她不吃任何固体食物。</span>

Most of Debbie's school friends will be<br><span class="zh">黛比的大多数同学都会</span>

waiting for her on the English coast.<br><span class="zh">在英格兰的海岸边等待着她。</span>

Among them will be Debbie's mother,<br><span class="zh">其中会包括黛比的母亲。</span>

who swam the Channel herself<br><span class="zh">她自己也曾经游过英吉利海峡</span>

when she was a girl.<br><span class="zh">当她还是个女孩的时候。</span>
