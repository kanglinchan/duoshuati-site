# 第 19 课：Sold Out

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

When will the writer see the play?<br><span class="zh">作者什么时候能看到这部剧呢？</span>

'The play may begin at any moment,' I said.<br><span class="zh">“演出随时可能开始，”我说。</span>

'It may have begun already,' Susan answered.<br><span class="zh">“可能已经开始了，”苏珊回答。</span>

I hurried to the ticket office.<br><span class="zh">我赶紧跑向售票处。</span>

'May I have two tickets please?' I asked.<br><span class="zh">“请给我两张票，好吗？”我问。</span>

'I'm sorry, we've sold out,' the girl said.<br><span class="zh">“很抱歉，票已经卖完了，”女孩说。</span>

'What a pity!' Susan exclaimed.<br><span class="zh">“真遗憾！”苏珊感叹道。</span>

Just then, a man hurried to the ticket office.<br><span class="zh">就在这时，一个男人匆匆跑到了售票处。</span>

'Can I return these two tickets?' he asked.<br><span class="zh">“我可以退掉这两张票吗？”他问道。</span>

'Certainly,' the girl said.<br><span class="zh">“当然可以，”女孩回答。</span>

I went back to the ticket office at once.<br><span class="zh">我立刻又回到了售票处。</span>

'Could I have those two tickets please?' I asked.<br><span class="zh">“请把那两张票给我，好吗？”我问。</span>

'Certainly,' the girl said,<br><span class="zh">“当然可以，”女孩说道。</span>

'but they're for next Wednesday's performance.<br><span class="zh">但这是下周三演出的票。</span>

Do you still want them?'<br><span class="zh">你还要吗？</span>

'I might as well have them,' I said sadly.<br><span class="zh">“那我就要了吧，”我沮丧地说。</span>
