# 第 70 课：Red for Danger

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How was the drunk removed from the ring?<br><span class="zh">那个醉汉是如何被从斗牛场里带走的呢？</span>

During a bullfight, a drunk suddenly wandered into the middle of the ring.<br><span class="zh">在一场斗牛比赛中，一个醉汉突然走进了斗牛场的中央。</span>

The crowd began to shout, but the drunk was unaware of the danger.<br><span class="zh">人群开始大声呼喊，但醉汉并没有意识到危险。</span>

The bull was busy with the matador at the time,<br><span class="zh">当时公牛正与斗牛士搏斗，</span>

but it suddenly caught sight of the drunk who was shouting rude remarks and waving a red cap.<br><span class="zh">突然它注意到了那个醉汉——醉汉正在大声说脏话，并挥舞着一顶红色帽子。</span>

Apparently sensitive to criticism,<br><span class="zh">显然，这头公牛对批评很敏感，</span>

the bull forgot all about the matador and charged at the drunk.<br><span class="zh">它立刻忘记了斗牛士的存在，冲向了醉汉。</span>

The crowd suddenly grew quiet.<br><span class="zh">人群顿时安静了下来。</span>

The drunk, however, seemed quite sure of himself.<br><span class="zh">然而，醉汉似乎对自己很有信心。</span>

When the bull got close to him, he clumsily stepped aside to let it pass.<br><span class="zh">当公牛靠近时，他笨拙地躲到一边，让公牛从自己身边经过。</span>

The crowd broke into cheers and the drunk bowed.<br><span class="zh">人群爆发出欢呼声，醉汉也鞠了一躬。</span>

By this time, however three men had come into the ring and they quickly dragged the drunk to safety.<br><span class="zh">然而这时，已经有三个人走进了斗牛场，他们迅速把醉汉拖到了安全的地方。</span>

Even the bull seemed to feel sorry for him,<br><span class="zh">就连那头公牛似乎也对他感到同情。</span>

for it looked on sympathetically until the drunk was out of the way before once more turning its attention to the matador.<br><span class="zh">它同情地看着那个醉汉，直到他被带走后，才再次把注意力转向斗牛士。</span>
