# 第 55 课：Not a Gold Mine

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What did the team find?<br><span class="zh">团队发现了什么？</span>

Dreams of finding lost treasure almost came true recently.<br><span class="zh">最近，找到失宝藏的梦想几乎成真了。</span>

A new machine called 'The Revealer' has been invented<br><span class="zh">一种名为“The Revealer”的新机器已经被发明出来了。</span>

and it has been used to detect gold which has been buried in the ground.<br><span class="zh">它已被用于探测埋藏在地下的黄金。</span>

The machine was used in a cave near the seashore where--it is said--pirates used to hide gold.<br><span class="zh">这台机器被用于海边附近的一个洞穴中；据说，海盗们过去常常在那里藏匿黄金。</span>

The pirates would often bury gold in the cave and then fail to collect it.<br><span class="zh">海盗们经常把黄金埋藏在洞穴里，但后来却没能把那些黄金取出来。</span>

Armed with the new machine, a search party went into the cave hoping to find buried treasure.<br><span class="zh">带着这台新机器，一支搜寻队进入了洞穴，希望能找到埋藏的宝藏。</span>

The leader of the party was examining the soil near the entrance to the cave when the machine showed that there was gold under the ground.<br><span class="zh">当队长正在检查洞穴入口附近的土壤时，机器显示地下有黄金。</span>

Very excited, the party dug a hole two feet deep.<br><span class="zh">非常兴奋，大家一起挖了一个两英尺深的坑。</span>

They finally found a small gold coin which was almost worthless.<br><span class="zh">他们终于找到了一枚小金币，但这枚金币几乎毫无价值。</span>

The party then searched the whole cave thoroughly but did not find anything except an empty tin trunk.<br><span class="zh">随后，他们彻底搜查了整个洞穴，但除了一个空铁皮箱外什么也没找到。</span>

In spite of this, many people are confident that 'The Revealer' may reveal something of value fairly soon.<br><span class="zh">尽管如此，许多人仍然相信，“揭示者”很快就会揭示出有价值的东西。</span>
