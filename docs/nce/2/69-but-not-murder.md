# 第 69 课：But not Murder

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Do you think that the writer passed his driving test? Why?<br><span class="zh">你认为那位作者通过了驾驶考试吗？为什么？</span>

I was being tested for a driving licence for the third time.<br><span class="zh">这是我第三次参加驾照考试了。</span>

I had been asked to drive in heavy traffic and had done so successfully.<br><span class="zh">我曾被要求在繁忙的交通中驾驶，而且我成功地做到了。</span>

After having been instructed to drive out of town, I began to acquire confidence.<br><span class="zh">在接到“开车离开城镇”的指令后，我开始有了信心。</span>

Sure that I had passed, I was almost beginning to enjoy my test.<br><span class="zh">当我确定自己已经通过了考试后，我甚至开始享受这个考试过程了。</span>

The examiner must have been pleased with my performance, for he smiled and said, 'Just one more thing Mr. Eames.<br><span class="zh">考官一定对我的表现很满意，因为他微笑着对我说：“还有一件事，埃姆斯先生。”</span>

Let us suppose that a child suddenly crosses the road in front of you.<br><span class="zh">假设突然有一个孩子在你面前横穿马路。</span>

As soon as I tap on the window, you must stop within five feet.'<br><span class="zh">“我一敲窗户，你就必须在五英尺内停下来。”</span>

I continued driving and after some time, the examiner tapped loudly.<br><span class="zh">我继续开车，过了一会儿，考官大声地敲了敲车窗。</span>

Though the sound could be heard clearly, it took me a long time to react.<br><span class="zh">虽然那个声音听得非常清楚，但我还是花了很长时间才做出反应。</span>

I suddenly pressed the brake pedal hard and we were both thrown forward.<br><span class="zh">我突然猛踩刹车，结果我们俩都被向前甩了出去。</span>

The examiner looked at me sadly.<br><span class="zh">考官难过地看着我。</span>

'Mr. Eames,' he said, in a mournful voice, 'you have just killed that child!'<br><span class="zh">“埃姆斯先生，”他用悲伤的语气说道，“你刚刚撞死了那个孩子！”</span>
