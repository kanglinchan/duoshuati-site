# 第 83 课：After the Elections

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did Patrick keep on asking the same question?<br><span class="zh">为什么帕特里克一直重复同一个问题？</span>

The former Prime Minister, Mr. Wentworth Lane, was defeated in the recent elections.<br><span class="zh">前首相温特沃斯·莱恩先生在最近的选举中落败了。</span>

He is now retiring from political life and has gone abroad.<br><span class="zh">我的朋友帕特里克一直是莱恩先生激进进步党的狂热反对者。</span>

My friend, Patrick, has always been a fanatical opponent of Mr. Lane's Radical Progressive Party.<br><span class="zh">我的朋友帕特里克一直是莱恩先生领导的激进进步党的强烈反对者。</span>

After the elections, Patrick went to the former Prime Minister's house.<br><span class="zh">选举结束后，帕特里克去了前首相的住所。</span>

When he asked if Mr. Lane lived there, the policeman on duty told him that since his defeat, the ex-Prime Minister had gone abroad.<br><span class="zh">当他询问莱恩先生是否住在那里时，值班的警察告诉他，自从落败后，前首相就已经出国了。</span>

On the following day Patrick went to the house again.<br><span class="zh">第二天，帕特里克再次来到那栋房子前。</span>

The same policeman was just walking slowly past the entrance, when Patrick asked the same question.<br><span class="zh">还是那位警察，他正缓缓地从门口走过，这时帕特里克又问了同样的问题。</span>

Though a little suspicious this time the policeman gave him the same answer.<br><span class="zh">虽然这次警察有些起疑，但他还是给出了同样的回答。</span>

The day after, Patrick went to the house once more and asked exactly the same question.<br><span class="zh">第三天，帕特里克再次来到那里，问了完全相同的问题。</span>

This time, the policeman lost his temper.<br><span class="zh">这一次，警察终于发火了。</span>

'I told you yesterday and the day before yesterday,' he shouted,<br><span class="zh">“我昨天和前天都告诉过你！”他大声说道。</span>

'Mr.Lane was defeated in the elections.<br><span class="zh">莱恩先生在选举中落败了。</span>

He has retired from political life and gone to live abroad!'<br><span class="zh">他已经退出政坛，到国外定居去了！</span>

'I know,' answered Patrick,' but I love to hear you say it!'<br><span class="zh">“我知道，”帕特里克回答道，“可我就是爱听你这么说！”</span>
