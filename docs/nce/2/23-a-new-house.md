# 第 23 课：A New House

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why is the new house special?<br><span class="zh">为什么这栋新房子这么特别呢？</span>

I had a letter from my sister yesterday.<br><span class="zh">我昨天收到了姐姐的一封信。</span>

She lives in Nigeria.<br><span class="zh">她住在尼日利亚。</span>

In her letter, she said that she would come to England next year.<br><span class="zh">她在信中说，她明年会来英格兰。</span>

If she comes, she will get a surprise.<br><span class="zh">如果她来了，她会收到一个惊喜。</span>

We are now living in a beautiful new house in the country.<br><span class="zh">我们现在住在乡下的一栋漂亮的新房子里。</span>

Work on it had begun before my sister left.<br><span class="zh">我妹妹离开前，这栋房子就已经开始建了。</span>

The house was completed five months ago.<br><span class="zh">这栋房子是在五个月前建成的。</span>

In my letter, I told her that she could stay with us.<br><span class="zh">我在信里告诉她，她可以和我们住在一起。</span>

The house has many large rooms and there is a lovely garden.<br><span class="zh">这栋房子有很多宽敞的房间，还有一个非常漂亮的花园。</span>

It is a very modern house, so it looks strange to some people.<br><span class="zh">这是一栋非常现代化的房子，因此有些人会觉得它看起来很奇怪。</span>

It must be the only modern house in the district.<br><span class="zh">这一定是这个地区唯一的一栋现代化房子了。</span>
