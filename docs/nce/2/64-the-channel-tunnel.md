# 第 64 课：The Channel Tunnel

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why was the first tunnel not completed?<br><span class="zh">为什么第一条隧道没有完成呢？</span>

In 1858, a French engineer, Aime Thome de Gamond,<br><span class="zh">1858年，一位名叫艾姆·托姆·德·加蒙（Aime Thome de Gamond）的法国工程师……</span>

arrived in England with a plan for a 21-mile tunnel under the English Channel.<br><span class="zh">他带着一个在英吉利海峡下建造一条21英里隧道的计划来到了英格兰。</span>

He said that it would be possible to build a platform in the centre of the Channel.<br><span class="zh">他说，在英吉利海峡中央建造一个平台是可行的。</span>

This platform would serve as a port and a railway station.<br><span class="zh">这个平台将用作港口和火车站。</span>

The tunnel would be well-ventilated if tall chimneys were built above sea level.<br><span class="zh">如果在海平面上方建造高耸的烟囱，这条隧道将会拥有良好的通风条件。</span>

In 1860, a better plan was put forward by an Englishman, William Low.<br><span class="zh">1860年，一位名叫威廉·洛（William Low）的英国人提出了一个更完善的方案。</span>

He suggested that a double railway-tunnel should be built.<br><span class="zh">他建议建造一条双线铁路隧道。</span>

This would solve the problem of ventilation for if a train entered this tunnel, it would draw in fresh air behind it.<br><span class="zh">这样就能解决通风问题，因为如果火车进入这条隧道，它就会把新鲜空气吸进来。</span>

Forty-two years later a tunnel was actually begun.<br><span class="zh">四十二年后，一条隧道真的开始修建了。</span>

If, at the time, the British had not feared invasion, it would have been completed.<br><span class="zh">如果当时英国人不担心遭到入侵的话，这项工程就会完成了。</span>

The world had to wait almost another 100 years for the Channel Tunnel.<br><span class="zh">世界又等待了近100年，才看到了英法海底隧道的建成。</span>

It was officially opened on March 7, 1994, finally connecting Britain to the European continent.<br><span class="zh">它于1994年3月7日正式启用，终于将英国与欧洲大陆连接了起来。</span>
