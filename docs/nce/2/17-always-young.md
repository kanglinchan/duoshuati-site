# 第 17 课：Always Young

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why doesn't Aunt Jennifer tell anyone how old she is?<br><span class="zh">为什么詹妮弗阿姨从不告诉别人她的真实年龄呢？</span>

My aunt Jennifer is an actress.<br><span class="zh">我的阿姨詹妮弗是一名演员。</span>

She must be at least thirty-five years old.<br><span class="zh">她至少有三十五岁了。</span>

In spite of this, she often appears on the stage as a young girl.<br><span class="zh">尽管如此，她仍然经常以年轻女孩的形象出现在舞台上。</span>

Jennifer will have to take part in a new play soon.<br><span class="zh">詹妮弗很快将出演一部新剧。</span>

This time, she will be a girl of seventeen.<br><span class="zh">这一次，她将是一个十七岁的女孩。</span>

In the play, she must appear in a bright red dress and long black stockings.<br><span class="zh">在剧中，她必须穿着鲜红色的连衣裙和黑色长筒袜出场。</span>

Last year in another play,<br><span class="zh">去年，在另一部戏剧中……</span>

she had to wear short socks and a bright, orange-coloured dress.<br><span class="zh">她不得不穿短袜和一条鲜艳的橙色连衣裙。</span>

If anyone ever asks her how old she is,<br><span class="zh">如果有人问她多大了，</span>

she always answers, 'Darling, it must be terrible to be grown up!'<br><span class="zh">她总是回答：“亲爱的，长大一定很可怕吧！”</span>
