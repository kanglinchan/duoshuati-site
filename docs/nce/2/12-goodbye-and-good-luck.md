# 第 12 课：Goodbye and Good Luck

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Where is Captain Alison going and how?<br><span class="zh">艾莉森上尉要去哪里？怎么去？</span>

Our neighbour, Captain Charles Alison, will sail from Portsmouth tomorrow.<br><span class="zh">我们的邻居查尔斯·艾利森上尉明天将从朴次茅斯启航。</span>

We'll meet him at the harbour early in the morning.<br><span class="zh">我们将在清晨在港口与他见面。</span>

He will be in his small boat, Topsail.<br><span class="zh">他会待在他的小船'托普赛尔'号上。</span>

Topsail is a famous little boat.<br><span class="zh">“Topsail”是一艘非常著名的小船。</span>

It has sailed across the Atlantic many times.<br><span class="zh">它已经多次横渡了大西洋。</span>

Captain Alison will set out at eight o'clock,<br><span class="zh">艾莉森船长八点就要出发了，</span>

so we'll have plenty of time.<br><span class="zh">所以我们会有足够的时间。</span>

We'll see his boat and then we'll say goodbye to him.<br><span class="zh">我们会去看看他的船，然后跟他告别。</span>

He will be away for two months.<br><span class="zh">他将会离开两个月。</span>

We are very proud of him.<br><span class="zh">我们为他感到非常骄傲。</span>

He will take part in an important race across the Atlantic.<br><span class="zh">他将参加一场横跨大西洋的重要比赛。</span>
