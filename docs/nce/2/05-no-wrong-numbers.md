# 第 5 课：No Wrong Numbers

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What does 'No wrong numbers' mean?<br><span class="zh">“没有错误的数字”是什么意思？</span>

Mr.James Scott has a garage in Silbury<br><span class="zh">詹姆斯·斯科特先生在塞尔伯里（Silbury）有一间车库。</span>

and now he has just bought another garage in Pinhurst.<br><span class="zh">现在他又在 Pinhurst 买了一个车库。</span>

Pinhurst is only five miles from Silbury,<br><span class="zh">平赫斯特（Pinhurst）距离西尔伯里（Silbury）只有五英里。</span>

but Mr. Scott cannot get a telephone for his new garage,<br><span class="zh">但是斯科特先生无法为他的新车库配备一部电话。</span>

so he has just bought twelve pigeons.<br><span class="zh">所以他刚刚买了十二只鸽子。</span>

Yesterday, a pigeon carried the first message from Pinhurst to Silbury.<br><span class="zh">昨天，一只鸽子把第一封信从平赫斯特送到了塞尔伯里。</span>

The bird covered the distance in three minutes.<br><span class="zh">这只鸽子花了三分钟飞完了这段路程。</span>

Up to now,<br><span class="zh">到目前为止，</span>

Mr.Scott has sent a great many requests for spare parts<br><span class="zh">斯科特先生已经从一个车库向另一个车库发送了大量索取备件的请求</span>

and other urgent messages from one garage to the other.<br><span class="zh">以及其他紧急信息。</span>

In this way, he has begun his own private 'telephone' service.<br><span class="zh">通过这种方式，他开始了自己的私人“电话”服务。</span>
