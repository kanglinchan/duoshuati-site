# 第 43 课：Over the South Pole

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How was the plane able to clear the mountains?<br><span class="zh">这架飞机是如何成功飞越那些山脉的呢？</span>

In 1929, three years after his flight over the North Pole,<br><span class="zh">1929年，在他飞越北极三年后……</span>

the American explorer, R.E. Byrd,<br><span class="zh">美国探险家 R.E. 比尔德（R.E. Byrd）</span>

successfully flew over the South Pole for the first time.<br><span class="zh">它首次成功飞越了南极。</span>

Though, at first, Byrd and his men were able to take a<br><span class="zh">不过，起初伯德和他的手下们确实拍下了大量的照片，</span>

great many photographs of the mountains that lay below,<br><span class="zh">大量的照片记录了下方那些山脉的景象。</span>

they soon ran into serious trouble.<br><span class="zh">他们很快就遇到了严重的麻烦。</span>

At one point, it seemed certain that their plane would crash.<br><span class="zh">有那么一刻，人们确信他们的飞机将会坠毁。</span>

It could only get over the mountains if it rose to 10, 000 feet.<br><span class="zh">只有当它上升到 10,000 英尺的高度时，才能翻越那些山脉。</span>

Byrd at once ordered his men to throw out two heavy food sacks.<br><span class="zh">伯德立刻命令他的手下扔掉两个沉重的食物袋。</span>

The plane was then able to rise and it cleared the mountains by 400 feet.<br><span class="zh">飞机于是得以爬升，并以400英尺的高度飞越了山脉。</span>

Byrd now knew that he would be able to reach the South Pole which was 300 miles away,<br><span class="zh">伯德现在知道，他能够到达300英里外的南极点了，</span>

for there were no more mountains in sight.<br><span class="zh">因为眼前已经没有更多的山脉了。</span>

The aircraft was able to fly over the endless white plains without difficulty.<br><span class="zh">飞机得以毫不费力地飞越那片无尽的白色平原。</span>
