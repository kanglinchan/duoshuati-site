# 第 27 课：A Wet Night

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What happened to the boys in the night?<br><span class="zh">那些男孩们晚上发生了什么事？</span>

Late in the afternoon, the boys put up their tent in the middle of a field.<br><span class="zh">傍晚时分，男孩们在田野中央搭起了帐篷。</span>

As soon as this was done, they cooked a meal over an open fire.<br><span class="zh">帐篷一搭好，他们就在篝火上做饭了。</span>

They were all hungry and the food smelled good.<br><span class="zh">大家都很饿，食物闻起来非常香。</span>

After a wonderful meal, they told stories and sang songs by the campfire.<br><span class="zh">吃完美味的饭菜后，他们在篝火旁讲故事、唱歌。</span>

But some time later it began to rain.<br><span class="zh">但不久之后，天开始下雨了。</span>

The boys felt tired so they put out the fire and crept into their tent.<br><span class="zh">男孩们感到累了，于是他们熄灭了篝火，钻进了帐篷。</span>

Their sleeping bags were warm and comfortable, so they all slept soundly.<br><span class="zh">他们的睡袋温暖舒适，大家都睡得很香。</span>

In the middle of the night, two boys woke up and began shouting.<br><span class="zh">半夜时分，有两个男孩突然醒来，开始大喊大叫。</span>

The tent was full of water!<br><span class="zh">帐篷里充满了水！</span>

They all leapt out of their sleeping bags and hurried outside.<br><span class="zh">他们一下子从睡袋里跳出来，赶紧跑到外面。</span>

It was raining heavily and they found that a stream had formed in the field.<br><span class="zh">雨下得很大，他们发现田野里形成了一条小溪。</span>

The stream wound its way across the field and then flowed right under their tent!<br><span class="zh">小溪蜿蜒流过田野，然后从他们的帐篷下方流过！</span>
