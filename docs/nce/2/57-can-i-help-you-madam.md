# 第 57 课：Can I Help You, Madam

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Did the woman get what she wanted?<br><span class="zh">那个女人得到了她想要的东西吗？</span>

A woman in jeans stood at the window of an expensive shop.<br><span class="zh">一个穿着牛仔裤的女人站在一家高档商店的窗前。</span>

Though she hesitated for a moment,<br><span class="zh">虽然她犹豫了一会儿，</span>

she finally went in and asked to see a dress that was in the window.<br><span class="zh">她终于走进店里，要求看一看橱窗里的那件连衣裙。</span>

The assistant who served her did not like the way she was dressed.<br><span class="zh">接待她的店员不喜欢她的穿着打扮。</span>

Glancing at her scornfully, he told her that the dress was sold.<br><span class="zh">他轻蔑地瞥了她一眼，然后告诉她那条裙子已经卖掉了。</span>

The woman walked out of the shop angrily and decided to punish the assistant next day.<br><span class="zh">那位女士怒气冲冲地走出商店，决定第二天要教训一下那个店员。</span>

She returned to the shop the following morning dressed in a fur coat,<br><span class="zh">第二天早上，她穿着一件皮草大衣回到了店里。</span>

with a handbag in one hand and a long umbrella in the other.<br><span class="zh">一只手拿着手提包，另一只手拿着一把长伞。</span>

After seeking out the rude assistant,<br><span class="zh">找到那个无礼的店员后，</span>

she asked for the same dress.<br><span class="zh">她又要看同一条裙子。</span>

Not realizing who she was, the assistant was eager to serve her this time.<br><span class="zh">由于没认出她是谁，这次店员殷勤地为她服务。</span>

With great difficulty, he climbed into the shop window to get the dress.<br><span class="zh">他费了很大的劲才爬进商店的橱窗里，去拿那条裙子。</span>

As soon as she saw it, the woman said she did not like it.<br><span class="zh">她一看到那件衣服，就说不喜欢。</span>

She enjoyed herself making the assistant bring almost everything in the window<br><span class="zh">她乐此不疲地让店员把橱窗里几乎所有的东西都拿了出来，</span>

before finally buying the dress she had first asked for.<br><span class="zh">最后才买下她最初要看的那条裙子。</span>
