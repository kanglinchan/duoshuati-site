# 第 54 课：Sticky Fingers

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What two interruptions did the writer have?<br><span class="zh">作者遇到了哪两种打扰？</span>

After breakfast, I sent the children to school and then I went to the shops.<br><span class="zh">早餐后，我送孩子们去了学校，然后去了商店。</span>

It was still early when I returned home.<br><span class="zh">当我回到家时，时间还早。</span>

The children were at school, my husband was at work and the house was quiet.<br><span class="zh">孩子们都在学校，我丈夫也在上班，家里一片安静。</span>

So I decided to make some meat pies.<br><span class="zh">所以我决定做一些肉馅派。</span>

In a short time I was busy mixing butter<br><span class="zh">没过多久，我就忙着搅拌黄油了。</span>

and flour and my hands were soon covered with sticky pastry.<br><span class="zh">和面粉，很快我的双手就沾满了粘糊糊的油酥面团。</span>

At exactly that moment, the telephone rang.<br><span class="zh">就在那一刻，电话响了。</span>

Nothing could have been more annoying.<br><span class="zh">没有什么比这更让人恼火了。</span>

I picked up the receiver between two sticky fingers and was dismayed when I recognized the voice of Helen Bates.<br><span class="zh">我用两个沾满油酥的手指拿起听筒，当听出是海伦·贝茨的声音时，我感到非常沮丧。</span>

It took me ten minutes to persuade her to ring back later. At last I hung up the receiver.<br><span class="zh">我花了十分钟的时间才说服她稍后回电话。最后，我终于挂断了电话。</span>

What a mess! There was pastry on my fingers, on the telephone, and on the doorknobs.<br><span class="zh">真是一团糟！我的手指上、电话上，还有门把手上都沾满了油酥面团。</span>

I had no sooner got back to the kitchen than the doorbell rang loud enough to wake the dead.<br><span class="zh">我刚回到厨房，门铃就响得震天响，仿佛连死人都会被吵醒。</span>

This time it was the postman and he wanted me to sign for a registered letter!<br><span class="zh">这次来的是邮递员，他让我签收一封挂号信！</span>
