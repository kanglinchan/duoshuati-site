# 第 76 课：April Fools' Day

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What was the joke?<br><span class="zh">那个笑话是什么？</span>

'To end our special news bulletin,'<br><span class="zh">“为了结束我们的特别新闻播报，”</span>

said the voice of the television announcer, 'we're going over to the macaroni fields of Calabria.<br><span class="zh">电视播音员的声音说道：“我们现在正前往卡拉布里亚地区的通心粉种植区。”</span>

Macaroni has been grown in this area for over six hundred years.<br><span class="zh">在这个地区，人们种植通心粉已经有六百多年的历史了。</span>

Two of the leading growers Giuseppe Moldova and Riccardo Brabante,<br><span class="zh">两位主要的种植者——朱塞佩·莫尔多瓦（Giuseppe Moldova）和里卡多·布拉班特（Riccardo Brabante）</span>

tell me that they have been expecting a splendid crop this year and harvesting has begun earlier than usual.<br><span class="zh">告诉我，他们一直期待今年会有大丰收，而且收割工作也比往年提前开始了。</span>

Here you can see two workers who, between them,<br><span class="zh">在这里，你可以看到两名工人；他们两人一起</span>

have just finished cutting three cartloads of golden brown macaroni stalks.<br><span class="zh">刚刚割完了三车金黄色的通心粉秸秆。</span>

The whole village has been working day and night gathering and threshing this year's crop before the September rains.<br><span class="zh">全村人都在日夜忙碌，收割和脱粒今年的庄稼，赶在九月雨季到来之前完成。</span>

On the right, you can see Mrs. Brabante herself.<br><span class="zh">在右边，你可以看到布拉班特夫人本人。</span>

She has been helping her husband for thirty years now.<br><span class="zh">她已经帮丈夫干了三十年了。</span>

Mrs. Brabante is talking to the manager of the local factory where the crop is processed.<br><span class="zh">布拉班特夫人正在和当地加工厂的经理说话，这家工厂负责加工这些通心粉。</span>

This last scene shows you what will happen at the end of the harvest:<br><span class="zh">这最后一个镜头展示了丰收结束时会发生什么：</span>

the famous Calabrian macaroni-eating competition!<br><span class="zh">那就是著名的卡拉布里亚地区“吃通心粉比赛”！</span>

Signor Fratelli, the present champion, has won it every year since 1991.<br><span class="zh">现任冠军弗拉泰利先生（Signor Fratelli）自1991年以来每年都夺冠。</span>

And that ends our special bulletin for today, Thursday, April 1st.<br><span class="zh">今天的特别播报就到这里，今天是4月1日，星期四。</span>

We're now going back to the studio.'<br><span class="zh">现在我们回到演播室。</span>
