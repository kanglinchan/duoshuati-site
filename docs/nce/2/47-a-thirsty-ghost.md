# 第 47 课：A Thirsty Ghost

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What evidence is there of a ghost?<br><span class="zh">有什么证据表明有鬼存在呢？</span>

A public house which was recently bought by Mr.Ian Thompson is up for sale.<br><span class="zh">一家最近被伊恩·汤普森先生购买的酒吧正在出售中。</span>

Mr.Thompson is going to sell it because it is haunted.<br><span class="zh">汤普森先生打算卖掉它，因为那里闹鬼。</span>

He told me that he could not go to sleep one night<br><span class="zh">他告诉我，有一天晚上他无法入睡</span>

because he heard a strange noise coming from the bar.<br><span class="zh">因为他听到从酒吧里传来一阵奇怪的声音。</span>

The next morning,<br><span class="zh">第二天早上，</span>

he found that the doors had been blocked by chairs and the furniture had been moved.<br><span class="zh">他发现门被椅子堵住了，家具也被挪动了。</span>

Though Mr.Thompson had turned the lights off before he went to bed,<br><span class="zh">尽管汤普森先生在睡觉前已经关掉了灯，</span>

they were on in the morning.<br><span class="zh">早上灯却亮着。</span>

He also said that he had found five empty whisky bottles<br><span class="zh">他还说，他发现了五个空威士忌酒瓶。</span>

which the ghost must have drunk the night before.<br><span class="zh">肯定是鬼前一天晚上喝掉的。</span>

When I suggested that some villagers must have come<br><span class="zh">当我提出“肯定有一些村民来过这里”这个想法时……</span>

in for a free drink, Mr.Thompson shook his head.<br><span class="zh">汤普森先生摇了摇头。</span>

The villagers have told him that<br><span class="zh">村民们告诉他：</span>

they will not accept the pub even if he gives it away.<br><span class="zh">“就算他把酒吧免费送出去，他们也不会接受的。”</span>
