# 第 48 课：Did You Want to Tell Me Something

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did the writer become very worried?<br><span class="zh">为什么作者会变得非常担心呢？</span>

Dentists always ask questions when it is impossible for you to answer.<br><span class="zh">牙医总是在你无法回答问题的时候提问。</span>

My dentist had just pulled out one of my teeth and had told me to rest for a while.<br><span class="zh">我的牙医刚刚拔掉了我的一颗牙齿，然后让我休息一会儿。</span>

I tried to say something,<br><span class="zh">我试图说些什么，</span>

but my mouth was full of cotton wool.<br><span class="zh">但我的嘴里塞满了棉花。</span>

He knew I collected match boxes and asked me whether my collection was growing.<br><span class="zh">他知道我收集火柴盒，便问我收藏的火柴盒是不是越来越多了。</span>

He then asked me how my brother was and whether I liked my new job in London.<br><span class="zh">接着他又问我弟弟的情况，以及我是否喜欢在伦敦的新工作。</span>

In answer to these questions I either nodded or made strange noises.<br><span class="zh">对于这些问题，我只能点头或者发出一些奇怪的声音作为回答。</span>

Meanwhile, my tongue was busy searching out the hole where the tooth had been.<br><span class="zh">与此同时，我的舌头不停地探寻着那颗牙齿被拔掉后留下的窟窿。</span>

I suddenly felt very worried, but could not say anything.<br><span class="zh">我突然感到非常担心，但却什么也说不出来。</span>

When the dentist at last removed the cotton wool from my mouth,<br><span class="zh">直到牙医终于把嘴里的棉花取出来，</span>

I was able to tell him that he had pulled out the wrong tooth.<br><span class="zh">我才告诉他，他拔错了牙齿。</span>
