# 第 20 课：One Man in a Boat

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why is fishing the writer's favourite sport?<br><span class="zh">为什么钓鱼是这位作家最喜爱的运动呢？</span>

Fishing is my favourite sport.<br><span class="zh">钓鱼是我最喜欢的运动。</span>

I often fish for hours without catching anything.<br><span class="zh">我经常花好几个小时钓鱼，却什么也没钓到。</span>

But this does not worry me.<br><span class="zh">但这并不让我担心。</span>

Some fishermen are unlucky.<br><span class="zh">有些渔民运气不好。</span>

Instead of catching fish,<br><span class="zh">与其去捕鱼，</span>

they catch old boots and rubbish.<br><span class="zh">他们收集旧靴子和垃圾。</span>

I am even less lucky.<br><span class="zh">我的运气更差了。</span>

I never catch anything  --  not even old boots.<br><span class="zh">我从来什么也抓不到——连一双旧靴子都抓不到。</span>

After having spent whole mornings on the river,<br><span class="zh">在河边度过了整个上午之后……</span>

I always go home with an empty bag.<br><span class="zh">我每次回家时，包里总是空空的。</span>

'You must give up fishing!' my friends say. 'It's a waste of time.'<br><span class="zh">“你必须放弃钓鱼！”我的朋友们说，“那纯粹是浪费时间。”</span>

But they don't realize one important thing.<br><span class="zh">但他们没有意识到一件重要的事情：</span>

I'm not really interested in fishing.<br><span class="zh">我其实对钓鱼并不感兴趣。</span>

I am only interested in sitting in a boat and doing nothing at all!<br><span class="zh">我真正感兴趣的，只是坐在船上什么都不做而已！</span>
