# 第 71 课：A Famous Clock

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Has Big Ben ever gone wrong?<br><span class="zh">大本钟曾经出过故障吗？</span>

When you visit London, one of the first things you will see is Big Ben,<br><span class="zh">当你游览伦敦时，首先会看到的大概就是大本钟（Big Ben）了。</span>

the famous clock which can be heard all over the world on the B.B.C.<br><span class="zh">那座著名的钟，它的钟声可以通过英国广播公司（BBC）的广播传遍全世界。</span>

If the Houses of Parliament had not been burned down in 1834,<br><span class="zh">如果英国议会大厦没有在1834年被烧毁的话……</span>

the great clock would never have been erected.<br><span class="zh">那座大钟就永远也不会被建造起来了。</span>

Big Ben takes its name from Sir Benjamin Hall who was responsible for the making of the clock when the new Houses of Parliament were being built.<br><span class="zh">大本钟的名字来源于本杰明·霍尔爵士（Sir Benjamin Hall）——他在英国新议会大厦建造期间负责这座钟的制造工作。</span>

It is not only of immense size, but is extremely accurate as well.<br><span class="zh">它不仅体积庞大，而且精度极高。</span>

Officials from Greenwich Observatory have the clock checked twice a day.<br><span class="zh">格林尼治天文台的官员每天会检查这台时钟两次。</span>

On the B.B.C. you can hear the clock when it is actually striking because microphones are connected to the clock tower.<br><span class="zh">在BBC的广播中，你可以听到钟声的实际敲击声——因为麦克风是直接连接到钟楼的。</span>

Big Ben has rarely gone wrong.<br><span class="zh">大本钟几乎从未出过故障。</span>

Once, however, it failed to give the correct time.<br><span class="zh">然而，有一次它没能准确地显示时间。</span>

A painter who had been working on the tower hung a pot of paint on one of the hands and slowed it down!<br><span class="zh">一位在钟塔上工作的油漆工，把一罐油漆挂在了一根指针上，结果使钟走慢了！</span>
