# 第 50 课：Taken for a Ride

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did the writer not get off the bus at Woodford Green?<br><span class="zh">为什么作者没有在伍德福德格林（Woodford Green）下车呢？</span>

I love travelling in the country,<br><span class="zh">我喜欢在乡村旅行。</span>

but I don't like losing my way.<br><span class="zh">但我不喜欢迷路。</span>

I went on an excursion recently,<br><span class="zh">我最近去参加了一次短途旅行。</span>

but my trip took me longer than I expected.<br><span class="zh">但是我的旅行花费的时间比我预期的要长。</span>

'I'm going to Woodford Green,'<br><span class="zh">“我要去伍德福德格林。”</span>

I said to the conductor as I got on the bus,<br><span class="zh">当我登上公交车时，我对售票员说：</span>

'but I don't know where it is.'<br><span class="zh">“但我不知道它在哪里。”</span>

'I'll tell you where to get off.' answered the conductor.<br><span class="zh">“我会告诉您在哪下车。”售票员回答道。</span>

I sat in the front of the bus to get a good view of the countryside.<br><span class="zh">我坐在公交车的前排，以便能更好地欣赏乡村的景色。</span>

After some time, the bus stopped.<br><span class="zh">过了一段时间，公交车停了下来。</span>

Looking round, I realized with a shock that I was the only passenger left on the bus.<br><span class="zh">环顾四周后，我震惊地发现自己是公交车上唯一剩下的乘客。</span>

'You'll have to get off here,'<br><span class="zh">“你得从这里下去。”</span>

the conductor said. 'This is as far as we go.'<br><span class="zh">售票员说：“我们就到这儿了。”</span>

'Is this Woodford Green?' I asked.<br><span class="zh">“这是伍德福德格林（Woodford Green）吗？”我问道。</span>

'Oh dear,' said the conductor suddenly.<br><span class="zh">“哦，天哪，”售票员突然说道。</span>

'I forgot to put you off.'<br><span class="zh">“我忘了让您下车。”</span>

'It doesn't matter,' I said. 'I'll get off here.'<br><span class="zh">“没关系，”我说，“我就在这儿下车。”</span>

'We're going back now,' said the conductor.<br><span class="zh">“我们现在要返回了，”售票员说。</span>

'Well, in that case,<br><span class="zh">“嗯，既然是这样的话，</span>

I prefer to stay on the bus,' I answered.<br><span class="zh">“我更愿意待在公交车上，”我回答道。</span>
