# 第 85 课：Never Too Old To Learn

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How long has Mr. Page been teaching?<br><span class="zh">佩奇先生教书已经有多久了？</span>

I have just received a letter from my old school, informing me that my former headmaster, Mr. Stuart Page, will be retiring next week.<br><span class="zh">我刚刚收到我母校寄来的一封信，信中告诉我，我的前任校长斯图尔特·佩奇先生将于下周退休。</span>

Pupils of the school old and new, will be sending him a present to mark the occasion.<br><span class="zh">学校的历届学生都会送他一份礼物来纪念这个时刻。</span>

All those who have contributed towards the gift will sign their names in a large album which will be sent to the headmaster's home.<br><span class="zh">所有为这份礼物出过力的人都会在一本大纪念册上签名，这本纪念册将被送到校长家中。</span>

We shall all remember Mr. Page for his patience and understanding and for the kindly encouragement he gave us when we went so unwillingly to school.<br><span class="zh">我们都会永远铭记佩奇先生——他总是充满耐心与理解，每当我们极不情愿地去上学时，他总会给予我们鼓励与支持。</span>

A great many former pupils will be attending a farewell dinner in his honour next Thursday.<br><span class="zh">许多他的老同学将会参加下周四为他举办的告别晚宴。</span>

It is a curious coincidence that the day before his retirement, Mr. Page will have been teaching for a total of forty years.<br><span class="zh">巧合的是，在佩奇先生退休的前一天，他教书正好满四十年。</span>

After he has retired he will devote himself to gardening.<br><span class="zh">退休后，他将全身心投入到园艺活动中去。</span>

For him, this will be an entirely new hobby.<br><span class="zh">对他来说，这将是一个全新的爱好。</span>

But this does not matter, for, as he has often remarked, one is never too old to learn.<br><span class="zh">但这并不重要，因为正如他经常说的那样：人永远都不应该停止学习。</span>
