# 第 18 课：He Often does This

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What had happened to the writer's bag?<br><span class="zh">作者的包发生了什么事？</span>

After I had had lunch at a village pub, I looked for my bag.<br><span class="zh">在一家乡村酒吧吃过午饭后，我开始寻找我的包。</span>

I had left it on a chair beside the door and now it wasn't there!<br><span class="zh">我之前把它放在门旁边的椅子上，但现在它不见了！</span>

As I was looking for it, the landlord came in.<br><span class="zh">我正在找包的时候，店主进来了。</span>

'Did you have a good meal?' he asked.<br><span class="zh">“你吃得怎么样？”他问道。</span>

'Yes, thank you,' I answered, 'but I can't pay the bill. I haven't got my bag.'<br><span class="zh">“是的，谢谢您，”我回答道，“但我付不了账单。我的包不见了。”</span>

The landlord smiled and immediately went out.<br><span class="zh">店主笑了笑，马上就出去了。</span>

In a few minutes he returned with my bag and gave it back to me.<br><span class="zh">几分钟后，他拿着我的包回来了，并把它交还给了我。</span>

'I'm very sorry,' he said.<br><span class="zh">“我非常抱歉，”他说。</span>

'My dog had taken it into the garden. He often does this!'<br><span class="zh">“我的狗把它叼到花园里去了。它老是干这种事！”</span>
