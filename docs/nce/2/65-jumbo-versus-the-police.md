# 第 65 课：Jumbo Versus the Police

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did the police have to push Jumbo off the main street?<br><span class="zh">为什么警察不得不把 Jumbo 从主街上推走？</span>

Last Christmas, the circus owner, Jimmy Gates, decided to take some presents to a children's hospital.<br><span class="zh">去年圣诞节，马戏团老板吉米·盖茨决定把一些礼物送到儿童医院去。</span>

Dressed up as Father Christmas and accompanied by a 'guard of honour' of six pretty girls,<br><span class="zh">他装扮成圣诞老人，由六位漂亮的女孩组成的“仪仗队”陪同着他。</span>

he set off down the main street of the city riding a baby elephant called Jumbo.<br><span class="zh">他骑着一只名叫“Jumbo”的小象，沿着城市的主街道出发了。</span>

He should have known that the police would never allow this sort of thing.<br><span class="zh">他本应该知道，警察绝不会允许这种事情发生的。</span>

A policeman approached Jimmy and told him he ought to have gone along a side street as Jumbo was holding up the traffic.<br><span class="zh">一名警察走过来对吉米说：“你应该走小路，因为‘Jumbo’正在阻碍交通。”</span>

Though Jimmy agreed to go at once, Jumbo refused to move.<br><span class="zh">虽然吉米立刻同意离开，但“Jumbo”却拒绝移动。</span>

Fifteen policemen had to push very hard to get him off the main street.<br><span class="zh">十五名警察费了很大劲才把他从主街上弄走。</span>

The police had a difficult time, but they were most amused.<br><span class="zh">警察们遇到了不少困难，不过他们觉得这件事非常有趣（或者说：虽然过程很艰难，但他们觉得这件事很有趣）。</span>

'Jumbo must weigh a few tons,' said a policeman afterwards,<br><span class="zh">“Jumbo肯定有好几吨重，”一名警察事后说道，</span>

'so it was fortunate that we didn't have to carry him.<br><span class="zh">“所以我们很幸运，不必抬着他走。</span>

Of course, we should arrest him, but as he has a good record, we shall let him off this time.'<br><span class="zh">“当然，我们应该逮捕他，但鉴于他表现良好，这次我们就放他一马吧。</span>
