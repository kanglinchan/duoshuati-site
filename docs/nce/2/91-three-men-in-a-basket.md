# 第 91 课：Three Men in a Basket

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Where was the station's Commanding Officer?<br><span class="zh">空军基地的指挥官在哪里？</span>

A pilot noticed a balloon which seemed to be making for a Royal Air Force station nearby.<br><span class="zh">一名飞行员注意到一个气球，它似乎正朝附近的一个英国皇家空军基地飞去。</span>

He informed the station at once, but no one there was able to explain the mystery.<br><span class="zh">他立即通知了基地，但那里没有人能够解开这个谜团。</span>

The officer in the control tower was very angry when he heard the news,<br><span class="zh">当控制塔里的官员听到这个消息时，他非常生气。</span>

because balloons can be a great danger to aircraft.<br><span class="zh">因为气球对飞机来说可能构成很大的危险。</span>

He said that someone might be spying on the station and the pilot was ordered to keep track of the strange object.<br><span class="zh">他说可能有人在监视这个基地，因此飞行员被命令密切关注那个奇怪的物体。</span>

The pilot managed to circle the balloon for some time.<br><span class="zh">飞行员设法绕着气球盘旋了一段时间。</span>

He could make out three men in a basket under it and one of them was holding a pair of binoculars.<br><span class="zh">他能看清气球下面的篮子里有三个人，其中一人正拿着一副双筒望远镜。</span>

When the balloon was over the station, the pilot saw one of the men taking photographs.<br><span class="zh">当气球飞到基地上空时，飞行员看到其中一个人正在拍照。</span>

Soon afterwards, the balloon began to descend and it landed near an airfield.<br><span class="zh">不久之后，气球开始下降，并降落在了一个机场附近。</span>

The police were called in, but they could not arrest anyone,<br><span class="zh">警察被叫来了，但他们没能逮捕任何人。</span>

for the basket contained two Members of Parliament and the Commanding Officer of the station!<br><span class="zh">因为篮子里装着两名国会议员和该基地的指挥官！</span>

As the Commanding Officer explained later, one half of the station did not know what the other half was doing!<br><span class="zh">正如指挥官后来解释的那样，该基地的一半人员根本不知道另一半在做什么！</span>
