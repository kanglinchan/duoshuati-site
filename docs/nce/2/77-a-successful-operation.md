# 第 77 课：A Successful Operation

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Did the doctors find out how the woman died?<br><span class="zh">医生们查明这名妇女的死因了吗？</span>

The mummy of an Egyptian woman who died in 800 B.C. has just had an operation.<br><span class="zh">这位埃及女性的木乃伊（她死于公元前800年）刚刚接受了一次手术。</span>

The mummy is that of Shepenmut who was once a singer in the Temple of Thebes.<br><span class="zh">这具木乃伊属于谢彭穆特（Shepenmut），她曾经是底比斯神庙中的一名歌手。</span>

As there were strange marks on the X-ray plates taken of the mummy,<br><span class="zh">由于在拍摄木乃伊的X光片上发现了奇怪的痕迹，</span>

doctors have been trying to find out whether the woman died of a rare disease.<br><span class="zh">医生们一直在试图确定这名女性是否死于一种罕见的疾病。</span>

The only way to do this was to operate.<br><span class="zh">唯一的方法就是进行手术。</span>

The operation, which lasted for over four hours,<br><span class="zh">这次手术持续了四个多小时。</span>

proved to be very difficult because of the hard resin which covered the skin.<br><span class="zh">由于皮肤上覆盖着一层坚硬的树脂，手术被证明非常困难。</span>

The doctors removed a section of the mummy and sent it to a laboratory.<br><span class="zh">医生们取下木乃伊的一部分，把它送到了实验室。</span>

They also found something which the X-ray plates did not show: a small wax figure of the god Duamutef.<br><span class="zh">他们还发现了一些X光片未能显示的东西：一个微小的、用蜡制成的杜阿穆特夫神像。</span>

This god which has the head of a cow was normally placed inside a mummy.<br><span class="zh">这个长着牛头的神像通常被放置在木乃伊的体内。</span>

The doctors have not yet decided how the woman died.<br><span class="zh">医生们尚未确定这名妇女的死因。</span>

They feared that the mummy would fall to pieces when they cut it open,<br><span class="zh">他们担心在打开木乃伊时，木乃伊会碎成碎片。</span>

but fortunately this has not happened.<br><span class="zh">但幸运的是，这种情况并没有发生。</span>

The mummy successfully survived the operation.<br><span class="zh">这具木乃伊成功度过了手术。</span>
