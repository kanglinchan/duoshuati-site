# 第 92 课：Asking for Trouble

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why did the policeman ask the writer to come to the police station?<br><span class="zh">为什么警察让那位作家去警察局呢？</span>

It must have been about two in the morning when I returned home.<br><span class="zh">我回到家时，大概已经是凌晨两点左右了。</span>

I tried to wake up my wife by ringing the doorbell,<br><span class="zh">我试着按门铃叫醒我的妻子，</span>

but she was fast asleep,<br><span class="zh">但她睡得很熟，</span>

so I got a ladder from the shed in the garden, put it against the wall,<br><span class="zh">于是我从花园里的棚屋里拿了一把梯子，把它靠在墙上，</span>

and began climbing towards the bedroom window.<br><span class="zh">然后开始往卧室的窗户爬去。</span>

I was almost there when a sarcastic voice below said.<br><span class="zh">我刚快爬到窗户时，下面传来一个讽刺的声音说：</span>

'I don't think the windows need cleaning at this time of the night.'<br><span class="zh">‘我看这大半夜的，窗户不需要擦吧。’</span>

I looked down and nearly fell off the ladder when I saw a policeman.<br><span class="zh">我低头一看，看到一名警察时，差点从梯子上摔下来。</span>

I immediately regretted answering in the way I did, but I said, 'I enjoy cleaning windows at night.'<br><span class="zh">我立刻后悔自己那样回答，但还是说：‘我就是喜欢在晚上擦窗户。’</span>

'So do I,' answered the policeman in the same tone.<br><span class="zh">‘是啊，’警察用同样讽刺的语气回答。</span>

Excuse my interrupting you.<br><span class="zh">请原谅我打扰你。</span>

I hate to interrupt a man when he's busy working, but would you mind coming with me to the station?'<br><span class="zh">“我真不想在别人忙的时候打扰他，不过您能跟我去一趟警察局吗？”</span>

'Well, I'd prefer to stay here,' I said.<br><span class="zh">“嗯，我宁愿待在这里，”我说。</span>

'You see, I've forgotten my key.'<br><span class="zh">“是这样的，我忘带钥匙了。”</span>

'Your what?' he called.<br><span class="zh">“你说什么？”他大声问。</span>

'My key,' I shouted.<br><span class="zh">“我的钥匙！”我喊道。</span>

Fortunately, the shouting woke up my wife who opened the window just as the policeman had started to climb towards me.<br><span class="zh">幸运的是，我的喊声吵醒了妻子。就在警察开始朝我爬来时，她打开了窗户。</span>
