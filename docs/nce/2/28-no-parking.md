# 第 28 课：No Parking

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What is Jasper White's problem?<br><span class="zh">贾斯珀·怀特遇到了什么麻烦？</span>

Jasper White is one of those rare people who believes in ancient myths.<br><span class="zh">贾斯珀·怀特是少数相信古代神话的人之一。</span>

He has just bought a new house in the city, but ever since he moved in, he has had trouble with cars and their owners.<br><span class="zh">他刚在城里买了一套新房子，但从搬进去之后，他就一直为汽车和车主们的事而烦恼。</span>

When he returns home at night, he always finds that someone has parked a car outside his gate.<br><span class="zh">每当他晚上回到家时，总会发现有人把车停在了他家大门的外面。</span>

Because of this, he has not been able to get his own car into his garage even once.<br><span class="zh">正因为如此，他一次都无法将自己的汽车开进自己的车库。</span>

Jasper has put up 'No Parking' signs outside his gate, but these have not had any effect.<br><span class="zh">贾斯珀在他的大门外竖起了“禁止停车”的标志，但这些标志根本没有任何作用。</span>

Now he has put an ugly stone head over the gate.<br><span class="zh">现在，他在大门上方安放了一个丑陋的石像头。</span>

It is one of the ugliest faces I have ever seen.<br><span class="zh">这是我见过的最丑陋的面孔之一。</span>

I asked him what it was and he told me that it was Medusa, the Gorgon.<br><span class="zh">我问他那是什么，他告诉我那是蛇发女妖美杜莎。</span>

Jasper hopes that she will turn cars and their owners to stone.<br><span class="zh">贾斯珀希望她能够将汽车及其车主变成石头。</span>

But none of them has been turned to stone yet!<br><span class="zh">但是，它们当中还没有任何一个变成石头呢！</span>
