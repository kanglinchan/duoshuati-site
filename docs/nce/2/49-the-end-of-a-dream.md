# 第 49 课：The End of a Dream

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How did the dream end?<br><span class="zh">这个梦是怎么结束的？</span>

Tired of sleeping on the floor,<br><span class="zh">受够了睡在地板上……</span>

a young man in Teheran saved up for years to buy a real bed.<br><span class="zh">在德黑兰，一个年轻人为了买一张真正的床，攒了多年的钱。</span>

For the first time in his life,<br><span class="zh">他生平第一次……</span>

he became the proud owner of a bed which had springs and a mattress.<br><span class="zh">他骄傲地拥有了一张配有弹簧和床垫的床。</span>

Because the weather was very hot,<br><span class="zh">因为天气非常炎热，</span>

he carried the bed on to the roof of his house.<br><span class="zh">他把那张床搬到了自己房子的屋顶上。</span>

He slept very well for the first two nights,<br><span class="zh">他在头两个晚上睡得非常好。</span>

but on the third night, a storm blew up.<br><span class="zh">但是到了第三天晚上，一场暴风雨突然降临了。</span>

A gust of wind swept the bed off the roof and sent it crashing into the courtyard below.<br><span class="zh">一阵大风把床从屋顶上吹了下去，哐当一声掉进了下面的院子里。</span>

The young man did not wake up<br><span class="zh">那个年轻人没有醒来。</span>

until the bed had struck the ground.<br><span class="zh">直到床砸到地上他才醒来。</span>

Although the bed was smashed to pieces,<br><span class="zh">尽管床被砸得粉碎，</span>

the man was miraculously unhurt.<br><span class="zh">那个人却奇迹般地毫发无伤。</span>

When he woke up, he was still on the mattress.<br><span class="zh">当他醒来时，发现自己仍然躺在床垫上。</span>

Glancing at the bits of wood and metal that lay around him,<br><span class="zh">他瞥了一眼散落在周围的木头和金属碎片，</span>

the man sadly picked up the mattress and carried it into his house.<br><span class="zh">他伤心地捡起床垫，把它搬回了屋里。</span>

After he had put it on the floor,<br><span class="zh">把床垫放在地板上后，</span>

he promptly went to sleep again.<br><span class="zh">他立刻又睡着了。</span>
