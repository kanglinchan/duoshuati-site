# 第 75 课：SOS

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

How did the woman get help?<br><span class="zh">这个女人是如何得到帮助的？</span>

When a light passenger plane flew off course some time ago,<br><span class="zh">不久前，一架轻型客机偏离了预定航线。</span>

it crashed in the mountains and its pilot was killed.<br><span class="zh">飞机在山区坠毁，飞行员不幸遇难。</span>

The only passengers, a young woman and her two baby daughters, were unhurt.<br><span class="zh">唯一的乘客是一位年轻女子和她的两个女儿，她们都没有受伤。</span>

It was the middle of winter.<br><span class="zh">当时正值隆冬。</span>

Snow lay thick on the ground.<br><span class="zh">地上积满了厚厚的雪。</span>

The woman knew that the nearest village was miles away.<br><span class="zh">那个女人知道，最近的村庄离这里有好几英里远。</span>

When it grew dark, she turned a suitcase into a bed and put the children inside it,<br><span class="zh">天黑后，她把一个行李箱改成了“床”，然后把孩子们放了进去。</span>

covering them with all the clothes she could find.<br><span class="zh">她用所有能找到的衣服把他们盖住了。</span>

During the night, it got terribly cold.<br><span class="zh">到了晚上，天气变得非常寒冷。</span>

The woman kept as near as she could to the children and even tried to get into the case herself, but it was too small.<br><span class="zh">那位女士尽可能地靠近孩子们，甚至还试图自己钻进箱子里，但箱子太小了。</span>

Early next morning,<br><span class="zh">第二天清晨，</span>

she heard planes passing overhead and wondered how she could send a signal.<br><span class="zh">她听到飞机从头顶飞过，思考着该如何发出求救信号。</span>

Then she had an idea. She stamped out the letters 'SOS' in the snow.<br><span class="zh">接着她想到了一个主意。她在雪地上踩出了“SOS”三个字母。</span>

Fortunately, a pilot saw the signal and sent a message by radio to the nearest town.<br><span class="zh">幸运的是，一名飞行员看到了这个求救信号，并通过无线电向最近的城镇发送了消息。</span>

It was not long before a helicopter arrived on the scene to rescue the survivors of the plane crash.<br><span class="zh">很快，一架直升机赶到现场，营救了这次空难的幸存者。</span>
