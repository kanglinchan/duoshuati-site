# 第 15 课：Good News

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What was the good news?<br><span class="zh">那条好消息是什么呢？</span>

The secretary told me that Mr. Harmsworth would see me.<br><span class="zh">秘书告诉我，哈姆斯沃思先生会见我。</span>

I felt very nervous when I went into his office.<br><span class="zh">当我走进他的办公室时，我感到非常紧张。</span>

He did not look up from his desk when I entered.<br><span class="zh">我进去的时候，他头也没抬，眼睛一直盯着书桌。</span>

After I had sat down,<br><span class="zh">在我坐下之后，</span>

he said that business was very bad.<br><span class="zh">他说生意非常糟糕。</span>

He told me that the firm could not afford to pay such large salaries.<br><span class="zh">他告诉我，公司无力支付如此高的薪水。</span>

Twenty people had already left.<br><span class="zh">已经有二十个人离开了。</span>

I knew that my turn had come.<br><span class="zh">我知道轮到我了。</span>

'Mr. Harmsworth,' I said in a weak voice.<br><span class="zh">“哈姆斯沃思先生，”我虚弱地说道。</span>

'Don't interrupt,' he said.<br><span class="zh">“不要打断我，”他说。</span>

Then he smiled and told me I would receive an extra thousand pounds a year!<br><span class="zh">然后他笑了，告诉我每年还能多拿一千英镑！</span>
