# 第 88 课：Trapped in a Mine

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why is the rescue taking so long?<br><span class="zh">为什么救援工作需要这么长时间？</span>

Six men have been trapped in a mine for seventeen hours.<br><span class="zh">六名男子已在矿井中被困了十七个小时。</span>

If they are not brought to the surface soon they may lose their lives.<br><span class="zh">如果他们不能尽快被救上来，他们可能会丧命。</span>

However, rescue operations are proving difficult.<br><span class="zh">然而，救援工作却非常困难。</span>

If explosives are used, vibrations will cause the roof of the mine to collapse.<br><span class="zh">如果使用了炸药，震动会导致矿井的屋顶坍塌。</span>

Rescue workers are therefore drilling a hole on the north side of the mine.<br><span class="zh">因此，救援人员正在矿井的北侧钻孔。</span>

They intend to bring the men up in a special capsule.<br><span class="zh">他们打算用一个特制的救生舱把这些矿工救上来。</span>

If there had not been a hard layer of rock beneath the soil,<br><span class="zh">如果土壤下面没有坚硬的岩层的话……</span>

they would have completed the job in a few hours.<br><span class="zh">他们本可以在几个小时内完成这项工作。</span>

As it is, they have been drilling for 16 hours and they still have a long way to go.<br><span class="zh">到目前为止，他们已经连续钻探了16个小时，但距离完成工作还有很长的路要走。</span>

Meanwhile, a microphone, which was lowered into the mine two hours ago,<br><span class="zh">与此同时，两小时前被放入矿井中的那个麦克风……</span>

has enabled the men to keep in touch with their closest relatives.<br><span class="zh">这让这些矿工能够与他们最亲近的家人保持联系。</span>

Though they are running out of food and drink,<br><span class="zh">尽管他们的食物和饮用水即将耗尽，</span>

the men are cheerful and confident that they will get out soon.<br><span class="zh">这些矿工依然很乐观，相信自己很快就能获救。</span>

They have been told that rescue operations are progressing smoothly.<br><span class="zh">他们被告知救援行动正在顺利推进。</span>

If they knew how difficult it was to drill through the hard rock, they would lose heart.<br><span class="zh">如果他们知道在坚硬的岩石中钻孔有多么困难，他们恐怕会失去信心。</span>
