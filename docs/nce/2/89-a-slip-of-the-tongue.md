# 第 89 课：A Slip of the Tongue

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Who made the only funny joke that evening and why?<br><span class="zh">谁在那个晚上讲了唯一一个有趣的笑话？为什么呢？</span>

People will do anything to see a free show--even if it is a bad one.<br><span class="zh">人们会不惜一切代价去看一场免费的演出——哪怕这场演出很糟糕。</span>

When the news got round that a comedy show would be presented<br><span class="zh">当消息传开，说 P. and U. Bird Seed 公司将在我们当地的电影院举办一场喜剧演出时，我们都纷纷赶去观看。</span>

at our local cinema by the P. and U. Bird Seed Company, we all rushed to see it.<br><span class="zh">在我们当地的电影院（该电影院由 P. and U. Bird Seed 公司运营），我们都纷纷赶去观看这部电影。</span>

We had to queue for hours to get in and there must have been several hundred people present just before the show began.<br><span class="zh">我们不得不排队等待数小时才能进入现场；在演出开始前，现场肯定有几百人。</span>

Unfortunately, the show was one of the dullest we have ever seen.<br><span class="zh">不幸的是，这部剧是我们看过的最无聊的剧集之一。</span>

Those who failed to get in need not have felt disappointed,<br><span class="zh">那些没能进去的人大可不必感到失望，</span>

as many of the artistes who should have appeared did not come.<br><span class="zh">因为许多本该出场的演员都没有来。</span>

The only funny things we heard that evening came from the advertiser at the beginning of the programme.<br><span class="zh">那天晚上我们听到的唯一有趣的话，来自节目开始时的那位广告播报员。</span>

He was obviously very nervous and for some minutes stood awkwardly before the microphone.<br><span class="zh">他显然非常紧张，在麦克风前尴尬地站了好几分钟。</span>

As soon as he opened his mouth, everyone burst out laughing.<br><span class="zh">他一开口，所有人立刻都笑了起来。</span>

We all know what the poor man should have said,<br><span class="zh">我们都知道那个可怜的人本该说什么，</span>

but what he actually said was: 'This is the Poo and Ee Seed Bird Company. Good ladies, evening and gentlemen!'<br><span class="zh">但他实际上说的是：‘这是‘Poo and Ee Seed Bird Company’。女士们、先生们，晚上好！’</span>
