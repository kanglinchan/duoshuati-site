# 第 78 课：The Last One

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

For how long did the writer give up smoking?<br><span class="zh">作者戒烟坚持了多久？</span>

After reading an article entitled 'Cigarette Smoking and Your Health' I lit a cigarette to calm my nerves.<br><span class="zh">在读完一篇题为《吸烟与健康》的文章后，我点燃了一支烟来缓解自己的紧张情绪。</span>

I smoked with concentration and pleasure as I was sure that this would be my last cigarette.<br><span class="zh">我专注而愉悦地抽着这支烟，因为我知道这将是我的最后一支烟了。</span>

For a whole week I did not smoke at all and during this time my wife suffered terribly.<br><span class="zh">整整一个星期里，我都没有抽烟；而在这期间，我的妻子却承受了极大的痛苦。</span>

I had all the usual symptoms of someone giving up smoking: a bad temper and an enormous appetite.<br><span class="zh">我出现了所有典型的戒烟后症状：脾气变得暴躁，食欲变得异常旺盛。</span>

My friends kept on offering me cigarettes and cigars.<br><span class="zh">我的朋友们一直不停地给我提供香烟和雪茄。</span>

They made no effort to hide their amusement whenever I produced a packet of sweets from my pocket.<br><span class="zh">每当我从口袋里掏出一包糖果时，他们总是毫不掩饰自己的好笑。</span>

After seven days of this I went to a party.<br><span class="zh">这样过了七天之后，我去参加了一个聚会。</span>

Everybody around me was smoking and I felt extremely uncomfortable.<br><span class="zh">我周围的每个人都在抽烟，这让我感到非常不舒服。</span>

When my old friend Brian urged me to accept a cigarette, it was more than I could bear.<br><span class="zh">当我的老朋友布莱恩坚持要我抽一支烟时，我真的再也无法忍受了。</span>

I took one guiltily, lit it and smoked with satisfaction.<br><span class="zh">我带着一丝内疚感拿了一支烟，点燃它后，心满意足地抽了起来。</span>

My wife was delighted that things had returned to normal once more.<br><span class="zh">我的妻子很高兴，因为一切终于恢复了正常。</span>

Anyway, as Brian pointed out, it is the easiest thing in the world to give up smoking.<br><span class="zh">不管怎样，正如布莱恩指出的那样，戒烟是世界上最容易的事情。</span>

He himself has done it lots of times!<br><span class="zh">他自己也做过很多次了！</span>
