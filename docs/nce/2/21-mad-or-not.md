# 第 21 课：Mad or Not

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

Why do people think the writer is mad?<br><span class="zh">为什么人们认为这位作者疯了？</span>

Aeroplanes are slowly driving me mad.<br><span class="zh">飞机正慢慢地把我逼疯。</span>

I live near an airport and passing planes can be heard night and day.<br><span class="zh">我住在机场附近，无论白天还是黑夜，都能听到飞机的轰鸣声。</span>

The airport was built years ago, but for some reason it could not be used then.<br><span class="zh">这个机场几年前就建好了，但不知为何当时无法投入使用。</span>

Last year, however, it came into use.<br><span class="zh">然而去年，它终于开始运营了。</span>

Over a hundred people must have been driven away from their homes by the noise.<br><span class="zh">肯定有一百多人被噪音逼得离开了家。</span>

I am one of the few people left.<br><span class="zh">我是少数还留在这里的人之一。</span>

Sometimes I think this house will be knocked down by a passing plane.<br><span class="zh">有时候我觉得这房子会被飞过的飞机撞倒。</span>

I have been offered a large sum of money to go away, but I am determined to stay here.<br><span class="zh">有人出高价让我搬走，但我坚决要留在这里。</span>

Everybody says I must be mad and they are probably right.<br><span class="zh">大家都说我一定是疯了，也许他们是对的。</span>
