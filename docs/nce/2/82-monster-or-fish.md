<NcePair en="Occasionally, unusual creatures are washed to the shore, but they are rarely caught out at sea." zh="偶尔，一些不寻常的生物会被冲上岸，但在海上捕捉到它们的情况非常罕见。" />

<NcePair en="Some time ago, however, a peculiar fish was caught near Madagascar." zh="然而，不久前，在马达加斯加附近捕获了一条奇特的鱼。" />

<NcePair en="A small fishing boat was carried miles out to sea by the powerful fish as it pulled on the line." zh="这条大鱼使劲拉着渔线，把一艘小渔船拖到了几英里外的海里。" />

<NcePair en="Realizing that this was no ordinary fish, the fisherman made every effort not to damage it in any way." zh="意识到这绝不是一条普通的鱼，渔民竭尽全力避免对其造成任何伤害。" />

<NcePair en="When it was eventually brought to shore, it was found to be over thirteen feet long." zh="当这条鱼最终被带到岸上时，人们发现它的长度超过了十三英尺。" />

<NcePair en="It had a head like a horse big blue eyes, shining silver skin, and a bright red tail." zh="它有着马一样的头部、大大的蓝色眼睛、闪亮的银色皮肤以及鲜红色的尾巴。" />

<NcePair en="The fish, which has since been set to a museum where it is being examined by a scientist, is called an oarfish." zh="这条鱼后来被送往一家博物馆，由一位科学家进行研究。它被称为“桨鱼”。" />

<NcePair en="Such creatures have rarely been seen alive by man as they live at a depth of six hundred feet." zh="由于这种生物生活在六百英尺深的海域，人类很少有机会亲眼见到它们的活体。" />
