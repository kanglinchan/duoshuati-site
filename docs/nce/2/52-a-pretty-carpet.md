# 第 52 课：A Pretty Carpet

> [新概念英语 · 第二册 实践与进步](/nce/ "返回目录")

What is the writer's carpet made of?<br><span class="zh">作者的“地毯”（这里可能指作者的办公环境或工作场所）是由什么材料制成的呢？</span>

We have just moved into a new house and I have been working hard all morning.<br><span class="zh">我们刚搬进了一栋新房子，我一整个早上都在努力工作。</span>

I have been trying to get my new room in order.<br><span class="zh">我一直在努力把我的新房间整理得井井有条。</span>

This has not been easy because I own over a thousand books.<br><span class="zh">这并不容易，因为我有超过一千本书。</span>

To make matters worse,<br><span class="zh">更糟糕的是……</span>

the room is rather small,<br><span class="zh">这个房间相当小。</span>

so I have temporarily put my books on the floor.<br><span class="zh">所以我暂时把我的书放在了地板上。</span>

At the moment, they cover every inch of floor space<br><span class="zh">目前，它们已经覆盖了每一寸的地面空间。</span>

and I actually have to walk on them to get in or out of the room.<br><span class="zh">实际上，我必须踩在这些东西上才能进入或离开房间。</span>

A short while ago, my sister helped me to carry one of my old bookcases up the stairs.<br><span class="zh">不久前，我的姐姐帮我把其中一个旧书柜搬上了楼梯。</span>

She went into my room and got a big surprise when she saw all those books on the floor.<br><span class="zh">她走进我的房间，看到地板上堆满了书，顿时感到非常惊讶。</span>

'This is the prettiest carpet I have ever seen,' she said.<br><span class="zh">“这是我见过最漂亮的地毯了，”她说。</span>

She gazed at it for some time then added,<br><span class="zh">她凝视着它一会儿，然后补充道：</span>

'You don't need bookcases at all.<br><span class="zh">“你根本不需要书架。</span>

You can sit here in your spare time and read the carpet!'<br><span class="zh">你可以在空闲时间坐在这里，直接在地毯上阅读！”</span>
