# 第 14 课：The Butterfly Effect

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

The Butterfly Effect<br><span class="zh">蝴蝶效应</span>

Why do small errors make it impossible to predict the weather system with a high degree of accuracy?<br><span class="zh">为什么微小的误差会导致无法高精度地预测天气系统呢？</span>

Beyond two or three days, the world's best weather forecasts are speculative,<br><span class="zh">超过六七天之后，它们就毫无价值了。</span>

and beyond six or seven they are worthless.<br><span class="zh">超过六七个之后，它们就毫无价值了。</span>

The Butterfly Effect is the reason.<br><span class="zh">这就是“蝴蝶效应”的原因。</span>

For small pieces of weather--<br><span class="zh">对于那些微小的、局部的天气变化（或现象）……</span>

and to a global forecaster, small can mean thunderstorms and blizzards--<br><span class="zh">对于全球天气预报员来说，“小”可能指雷暴和暴风雪。</span>

any prediction deteriorates rapidly.<br><span class="zh">任何预测的结果都会迅速恶化（或：任何预测的准确性都会迅速下降）。</span>

Errors and uncertainties multiply, cascading upward through a chain of turbulent features,<br><span class="zh">误差和不确定性会成倍增加，通过一连串湍流特征向上扩散，</span>

from dust devils and squalls up to continent-size eddies that only satellites can see.<br><span class="zh">从尘旋和小型风暴，到只有卫星才能观测到的、覆盖整个大陆范围的巨大涡旋……</span>

The modern weather models work with a grid of points of the order of sixty miles apart,<br><span class="zh">现代天气模型使用的是间距约60英里的网格点，</span>

and even so, some starting data has to be guessed,<br><span class="zh">即便如此，一些初始数据仍需推测，</span>

since ground stations and satellites cannot see everywhere.<br><span class="zh">因为地面站和卫星无法覆盖所有区域。</span>

But suppose the earth could be covered with sensors spaced one foot apart,<br><span class="zh">但是，假设地球表面能够被安装上间距为 1 英尺（约 30 厘米）的传感器……</span>

rising at one-foot intervals all the way to the top of the atmosphere.<br><span class="zh">并以1英尺的间隔一直延伸到大气层顶部。</span>

Suppose every sensor gives perfectly accurate readings of temperature,<br><span class="zh">假设每个传感器都能提供完全准确的温度读数……</span>

pressure, humidity, and any other quantity a meteorologist would want.<br><span class="zh">压力、湿度，以及气象学家可能需要的任何其他气象参数。</span>

Precisely at noon an infinitely powerful computer takes all the data and calculates what will happen at each point<br><span class="zh">正午时分，一台性能无限的计算机采集所有数据，计算每个点在</span>

at 12.01, then 12.02, then 12.03...<br><span class="zh">12点01分，然后是12点02分，接着是12点03分……</span>

The computer will still be unable to predict whether Princeton, New Jersey, will have sun or rain on a day one month away.<br><span class="zh">计算机仍无法预测一个月后新泽西州普林斯顿是晴天还是雨天。</span>

At noon the spaces between the sensors will hide fluctuations that the computer will not know about,<br><span class="zh">正午时分，传感器之间的空隙会隐藏计算机不知道的波动，</span>

tiny deviations from the average.<br><span class="zh">这些是相对于平均值的微小偏差。</span>

By 12.01, those fluctuations will already have created small errors one foot away.<br><span class="zh">到12点01分，这些波动已经会在1英尺外造成小误差。</span>

Soon the errors will have multiplied to the ten-foot scale, and so on up to the size of the globe.<br><span class="zh">很快误差会扩大到10英尺范围，并以此类推，直到覆盖全球。</span>
