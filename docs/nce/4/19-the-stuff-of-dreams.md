<NcePair en="It is fairly clear that the sleeping period must have some function, and because there is so much of it the function would seem to be important." zh="很明显，睡眠一定具有某种功能，而且由于这种状态持续的时间很长，因此这种功能必定非常重要。" />

<NcePair en="Speculations about its nature have been going on for literally thousands of years," zh="关于睡眠本质的猜测已经持续了数千年之久。" />

<NcePair en="and one odd finding that makes the problem puzzling is that it looks very much as if sleeping is not simply a matter of giving the body a rest." zh="一个令人困惑的现象是：睡眠似乎并不仅仅是让身体休息的过程。" />

<NcePair en="'Rest', in terms of muscle relaxation and so on, can be achieved by a brief period lying, or even sitting down." zh="从肌肉放松的角度来看，即使只是短暂地躺下或坐着，也能达到休息的效果。" />

<NcePair en="The body's tissues are self-repairing and self-restoring to a degree, and function best when more or less continuously active." zh="人体组织本身具备自我修复和恢复的功能，而且在保持一定程度的活动状态下，其功能才能达到最佳状态。" />

<NcePair en="In fact a basic amount of movement occurs during sleep which is specifically concerned with preventing muscle inactivity." zh="事实上，睡眠期间确实会发生一些活动，这些活动专门用于防止肌肉长时间处于静止状态。" />

<NcePair en="If it is not a question of resting the body, then perhaps it is the brain that needs resting?" zh="如果睡眠的目的不是为了让身体休息，那么或许大脑才是需要休息的对象？" />

<NcePair en="This might be a plausible hypothesis were it not for two factors." zh="如果不是有两个因素的话，这或许是一个合理的假设。" />

<NcePair en="First the electroencephalograph (which is simply a device for recording the electrical activity of the brain by attaching electrodes to the scalp)" zh="首先，脑电图（这是一种通过将电极贴在头皮上来记录大脑电活动的简单装置）" />

<NcePair en="shows that while there is a change in the pattern of activity during sleep," zh="研究表明，虽然睡眠期间的活动模式会发生变化，" />

<NcePair en="there is no evidence that the total amount of activity is any less." zh="没有证据表明总体活动量有任何减少。" />

<NcePair en="The second factor is more interesting and more fundamental." zh="第二个因素更加有趣，也更为根本。" />

<NcePair en="Some years ago an American psychiatrist named William Dement published experiments dealing with the recording of eye-movements during sleep." zh="几年前，一位名叫威廉·德门特（William Dement）的美国精神病学家发表了一项研究，该研究记录了人们在睡眠期间的眼球运动情况。" />

<NcePair en="He showed that the average individual's sleep cycle is punctuated with peculiar bursts of eye-movements, some drifting and slow, others jerky and rapid." zh="他发现，普通人的睡眠周期中会伴随着一些特殊的眼球运动现象：有些眼球运动较为缓慢、平稳，而另一些则突然、快速地发生。" />

<NcePair en="People woken during these periods of eye-movements generally reported that they had been dreaming." zh="在眼球运动期间被唤醒的人通常报告说他们正在做梦。" />

<NcePair en="When woken at other times they reported no dreams." zh="在其他时间被唤醒时，他们表示自己没有做任何梦。" />

<NcePair en="If one group of people were disturbed from their eye-movement sleep for several nights on end," zh="如果有一组人的眼球运动睡眠（REM sleep）连续几个晚上都被中断的话……" />

<NcePair en="and another group were disturbed for an equal period of time but when they were not exhibiting eye-movements," zh="另一组人在同样长的时间内受到干扰，但干扰时他们并没有表现出眼球运动，" />

<NcePair en="the first group began to show some personality disorders while the others seemed more or less unaffected." zh="第一组开始出现一些人格障碍的症状，而其他组则几乎没有受到影响。" />

<NcePair en="The implications of all this were that it was not the disturbance of sleep that mattered, but the disturbance of dreaming." zh="所有这些现象表明，重要的不是睡眠受到干扰，而是做梦受到干扰。" />
