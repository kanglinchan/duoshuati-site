# 第 3 课：Matterhorn Man

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Matterhorn man<br><span class="zh">马特洪峰人</span>

What was the main objective of early mountain climbers?<br><span class="zh">早期登山者的主要目标是什么？</span>

Modern alpinists try to climb mountains by a route which will give them good sport,<br><span class="zh">现代登山者试图通过能给他们带来良好运动体验的路线来登山，</span>

and the more difficult it is, the more highly it is regarded.<br><span class="zh">难度越大，其价值或地位也就越高。</span>

In the pioneering days however, this was not the case at all.<br><span class="zh">然而，在早期的开拓岁月里，情况完全不是这样的。</span>

The early climbers were looking for the easiest way to the top because the summit was the prize they sought,<br><span class="zh">早期的登山者们都在寻找通往山顶的最简单路径，因为山顶就是他们追求的目标。</span>

especially if it had never been attained before.<br><span class="zh">尤其是当这个目标以前从未被实现过的时候。</span>

It is true that during their explorations they often faced difficulties and dangers of the most perilous nature,<br><span class="zh">的确，在他们的探索过程中，他们经常面临极其危险和充满挑战的处境。</span>

equipped in a manner which would make a modern climber shudder at the thought,<br><span class="zh">他们的装备会让现代登山者一想起来就不寒而栗。</span>

but they did not go out of their way to court such excitement.<br><span class="zh">但他们并不是刻意去寻求这种刺激。</span>

They had a single aim, a solitary goal -- the top!<br><span class="zh">他们只有一个目标，一个唯一的目的——那就是登上巅峰！</span>

It is hard for us to realize nowadays how difficult it was for the pioneers.<br><span class="zh">如今，我们很难想象那些开拓者当时所面临的困难有多大。</span>

Except for one or two places such as Zermatt and Chamonix, which had rapidly become popular.<br><span class="zh">除了像泽马特和夏蒙尼这样迅速变得出名的一两个地方，</span>

Alpine villages tended to be impoverished settlements cut off from civilization by the high mountains.<br><span class="zh">阿尔卑斯山区的村庄往往都是贫困的聚居地，被高大的山脉与外界隔离开来。</span>

Such inns as there were were generally dirty and flea-ridden;<br><span class="zh">那些当时的客栈通常都很脏，而且到处都是跳蚤。</span>

the food simply local cheese accompanied by bread often twelve months old,<br><span class="zh">这些食物只是普通的本地奶酪，配着面包；而那些面包往往已经存放了十二个月之久。</span>

all washed down with coarse wine.<br><span class="zh">所有这些都用粗劣的葡萄酒送下肚。</span>

Often a valley boasted no inn at all, and climbers found shelter wherever they could --<br><span class="zh">经常是一个山谷连一家客栈都没有，登山者们只能找到什么地方就在什么地方栖身——</span>

sometimes with the local priest (who was usually as poor as his parishioners),<br><span class="zh">有时候会和当地的牧师一起（这位牧师通常和他的教区居民一样贫穷）。</span>

sometimes with shepherds or cheese-makers.<br><span class="zh">有时候和牧羊人或奶酪制作者在一起。</span>

Invariably the background was the same:dirt and poverty, and very uncomfortable.<br><span class="zh">背景总是一样的：肮脏、贫穷，而且非常不舒服。</span>

For men accustomed to eating seven-course dinners and sleeping between fine linen sheets at home,<br><span class="zh">对于那些习惯了在家享用七道菜的晚餐、并睡在柔软的亚麻床单上的男人来说，</span>

the change to the Alps must have been very hard indeed.<br><span class="zh">转变到阿尔卑斯山区的生活对他们来说一定确实非常艰难。</span>
