# 第 28 课：Patients and Doctors

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Patients and doctors<br><span class="zh">患者与医生</span>

What are patients looking for when they visit the doctor?<br><span class="zh">患者去看医生时，他们在寻找什么？</span>

This is a sceptical age,<br><span class="zh">这是一个充满怀疑的时代。</span>

but although our faith in many of the things in which our forefathers fervently believed has weakened,<br><span class="zh">尽管我们对祖先们坚信的许多事物的信心已经减弱，</span>

our confidence in the curative properties of the bottle of medicine remains the same as theirs.<br><span class="zh">尽管我们对祖先们坚信的许多事物的信心已经减弱，</span>

This modern faith in medicines is proved by the fact<br><span class="zh">但我们对药物疗效的信任却依然与他们一样坚定。</span>

that the annual drug bill of the Health Services is mounting to astronomical figures.<br><span class="zh">这种对药物的现代信仰，从卫生服务部门的年度药品开支不断攀升至天文数字这一事实中得到了证明，</span>

and shows no signs at present of ceasing to rise.<br><span class="zh">而且目前这种趋势还没有停止的迹象。</span>

The majority of the patients attending the medical out-patients departments of our hospitals feel that<br><span class="zh">大多数前往医院门诊部就诊的患者认为，</span>

they have not received adequate treatment unless they are able to carry home with them<br><span class="zh">除非他们能带回家</span>

some tangible remedy in the shape of a bottle of medicine,<br><span class="zh">一瓶药、一盒药片或一小罐药膏这样的实在疗法，</span>

a box of pills, or a small jar of ointment,<br><span class="zh">一盒药片，或者一小罐药膏。</span>

and the doctor in charge of the department is only too ready to provide them with these requirements.<br><span class="zh">负责该科室的医生也非常乐意满足他们的这些要求。</span>

There is no quicker method of disposing of patients than by giving them what they are asking for,<br><span class="zh">没有比给患者他们所要的东西更快打发他们的方法了。</span>

and since most medical men in the Health Services are overworked<br><span class="zh">而且由于卫生系统中的大多数医生都工作过度</span>

and have little time for offering time-consuming and little-appreciated advice on such subjects as diet,<br><span class="zh">因此，他们几乎没有时间去提供那些耗时且不太受重视的建议——比如关于饮食方面的建议。</span>

right living, and the need for abandoning bad habits etc.,<br><span class="zh">正确的生活方式，以及摒弃不良习惯的必要性等等。</span>

the bottle, the box, and the jar are almost always granted them.<br><span class="zh">药瓶、药盒和药罐几乎总是会满足他们的要求。</span>

Nor is it only the ignorant and ill-educated person who has such faith in the bottle of medicine.<br><span class="zh">并不是只有那些无知或受教育程度低的人才会对药瓶（或药物）抱有如此大的信任（或依赖）。</span>

It is recounted of Thomas Carlyle that when he heard of the illness of his friend, Henry Taylor,<br><span class="zh">据记载，当托马斯·卡莱尔得知他的朋友亨利·泰勒生病了之后……</span>

he went off immediately to visit him,<br><span class="zh">他立刻动身去拜访他了。</span>

carrying with him in his pocket what remained a bottle of medicine formerly prescribed for an indisposition of Mrs. Carlyle's.<br><span class="zh">口袋里还装着一瓶剩下的药，这瓶药原本是给卡莱尔夫人开的，用于治疗她的小毛病。</span>

Carlyle was entirely ignorant of what the bottle in his pocket contained,<br><span class="zh">卡莱尔完全不知道自己口袋里的瓶子里装着什么。</span>

of the nature of the illness from which his friend was suffering,<br><span class="zh">关于他的朋友所患疾病的性质……</span>

and of what had previously been wrong with his wife,<br><span class="zh">以及他妻子之前得了什么病。</span>

but a medicine that had worked so well in one form of illness would surely be of equal benefit in another,<br><span class="zh">但是，一种在某种疾病治疗中效果非常好的药物，肯定也会对另一种疾病产生同样的治疗效果。</span>

and comforted by the thought of the help he was bringing to his friend,<br><span class="zh">并且因为想到自己能为朋友提供帮助而感到欣慰（或：感到安慰）。</span>

he hastened to Henry Taylor's house.<br><span class="zh">他急忙赶往亨利·泰勒的家中。</span>

History does not relate whether his friend accepted his medical help, but in all probability he did.<br><span class="zh">历史没有记载他的朋友是否接受了他的医疗帮助，但很可能接受了。</span>

The great advantage of taking medicine is that it makes no demands on the taker<br><span class="zh">吃药的最大好处是它对服药者没有任何要求，</span>

beyond that of putting up for a moment with a disgusting taste,<br><span class="zh">除了暂时忍受一下难吃的味道之外。</span>

and that is what all patients demand of their doctors--to be cured at no inconvenience to themselves.<br><span class="zh">而这正是所有患者对医生所要求的：能够得到治疗，同时自己不必承受任何不便或麻烦。</span>
