# 第 48 课：Planning a Share Portfolio

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Planning a share portfolio<br><span class="zh">规划股票投资组合</span>

How does the older investor differ in his approach to investment from the younger investor?<br><span class="zh">年长的投资者在投资方式上与年轻的投资者有何不同？</span>

There is no shortage of tipsters around offering 'get-rich-quick' opportunities.<br><span class="zh">到处都有那些提供“快速致富”机会的所谓投资顾问。</span>

But if you are a serious private investor, leave the Las Vegas mentality to those with money to fritter.<br><span class="zh">但如果你是一位认真的私人投资者，那就把那种“拉斯维加斯式”的消费心态留给那些有钱挥霍的人吧。</span>

The serious investor needs a proper 'portfolio' --a well-planned selection of investments, with a definite structure and a clear aim.<br><span class="zh">一位精明的投资者需要一个合理的“投资组合”——即一系列经过精心策划的投资项目，这些投资项目应具有明确的结构和清晰的目标。</span>

But exactly how does a newcomer to the stock market go about achieving that?<br><span class="zh">但是，对于一个刚进入股票市场的新人来说，究竟应该如何实现这一目标呢？</span>

Well, if you go to five reputable stock brokers and ask them what you should do with your money,<br><span class="zh">那么，如果你去找五家信誉良好的股票经纪公司，询问他们你应该如何处理自己的资金，</span>

you're likely to get five different answers,<br><span class="zh">你很可能会得到五个不同的答案。</span>

--  even if you give all the relevant information about your age, family, finances and what you want from your investments.<br><span class="zh">——即使你提供了所有关于自己年龄、家庭状况、财务状况以及你对投资的具体期望的相关信息。</span>

Moral? There is no one 'right' way to structure a portfolio.<br><span class="zh">结论？其实，并没有一种“正确”的方式来构建投资组合。</span>

However, there are undoubtedly some wrong ways, and you can be sure that none of our five advisers<br><span class="zh">然而，无疑存在一些错误的做法；您可以放心，我们的五位顾问中没有任何一个人会采用这些错误的做法。</span>

would have suggested sinking all (or perhaps any) of your money into Periwigs.<br><span class="zh">会建议你把所有的钱（或者哪怕一部分钱）都投入到 Periwigs 公司。</span>

So what should you do?<br><span class="zh">那么你应该怎么做呢？</span>

We'll assume that you have sorted out the basics--like mortgages, pensions, insurance and access to sufficient cash reserves.<br><span class="zh">我们假设你已经处理好了所有基本问题，比如抵押贷款、养老金、保险以及确保自己拥有足够的现金储备。</span>

You should then establish your own individual aims.<br><span class="zh">然后，你应该确立自己个人的奋斗目标（或目标）。</span>

These are partly a matter of personal circumstances, partly a matter of psychology.<br><span class="zh">这部分是由于个人情况造成的，另一部分则是心理因素在起作用。</span>

For instance, if you are older you have less time to recover from any major losses, and you may well wish to boost your pension income.<br><span class="zh">例如，如果你年纪较大，那么你从重大损失中恢复过来的时间就会更少，因此你可能会希望增加自己的养老金收入。</span>

So preserving your capital and generating extra income are your main priorities.<br><span class="zh">因此，保护你的资本并赚取额外收入是你最主要的优先事项。</span>

In this case, you'd probably construct a portfolio with some shares (but not high risk ones), along with gilts, cash deposits,<br><span class="zh">在这种情况下，你可能会构建一个投资组合，其中包含一些股票（但不是高风险股票）、英国国债（gilts）以及现金存款。</span>

and perhaps convertibles or the income shares of split capital investment trusts.<br><span class="zh">也许还包括可转换债券（convertibles），或是分割资本投资信托的收益份额（income shares）。</span>

If you are younger, and in a solid financial position,<br><span class="zh">如果你比较年轻，且财务状况良好，</span>

you may decide to take an aggressive approach--but only if you're blessed with a sanguine disposition and won't suffer sleepless nights over share prices.<br><span class="zh">你可以选择采取一种激进的做法——但前提是你必须具备乐观的心态，且不会因为股价的波动而夜不能寐。</span>

If you recognize yourself in this description,<br><span class="zh">如果你在这段描述中看到了自己的影子……</span>

you might include a couple of heady growth stocks in your portfolio, alongside your more pedestrian investments.<br><span class="zh">你可以在自己的投资组合中，除了那些较为稳健的投资之外，也加入一些高增长性的股票。</span>

Once you have decided on your investment aims you can then decide where to put your money.<br><span class="zh">一旦你确定了自己的投资目标，就可以决定将资金投向何处了。</span>

The golden rule here is spread your risk--if you put all of your money into Periwigs International,<br><span class="zh">这里的黄金法则是分散风险：如果你把所有的钱都投到 Periwigs International 公司里，</span>

you're setting yourself up as a hostage to fortune.<br><span class="zh">你就是在听天由命了。</span>
