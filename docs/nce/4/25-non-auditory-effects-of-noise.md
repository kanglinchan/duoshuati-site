# 第 25 课：Non-Auditory Effects of Noise

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Non-auditory effects of noise<br><span class="zh">噪声的非听觉影响</span>

What conclusion does the author draw about noise and health in this piece?<br><span class="zh">作者在这篇文章中关于“噪音与健康”得出了什么结论？</span>

Many people in industry and the Services,<br><span class="zh">那些对噪音有实际经验的人</span>

who have practical experience of noise,<br><span class="zh">那些具有处理噪声问题实际经验的人</span>

regard any investigation of this question as a waste of time;<br><span class="zh">认为对这个问题进行任何调查都是浪费时间；</span>

they are not prepared even to admit the possibility that noise affects people.<br><span class="zh">他们甚至不愿意承认“噪音会影响人们”这一可能性。</span>

On the other hand,<br><span class="zh">另一方面，</span>

those who dislike noise will sometimes use most inadequate evidence<br><span class="zh">那些不喜欢噪音的人，有时会使用非常不充分的证据</span>

to support their pleas for a quieter society.<br><span class="zh">以支持他们关于营造一个更加宁静社会的诉求。</span>

This is a pity, because noise abatement really is a good cause,<br><span class="zh">这真是太遗憾了，因为减少噪音确实是一个非常有益的事业（或：减少噪音是一件很有意义的事情）。</span>

and it is likely to be discredited if it gets to be associated with bad science.<br><span class="zh">如果这种理论或方法与糟糕的科学实践（即不严谨、不科学的研究方法）联系在一起，它很可能会被人们质疑或否定（即失去可信度）。</span>

One allegation often made is that noise produces mental illness.<br><span class="zh">一个常见的指控是：噪音会导致精神疾病。</span>

A recent article in a weekly newspaper, for instance,<br><span class="zh">例如，最近一家周刊上发表了一篇文章……</span>

was headed with a striking illustration of a lady in a state of considerable distress,<br><span class="zh">文章的标题配有一幅引人注目的插图，描绘了一位极度痛苦的女士</span>

with the caption 'She was yet another victim, reduced to a screaming wreck'.<br><span class="zh">配文写道：“她只是又一个受害者，被折磨成了一个只会尖叫的废人”。</span>

On turning eagerly to the text, one learns that the lady was a typist<br><span class="zh">当人们急切地阅读这段文字时，会发现那位女士原来是一名打字员。</span>

who found the sound of office typewriters worried her more and more<br><span class="zh">她发现办公室打字机的声音让她越来越焦虑</span>

until eventually she had to go into a mental hospital.<br><span class="zh">直到最后，她不得不被送进精神病院。</span>

Now the snag in this sort of anecdote is of course that one cannot distinguish cause and effect.<br><span class="zh">当然，这类轶事的一个问题在于人们无法区分其中的因果关系（即哪些事件是原因，哪些是结果）。</span>

Was the noise a cause of the illness,<br><span class="zh">这种噪音是导致疾病的原因吗？</span>

or were the complaints about noise merely a symptom?<br><span class="zh">还是说，那些关于噪音的投诉只是一种症状？</span>

Another patient might equally well complain<br><span class="zh">另一个患者也很可能会提出同样的抱怨。</span>

that her neighbours were combining to slander her and persecute her,<br><span class="zh">说她的邻居们联合起来诽谤她、迫害她</span>

and yet one might be cautious about believing this statement.<br><span class="zh">然而，人们或许会对这一说法持谨慎态度（即不太相信它）。</span>

What is needed in the case of noise is a study of large numbers of people living under noisy conditions,<br><span class="zh">在噪声环境下，我们需要对大量生活在嘈杂环境中的人进行研究。</span>

to discover whether they are mentally ill more often than other people are.<br><span class="zh">为了查明他们是否比普通人更容易患上精神疾病。</span>

Some time ago the United States Navy, for instance,<br><span class="zh">例如，不久前美国海军就……</span>

examined a very large number of men working on aircraft carriers:<br><span class="zh">对大量在航空母舰上工作的男性进行了检查。</span>

the study was known as Project Anehin.<br><span class="zh">这项研究被称为“Project Anehin”。</span>

It can be unpleasant to live even several miles from an aerodrome;<br><span class="zh">即使住在离机场几英里远的地方，也可能很不愉快；</span>

if you think what it must be like to share the deck of a ship with several squadrons of jet aircraft,<br><span class="zh">如果你想象一下：与几支喷气式飞机中队共享同一艘船的甲板，那会是什么样子……</span>

you will realize that a modern navy is a good place to study noise.<br><span class="zh">你会发现，现代海军是一个研究噪声问题的理想场所。</span>

But neither psychiatric interviews nor objective tests<br><span class="zh">但无论是精神病学访谈还是客观测试</span>

were able to show any effects upon these American sailors.<br><span class="zh">都未能显示对这些美国水手有任何影响。</span>

This result merely confirms earlier American and British studies:<br><span class="zh">这一结果仅仅证实了此前美国和英国的研究结果而已。</span>

if there is any effect of noise upon mental health,<br><span class="zh">如果噪音对心理健康有任何影响的话……</span>

it must be so small that present methods of psychiatric diagnosis cannot find it.<br><span class="zh">它一定非常微小，以至于现有的精神疾病诊断方法无法检测到它。</span>

That does not prove that it does not exist; but it does mean<br><span class="zh">这并不能证明该事物根本不存在；不过这确实意味着……</span>

that noise is less dangerous than, say being brought up in an orphanage<br><span class="zh">它一定比，比如说在孤儿院长大，要安全得多</span>

--which really is a mental health hazard.<br><span class="zh">——那才真正是精神健康的危害。</span>
