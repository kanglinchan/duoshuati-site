# 第 35 课：Space Odyssey

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Space odyssey<br><span class="zh">《太空漫游》</span>

When will it be possible for us to think seriously about colonising Mars?<br><span class="zh">我们什么时候才能真正认真地考虑在火星上建立殖民地呢？</span>

The Moon is likely to become the industrial hub of the Solar System supplying the rocket fuels for its ships,<br><span class="zh">月球很可能会成为太阳系的工业中心，为宇宙飞船提供所需的火箭燃料。</span>

easily obtainable from the lunar rocks in the form of liquid oxygen.<br><span class="zh">这种物质可以从月球岩石中轻易提取出来，其形式为液态氧。</span>

The reason lies in its gravity.<br><span class="zh">原因在于它的重力。</span>

Because the Moon has only an eightieth of the Earth's mass,<br><span class="zh">因为月球的质量只有地球的八十分之一，</span>

it requires 97% less energy<br><span class="zh">它所需的能量减少了 97%。</span>

to travel the quarter of a million miles from the Moon to Earth-orbit than the 200 mile-journey from Earth's surface into orbit!<br><span class="zh">从月球到地球轨道（长达二十五万英里）所需的能量，比从地球表面进入轨道（二百英里）所需的能量要少97%！</span>

This may sound fantastic, but it is easily calculated.<br><span class="zh">这听起来可能令人难以置信，但这个结论是很容易计算出来的。</span>

To escape from the Earth in a rocket, one must travel at seven miles per second.<br><span class="zh">要乘坐火箭逃离地球，必须以每秒七英里的速度飞行。</span>

The comparable speed from the Moon is only 1.5 miles per second.<br><span class="zh">从月球出发所需的相应速度仅为每秒1.5英里。</span>

Because the gravity on the Moon's surface is only a sixth of Earth's (remember how easily the Apollo astronauts bounded along),<br><span class="zh">因为月球表面的重力只有地球的六分之一（还记得阿波罗宇航员在月球上行走时是多么轻松自如吗？）</span>

it takes much less energy to accelerate to that 1.5mps than it does on Earth.<br><span class="zh">在月球上，将物体加速到每秒1.5英里所需的能量要比在地球上少得多。</span>

Moon dwellers will be able to fly in space at only three percent of the cost of similar journeys by their terrestrial cousins.<br><span class="zh">月球居民在太空中旅行的费用，将仅为他们的地球表亲进行类似旅程所需费用的3%。</span>

Arthur C.Clark once suggested a revolutionary idea passes through three phases:<br><span class="zh">阿瑟·C·克拉克曾提出一个观点：一个革命性的想法通常会经历三个阶段：</span>

1.  'It's impossible--don't waste my time.'<br><span class="zh">“这不可能——别浪费我的时间了。”</span>

2.  'It's possible, but not worth doing.'<br><span class="zh">2. “这是可能的，但并不值得去做。”</span>

3.  'I said it was a good idea all along.'<br><span class="zh">3. “我一直都说这是个好主意。”</span>

The idea of colonising Mars--a world 160 times more distant than the Moon--will move decisively from the second phase to the third,<br><span class="zh">当有相当数量的人能够在太空中永久居住时，将火星（一个距离地球比月球远160倍的天体）殖民化的想法，将果断地从第二阶段进入第三阶段。</span>

when a significant number of people are living permanently in space.<br><span class="zh">当有相当数量的人能够在太空中永久居住时……</span>

Mars has an extraordinary fascination for would-be voyagers.<br><span class="zh">火星对那些渴望踏上太空之旅的人来说，具有难以抗拒的吸引力。</span>

America, Russia and Europe are filled with enthusiasts--many of them serious and senior scientists who dream of sending people to it.<br><span class="zh">美国、俄罗斯和欧洲都有许多热衷者——其中许多是严肃资深的科学家，他们梦想着将人类送上火星。</span>

Their aim is understandable.<br><span class="zh">他们的目标是可以理解的。</span>

It is the one world in the Solar System that is most like the Earth.<br><span class="zh">它是太阳系中与地球最为相似的行星。</span>

It is a world of red sandy deserts (hence its name--the Red Planet),<br><span class="zh">这是一个遍布红色沙质沙漠的世界（因此它被称为“红色星球”）。</span>

cloudless skies, savage sandstorms, chasms wider than the Grand Canyon and at least one mountain more than twice as tall as Everest.<br><span class="zh">万里无云的天空、猛烈的沙尘暴、比大峡谷还要宽阔的峡谷，以及至少有一座比珠穆朗玛峰高出两倍以上的山峰。</span>

It seems ideal for settlement.<br><span class="zh">这似乎非常适合用于定居。</span>
