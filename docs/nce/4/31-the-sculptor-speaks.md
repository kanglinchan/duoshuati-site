# 第 31 课：The Sculptor Speaks

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

<NcePair en="The sculptor speaks" zh="雕塑家之言" />

<NcePair en="What do you have to be able to do to appreciate sculpture?" zh="要真正欣赏雕塑，你需要具备哪些条件或能力呢？" />

<NcePair en="Appreciation of sculpture depends upon the ability to respond to form in 3 dimensions." zh="对雕塑的欣赏，取决于人们能否理解和回应三维空间中的形态（即雕塑的立体结构与形态特征）。" />

<NcePair en="That is perhaps why sculpture has been described as the most difficult of all arts;" zh="也许正因为如此，雕塑才被人们称为所有艺术形式中最难掌握的一种。" />

<NcePair en="certainly it is more difficult than the arts which involve appreciation of flat forms, shape in only two dimensions." zh="当然，这比那些只需要欣赏二维平面形状的艺术形式要困难得多。" />

<NcePair en="Many more people are 'form-blind' than colour-blind." zh="有'形式盲'的人比有色盲的人多得多" />

<NcePair en="The child learning to see, first distinguishes only two-dimensional shape; it cannot judge distances, depths." zh="正在学习视力的孩子最初只能识别二维的形状；他们还无法判断距离或物体的深度。" />

<NcePair en="Later, for its personal safety and practical needs, it has to develop (partly by means of touch) the ability to judge roughly 3-dimensonal distances." zh="后来，为了自身安全和实际需要，它必须（部分通过触觉）培养出大致判断三维距离的能力" />

<NcePair en="But having satisfied the requirements of practical necessity, most people go no further." zh="然而，在满足了实际需求之后，大多数人就不会再进一步去追求更高的目标了。" />

<NcePair en="Though they may attain considerable accuracy in the perception of flat form," zh="尽管他们在识别平面形状方面可以达到相当高的准确度" />

<NcePair en="they do not make the further intellectual and emotional effort needed to comprehend form in its full spatial existence." zh="他们没有付出进一步的智力与情感努力，去全面理解“形式”在其空间存在中的本质（即形式在三维空间中的具体表现方式）。" />

<NcePair en="This is what the sculptor must do." zh="这就是雕塑家必须做的事情。" />

<NcePair en="He must strive continually to think of and use, form in its full spatial completeness." zh="他必须不断努力去思考和运用形式在空间中的完整性" />

<NcePair en="He gets the solid shape as it were, inside his head--he thinks of it, whatever its size, as if he were holding it completely enclosed in the hollow of his hand." zh="他在脑海中清晰地想象出那个物体的形状；无论这个物体的实际大小如何，他都仿佛能够将其完全握在手中一样。" />

<NcePair en="He mentally visualizes a complex form from all round itself;" zh="他在脑海中从各个角度想象出一个复杂的形体" />

<NcePair en="he knows while he looks at one side what the other side is like; he identifies himself with its centre of gravity, its mass, its weight;" zh="他看着一面时就知道另一面是什么样子；他体会其重心、质量和重量" />

<NcePair en="he realizes its volume as the space that the shape displaces in the air." zh="他认识到这个物体的“体积”，其实就是该物体在空气中占据的空间（即该物体将空气“挤开”后所形成的空间）。" />

<NcePair en="And the sensitive observer of sculpture must also learn to feel shape simply as shape, not as description or reminiscence." zh="对于雕塑而言，敏锐的观察者也必须学会纯粹地将形状视为形状本身来感知它，而不是将其视为某种描述或回忆的象征。" />

<NcePair en="He must, for example, perceive an egg as a simple single solid shape quite apart from its significance as food," zh="例如，他必须将鸡蛋仅仅视为一个简单的、独立的固体形状，而忽略其作为食物的意义。" />

<NcePair en="or from the literary idea that it will become a bird." zh="也不把它看作会变成鸟的文学意象" />

<NcePair en="And so with solids such as a shell, a nut, a plum, a pear, a tadpole, a mushroom," zh="同样，对于贝壳、坚果、李子、梨、蝌蚪、蘑菇等固体" />

<NcePair en="a mountain peak, a kidney, a carrot, a tree-trunk, a bird, a bud, a lark, a ladybird, a bulrush, a bone." zh="山峰、肾脏、胡萝卜、树干、鸟、花蕾、云雀、瓢虫、香蒲、骨头" />

<NcePair en="From these he can go on to appreciate more complex forms or combinations of several forms." zh="从这些出发，他可以进而欣赏更复杂的形式或多种形式的组合" />
