# 第 12 课：Banks and Their Customers

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Banks and their customers<br><span class="zh">银行及其客户</span>

Why is there no risk to the customer when a bank prints the customer's name on his cheques?<br><span class="zh">为什么当银行在客户的支票上打印客户的名字时，这对客户来说没有任何风险呢？</span>

When anyone opens a current account at a bank, he is lending the bank money,<br><span class="zh">当任何人向银行开设一个活期账户时，实际上就是在把钱借给银行。</span>

repayment of which he may demand at any time either in cash or by drawing a cheque in favour of another person.<br><span class="zh">他可以随时要求偿还这笔款项，既可以要求以现金形式支付，也可以要求开一张支票转给另一个人。</span>

Primarily, the banker-customer relationship is that of debtor and creditor--<br><span class="zh">从根本上说，银行与客户之间的关系就是债务人与债权人的关系。</span>

who is which depending on whether the customer's account is in credit or is overdrawn.<br><span class="zh">谁是债务人、谁是债权人，取决于该客户的账户是有结余还是透支。</span>

But, in addition to that basically simple concept,<br><span class="zh">但是，除了这个基本上很简单的概念之外，</span>

the bank and its customer owe a large number of obligations to one another.<br><span class="zh">银行和客户彼此都负有大量的义务。</span>

Many of these obligations can give rise to problems and complications but a bank customer, unlike, say, a buyer of goods,<br><span class="zh">这些义务中的许多都可能引发问题和复杂情况，但银行客户与商品购买者不同，</span>

cannot complain that the law is loaded against him.<br><span class="zh">不能抱怨法律对他不公。</span>

The bank must obey its customer's instructions, and not those of anyone else.<br><span class="zh">银行必须遵守客户的指示，而不能听从任何其他人的要求。</span>

When, for example, a customer first opens an account, he instructs the bank to debit his account only in respect of cheques drawn by himself.<br><span class="zh">例如，客户首次开户时，会指示银行仅凭他本人签发的支票从账户扣款。</span>

He gives the bank specimens of his signature, and there is a very firm rule<br><span class="zh">他向银行提供了自己的签名样本，而银行有一项非常严格的规定。</span>

that the bank has no right or authority to pay out a customer's money on a cheque<br><span class="zh">银行无权也无理由根据伪造签名的支票支付客户的款项。</span>

on which its customer's signature has been forged.<br><span class="zh">（即支票上的签名是伪造的）。</span>

It makes no difference that the forgery may have been a very skillful one:<br><span class="zh">无论伪造技术多么高明，都无关紧要：</span>

the bank must recognize its customer's signature.<br><span class="zh">银行必须认出客户的真实签名。</span>

For this reason there is no risk to the customer in the practice, adopted by banks, of printing the customer's name on his cheques.<br><span class="zh">因此，银行采用在支票上印制客户姓名的做法对客户来说没有任何风险。</span>

If this facilitates forgery, it is the bank which will lose, not the customer.<br><span class="zh">如果这种做法导致了伪造行为的发生，那么遭受损失的将是银行，而不是客户。</span>
