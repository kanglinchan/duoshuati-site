# 第 33 课：Education

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Education<br><span class="zh">教育</span>

Why is education democratic in bookless tribal societies?<br><span class="zh">为什么在没有书籍的部落社会中，教育是民主的？</span>

Education is one of the key words of our time.<br><span class="zh">“教育”是我们这个时代的核心关键词之一。</span>

A man without an education, many of us believe,<br><span class="zh">我们中的许多人认为，一个没有受过教育的人……</span>

is an unfortunate victim of adverse circumstances, deprived of one of the greatest twentieth-century opportunities.<br><span class="zh">是恶劣环境的不幸受害者，被剥夺了20世纪最伟大的机遇之一。</span>

Convinced of the importance of education, modern states 'invest' in institutions of learning to get back 'interest'<br><span class="zh">确信教育的重要性，现代国家‘投资’于教育机构，以‘利息’的形式</span>

in the form of a large group of enlightened young men and women who are potential leaders.<br><span class="zh">收回一群有教养的青年男女，他们是潜在的领导者。</span>

Education, with its cycles of instruction so carefully worked out,<br><span class="zh">教育，其教学流程都是经过精心设计的……</span>

punctuated by textbooks--those purchasable wells of wisdom--what would civilization be like without its benefits?<br><span class="zh">被教科书——那些可以购买的智慧之井——所点缀，如果没有它的好处，文明会是什么样子？</span>

So much is certain: that we would have doctors and preachers,<br><span class="zh">有几点是确定的：我们一定会拥有医生和传教士。</span>

lawyers and defendants marriages and births--but our spiritual outlook would be different.<br><span class="zh">律师和被告，婚姻和出生——但我们的精神面貌会有所不同。</span>

We would lay less stress on 'facts and figures' and more on a good memory,<br><span class="zh">我们会减少对“事实和数据”的依赖，而更加重视良好的记忆力。</span>

on applied psychology, and on the capacity of a man to get along with his fellow-citizens.<br><span class="zh">应用心理学，以及一个人与同胞相处的能力。</span>

If our educational system were fashioned after its bookless past we would have the most democratic form of 'college' imaginable.<br><span class="zh">如果我们的教育体系模仿其无书籍的过去，我们会有可以想象的最民主的‘大学’形式。</span>

Among tribal people all knowledge inherited by tradition is shared by all;<br><span class="zh">在部落社会中，所有通过传统传承下来的知识都是大家共同拥有的。</span>

it is taught to every member of the tribe so that in this respect everybody is equally equipped for life.<br><span class="zh">它被传授给部落的每个成员，因此在这方面每个人都同样为生活做好了准备。</span>

It is the ideal condition of the 'equal start' which only our most progressive forms of modern education try to regain.<br><span class="zh">这是‘平等起步’的理想条件，只有我们最进步的现代教育形式才试图重新获得。</span>

In primitive cultures the obligation to seek and to receive the traditional instruction is binding to all.<br><span class="zh">在原始文化中，寻求并接受传统教诲的义务对所有人都是具有约束力的。</span>

There are no'illiterates' --if the term can be applied to peoples without a script--while our own compulsory school attendance became law in Germany in 1642,<br><span class="zh">没有‘文盲’——如果这个词可以适用于没有文字的民族——而我们的义务教育在1642年成为德国的法律，</span>

in France in 1806, and in England in 1876 and is still nonexistent in a number of 'civilized' nations.<br><span class="zh">1806年在法国，1876年在英国，而在许多‘文明’国家仍然不存在。</span>

This shows how long it was before we deemed it necessary to make sure<br><span class="zh">这表明过了多久我们才认为有必要确保</span>

that all our children could share in the knowledge accumulated by the 'happy few' during the past centuries.<br><span class="zh">我们所有的孩子都能分享过去几个世纪里‘少数幸运儿’积累的知识。</span>

Education in the wilderness is not a matter of monetary means.<br><span class="zh">荒野中的教育不是金钱手段的问题。</span>

All are entitled to an equal start.<br><span class="zh">每个人都应该拥有平等的起点。</span>

There is none of the hurry which, in our society, often hampers the full development of a growing personality.<br><span class="zh">没有那种在我们社会中常常阻碍成长个性全面发展的匆忙。</span>

There, a child grows up under the everpresent attention of his parents;<br><span class="zh">在那里，一个孩子在父母无时无刻不在的关注下长大。</span>

therefore the jungles and the savannahs know of no 'juvenile delinquency'.<br><span class="zh">因此，丛林和热带草原不知道有‘少年犯罪’。</span>

No necessity of making a living away from home results in neglect of children, and no father is confronted with his inability to 'buy' an education for his child.<br><span class="zh">没有离家谋生的必要导致对孩子的忽视，也没有父亲面对自己无力为孩子‘购买’教育的困境。</span>
