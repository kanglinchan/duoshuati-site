<NcePair en="Some old people are oppressed by the fear of death." zh="有些老年人被对死亡的恐惧所折磨（或：有些老年人深受对死亡的恐惧的困扰）。" />

<NcePair en="In the young there is a justification for this feeling." zh="在年轻人身上，这种情感是情有可原的。" />

<NcePair en="Young men who have reason to fear that they will be killed in battle" zh="那些有理由担心自己会在战斗中丧生的年轻人们" />

<NcePair en="may justifiably feel bitter in the thought" zh="有理由感到愤懑（或痛苦）" />

<NcePair en="that they have been cheated of the best things that life has to offer." zh="他们被剥夺了生活中最美好的东西。" />

<NcePair en="But in an old man who has known human joys and sorrows," zh="但对于一个经历过人生种种喜怒哀乐的老人来说，" />

<NcePair en="and has achieved whatever work it was in him to do," zh="并且已经完成了他所能完成的所有工作。" />

<NcePair en="the fear of death is somewhat abject and ignoble." zh="对死亡的恐惧在某种程度上是卑劣且可耻的。" />

<NcePair en="The best way to overcome it--so at least it seems to me--" zh="克服这个问题的最佳方法——至少在我看来是这样……" />

<NcePair en="is to make your interests gradually wider and more impersonal," zh="其目的在于逐渐拓宽自己的视野，使自己的兴趣变得更为广泛且不再那么个人化；" />

<NcePair en="until bit by bit the walls of the ego recede," zh="直到自我的界限一点点消融，" />

<NcePair en="and your life becomes increasingly merged in the universal life." zh="自己的生命逐渐融入到宇宙的生命之中。" />

<NcePair en="An individual human existence should be like a river--" zh="一个人的存在应当像一条河流——" />

<NcePair en="small at first, narrowly contained within its banks," zh="起初很小，被两岸紧紧束缚；" />

<NcePair en="and rushing passionately past boulders and over waterfalls." zh="它激情澎湃地冲过巨石、飞跃过瀑布。" />

<NcePair en="Gradually the river grows wider, the banks recede, the waters flow more quietly," zh="随着时间的推移，河流变得越来越宽广，两岸逐渐后退，水流也变得更加平缓；" />

<NcePair en="and in the end, without any visiblebreak, they become merged in the sea," zh="最终，它毫无阻碍地融入大海，" />

<NcePair en="and painlessly lose their individual being." zh="悄无声息地失去了自身的独立性。" />

<NcePair en="The man who, in old age can see his life in this way," zh="那些在晚年能够以这种方式看待自己人生的人，" />

<NcePair en="will not suffer from the fear of death," zh="就不会害怕死亡，" />

<NcePair en="since the things he cares for will continue." zh="因为他们所珍视的一切都会继续存在下去。" />

<NcePair en="And if, with the decay of vitality, weariness increases," zh="那么，随着生命力的衰退，疲劳感也会随之增加。" />
