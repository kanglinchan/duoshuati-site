# 第 11 课：How to Grow Old

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

How to grow old<br><span class="zh">如何安享晚年</span>

What, according to the author, is the best way to overcome the fear of death as you get older?<br><span class="zh">根据作者的观点，随着年龄的增长，克服对死亡的恐惧的最佳方法是什么？</span>

Some old people are oppressed by the fear of death.<br><span class="zh">有些老年人被对死亡的恐惧所折磨（或：有些老年人深受对死亡的恐惧的困扰）。</span>

In the young there is a justification for this feeling.<br><span class="zh">在年轻人身上，这种情感是情有可原的。</span>

Young men who have reason to fear that they will be killed in battle<br><span class="zh">那些有理由担心自己会在战斗中丧生的年轻人们</span>

may justifiably feel bitter in the thought<br><span class="zh">有理由感到愤懑（或痛苦）</span>

that they have been cheated of the best things that life has to offer.<br><span class="zh">他们被剥夺了生活中最美好的东西。</span>

But in an old man who has known human joys and sorrows,<br><span class="zh">但对于一个经历过人生种种喜怒哀乐的老人来说，</span>

and has achieved whatever work it was in him to do,<br><span class="zh">并且已经完成了他所能完成的所有工作。</span>

the fear of death is somewhat abject and ignoble.<br><span class="zh">对死亡的恐惧在某种程度上是卑劣且可耻的。</span>

The best way to overcome it--so at least it seems to me--<br><span class="zh">克服这个问题的最佳方法——至少在我看来是这样……</span>

is to make your interests gradually wider and more impersonal,<br><span class="zh">其目的在于逐渐拓宽自己的视野，使自己的兴趣变得更为广泛且不再那么个人化；</span>

until bit by bit the walls of the ego recede,<br><span class="zh">直到自我的界限一点点消融，</span>

and your life becomes increasingly merged in the universal life.<br><span class="zh">自己的生命逐渐融入到宇宙的生命之中。</span>

An individual human existence should be like a river--<br><span class="zh">一个人的存在应当像一条河流——</span>

small at first, narrowly contained within its banks,<br><span class="zh">起初很小，被两岸紧紧束缚；</span>

and rushing passionately past boulders and over waterfalls.<br><span class="zh">它激情澎湃地冲过巨石、飞跃过瀑布。</span>

Gradually the river grows wider, the banks recede, the waters flow more quietly,<br><span class="zh">随着时间的推移，河流变得越来越宽广，两岸逐渐后退，水流也变得更加平缓；</span>

and in the end, without any visiblebreak, they become merged in the sea,<br><span class="zh">最终，它毫无阻碍地融入大海，</span>

and painlessly lose their individual being.<br><span class="zh">悄无声息地失去了自身的独立性。</span>

The man who, in old age can see his life in this way,<br><span class="zh">那些在晚年能够以这种方式看待自己人生的人，</span>

will not suffer from the fear of death,<br><span class="zh">就不会害怕死亡，</span>

since the things he cares for will continue.<br><span class="zh">因为他们所珍视的一切都会继续存在下去。</span>

And if, with the decay of vitality, weariness increases,<br><span class="zh">那么，随着生命力的衰退，疲劳感也会随之增加。</span>

the thought of rest will be not unwelcome.<br><span class="zh">一想到可以休息，就不会不受欢迎了。</span>

I should wish to die while still at work,<br><span class="zh">我真希望自己还能在工作的时候就死去。</span>

knowing that others will carry on what I can no longer do,<br><span class="zh">知道其他人会继续做那些我再也无法完成的事情……</span>

and content in the thought that what was possible has been done.<br><span class="zh">并且心满意足地想着：一切力所能及的事情都已经做到了。</span>
