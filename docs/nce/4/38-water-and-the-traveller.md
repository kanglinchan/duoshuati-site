# 第 38 课：Water and the Traveller

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Water and the traveller<br><span class="zh">水与旅行者</span>

What does this text describe?<br><span class="zh">这段文字描述了什么？</span>

Contamination of water supplies is usually due to poor sanitation close to water sources,<br><span class="zh">水源的污染通常是由于水源附近卫生条件差所导致的。</span>

sewage disposal into the sources themselves, leakage of sewage into distribution systems or contamination with industrial or farm waste.<br><span class="zh">污水被直接排放到水源中；污水泄漏到供水系统中；或者水源被工业或农业废弃物污染。</span>

Even if a piped water supply is safe at its source, it is not always safe by the time it reaches the tap.<br><span class="zh">即使管道输送的自来水在源头是安全的，但在到达水龙头时也不一定还是安全的。</span>

Intermittent tap-water supplies should be regarded as particularly suspect.<br><span class="zh">间歇性的自来水供应应被视为特别可疑。</span>

Travellers on short trips to areas with water supplies of uncertain quality<br><span class="zh">前往水质不稳定的地区的短途旅行者</span>

should avoid drinking tap-water, or untreated water from any other source.<br><span class="zh">应避免饮用自来水或来自其他任何来源的未经处理的水。</span>

It is best to keep to hot drinks, bottled or canned drinks of well-known brand names-international standard of water treatment are usually followed at bottling plants.<br><span class="zh">最好选择热饮，或者选择知名品牌生产的瓶装或罐装饮料。这些饮料的生产工厂通常采用国际标准的水处理工艺。</span>

Carbonated drinks are acidic, and slightly safer.<br><span class="zh">碳酸饮料是酸性的，因此稍微安全一些。</span>

Make sure that all bottles are opened in your presence, and that their rims are clean and dry.<br><span class="zh">请确保所有瓶子都在你的监督下被打开，并且瓶口的边缘保持干净、干燥的状态。</span>

Boiling is always a good way of treating water.<br><span class="zh">煮沸始终是处理水的一种有效方法。</span>

Some hotels supply boiled water on request and this can be used for drinking, or for brushing teeth.<br><span class="zh">有些酒店会根据客人的要求提供煮沸的水，这种水可以用来饮用或刷牙。</span>

Portable boiling elements that can boil small quantities of water are useful when the right voltage of electricity is available.<br><span class="zh">在有合适电压的情况下，能烧开少量水的便携式煮水器很有用。</span>

Refuse politely any cold drink from an unknown source.<br><span class="zh">应礼貌地拒绝任何来源不明的冷饮。</span>

Ice is only as safe as the water from which it is made,<br><span class="zh">冰的安全性取决于制作它的水。</span>

and should not be put in drinks unless it is known to be safe.<br><span class="zh">除非确定它是安全的，否则不应该将其添加到饮料中。</span>

Drinks can be cooled by placing them on ice rather than adding ice to them.<br><span class="zh">饮料可以通过将其放在冰块上来冷却，而不是在饮料中加入冰块。</span>

Alcohol may be a medical disinfectant, but should not be relied upon to sterilize water.<br><span class="zh">酒精虽然可以用作医疗消毒剂，但不应依赖它来对水进行杀菌处理。</span>

Ethanol is more effective at a concentration of 50-70 percent;<br><span class="zh">乙醇在浓度为50%至70%时效果更佳。</span>

below 20 per cent, its bactericidal action is negligible.<br><span class="zh">当其浓度低于20%时，其杀菌效果几乎可以忽略不计。</span>

Spirits labelled 95 proof contain only about 47 per cent alcohol.<br><span class="zh">标有95 proof的烈酒只含约47%的酒精。</span>

Beware of methylated alcohol,<br><span class="zh">要警惕甲醇酒精，</span>

which is very poisonous and should never be added to drinking water.<br><span class="zh">这种物质具有很强的毒性，绝对不能被添加到饮用水中。</span>

If no other safe water supply can be obtained,<br><span class="zh">如果无法获得其他安全的水源，</span>

tap water that is too hot to touch can be left to cool and is generally safe to drink.<br><span class="zh">烫得无法触摸的自来水可以放凉后饮用，通常是安全的。</span>

Those planning a trip to remote areas, or intending to live in countries where drinking water is not readily available,<br><span class="zh">那些计划前往偏远地区旅行，或者打算生活在饮用水不易获得的国家的人，</span>

should know about the various possible methods for making water safe.<br><span class="zh">应该了解各种使水变得安全的方法。</span>
