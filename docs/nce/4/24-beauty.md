# 第 24 课：Beauty

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Beauty<br><span class="zh">美丽</span>

What do glimpses of beauty, either in nature or art, often suggest to the human mind?<br><span class="zh">无论是自然界中的美景，还是艺术作品中的美感，它们往往能给人类的心灵带来怎样的启示呢？</span>

A young man sees a sunset and,<br><span class="zh">一个年轻人看到了日落，</span>

unable to understand or to express the emotion that it rouses in him,<br><span class="zh">无法理解这种情感在他心中所引发的反应，也无法表达出自己所感受到的情绪。</span>

concludes that it must be the gateway to a world that lies beyond<br><span class="zh">得出结论：这一定是通往另一个未知世界的入口。</span>

It is difficult for any of us in moments of intense aesthetic experience<br><span class="zh">在那些充满强烈审美体验的时刻，我们任何人都很难抗拒这样一种想法：</span>

to resist the suggestion that we are catching a glimpse of a light<br><span class="zh">我们似乎瞥见了某种光芒，</span>

that shines down to us from a different realm of existence, different and,<br><span class="zh">那种光芒是从另一个存在领域照射到我们这里的；那个领域与我们截然不同……</span>

because the experience is intensely moving, in some way higher.<br><span class="zh">因为这种体验非常令人感动，从某种意义上来说，它的价值甚至更高（即这种体验带来的意义或影响更为深远）。</span>

And, though the gleams blind and dazzle, yet do they convey a hint of beauty and serenity<br><span class="zh">尽管那些光芒刺眼、令人眩目，但它们依然透露出一种美丽与宁静的气息。</span>

greater than we have known or imagined. Greater too than we can describe;<br><span class="zh">比我们以往所知道或想象的都要伟大；同时也超出了我们的描述能力。</span>

for language, which was invented to convey the meanings of this world<br><span class="zh">因为语言是为了传达这个世界的意义而创造的，</span>

cannot readily be fitted to the uses of another.<br><span class="zh">所以它很难被直接应用于其他目的。</span>

That all great art has this power of suggesting a world beyond is undeniable.<br><span class="zh">在某些时刻，大自然本身也具备这种能力。</span>

In some moods, nature shares it.<br><span class="zh">在某些心境下，大自然也具有这种力量。</span>

There is no sky in June so blue that it does not point forward to a bluer,<br><span class="zh">六月的蓝天再蓝，也会让人向往更蓝的天空；</span>

no sunset so beautiful that it does not waken the vision of a greater beauty,<br><span class="zh">日落再美，也会唤起人们对更美之景的想象，</span>

a vision which passes before it is fully glimpsed,<br><span class="zh">只留下一种难以言喻的渴望与遗憾。</span>

and in passing leaves an indefinable longing and regret.<br><span class="zh">但是，如果这个世界不仅仅是一个糟糕的玩笑，</span>

But, if this world is not merely a bad joke,<br><span class="zh">如果生命不是在星辰冷冽光芒中的短暂闪光，</span>

life a vulgar flare amid the cool radiance of the stars,<br><span class="zh">如果存在不是对那些神秘事物的徒劳嘲笑……</span>

and existence an empty laugh braying across the mysteries;<br><span class="zh">那么，这些关于“背后、超越这个世界的事物”的暗示，</span>

if these intimations of a something behind and beyond<br><span class="zh">就一定有着更深层的意义。</span>

are not evil humour born of indigestion,<br><span class="zh">不是消化不良引起的坏情绪，</span>

or whimsies sent by the devil to mock and madden us, if, in a word<br><span class="zh">也不是魔鬼派来嘲笑和折磨我们的奇想——总之，</span>

beauty means something yet we must not seek to interpret the meaning.<br><span class="zh">“美”确实蕴含着某种意义，但我们却不应试图去解读这种意义。</span>

If we glimpse the unutterable, it is unwise to try to utter it,<br><span class="zh">如果我们瞥见了那不可言说之物，试图言说它是不明智的，</span>

nor should we seek to invest with significance that which we cannot grasp.<br><span class="zh">我们也不应该试图赋予那些我们无法理解的事物以意义。</span>

Beauty in terms of our human meanings is meaningless.<br><span class="zh">用人类的意义来衡量，美是没有意义的。</span>
