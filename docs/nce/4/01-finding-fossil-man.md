# 第 1 课：Finding Fossil Man

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Finding fossil man<br><span class="zh">寻找“化石人类”</span>

Why are legends handed down by storytellers useful?<br><span class="zh">为什么说书人流传下来的传说很有用？</span>

We can read of things that happened 5, 000 years ago in the Near East,<br><span class="zh">我们可以了解到发生在5000年前近东地区的事情。</span>

where people first learned to write.<br><span class="zh">人们最初学习书写的地方。</span>

But there are some parts of the world where even now people cannot write.<br><span class="zh">但是，在世界上的一些地方，直到现在人们仍然无法写字。</span>

The only way that they can preserve their history is to recount it as sagas legends handed down from one generation of storytellers to another.<br><span class="zh">他们保存历史的唯一方法，就是把这些故事当作英雄传说，由说书人一代代地传下去。</span>

These legends are useful because they can tell us something about migrations of people who lived long ago,<br><span class="zh">这些传说很有价值，因为它们能为我们提供关于很久以前人们迁徙情况的线索。</span>

but none could write down what they did.<br><span class="zh">但没有人能记录下他们做过的事情。</span>

Anthropologists wondered where the remote ancestors of the Polynesian peoples now living in the Pacific Islandscame from.<br><span class="zh">人类学家一直想知道，如今居住在太平洋岛屿上的波利尼西亚人的远古祖先究竟来自何处。</span>

The sagas of these people explain that some of them came from Indonesia about 2, 000 years ago.<br><span class="zh">这些人的英雄传说解释说，其中一些人大约在2000年前从印度尼西亚过来。</span>

But the first people who were like ourselves lived so long ago that even their sagas, if they had any, are forgotten.<br><span class="zh">但是，那些与我们相似的最早的人类生活在太久以前了，以至于他们的传说（如果有的话）早已被人们遗忘了。</span>

So archaeologists have neither history nor legends to help them to find out where the first 'modern men' came from.<br><span class="zh">因此，考古学家既没有历史记录，也没有传说可以帮助他们查明最早的“现代人类”来自何处。</span>

Fortunately, however, ancient men made tools of stone, especially flint,<br><span class="zh">然而，幸运的是，古代人类使用了石头（尤其是燧石）来制作工具。</span>

because this is easier to shape than other kinds.<br><span class="zh">因为它比其他种类的石头更容易成形。</span>

They may also have used wood and skins, but these have rotted away.<br><span class="zh">他们可能还使用过木材和兽皮，但这些材料都已经腐烂掉了。</span>

Stone does not decay, and so the tools of long ago have remained when even the bones of the men who made them have disappeared without trace.<br><span class="zh">石头不会腐烂，所以即使制造工具的人的骨头早已消失得无影无踪，远古的工具却保存了下来。</span>
