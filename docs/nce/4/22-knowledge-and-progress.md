# 第 22 课：Knowledge and Progress

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Knowledge and progress<br><span class="zh">知识与进步</span>

In what two areas have people made no 'progress' at all?<br><span class="zh">在哪些领域，人们完全没有取得任何进展？</span>

Why does the idea of progress loom so large in the modern world?<br><span class="zh">为什么“进步”的概念在现代社会中显得如此重要（或者说，为什么人们对“进步”的追求如此强烈）？</span>

Surely because progress of a particular kind is actually taking place around us<br><span class="zh">肯定是因为某种形式的进步正在我们周围实实在在地发生着。</span>

and is becoming more and more manifest.<br><span class="zh">而且这种现象正变得越来越明显（或：这种现象正逐渐变得清晰可见）。</span>

Although mankind has undergone no general improvement in intelligence or morality,<br><span class="zh">尽管人类的智力或道德水平并没有普遍性的提升，</span>

it has made extraordinary progress in the accumulation of knowledge.<br><span class="zh">在知识的积累方面，它取得了非凡的进展。</span>

Knowledge began to increase as soon as the thoughts of one individual<br><span class="zh">一旦某个人的思想能够通过语言传达给另一个人，知识就开始增加了。</span>

could be communicated to another by means of speech.<br><span class="zh">通过语言的方式。</span>

With the invention of writing, a great advance was made,<br><span class="zh">随着文字的发明，人类社会取得了巨大的进步。</span>

for knowledge could then be not only communicated but also stored.<br><span class="zh">这样一来，知识不仅可以被传播，还可以被储存起来。</span>

Libraries made education possible, and education in its turn added to libraries:<br><span class="zh">图书馆使教育成为可能，而教育又反过来促进了图书馆的发展（即促进了图书馆资源的丰富与建设）。</span>

the growth of knowledge followed a kind of compound interest law,<br><span class="zh">知识的增长遵循了一种类似于“复利”的规律。</span>

which was greatly enhanced by the invention of printing.<br><span class="zh">这一情况因印刷术的发明而得到了极大的促进。</span>

All this was comparatively slow until, with the coming of science,<br><span class="zh">在科学出现之前，所有这些发展都相对缓慢。</span>

the tempo was suddenly raised.<br><span class="zh">节奏突然加快了。</span>

Then knowledge began to be accumulated according to a systematic plan.<br><span class="zh">于是，知识开始按照系统的计划被逐步积累起来。</span>

The trickle became a stream: the stream has now become a torrent.<br><span class="zh">起初只是细小的水流，后来逐渐汇聚成了一条小溪；如今，这条小溪已经变成了湍急的急流。</span>

Moreover, as soon as new knowledge is acquired, it is now turned to practical account.<br><span class="zh">此外，一旦获得了新的知识，就会立即将其应用于实际中。</span>

What is called 'modern civilization'<br><span class="zh">所谓的'现代文明'</span>

is not the result of a balanced development of all man's nature,<br><span class="zh">这并不是人类所有天性得到平衡发展的结果。</span>

but of accumulated knowledge applied to practical life.<br><span class="zh">而是积累的知识应用于实际生活的结果。</span>

The problem now facing humanity is:<br><span class="zh">人类现在面临的问题是：</span>

What is going to be done with all this knowledge?<br><span class="zh">这些知识将会被用来做什么呢？</span>

As is so often pointed out, knowledge is a two-edged weapon<br><span class="zh">正如人们经常指出的那样，知识是一把双刃剑。</span>

which can be used equally for good or evil.<br><span class="zh">既可以用于行善，也可以用于作恶。</span>

It is now being used indifferently for both.<br><span class="zh">而现在，它被不加区分地用于这两种目的。</span>

Could any spectacle, for instance, be more grimly whimsical<br><span class="zh">比如说，还有哪种场景能比这更加荒诞、又充满阴郁的幽默感呢？</span>

than that of gunners using science to shatter men's bodies<br><span class="zh">炮手们利用科学来摧毁人体，</span>

while, close at hand, surgeons use it to restore them?<br><span class="zh">而近在咫尺的外科医生却用它来修复人体？</span>

We have to ask ourselves very seriously<br><span class="zh">我们必须非常严肃地问自己一个问题。</span>

what will happen if this twofold use of knowledge,<br><span class="zh">如果这种对知识的双重使用，</span>

with its ever-increasing power, continues.<br><span class="zh">在其力量不断增强的情况下继续下去，会发生什么？</span>
