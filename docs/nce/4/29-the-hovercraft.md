<NcePair en="In 1953, a former electronics engineer in his fifties, Christopher Cockerell," zh="1953年，一位五十多岁的前电子工程师克里斯托弗·科克雷尔（Christopher Cockerell）……" />

<NcePair en="who had turned to boat-building on the Norfolk Broads," zh="谁开始在诺福克河口地区从事造船业呢？" />

<NcePair en="suggested an idea on which he had been working for many years to the British Government and industrial circles." zh="他向英国政府和工业界提出了一项自己多年来一直在研究的想法。" />

<NcePair en="It was the idea of supporting a craft on a 'pad', or cushion, of low-pressure air, ringed with a curtain of higher pressure air." zh="这个想法的核心在于：利用一层低压空气作为“垫子”或“支撑层”来托住某个物体（比如飞行器），而该低压空气层则被一层高压空气所包围。" />

<NcePair en="Ever since, people have had difficulty in deciding whether the craft should be ranged among ships, planes," zh="从那以后，人们就一直难以确定这种交通工具应该被归类为船舶、飞机中的哪一类。" />

<NcePair en="or land vehicles--for it is something in between a boat and an aircraft." zh="或者陆地交通工具——因为它介于船和飞机之间。" />

<NcePair en="As a shipbuilder, Cockerell was trying to find a solution to the problem of the wave resistance which wastes a good deal of a surface ship's power and limits its speed." zh="作为一名造船工程师，科克雷尔一直在努力寻找解决船舶波浪阻力问题的方法。波浪阻力会浪费船舶大量动力，并限制其航行速度。" />

<NcePair en="His answer was to lift the vessel out of the water by making it ride on a cushion of air, no more than one or two feet thick." zh="他的解决方案是让船漂浮在一片空气“垫层”上，这片空气垫层的厚度不超过一两英尺。" />

<NcePair en="This is done by a great number of ring-shaped air jets on the bottom of the craft." zh="这是通过飞船底部大量环形喷气装置来实现的。" />

<NcePair en="It 'flies', therefore, but it cannot fly higher--its action depends on the surface, water or ground, over which it rides." zh="因此，它确实能够“飞行”，但它无法飞得更高；它的运动方式取决于它所依附的表面——无论是水面还是地面。" />

<NcePair en="The first tests on the Solent in 1959 caused a sensation." zh="1959年，对“Solent”进行的初步测试引起了轰动。" />

<NcePair en="The hovercraft travelled first over the water, then mounted the beach, climbed up the dunes, and sat down on a road." zh="气垫船先在水面上行驶，然后驶上了沙滩，接着爬上了沙丘，最后停在了道路上。" />

<NcePair en="Later it crossed the Channel, riding smoothly over the waves, which presented no problem." zh="后来，它穿过了英吉利海峡，平稳地掠过海浪——整个过程没有任何问题。" />

<NcePair en="Since that time, various types of hovercraft have appeared and taken up regular service." zh="从那时起，各种类型的气垫船相继问世，并开始被广泛使用（即投入日常运营）。" />

<NcePair en="The hovercraft is particularly useful in large areas with poor communications such as Africa or Australia;" zh="气垫船在通信条件较差的广大地区（如非洲或澳大利亚）特别有用。" />

<NcePair en="it can become a 'flying fruit-bowl', carrying bananas from the plantations to the ports;" zh="它可以变成一个“会飞的果盘”，负责将香蕉从种植园运送到港口。" />

<NcePair en="giant hovercraft liners could span the Atlantic;" zh="巨大的气垫船客轮或许能够横渡大西洋。" />
