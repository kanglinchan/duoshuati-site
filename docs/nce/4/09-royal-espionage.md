# 第 9 课：Royal Espionage

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Royal espionage<br><span class="zh">皇家间谍活动</span>

What important thing did King Alfred learn when he penetrated the Danish camp of Guthrum?<br><span class="zh">当阿尔弗雷德国王突袭古思伦的丹麦营地时，他学到了什么重要的东西？</span>

Alfred the Great acted as his own spy, visiting Danish camps disguised as a minstrel.<br><span class="zh">阿尔弗雷德大帝亲自担任间谍，他伪装成吟游诗人，潜入丹麦的营地进行侦察。</span>

In those days wandering minstrels were welcome everywhere.<br><span class="zh">在那些日子里，流浪的吟游诗人随处都受到欢迎。</span>

They were not fighting men, and their harp was their passport.<br><span class="zh">他们并非战士，他们的竖琴就是他们的通行证。</span>

Alfred had learned many of their ballads in his youth and could vary his programme with acrobatic tricks and simple conjuring.<br><span class="zh">阿尔弗雷德在年轻时就学会了他们许多民谣歌曲；他还可以通过表演杂技技巧和简单的魔术来丰富自己的表演节目。</span>

While Alfred's little army slowly began to gather at Athelney, the king himself set out to penetrate the camp of Guthrum, the commander of the Danish invaders.<br><span class="zh">当阿尔弗雷德的小军队开始在阿瑟尔尼集结时，国王本人则动身前往丹麦入侵者指挥官古思伦的营地。</span>

These had settled down for the winter at Chippenham: thither Alfred went.<br><span class="zh">丹麦军队已经在奇彭纳姆扎营过冬了；阿尔弗雷德也前往了那里。</span>

He noticed at once that discipline was slack: the Danes had the self-confidence of conquerors, and their security precautions were casual.<br><span class="zh">他立刻察觉到纪律松弛的情况：丹麦人拥有征服者的自信，他们的安全防范措施也非常松懈。</span>

They lived well, on the proceeds of raids on neighbouring regions.<br><span class="zh">他们靠着对邻近地区的劫掠所得，过上了富足的生活。</span>

There they collected women as well as food and drink, and a life of ease had made them soft.<br><span class="zh">他们在那里掳掠妇女、搜刮食物和酒水；安逸的生活使他们变得软弱。</span>

Alfred stayed in the camp a week before he returned to Athelney.<br><span class="zh">阿尔弗雷德在营地待了一周后，才返回阿瑟尔尼。</span>

The force there assembled was trivial compared with the Danish horde.<br><span class="zh">那里集结的兵力与丹麦大军相比微不足道。</span>

But Alfred had deduced that the Danes were no longer fit for prolonged battle: and that their commissariat had no organization, but depended on irregular raids.<br><span class="zh">但阿尔弗雷德推断出：丹麦军队已不再适合持久战；他们的后勤补给毫无组织，只能依靠零星的劫掠。</span>

So, faced with the Danish advance, Alfred did not risk open battle but harried the enemy.<br><span class="zh">因此，在面对丹麦军队的进攻时，阿尔弗雷德没有选择正面交锋，而是不断骚扰敌人。</span>

He was constantly on the move, drawing the Danes after him.<br><span class="zh">他始终处于移动状态，引诱丹麦军队跟随自己行动。</span>

His patrols halted the raiding parties: hunger assailed the Danish army.<br><span class="zh">他的巡逻队阻止了敌人的劫掠行动；饥饿开始困扰丹麦军队。</span>

Now Alfred began a long series of skirmishes -- and within a month the Danes had surrendered.<br><span class="zh">随后，阿尔弗雷德展开了一系列小规模战斗——一个月内，丹麦军队便投降了。</span>

The episode could reasonably serve as a unique epic of royal espionage!<br><span class="zh">这一事件完全可以被视为一部关于王室间谍活动的独特史诗！</span>
