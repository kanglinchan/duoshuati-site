# 第 30 课：Exploring the Sea-Floor

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Exploring the sea-floor<br><span class="zh">探索海底世界</span>

How did people probably imagine the sea-floor before it was investigated?<br><span class="zh">在人们开始对海底进行探索之前，他们可能是如何想象海底的样子的呢？</span>

Our knowledge of the oceans a hundred years ago was confined to the two-dimensional shape of the sea surface<br><span class="zh">一百年前，我们对海洋的了解仅限于海面的二维形态。</span>

and the hazards of navigation presented by the irregularities in depth of the shallow water close to the land.<br><span class="zh">以及由于靠近陆地的浅水区水深不均匀所带来的航行危险。</span>

The open sea was deep and mysterious,<br><span class="zh">广阔的海洋深邃而神秘。</span>

and anyone who gave more than a passing thought to the bottom confines of the oceans probably assumed that the sea-bed was flat.<br><span class="zh">任何稍微思考过海洋底部情况的人，很可能都认为海床是平坦的。</span>

Sir James Clark Ross had obtained a sounding of over 2, 400 fathoms in 1839,<br><span class="zh">詹姆斯·克拉克·罗斯爵士在1839年测得水深超过了2,400英寻（约4,267米）。</span>

but it was not until 1869, when H.M.S. Porcupine was put at the disposal of the Royal Society for several cruises<br><span class="zh">但直到 1869 年，“H.M.S. Porcupine”号才被交由皇家学会使用，用于多次科学考察航行。</span>

that a series of deep soundings was obtained in the Atlantic and the first samples were collected by dredging the bottom.<br><span class="zh">在大西洋进行了一系列深海探测，并通过挖掘海底的方式采集到了首批样本。</span>

Shortly after this the famous H.M.S. Challenger expedition established the study of the sea-floor<br><span class="zh">不久之后，著名的H.M.S.挑战者号探险队开始了对海底的研究工作。</span>

as a subject worthy of the most qualified physicists and geologists.<br><span class="zh">一个值得最顶尖物理学家和地质学家研究的课题。</span>

A burst of activity associated with the laying of submarine cables<br><span class="zh">与铺设海底电缆相关的一系列活动迅速展开。</span>

soon confirmed the Challenger's observation that many parts of the ocean were two to three miles deep,<br><span class="zh">很快证实了挑战者的观察结果：海洋的许多区域深度在两到三英里之间。</span>

and the existence of underwater features of considerable magnitude.<br><span class="zh">以及水下存在一些规模相当大的地质结构（或地貌特征）。</span>

Today, enough soundings are available to enable a relief map of the Atlantic to be drawn<br><span class="zh">如今，我们已经收集到了足够多的数据，可以绘制出大西洋的海底地形图（即“海底 relief map”）。</span>

and we know something of the great variety of the sea bed's topography.<br><span class="zh">我们对海底地形的多样性也有所了解。</span>

Since the sea covers the greater part of the earth's surface,<br><span class="zh">由于海洋覆盖了地球表面的大部分区域，</span>

it is quite reasonable to regard the sea floor as the basic form of the crust of the earth,<br><span class="zh">将海底视为地球地壳的基本构成部分，这是完全合理的。</span>

with superimposed upon it the continents,<br><span class="zh">大陆叠加其上，</span>

together with the islands and other features of the oceans.<br><span class="zh">连同海洋中的岛屿以及其他地形特征一起。</span>

The continents form rugged tablelands which stand nearly three miles above the floor of the open ocean.<br><span class="zh">这些大陆形成了崎岖的高原地带，其海拔高度几乎达到了开放海域底部的三英里（约4.8公里）。</span>

From the shore line, out to a distance which may be anywhere from a few miles to a few hundred miles,<br><span class="zh">从海岸线开始，延伸到距离海岸几英里到几百英里的范围内……</span>

runs the gentle slope of the continental shelf, geologically part of the continents.<br><span class="zh">沿着大陆架的平缓坡度延伸；从地质学角度来看，大陆架属于大陆的一部分。</span>

The real dividing line between continents and oceans occurs at the foot of a steeper slope.<br><span class="zh">实际上，大陆与海洋之间的分界线位于一个更陡峭的斜坡的底部。</span>

This continental slope usually starts at a place somewhere near the 100-fathom mark<br><span class="zh">这个大陆坡通常始于水深约100英寻（约183米）的位置。</span>

and in the course of a few hundred miles reaches the true ocean floor at 2, 500-3, 500 fathoms.<br><span class="zh">在延伸数百英里后，它最终抵达真正的海底，那里的水深为2,500至3,500英寻（约4,572至6,400米）。</span>

The slope averages about 1 in 30, but contains steep,<br><span class="zh">坡度的平均比例约为1:30（即每水平延伸30单位，垂直下降1单位），但其中也包含陡峭的、</span>

probably vertical, cliffs, and gentle sediment-covered terraces,<br><span class="zh">可能是垂直的悬崖，以及被松软沉积物覆盖的缓坡地形。</span>

and near its lower reaches there is a long tailing-off which is almost certainly the result of<br><span class="zh">在其下端附近，有一段逐渐变缓的延伸带，这几乎可以肯定是</span>

material transported out to deep water after being eroded from the continental masses.<br><span class="zh">这些物质是从大陆上被侵蚀后，被输送到深水区的。</span>
