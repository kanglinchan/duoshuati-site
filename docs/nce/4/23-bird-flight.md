# 第 23 课：Bird Flight

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Bird flight<br><span class="zh">鸟类的飞行</span>

What are the two main types of bird flight described by the author?<br><span class="zh">作者描述了哪两种主要的鸟类飞行方式？</span>

No two sorts of birds practise quite the same sort of flight;<br><span class="zh">没有两种鸟类的飞行方式是完全相同的。</span>

the varieties are infinite; but two classes may be roughly seen.<br><span class="zh">虽然种类繁多、无穷无尽，但大致上可以分为两类。</span>

Any ship that crosses the Pacific<br><span class="zh">任何横渡太平洋的船只</span>

is accompanied for many days by the smaller albatross,<br><span class="zh">在许多天里，它都伴随着体型较小的信天翁。</span>

which may keep company with the vessel for an hour<br><span class="zh">信天翁可能会与船只结伴一个小时，</span>

without visible or more than occasional movement of wing.<br><span class="zh">翅膀没有明显的运动，或者仅有偶尔的轻微摆动。</span>

The currents of air that the walls of the ship direct upwards,<br><span class="zh">那些被船体墙壁引导向上的气流……</span>

as well as in the line of its course,<br><span class="zh">以及沿着船只航行的方向，</span>

are enough to give the great bird with its immense wings<br><span class="zh">足以让这只拥有巨大翅膀的巨鸟</span>

sufficient sustenance and progress.<br><span class="zh">获得足够的动力和前进的力量。</span>

The albatross is the king of the gliders,<br><span class="zh">信天翁是滑翔鸟类中的“王者”。</span>

the class of fliers which harness the air to their purpose,<br><span class="zh">这类飞鸟能驾驭空气来实现飞行目的，</span>

but must yield to its opposition.<br><span class="zh">但也不得不屈服于逆风的阻力。</span>

In the contrary school, the duck is supreme.<br><span class="zh">而在另一类飞行方式中，鸭子是佼佼者。</span>

It comes nearer to the engines with which man has 'conquered' the air, as he boasts.<br><span class="zh">它越来越接近那些人类用来“征服”天空的飞行器了——正如人类所自豪地宣称的那样。</span>

Duck, and like them the pigeons, are endowed with steel-like muscles,<br><span class="zh">鸭子，和鸽子一样，拥有像钢铁一样强健的肌肉。</span>

that are a good part of the weight of the bird,<br><span class="zh">这些部分占据了鸟类体重的很大比例。</span>

and these will ply the short wings with such irresistible power<br><span class="zh">能以不可抗拒的力量驱动短小的翅膀，</span>

that they can bore for long distances through an opposing gale<br><span class="zh">顶着逆风长距离飞行，</span>

before exhaustion follows.<br><span class="zh">直到精疲力竭。</span>

Their humbler followers, such as partridges,<br><span class="zh">它们那些能力稍逊的同类，比如鹧鸪，</span>

have a like power of strong propulsion, but soon tire.<br><span class="zh">它们拥有强大的推进力，但很快就会疲劳。</span>

You may pick them up in utter exhaustion,<br><span class="zh">即使它们在极度疲惫的状态下被我们捡起，</span>

if wind over the sea has driven them to a long journey.<br><span class="zh">如果海上的风迫使它们进行了长途飞行。</span>

The swallow shares the virtues of both schools in highest measure.<br><span class="zh">燕子则将这两种飞行方式的优点发挥到了极致。</span>

It tires not, nor does it boast of its power; but belongs to the air<br><span class="zh">它不会感到疲劳，也不会夸耀自己的力量；</span>

travelling it may be six thousand miles to and from its northern nesting home,<br><span class="zh">往返于六千英里外的北方巢穴之间，</span>

feeding its flown young as it flies, and slipping through a medium<br><span class="zh">一边飞行一边喂养已经会飞的雏鸟，</span>

that seems to help its passage even when the wind is adverse.<br><span class="zh">穿行于似乎能助其前行的空气中，即使风向不利。</span>

Such birds do us good,<br><span class="zh">这样的鸟类对我们大有裨益，</span>

though we no longer take omens from their flight on this side and that;<br><span class="zh">尽管我们不再从它们忽东忽西的飞行中解读预兆了；</span>

and even the most superstitious villagers no longer take off their hats to the magpie and wish it good-morning.<br><span class="zh">就连最迷信的村民，也不会再向喜鹊脱帽道早安了。</span>
