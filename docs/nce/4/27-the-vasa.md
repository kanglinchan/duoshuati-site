# 第 27 课：The vasa

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

The 'Vasa'<br><span class="zh">“瓦萨号”</span>

What happened to the 'Vasa' almost immediately after she was launched?<br><span class="zh">“瓦萨号”在下水后几乎立刻就发生了什么？</span>

From the seventeenth-century empire of Sweden,<br><span class="zh">从十七世纪的瑞典帝国，</span>

the story of a galleon that sank at the start of her maiden voyage in 1628 must be one of the strangest tales of the sea.<br><span class="zh">这艘加莱昂帆船的故事堪称海洋史上最离奇的传说之一：它在1628年首次航行时便沉没了。</span>

For nearly three and a half centuries she lay at the bottom of Stockholm harbour until her discovery in 1956.<br><span class="zh">在将近三个半世纪的时间里，这艘船一直沉躺在斯德哥尔摩港的底部，直到1956年才被人发现。</span>

This was the Vasa, royal flagship of the great imperial fleet.<br><span class="zh">这艘船是“瓦萨号”（Vasa），属于那个伟大帝国舰队的皇家旗舰。</span>

King Gustavus Adolphus 'The Northern Hurricane',<br><span class="zh">古斯塔夫·阿道夫国王，被称为“北方飓风”……</span>

then at the height of his military success in the 'Thirty Years' War,<br><span class="zh">就在他在“三十年战争”中取得军事胜利的巅峰时期，</span>

had dictated her measurements and armament.<br><span class="zh">亲自规定了她的尺寸和武器装备。</span>

Triple gun-decks mounted sixty-four bronze cannon.<br><span class="zh">三层甲板上共安装了六十四门青铜大炮。</span>

She was intended to play a leading role in the growing might of Sweden.<br><span class="zh">她本应在瑞典日益增长的国力中扮演重要角色。</span>

As she was prepared for her maiden voyage on August 10, 1628, Stockholm was in a ferment.<br><span class="zh">1628年8月10日，当这艘船准备首航时，斯德哥尔摩一片沸腾。</span>

From the Skeppsbron and surrounding islands<br><span class="zh">从斯凯普斯布隆（Skeppsbron）和周围的岛屿上，</span>

the people watched this thing of beauty begin to spread her sails and catch the wind.<br><span class="zh">人们看着这艘美丽的船开始扬起帆，迎风启航。</span>

They had laboured for three years to produce this floating work of art;<br><span class="zh">他们花了三年时间才完成这件浮动的艺术品。</span>

she was more richly carved and ornamented than any previous ship.<br><span class="zh">它的雕刻和装饰比以往任何一艘船都要精美、复杂得多。</span>

The high stern castle was a riot of carved gods, demons, knights, kings, warriors,<br><span class="zh">高耸的船尾城堡上雕刻着神祇、恶魔、骑士、国王、武士，</span>

mermaids, cherubs; and zoomorphic animal shapes ablaze with red and gold and blue,<br><span class="zh">美人鱼、小天使，以及那些被红色、金色和蓝色装饰得绚丽多彩的动物形状……</span>

symbols of courage, power, and cruelty,<br><span class="zh">象征着勇气、力量与残酷的符号……</span>

were portrayed to stir the imaginations of the superstitious sailors of the day.<br><span class="zh">这些雕刻旨在激发当时迷信水手的想象力。</span>

Then the cannons of the anchored warships thundered a salute to which the Vasa fired in reply.<br><span class="zh">随后，停泊在港口的战舰们鸣响了礼炮，以示敬意；作为回应，瓦萨号（Vasa）也开炮回敬了。</span>

As she emerged from her drifting cloud of gun smoke with the water churned to foam beneath her bow,<br><span class="zh">当她从那弥漫着枪炮烟雾的云层中走出来时，船头下方的水面已经泛起了层层泡沫……</span>

her flags flying, pennants waving, sails filling in the breeze,<br><span class="zh">她的旗帜高高飘扬，小旗子随风摆动，船帆在微风中轻轻鼓动……</span>

and the red and gold or her superstructure ablaze with colour,<br><span class="zh">上层建筑的红色和金色熠熠生辉，</span>

she presented a more majestic spectacle than Stockholmers had ever seen before.<br><span class="zh">她展现了一场比斯德哥尔摩市民前所未见的壮丽景象。</span>

All gun-ports were open and the muzzles peeped wickedly from them.<br><span class="zh">所有炮窗都敞开着，炮口从中邪恶地探出。</span>

As the wind freshened there came a sudden squall and the ship made a strange movement, listing to port.<br><span class="zh">随着风势的增强，突然刮起了一阵狂风，船只也出现了异常的摇晃——它向左舷倾斜了过去。</span>

The Ordnance officer ordered all the port cannon to be heaved to starboard<br><span class="zh">军械官命令将所有左舷炮移到右舷</span>

to counteract the list but the steepening angle of the decks increased.<br><span class="zh">以抵消倾斜，但甲板的倾斜角度却越来越大。</span>

Then the sound of rumbling thunder reached the watchers on the shore,<br><span class="zh">接着，轰隆的雷声传到了岸边观望的人们耳边。</span>

as cargo, ballast, ammunition and 400 people went sliding and crashing down to the port side of the steeply listing ship.<br><span class="zh">货物、压舱物、弹药和400人滑落并撞向严重倾斜的船的左舷。</span>

The lower gun-ports were now below water and the inrush sealed the ship's fate.<br><span class="zh">船上的下层炮口此时已经浸没在水中，海水涌入船内，注定了这艘船的毁灭。</span>

In that first glorious hour, the mighty Vasa, which was intended to rule the Baltic,<br><span class="zh">在光荣的第一个小时里，这艘本应称霸波罗的海的巨舰“瓦萨号”</span>

sank with all flags flying--in the harbour of her birth.<br><span class="zh">它带着所有的旗帜，在自己出生的港口沉没了。</span>
