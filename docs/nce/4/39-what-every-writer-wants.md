# 第 39 课：What Every Writer Wants

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

What every writer wants<br><span class="zh">每位作家都想要的东西</span>

How do professional writers ignore what they were taught at school about writing?<br><span class="zh">专业作家是如何忽视他们在学校里学到的写作知识的呢？</span>

I have known very few writers,<br><span class="zh">我认识的作家其实很少。</span>

but those I have known and whom I respect, confess at once that they have little idea where they are going when they first set pen to paper.<br><span class="zh">但我认识且尊敬的人们都会坦承：当他们第一次拿起笔开始写作时，其实对自己要写什么、想要表达什么几乎一无所知（或者说，他们根本不知道自己该往哪个方向去写）。</span>

They have a character, perhaps two;<br><span class="zh">他们有一个角色，也许还有两个角色。</span>

they are in that condition of eager discomfort which passes for inspiration all admit radical changes of destination once the journey has begun;<br><span class="zh">他们处于一种急切不安的状态，这种状态被当作灵感；所有人都承认，一旦旅程开始，目的地就会发生根本性的变化。</span>

one, to my certain knowledge, spent nine months on a novel about Kashmir,<br><span class="zh">据我所知，有一个人花了九个月的时间来创作一部关于克什米尔的小说。</span>

then reset the whole thing in the Scottish Highlands.<br><span class="zh">然后把整个故事的背景改到了苏格兰高地。</span>

I never heard of anyone making a 'skeleton', as we were taught at school.<br><span class="zh">我从未听说过有人会像我们在学校学的那样先列提纲。</span>

In the breaking and remaking, in the timing interweaving, beginning afresh,<br><span class="zh">在不断的拆解和重组、在时间的交错穿插、在重新开始的过程中，</span>

the writer comes to discern things in his material which were not consciously in his mind when he began.<br><span class="zh">作者在处理材料时，会发现一些自己开始写作时并未刻意考虑过的细节或元素。</span>

This organic process, often leading to moments of extraordinary self-discovery, is of an indescribable fascination.<br><span class="zh">这个自然发生的过程常常会带来令人惊叹的自我发现时刻，其魅力实在难以用语言形容。</span>

A blurred image appears; he adds a brushstroke and another and it is gone<br><span class="zh">一个模糊的形象出现了；他添上一笔，又添上一笔，它就消失了</span>

but something was there, and he will not rest till he has captured it.<br><span class="zh">但那里确实有什么东西；他不会罢休，直到抓住它为止。</span>

Sometimes the yeast within a writer outlives a book he has written.<br><span class="zh">有时候，一个作家内心深处的“创造力”（或“灵感之源”）会比他写出的任何作品都更加持久、更加充满生命力。</span>

I have heard of writers who read nothing but their own books;<br><span class="zh">我听说过有些作家，他们只读自己写的书。</span>

like adolescents they stand before the mirror,<br><span class="zh">就像青少年一样，他们站在镜子前……</span>

and still cannot fathom the exact outline of the vision before them.<br><span class="zh">却仍然无法看清眼前这个构想的确切轮廓。</span>

For the same reason, writers talk interminably about their own books,<br><span class="zh">出于同样的原因，作家们总是滔滔不绝地谈论自己的作品。</span>

winkling out hidden meanings, super-imposing new ones,<br><span class="zh">揭示隐藏的含义，同时叠加新的意义……</span>

begging response from those around them.<br><span class="zh">恳求周围的人做出回应。</span>

Of course a writer doing this is misunderstood: he might as well try to explain a crime or a love affair.<br><span class="zh">当然，这样的作家总是会被误解；他们其实就像是在试图解释一件犯罪行为或一段爱情故事一样——这两者都是极其复杂、难以用语言完全表达的事情。</span>

He is also, incidentally, an unforgivable bore.<br><span class="zh">顺便说一下，他也是一个极其无趣、让人难以忍受的人。</span>

This temptation to cover the distance between himself and the reader,<br><span class="zh">这种想要缩短自己与读者之间距离的冲动（或：这种想要拉近两者关系的欲望）……</span>

to study his image in the sight of those who do not know him, can be his undoing: he has begun to write to please.<br><span class="zh">这种想要缩短自己与读者之间距离的诱惑，这种想在陌生人眼中审视自己形象的渴望，可能毁了他：他已经开始为取悦而写作了。</span>

A young English writer made the pertinent observation a year or two back<br><span class="zh">一两年前，一位年轻的英国作家提出了一个中肯的见解</span>

that the talent goes into the first draft, and the art into the drafts that follow.<br><span class="zh">也就是说，才华体现在最初的草稿中，而艺术性则体现在后续的修改稿中。</span>

For this reason also the writer, like any other artist,<br><span class="zh">正因如此，这位作家和其他艺术家一样，</span>

has no resting place, no crowd or movement in which he may take comfort,<br><span class="zh">没有安身之所，没有可以让他获得慰藉的群体或运动</span>

no judgment from outside which can replace the judgment from within.<br><span class="zh">没有任何外界的评判能取代内心的评判。</span>

A writer makes order out of the anarchy of his heart;<br><span class="zh">作家从自己内心中的混乱与无序中创造出秩序。</span>

he submits himself to a more ruthless discipline than any critic dreamed of,<br><span class="zh">他自我约束的严格程度，超出了任何批评者所能想象的。</span>

and when he flirts with fame, he is taking time off from living with himself,<br><span class="zh">当他追逐名利时，他其实是在暂时中断与自我相处的时光</span>

from the search for what his world contains at its inmost point.<br><span class="zh">中断对探索自己世界最核心内容的追求。</span>
