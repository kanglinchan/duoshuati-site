# 第 42 课：Recording an Earthquake

> [新概念英语 · 第四册 流利英语](/nce/ "返回目录")

Recording an earthquake<br><span class="zh">记录地震</span>

What does a pen have to do to record on paper the vibrations generated by an earthquake?<br><span class="zh">一支笔要如何才能在纸上记录下地震产生的震动呢？</span>

An earthquake comes like a thief in the night, without warning.<br><span class="zh">地震就像夜间的小偷一样，毫无预兆地突然降临。</span>

It was necessary, therefore, to invent instruments that neither slumbered nor slept.<br><span class="zh">因此，有必要发明那些既不会打盹也不会睡觉的仪器。</span>

Some devices were quite simple.<br><span class="zh">有些设备的设计非常简单。</span>

One, for instance, consisted of rods of various lengths and thicknesses which would stand up on end like ninepins.<br><span class="zh">例如，其中有一个装置由长度和厚度各不相同的杆子组成；这些杆子可以像九柱戏（ninepins）中的柱子一样竖立起来。</span>

When a shock came, it shook the rigid table upon which these stood.<br><span class="zh">当震动来临时，它震动了这些杆子所放置的坚硬桌子。</span>

If it were gentle, only the more unstable rods fell.<br><span class="zh">如果震动轻微，只有较不稳定的杆子会倒下。</span>

If it were severe, they all fell.<br><span class="zh">如果震动剧烈，它们就全部倒下。</span>

Thus the rods, by falling, and by the direction in which they fell,<br><span class="zh">因此，这些杆子通过倒下以及倒下的方向，</span>

recorded for the slumbering scientist the strength of a shock that was too weak to waken him,<br><span class="zh">为那位正在沉睡的科学家记录下了那次冲击的强度——不过那次冲击实在太微弱了，根本无法将他唤醒。</span>

and the direction from which it came.<br><span class="zh">以及它来自的方向。</span>

But instruments far more delicate than that were needed if any really serious advance was to be made.<br><span class="zh">但是，如果要取得真正重大的进展，就需要使用比那些仪器更加精密的设备。</span>

The ideal to be aimed at was to devise an instrument that could record with a pen on paper,<br><span class="zh">理想的目标是设计出一种能用笔在纸上记录的仪器，</span>

the movements of the ground or of the table as the quake passed by.<br><span class="zh">记录地震经过时地面或桌子的运动。</span>

While I write my pen moves, but the paper keeps still.<br><span class="zh">当我写字时，笔在动，但纸却静止不动。</span>

With practice, no doubt, I could in time learn to write by holding the pen still while the paper moved.<br><span class="zh">毫无疑问，通过练习，我最终能学会保持笔不动而让纸动来写字。</span>

That sounds a silly suggestion,<br><span class="zh">那听起来真是个愚蠢的建议。</span>

but that was precisely the idea adopted in some of the early instruments (seismometers) for recording earthquake waves.<br><span class="zh">但这正是某些早期地震仪在记录地震波时所采用的设计理念。</span>

But when table, penholder and paper are all moving, how is it possible to write legibly?<br><span class="zh">但是当桌子、笔架和纸张都在移动时，怎么可能还能写出清晰可读的文字呢？</span>

The key to a solution of that problem lay in an everyday observation.<br><span class="zh">解决那个问题的关键在于一个日常的观察结果。</span>

Why does a person standing in a bus or train tend to fall when a sudden start is made?<br><span class="zh">为什么当公交车或火车突然启动时，站在上面的人容易摔倒呢？</span>

It is because his feet move on, but his head stays still.<br><span class="zh">这是因为他的脚向前移动，而头部却保持静止。</span>

A simple experiment will help us a little further.<br><span class="zh">一个简单的实验能帮助我们进一步理解这个原理。</span>

Tie a heavy weight at the end of a long piece of string.<br><span class="zh">在一段较长的绳子末端系上一个重物。</span>

With the hand held high in the air, hold the string so that the weight nearly touches the ground.<br><span class="zh">将绳子高高举过头顶，使重物几乎触碰到地面。</span>

Now move the hand to and fro and around but not up and down.<br><span class="zh">现在让手前后左右移动，但不要上下移动。</span>

It will be found that the weight moves but slightly or not at all.<br><span class="zh">你会发现重物几乎不动，或者只轻微移动。</span>

Imagine a pen attached to the weight in such a way that its point rests upon a piece of paper on the floor.<br><span class="zh">想象一下，有一支笔被固定在重物上，其笔尖接触着地面上的一张纸。</span>

Imagine an earthquake shock shaking the floor, the paper, you and your hand.<br><span class="zh">再想象一场地震，地震使地面、纸张以及你的手都在震动。</span>

In the midst of all this movement, the weight and the pen would be still.<br><span class="zh">在所有这些运动中，重物和笔会保持静止。</span>

But as the paper moved from side to side under the pen point, its movement would be recorded in ink upon its surface.<br><span class="zh">但当纸张在笔尖下左右移动时，它的运动就会以墨迹记录在纸面上。</span>

It was upon this principle that the first instruments were made, but the paper was wrapped round a drum which rotated slowly.<br><span class="zh">最早的测量仪器就是基于这个原理制造的——只不过当时的纸张是缠绕在一个缓慢旋转的鼓上的。</span>

As long as all was still, the pen drew a straight line,<br><span class="zh">当一切保持静止时，笔会画出一条直线；</span>

but while the drum was being shaken, the line that the pen was drawing wriggled from side to side.<br><span class="zh">但当鼓被震动时，笔所画的线就会左右摆动。</span>

The apparatus thus described, however,<br><span class="zh">然而，上面描述的装置</span>

records only the horizontal component of the wave movement, which is, in fact, much more complicated.<br><span class="zh">只能记录波动的水平分量，而实际上波动要复杂得多。</span>

If we could actually see the path described by a particle,<br><span class="zh">如果我们能真的看到粒子所描绘的路径，</span>

such as a sand grain in the rock,<br><span class="zh">就像岩石中的一粒沙子一样……</span>

it would be more like that of a bluebottle buzzing round the room; it would be up and down, to and fro and from side to side.<br><span class="zh">它会更像一只绿头苍蝇在房间里嗡嗡飞舞：上下、前后、左右地移动。</span>

Instruments have been devised and can be so placed that all three elements can be recorded in different graphs.<br><span class="zh">已经设计出可以适当放置的仪器，从而能将这三个要素记录在不同的图表上。</span>

When the instrument is situated at more than 700 miles from the earthquake centre,<br><span class="zh">当该仪器距离地震中心超过700英里时，</span>

the graphic record shows three waves arriving one after the other at short intervals.<br><span class="zh">图纸上会显示出三列波以较短的时间间隔相继到达。</span>

The first records the arrival of longitudinal vibrations.<br><span class="zh">第一列波记录了纵向振动的到达。</span>

The second marks the arrival of transverse vibrations which travel more slowly and arrive several minutes after the first.<br><span class="zh">第二列波标志着横向振动的到达；这些振动传播较慢，在第一列波到达几分钟后才传来。</span>

These two have travelled through the earth.<br><span class="zh">这两列波已经穿过地球。</span>

It was from the study of these that so much was learnt about the interior of the earth.<br><span class="zh">正是通过研究这两列波，人类才对地球内部有了如此多的了解。</span>

The third, or main wave is the slowest and has travelled round the earth through the surface rocks.<br><span class="zh">第三列波即主波传播最慢，它是通过地表岩石环绕地球传播的。</span>
